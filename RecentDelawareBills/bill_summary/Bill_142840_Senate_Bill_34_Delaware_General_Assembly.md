## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To amend Senate Substitute No. 1 for Senate Bill No. 34 to require that a tenant, when giving notice of early termination to purchase a home, also provide proof that the tenant has paid any deposit required by the agreement of sale.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds a new documentation requirement tied to early lease termination when a tenant is purchasing a home.
- Specifically requires the tenant to provide **proof** that the tenant has paid **any deposit required** by the **agreement of sale**.
- The proof-of-deposit requirement applies **when the tenant provides notice of early termination** for the purpose of purchasing a home.
- The amendment changes bill text at **line 7** by replacing “sale.” with: “**and proof that the tenant has paid any deposit required by the agreement of sale.**”
- No dollar amounts, percentages, or specific deposit thresholds are stated in the amendment.
- No effective date or implementation deadline is stated in the provided text.
