## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Designate January of each year as “Muslim Heritage Month” in Delaware and formally recognize the holy month of Ramadan and Delaware’s Muslim residents as they observe Ramadan.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Designates **January** each year as **“Muslim Heritage Month”** in the State of Delaware.  
- Recognizes **Ramadan** as a month of “fasting, sacrifice, gratitude, and reflection,” and recognizes Muslim residents’ contributions to Delaware and the United States.  
- States Ramadan for the referenced year will be observed **February 18 to March 20** (lunar calendar timing noted).  
- Encourages Delawareans to celebrate Muslim Americans’ “ingenuity, creativity, cultures, and traditions,” and to raise awareness and appreciation of Ramadan.  
- Encourages **governments, schools, and businesses** to extend **religious accommodations, in accordance with existing law**, for Muslim residents, students, and workers during Ramadan and for **Eid ul-Fitr**.  
- Cites concerns about **anti-Muslim violence and rhetoric** and asserts educational initiatives are important to combating bigotry.  
- Is a **House Concurrent Resolution No. 95** (a resolution; it does not amend the Delaware Code in the provided text).
