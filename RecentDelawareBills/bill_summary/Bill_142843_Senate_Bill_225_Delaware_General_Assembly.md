## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Appropriates funds and sets conditions/limitations for Delaware state government operations for the fiscal year ending **June 30, 2027**, and amends certain statutory provisions (including pay and payroll-related provisions in Titles 14 and 29).  

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Makes operating appropriations across Delaware state agencies (and public/higher education) for the fiscal year ending **June 30, 2027**, with unspent/unencumbered balances generally reverting on **June 30, 2027** (with exceptions for encumbrances under **29 Del. C. § 6521**).  
- Establishes/updates statewide compensation provisions, including a **2.0%** general salary increase for many state employees effective the **first day of the first full pay cycle of the fiscal year**, plus minimum-pay adjustments to grade minimums.  
- Updates key benefit and cost rates, including Workers’ Compensation payroll recovery rate (**1.25%**), state employee pension rate (**24.67%**), judicial pension rate (**20.01%**), and new State Police pension rate (**37.90%**).  
- Changes public and higher education pay timing: effective the **2026-2027 school year**, public school/charter and specified higher education employees must be paid **bi-weekly throughout the year**, and **10/11/12-month** contractual employees’ salary is divided by **26** (not 22).  
- Increases the public education teacher base salary amount in **14 Del. C. § 1305(b)** from **$36,760** to **$39,370** (effective the first day of the first full pay cycle of the fiscal year).  
- Provides large agency appropriations and statewide contingencies (e.g., OMB contingencies, one-time items, salary/OEC contingency), and authorizes certain transfers/adjustments with approvals (often OMB + Controller General; sometimes Joint Finance Committee leadership).  
- Includes Tobacco Master Settlement Agreement allocations totaling **$21,958.2 ASF** (Appropriated Special Funds) for specified health-related and enforcement purposes, with committee recommendation deadlines (e.g., **November 15**, **May 1** reporting).  
- Sets recurring reporting deadlines (e.g., OMB vacancy report **January 15** annually; DTI charge accounting **September 15**; multiple program reports due **May 1**, **December 1**, etc.).  

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Predictable statewide funding framework:** Provides detailed appropriations for FY ending **June 30, 2027** and reversion rules to manage year-end balances.  
- **Across-the-board salary policy:** Provides a **2.0%** general salary increase for many state employees (first day of first full pay cycle of the fiscal year), potentially supporting recruitment/retention.  
- **Teacher compensation increase:** Raises the educator base salary amount to **$39,370** (from **$36,760**) under **14 Del. C. § 1305(b)**, increasing state salary schedule amounts derived from the index.  
- **Standardized payroll cadence for schools/higher education:** Requires bi-weekly payments throughout the year effective the **2026-2027 school year**, which may simplify payroll administration and standardize pay timing across institutions.  
- **Targeted program funding and oversight:** Includes dedicated appropriations and recurring reporting (e.g., Tobacco funds allocations; Delaware Health Care Claims Database conditions; law enforcement tuition reimbursement reporting; Equity Ombudsperson annual report).  

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative complexity and compliance burden:** Numerous approval, transfer, and reporting requirements (e.g., multiple reports due **May 1**, **December 1**, **January 15**, **September 15**, etc.) increase administrative workload.  
- **Reduced pay flexibility for certain entities:** Limits compensation changes without approvals in several areas (e.g., DTI compensation plan changes require approvals; selective market variation rules; restrictions on additional increases for Section 10 designated salaries).  
- **Mandated payroll method changes for schools/higher education:** The shift to year-round bi-weekly payments effective **2026-2027 school year** requires payroll system/process changes and may affect employee cashflow timing (timing change is specified; individual impacts depend on prior election).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Reversion rule:** Unspent balances generally revert to the General Fund on **June 30, 2027**, except funds encumbered under **29 Del. C. § 6521**.  
- **Workers’ Compensation payroll recovery rate:** **1.25%** (changed from 1.40% shown as prior value in markup).  
- **Family caregiving leave benefits rate:**  
  - **0.08% through December 31, 2025**;  
  - **Effective January 1, 2026**, split equally employee/employer at **0.04%**.  
- **Pension funding rates (for budgeting):**  
  - State employee pension rate **24.67%** (components: **13.29%** pension liability; **10.63%** retiree health insurance; **0.75%** OPEB fund).  
  - Judicial pension rate **20.01%**.  
  - New State Police pension rate **37.90%**.  
- **General salary increase:** **2.0%** for many departments (01–77) and DelTech Plan B, effective first day of first full pay cycle of fiscal year; with exceptions listed (e.g., Governor, Uniformed State Police, DTI employees, UD/DSU, National Guard, bargaining unit employees, etc.).  
- **Public education base salary:** Amends **14 Del. C. § 1305(b)** base salary amount to **$39,370**.  
- **Payroll method changes (schools/higher ed):**  
  - Amends **14 Del. C. § 1317** to require that **effective the 2026-2027 school year**, all employees of public schools, charter schools, and specified higher education be paid **bi-weekly throughout the year**.  
  - Amends **29 Del. C. § 2712(a)(1)** to specify that effective the **2026-2027 school year**, public school/charter and specified higher education contractual employees (10/11/12 month) receive the exact annual salary divided by **26**.  
- **Overhead cap on certain higher-ed contracts:** UD/DSU/DelTech overhead/administrative fee capped at **5%** for specified direct agreements (with stated exceptions).  
- **DTI restrictions:** Prohibits DTI from accessing/providing legislators’ emails/communications/calls at another agency’s request except with legislator consent, AG subpoena, warrant, or court order.
