## Purpose
To amend House Substitute No. 1 for House Bill 84 to clarify that the statute does **not** prohibit **any tax-exempt organization** from communicating with its employees about relevant policy issues.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Removes a limitation that referred specifically to an organization “as defined under United States Internal Revenue Code § 501(c)(3)” and replaces it with “organization.”
- Deletes language requiring such communications to be “done in a non-partisan manner.”
- Clarifies (per the synopsis) that the statute does not prohibit **any tax-exempt organization** from communicating with employees about policy issues relevant to:
  - the organization,
  - its mission, or
  - the people the organization serves.
- Expands the clarification beyond § 501(c)(3) organizations to a broader set of “tax exempt” organizations (as described in the synopsis).
- Removes the statutory condition tied to “non-partisan” communications (as reflected by the deleted clause).
- The amendment is contained in **House Amendment No. 1** to **House Substitute No. 1 for House Bill 84**.
