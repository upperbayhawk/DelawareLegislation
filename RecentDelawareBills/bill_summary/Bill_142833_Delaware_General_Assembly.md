## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To formally recognize **February 4, 2026** as **“USO Day”** in the State of Delaware and to honor **USO Delaware** for supporting military members and their families.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Designates **February 4, 2026** as **“USO Day”** in Delaware through a **Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 139**.
- Cites that the first USO Day was proclaimed for **February 4, 1971** by President Nixon (USO 30th anniversary).
- Notes **USO Delaware was established in 1991** to support military members and families transiting **Dover Air Force Base** and serving with the **436th Airlift Wing**, **512th Reserve Wing**, and the **Delaware National Guard**.
- States USO Delaware provides entertainment, nourishment, facilities, and comfort **at no cost** to deploying Armed Forces and supports service members before, during, and after deployment.
- Reports USO Delaware held **over 100** programs/events in Delaware **over the past year**.
- Reports **over 27,000 instances of service** provided in **2025** to active duty, reserve, and guard members and their families.
- States Delaware has supported **over 7,700 families of the Fallen** through USO Delaware’s Families of the Fallen program.
- States USO Delaware has **not missed a Dignified Transfer support request since 1991**.
