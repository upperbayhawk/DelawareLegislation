## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Rename the “Joint Legislative Oversight and Sunset Committee” to the “Joint Legislative Oversight Committee” across Delaware Code provisions, and make a related technical correction to the title of Chapter 102, Title 29.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Renames the “Joint Legislative Oversight and Sunset Committee” to the “Joint Legislative Oversight Committee.” (Synopsis; reflected in multiple Title 4 and Title 29 amendments)
- States the rename reflects the Committee’s evolved emphasis on “oversight and performance evaluation” rather than frequent “sunsetting.” (Synopsis)
- Expressly states the Act **does not eliminate or lessen** the Committee’s authority to “sunset” an entity. (Synopsis)
- Updates statutory references connected to: Alcoholic Beverage Control Commissioner staffing review (Title 4 § 303), legislative compensation (Title 29 § 710), interim rule review reporting (Title 29 § 910), FOIA open meetings carve-out list (Title 29 § 10004), professional regulation exam oversight (Title 29 § 8735), and Chapter 102 title/definitions/composition provisions. (Bill text)
- Maintains committee composition: **5 Senators** appointed by the President Pro Tempore and **5 Representatives** appointed by the Speaker. (Title 29 § 10203(a))
- Leaves the listed additional compensation amounts unchanged but applies them to the renamed committee: **$4,670** for Chair/Vice Chair; **$3,929** for members. (Title 29 § 710(c)(11)-(12))
- Makes a “technical correction” to the chapter title for Chapter 102, Title 29. (Synopsis; Section 5)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved public clarity/accuracy of committee name**: The synopsis states the prior name had “grown confusing to the public” because sunsetting was used rarely relative to oversight work.
- **Aligns statutory language with current committee practice**: The synopsis notes that since 2016 the committee reviewed **40 entities** and “sunsetted” **6**, and voted in **January 2026** to update its name.
