## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To designate **January 2026** as **“National Mentoring Month”** in the State of Delaware and encourage Delawareans to support mentoring efforts statewide.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Designates **January 2026** as **“National Mentoring Month”** in Delaware.  
- Encourages **all Delawareans** to support mentoring efforts that “empower and uplift” young people across the state.  
- Cites mentoring as an **evidence-based** prevention and intervention strategy benefiting youth across demographics.  
- Notes claimed mental health benefits (e.g., reduced unhealthy coping mechanisms; improved relationships; reduced parental stress).  
- Highlights claimed educational benefits (e.g., improved attendance, grades, test scores, classroom behavior; increased graduation; reduced dropout; higher college enrollment).  
- Calls out particular benefits for youth facing additional challenges (including **foster youth**) such as improved mental health, educational attainment, employment opportunities, and housing stability.  
- Frames mentoring as supporting juvenile justice/violence prevention and reducing likelihood of drug and alcohol use.  
- Identifies a “mentoring gap,” stating **nearly one-third** of U.S. young people lack a trusted adult outside the home.
