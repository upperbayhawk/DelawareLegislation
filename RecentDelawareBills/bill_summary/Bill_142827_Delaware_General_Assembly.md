## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To formally recognize **January 2026** as **“Human Trafficking Awareness Month”** in Delaware to increase awareness of human trafficking in the State.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Recognizes **January 2026** as **“Human Trafficking Awareness Month”** in Delaware (a **House Concurrent Resolution No. 86**).
- Frames human trafficking as a **public safety, public health, and child welfare** issue requiring prevention, early identification, intervention, and offender accountability.
- Cites national data: the **National Human Trafficking Hotline** reported **nearly 12,000 trafficking cases** and **more than 21,800 potential victims** in **2024**.
- Cites Delaware child-victim vulnerability: the **2024 Delaware Juvenile Trafficking Report** identified the **youngest suspected victim as 4 years old**.
- Highlights healthcare screening: **Nemours Children’s Health** completed **more than 32,500 screenings since 2020** for youth ages **12–18**; in **Calendar Year 2025**, **nearly 5,000 youth** were screened and **25 pediatric patients** were treated by forensic nurse examiners for trafficking as a chief complaint.
- Notes child-serving system screening: in **Calendar Year 2024**, the **CSE-IT** tool was used within the Department of Services for Children, Youth and Their Families, producing **124 youth screened statewide**, including **92 Report Line intake screenings**.
- Notes law enforcement activity: within the past **six months**, New Castle County Police identified **17 potential victims**, made **7 arrests**, and seized **more than $16,000** in illegal proceeds.
- Notes agency training: **DelDOT** reported **456 employees** completed Human Trafficking Awareness Training during **Fiscal Year 2025**.
