## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To formally recognize and celebrate Lunar New Year 2026 in Delaware and extend best wishes to residents who observe the holiday.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Recognizes Lunar New Year (also called the Spring Festival or Chinese New Year) as beginning **February 17, 2026** and being celebrated through **March 3, 2026** (per the lunar calendar).
- Notes **2026 is the Year of the Horse**, described as symbolizing strength, resilience, energy, progress, independence, and perseverance.
- Describes Lunar New Year as a time for family reunions, honoring ancestors, observing cultural traditions, and welcoming health and prosperity.
- Highlights the **Lantern Festival** as the culmination of celebrations, including lantern displays, lion/dragon dances, performances, parades, and fireworks.
- Acknowledges Lunar New Year is celebrated by many Asian cultures, including (non-exhaustive) **Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese, Malaysian, Filipino, Indonesian**, and other Asian and Pacific Islander communities.
- Recognizes contributions of Delaware’s **Asian American and Pacific Islander** communities to the State’s cultural, civic, educational, and economic life.
- States the General Assembly’s intent to promote inclusivity, respect, and representation by celebrating cultural heritage.
