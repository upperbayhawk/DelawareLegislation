## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 30, § 5402 of the Delaware Code to (1) eliminate the **state** realty transfer tax on **residential** property conveyances valued at **$350,000 or less**, and (2) **phase down** the **state** realty transfer tax rate on **residential** conveyances valued **more than $350,000 but less than $500,000** over **4 years**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a **$350,000 threshold**: for **residential** conveyances with property value **$350,000 or less**, **no state realty transfer tax** is due.  
- Establishes a **rate schedule** for **residential** conveyances **>$350,000 and <$500,000**, reducing the **state** rate over time.  
- The bill implements the reduction in **four steps**, with effective dates on **January 1** of **2027, 2028, 2029, and 2030**.  
- **Rate on $350k–$500k residential conveyances** changes as follows (state portion): **2.75% (2027)** → **2.5% (2028)** → **2.25% (2029)** → **2.0% (2030)**.  
- In jurisdictions where the **municipality/county has enacted the full 1.5% local realty transfer tax**, the state rate for the $350k–$500k band is lower: **2.25% (2027)** → **2.0% (2028)** → **1.75% (2029)** → **1.5% (2030)**.  
- The general rule that the tax is **payable at recording** and **apportioned equally between grantor and grantee** remains in the amended language for the affected provisions.  
- The synopsis states the reductions are **1/4% per year for 4 years** for the $350k–$500k residential band.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Direct tax relief for residential transactions at or below $350,000** by setting the **state tax to 0%** for that value band (bill text § 5402(a)(2)).  
- **Gradual reduction** of the state tax burden for **$350,000–$500,000** residential conveyances via a **step-down schedule** (2.75% to 2.0% by 2030) (bill text §§ 1–4; effective dates § 5).  
- Maintains the existing approach that the tax is **split equally** between buyer and seller (grantor/grantee) in the amended provisions.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)

- Creates **multiple effective-date versions** of § 5402 that change annually (2027–2030), which may increase **administrative complexity** for practitioners applying the correct rate by date.

## Key Policy Changes
- **New state exemption (residential ≤ $350,000):** “there shall be **no realty transfer tax payable to the state**” for residential property conveyances **having a property value of $350,000 or less** (§ 5402(a)(2)).  
- **Phased state rate reduction for residential >$350,000 and <$500,000:**
  - **Effective Jan. 1, 2027 (Section 1):** **2.75%**; if full local 1.5% enacted, **2.25%** (§ 5402(a)(3) as amended).  
  - **Effective Jan. 1, 2028 (Section 2):** **2.5%**; if full local 1.5% enacted, **2.0%**.  
  - **Effective Jan. 1, 2029 (Section 3):** **2.25%**; if full local 1.5% enacted, **1.75%**.  
  - **Effective Jan. 1, 2030 (Section 4):** **2.0%**; if full local 1.5% enacted, **1.5%**.  
- Keeps the rule in the amended provisions that the tax is **payable at the time of recording** and **apportioned equally between grantor and grantee**.

## Who Benefits
