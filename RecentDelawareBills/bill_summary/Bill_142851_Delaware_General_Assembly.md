## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Urge PJM Interconnection (PJM) to (1) extend capacity market “price collars” for 2 years at the current rate of **$325/MW-day**, and (2) reform PJM’s interconnection queue so new generation can come online faster.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- This measure is a **House Concurrent Resolution (HCR 94)**; it **urges** action by PJM but **does not change Delaware statute**.  
- The resolution asks PJM to extend capacity auction **price collars for 2 years** at the **current rate of $325/MW-day**.  
- It cites a **July 2025** PJM capacity auction record high price of **$329/MW-day** and states capacity prices have risen to **nine times their 2023 level**.  
- It asserts PJM price collars have saved customers **more than $8 billion**.  
- It notes the **December 2025** auction was capped at the same price, but that the price collars are **set to expire**.  
- It references PJM’s decision process on price collars, including a **survey and comment period ending January 30, 2026**.  
- It encourages PJM to reform the **interconnection queue** and accelerate processing of new interconnection applications.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Customer price protection (temporary):** Extending price collars at **$325/MW-day** is presented as a way to protect households and businesses from “even higher costs,” especially given the cited **$329/MW-day** result in **July 2025** and the statement that prices are **9×** 2023 levels.  
- **Supports near-term affordability goals:** The resolution cites past customer savings of **>$8 billion** attributed to price collars.  
- **Encourages faster new supply:** Queue reform is encouraged to “allow for increased generation capacity to come online faster” and to “get cheaper energy online more quickly.”

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Nonbinding effect:** As a concurrent resolution, it **urges** PJM action but does not require PJM to adopt the requested price collar extension or queue reforms.  
- **Higher-than-historic price level acknowledged:** The text states **$325/MW-day** is “significantly higher than historic prices,” implying continued high capacity price levels even with collars in place.  
- **Time-limited approach:** The resolution describes price collars as “temporary,” and does not itself implement the “real solutions” it lists (e.g., forecasting, queue clearing), relying on PJM follow-through.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Current expiration date not specified:** The resolution states the collars are “set to expire” but does not provide the specific expiration date.  
- **Definition/details of “price collars” not specified:** The resolution does not define how the collar operates (cap/floor mechanics) or which auctions/products it applies to.  
- **Scope of queue reforms not specified:** It encourages reforms but does not specify particular rule changes, performance metrics, timelines, or enforcement mechanisms for queue processing.  
- **Which “current rate” governs:** It requests extension at “the current rate of **$325/MW-day**,” while also citing **$329/MW-day** in July 2025; the relationship between these figures is not explained in the text.
