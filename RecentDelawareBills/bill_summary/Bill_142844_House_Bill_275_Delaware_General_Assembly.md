## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Authorize and appropriate funding for Delaware capital projects for the fiscal year ending **June 30, 2027**, including: issuance of State **general obligation (GO) bonds**, appropriations from the **Transportation Trust Fund (TTF)**, authorization of **Delaware Transportation Authority (DTA) revenue bonds**, appropriations of **General Funds**, use of **Escheat–Special Fund** one-time money, and **reprogramming** of certain prior appropriations. Also amends selected statutory provisions and sets administrative controls for project delivery and reporting.

---

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes issuance of up to **$354,920,000** in **20-year State GO bonds** for FY2027 capital projects (maturing **no later than 20 years** from issuance).  
- Provides for **local share of school bonds** totaling **$75,443,068**, “at the discretion of the school district.”  
- Appropriates **$221,173,915** from the **State General Fund** for specified capital purposes; unspent/unencumbered balances revert on **June 30, 2029**.  
- Appropriates **$78,935,531** in **one-time special funds** from the **Escheat–Special Fund balance** for specified projects (including K-12 school items and a $20,000,000 Community Transportation Fund item under Department of State).  
- Authorizes **$277,780,733** in FY2027 **Transportation Trust Fund** spending for DelDOT: **Road System $189,145,410; Grants/Allocations $41,000,000; Transit $5,278,400; Support System $42,356,923**.  
- Authorizes DTA to issue revenue bonds **not to exceed $26,741,437**, with **not more than $25,000,000 plus premium** for projects and up to **$1,741,437** for issuance costs/reserves.  
- Establishes multiple reporting, oversight, and procurement provisions (e.g., **quarterly bond-bill expenditure reports**, OMB construction management control, design-build authorization for **up to 12** OMB projects, CM/GC authorization for **up to 20** DelDOT projects).  
- Includes targeted statutory/policy changes, including raising the Escheat receipts cap deposited into the General Fund to **$614,000,000** (from **$554,000,000**) and setting new/modified requirements for school facilities (e.g., water bottle filling station minimums).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Funds a broad capital program** across agencies (grand total shown in the schedule as **$934,410,179** across bond authorizations, TTF, one-time special funds, and general funds).  
- **Transportation investment** is explicitly funded at **$277,780,733** (TTF) for road, transit, and support systems, with detailed project listings in Appendix A.  
- **Affordable housing support** via General Fund appropriations to DSHA totaling **$37,000,000** (e.g., **$19,000,000** Affordable Rental Housing Program; **$10,000,000** Urban Redevelopment; **$4,000,000** Strong Neighborhoods Housing Fund; **$1,500,000** Workforce Housing).  
- **Clean water and drinking water financing** supported through DHSS **Drinking Water State Revolving Fund: $8,000,052** and DNREC **Clean Water SRF: $5,700,000**, including a requirement that **at least $4,600,000** support affordability for low-income/traditionally underserved communities.  
- **School facility safety and upgrades** supported through one-time special funds including **$9,138,000** for School Safety and Security and **$9,298,949** for K-12 network IT infrastructure.  
- **Administrative controls** (quarterly reporting; oversight committees; approval requirements for security systems) are intended to improve visibility and coordination of capital spending.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Debt issuance**: authorizes up to **$354,920,000** in GO bonds plus DTA revenue bonds up to **$26,741,437**, increasing long-term obligations (bonds may mature up to **20 years** from issuance).  
- **Local fiscal responsibility for schools**: identifies **$75,443,068** as the **local share** for school bonds, at district discretion, which may require local financing decisions.  
- **Zoning/local control limitations**: several provisions exempt state projects from local zoning/permitting (e.g., DelDOT maintenance facilities; DNREC Auburn Valley elements; 312 Cherry Lane medically assisted treatment facility; certain school bus storage use), reducing local jurisdiction authority as written.  
- **Procurement waivers/alternative methods**: authorizes design-build and best-value processes with stated waivers in certain contexts (e.g., OMB community workforce/DBE pilot waives **29 Del. C. § 6962(d)(13)a.4.** for those pilot awards), which may reduce use of standard low-bid approaches for covered projects.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Effective date mechanics**: the bill states it “shall take effect in accordance with the provisions of state law,” without a single calendar effective date.  
- **Which OMB projects use design-build**: OMB may use design-build for **up to 12** projects, but only one is explicitly designated in text (**704 N. King Street renovation**). The remaining projects are not listed in the bill text.  
- **Community Workforce Agreement pilot details**: OMB “shall set the percentage of work” performed by local labor organizations, but the bill does not specify the percentage thresholds.  
- **School district “reorganized” definition for Enhanced MCI optional match**: the bill references “reorganized school districts” effective July 1 (year edited), but does not define “reorganized” within the provided text.  
- **Noise/objection standard for DelDOT off-hours work**: DelDOT may proceed if it “does not receive a significant number of objections,” but “significant number” is not defined.

## Fiscal or Administrative Impact
- **Reversion rules**
  - Section 1 authorizations: unexpended/unencumbered balances (excluding TTF balances) remaining by **June 30, 2029** are subject to reversion or reauthorization.  
  - General Fund appropriations: unexpended/unencumbered by **June 30, 2029** revert to the General Fund.
- **Reprogramming**
  - Directs State Treasurer to transfer and reauthorize up to **$38,096,517.12** from listed General Fund project balances to new purposes; the table also shows **$1,600,000.00** in reauthorizations/reprogramming in the capital schedule.
- **New/expanded reporting**
  - **Quarterly expenditure status reports** required for all agencies and public school districts receiving bond bill funds (format developed by OMB; submitted to OMB Director and Controller General).  
  - Additional agency-specific reports: e.g., DSHA Affordable Rental Housing report due **April 30**; DNREC waterway management report due **September 1** annually; DelDOT monthly report on SSPMF progress; OMB annual pilot report due **January 1**; DelDOT annual CTF report due **June 30**.
- **Statutory cap change**
  - Amends 29 Del. C. § 6102(s) to increase the maximum annual escheat receipts deposited into the General Fund from **$554,000,000** to **$614,000,000**.

  - **Water bottle filling stations** required in new schools and schools undergoing additions or major renovations, with minimums including:

    - **At least 1 near gyms/outdoor recreation/high-traffic areas**

  - **HVAC School Energy Audit**: required for newly constructed schools and schools undergoing HVAC system upgrades (audit through the Delaware Sustainable Energy Utility).
- **School safety fund eligibility condition**
  - LEAs employing constables must ensure completion of a “nationally recognized” school-based security/policing course and annual trainings; failure makes the LEA **ineligible for funding** under the School Safety and Security Fund.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Bonding / appropriations**
  - Authorizes State GO bonds up to **$354,920,000** (20-year max maturity) and identifies **$75,443,068** local share for school bonds.  
  - Appropriates **$221,173,915** General Funds and **$78,935,531** Escheat–Special Fund one-time funds for specified capital items.  
  - Authorizes DTA revenue bonds up to **$26,741,437** (project use capped at **$25,000,000 plus premium**; issuance costs/reserves up to **$1,741,437**).
- **Statutory amendment**
  - Raises annual cap on escheat receipts deposited into General Fund to **$614,000,000** (from **$554,000,000**) in 29 Del. C. § 6102(s).
- **Capital project oversight and procurement**
  - OMB designated as responsible for design/construction of all OMB-listed projects; sets timelines and reversion rules for unencumbered funds after “substantially complete” (occupied by **75% of planned tenants** or as determined by Appeals Board), with reversion **one year** after substantial completion.  
  - Authorizes design-build for **up to 12** OMB projects (with listed statutory provisions incorporated and others waived as specified); explicitly designates **704 N. King Street** as a design-build project.  
  - DelDOT authorized to use CM/GC delivery for **up to 20** transportation projects, with specified labor/bonding/trust protections.
- **Education facility requirements**
  - Water bottle filling stations required with specified minimum distribution and density requirements.  
  - CN square footage growth capped at **1%** for state share; overage paid locally.  
  - Market pressure disallowed for CNs approved **Nov. 2023 or later**.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Quarterly reporting compliance** required for agencies and school districts receiving bond bill funds (submission to OMB Director and Controller General).  
- **Security systems approval requirement**: before any state agency installs or modifies a security system, it must obtain approval from **OMB, DSHS, and DTI** (card access/security standards).  
- **DelDOT speed enforcement thresholds** (civil violations): authorized locations may ticket vehicles exceeding the posted limit by **6 miles per hour or more** (Main Street Newark business district; McKennans Church Rd; Milltown Rd), pursuant to amended **21 Del. C. § 4170A** as referenced.  
- **LEA eligibility condition for School Safety and Security Fund**: failure to meet constable training and list-reporting requirements renders the LEA **ineligible for funding** under the program.  
- **Fund recoveries (audit/claw-back)**: misuse/mishandling of certain funds (referencing U.S. Treasury rules) may be subject to **claw-back** and “other appropriate measures,” including possible **reduction or withholding of other State funds**.  
- **Tax ditch “inactive” rule**: DNREC may assume officer powers for tax ditch organizations with **0 officers for at least 1 year**, with authority to access inactive tax ditch funds for maintenance, expiring upon filling the vacancy.
