## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Recognize **January 27, 2026** as **International Holocaust Remembrance Day** in Delaware and encourage statewide observance through **education, reflection, and remembrance**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Formally recognizes **January 27, 2026** as **International Holocaust Remembrance Day** by concurrent resolution of the General Assembly.
- Notes the United Nations designation of January 27 as the official remembrance day, marking the liberation of **Auschwitz-Birkenau**.
- Cites Holocaust historical figures, including **at least 1.3 million** deported to Auschwitz (1940–1945) and **at least 1.1 million** exterminated there, “the vast majority” Jewish.
- States an estimated **six million Jewish people** were murdered during the Holocaust, described as **more than 60%** of Europe’s Jewish population.
- Highlights increased antisemitic hate crime incidents in the U.S.: **683 (2020)**, **1,832 (2023)**, and **1,938 (2024)**.
- Cites the American Jewish Committee’s 2024 report: **one-third** of American Jews report being personally targeted by antisemitism; among ages **18–29**, **41%** report being targeted at least once in the past year.
- References a specific violent incident: **May 21, 2025**, shooting of two Israeli Embassy staff members outside the Capital Jewish Museum in Washington, D.C.
- Encourages Delawareans to observe the day through **education, reflection, and remembrance** (encouragement; no mandates described).
