## Purpose
To officially name the hill west of Mill Creek where the Spring Grove Mill House sits (New Castle County Parcel No. **0803100018**) as **“General Washington’s Hill of Deception.”**

- Connects the naming to Revolutionary War events, including **General Weedon’s Foray at Spring Grove** and General Washington’s actions on **September 8, 1777**.
- References force sizes involved historically: **11,000** Continental soldiers and **16,000** British/Hessian forces (historical context, not a policy threshold).
