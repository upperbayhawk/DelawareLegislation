## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 9 to extend Delaware’s existing “use-value” property tax relief for land in agricultural, horticultural, and forest use to certain **qualified farm structures**, and to provide a uniform method for assessing those structures under the same framework.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Extends **real estate tax relief** currently available for qualifying agricultural/horticultural/forest **land** to certain **qualified farm structures** used for farm operations.  
- Defines **“qualified farm structure”** (including **poultry houses**) as structures used to **store, maintain, or house** farm implements, commodities/crops, livestock, poultry, and livestock products.  
- Keeps the existing requirement that property must be actively devoted to qualifying use for **at least the 2 successive years immediately preceding** the tax year at issue (upon application and approval).  
- Sets/retains an application deadline: **on or before February 1** of the year immediately preceding the tax year (moves to next business day if Feb. 1 is a weekend/holiday).  
- Requires annual eligibility determination for qualified farm structures, but allows **automatic renewal** unless eligibility changes.  
- Creates/clarifies “roll-back taxes” when a qualified farm structure changes to non-qualifying use; roll-back equals the **difference** between reduced taxes and what would have been owed under regular valuation standards.  
- Establishes payment/penalty rules for roll-back taxes: payment due within **90 days**; late penalty **6%** for first month, then **1% per month** thereafter; unpaid roll-back becomes a **lien**.  
- Fiscal note: projected **Kent County** school revenue loss of **$937,314.25 per year** (FY2026–FY2028 shown); **New Castle/Sussex indeterminable** until reassessments complete.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces property tax burden on farm structures** by extending the existing use-value relief mechanism beyond land to “qualified farm structures.” (Bill synopsis; §8329 as amended)
- **May support continued agricultural operations** by lowering operating costs tied to taxes on buildings like **poultry houses** and storage structures. (Synopsis; §8334(b))
- Provides a clearer, more uniform assessment approach by directing assessors to consider indicia of value established by the **State Farmland Evaluation Advisory Committee** for land **and qualified farm structures**. (§8335(a); §8337)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduces local school district revenues** because property taxes on assessed agricultural/horticultural/forest-use buildings are currently distributed to school districts. (Fiscal note assumption #3)
- Documented revenue loss in **Kent County** totals **$937,314.25** (including vocational-technical **Polytech** losses) based on 2024 reassessment data. (Fiscal note)
- **Fiscal impact for New Castle and Sussex Counties is unknown** until county reassessments are completed, creating budgeting uncertainty. (Fiscal note assumption #5)

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds qualified farm structures to the chapter’s stated purpose by providing “an equitable and uniform method of assessment of qualified farm structures.” (§8328(2))
- Expands use-value valuation statute to apply to **“land and qualified farm structures”** actively devoted to agricultural, horticultural, or forest use for at least **2 successive years** before the tax year at issue, on owner application and approval. (§8329)
- Updates definitions/sections so that **agricultural/horticultural/forest use** can apply to **land and qualified farm structures**. (§§8330–8333)
- Establishes eligibility definition for a qualified farm structure: a structure (including poultry houses) used to **store, maintain, or house** farm implements, commodities/crops, livestock, poultry, and livestock products. (§8334(b))
- Modifies assessment procedures to have assessors use Committee indicia of value for **land and qualified farm structures**; also revises treatment of “structures” language to focus on “qualified farm structures.” (§8335)
- Creates/clarifies roll-back tax application to qualified farm structures when applied to a non-qualifying use, including triggers such as **rezoning**, **site plan approval/certificate of occupancy** enabling nonag use, or **loss of eligibility**. (§8335(e)(2))
- Adds notice and penalty: if eligibility changes, owner must notify by **February 1 of the following year**; failure triggers a penalty of **20% of rollback taxes recoverable**. (§8336(d))
- Requires the Committee to publish ranges of values for land and qualified farm structures and make them available **on or before February 1 of each year**; requires review of formula at least every **2 years**; formula based on not less than the preceding **20 years** of land values (or appropriate assessed percentage reduction for structures). (§8337(b))

## Who Benefits
- Owners of **qualified farm structures** (including poultry houses and other farm operation structures) on land actively devoted to agricultural, horticultural, or forest use, who obtain reduced taxable valuation/assessment upon application and approval. (§8334(b); §8329)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Local school districts** (including vocational-technical school districts such as **Polytech**) that currently receive revenue from property taxes on assessed agricultural/horticultural/forest-use buildings. (Fiscal note)
- Taxing districts that may need to administer and enforce roll-back taxes, notices, penalties, and liens (administrative burden). (§8335(e); §8336)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date:** “effective upon signature of the Governor.” (Fiscal note assumption #1)
- **Application deadline for valuation:** **on or before February 1** of the year immediately preceding the tax year (next business day if Feb. 1 is a weekend/holiday). (§8334(a)(3)a; §8336(c) for structures)
- **Committee deadline:** On or before **February 1 of each year**, Committee provides value ranges to assessing authorities. (§8337(b))
- **Owner notice deadline if eligibility changes (structures):** on or before **February 1 of the following year** in which eligibility changes. (§8336(d)(1))
- **Roll-back tax payment timing:** due within **90 days** of due date. (§8335(e)(3))

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Roll-back taxes** imposed when a qualified farm structure changes to non-qualifying use; calculated as tax difference between preferential valuation and regular valuation (for each roll-back tax year involved). (§8335(e))
- “Change in use” includes: actual nonag use, rezoning to nonag category, approval of a site plan or issuance of certificate of occupancy allowing nonag use, or loss of eligibility. (§8335(e)(2))
- **Late payment penalty for roll-back taxes:** **6%** of amount due for the first month after the 90-day period, plus **1% per consecutive month** until paid. (§8335(e)(3))
- **Lien:** unpaid roll-back taxes become a **lien on the land** as of the due date. (§8335(e)(3); §8336(c))
- **Certification requirement (new applications):** landowner must execute a **recordable certification** acknowledging roll-back tax and lien consequences. (§8336(c))
- **Penalty for failure to notify** assessing authority of change in qualified farm structure use: **20% of the rollback taxes recoverable**, collected by the taxing district. (§8336(d)(2))
