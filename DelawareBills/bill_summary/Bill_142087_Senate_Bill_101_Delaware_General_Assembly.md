## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 16, Chapter 47 (Controlled Substances Act definitions) to expand the definition of “patient-practitioner relationship” to expressly include treatment for opioid use disorder (OUD) under a properly established provider-patient relationship as set out in **24 Del. C. § 6003**, for certain FDA-approved medications.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands the statutory definition of **“patient-practitioner relationship”** in **16 Del. C. § 4701(34)** for purposes of **prescribing drugs**.
- Adds a new qualifying scenario: treatment for **opioid use disorder** under a properly established relationship as provided by **24 Del. C. § 6003**.
- Limits this new scenario to **Schedule III through V medications** that are **approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration** for OUD treatment.
- Retains existing relationship pathways (e.g., at least **1 in-person medical evaluation**, referrals/consultation, cross-coverage, short-term continuation for new patient, hospital admission orders).
- Makes “technical corrections” to conform the text to the **Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual** (formatting/clarity changes).
- Does not state any new funding, appropriations, or fees in the bill text.
- Does not specify an effective date in the provided text.
