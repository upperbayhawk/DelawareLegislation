## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Encourage artistic endeavors, investment, and job creation in Delaware’s film, television, esports, and videogame industries by creating a Delaware Production Tax Credit for qualified production activities in the State. (30 Del. C. §1801A)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a **Delaware Production Tax Credit equal to 30%** of **qualified expenditures** incurred **in Delaware** for eligible media and digital interactive entertainment projects. (§1803A(a))
- Eligibility requires **more than $500,000** in qualified expenditures **in a tax year**; expenditures may be **aggregated across multiple projects** in the year. (§1803A(b))
- Credit may be applied against **personal income tax**, **corporation income tax**, or **bank franchise tax** liabilities. (§1803A(a); fiscal note Assumption #4)
- Credits are **transferable/saleable/assignable** and unused credits may be **carried forward up to 10 years** (non-refundable). (§1803A(c)-(d); §1804A)
- Annual cap: total credit awards may not exceed **$25,000,000 per fiscal year**. (§1810A)
- Requires **Delaware resident internship opportunities** as a condition of credit eligibility (per Commission rules/regulations). (§1803A(e))
- Imposes **6% Delaware income tax withholding** on payments to **loan-out companies** for services performed in Delaware, with penalties/interest for noncompliance. (§1805A)
- Administration is assigned to the **Delaware Motion Picture and Television Development Commission** in cooperation with the Department (Department of Finance/Department of Revenue functions referenced), including **audit requirements** and **biennial reporting**. (§1806A; §1808A; §1809A)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Incentivizes in-state spending** by granting a **30% credit** only for **expenditures incurred in Delaware** and **excluding out-of-state work/services**. (§1803A(a); §1802A(5)(c))
- **Targets larger-scale activity** via a **>$500,000 annual expenditure threshold**, potentially focusing the incentive on projects with substantial in-state economic activity. (§1803A(b))
- **Encourages workforce training** by requiring opportunities for **Delaware residents to serve as interns**. (§1803A(e))
- **Promotes accountability** through a required **audit** before credits are awarded and an ongoing **biennial report** including credits approved and economic/community impact. (§1806A(e); §1808A; §1809A)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **State revenue loss exposure up to $25,000,000 per fiscal year** due to the annual credit award cap. (§1810A; fiscal note “Maximum Revenue Loss” FY2028/FY2029)
- **Administrative costs** to implement and operate the program (estimated **$338,514 in FY2027**, including **$55,000 one-time** startup). (Fiscal note Assumption #6)
- **Complex compliance burden**: application requirements (budgets, proof of financing, cast/location lists, etc.) and a **required audit** may increase transaction costs for applicants. (§1806A(d)-(e); §1808A)
- **Withholding mandate**: production companies must withhold **6%** on payments to loan-out companies for services performed in Delaware, creating additional payroll/tax administration duties and potential penalty exposure. (§1805A)

## Key Policy Changes
- Establishes a new **Chapter 18A** in Title 30 creating a **Delaware Production Tax Credit**. (30 Del. C. Ch. 18A)
- Sets credit amount at **30%** of **qualified expenditures** for **qualified activities**. (§1803A(a))
- Defines **qualified activities** to include:
  - Media projects (feature films, series, commercials, webisodes, music videos, pilots, award shows, game shows, and **esports events**) for **commercial multi-market distribution**. (§1802A(3)a.1)
  - Digital interactive entertainment (including **videogames**, updates, and subsequent editions). (§1802A(3)a.2)
- Excludes certain content/activities from eligibility (e.g., **political advertising**, industrial/instructional videos, **obscene content** as defined in 11 Del. C. §1364, live/prerecorded athletic events other than esports, website development, infomercials/solicitation-based products). (§1802A(3)b)
- Sets eligibility threshold: **qualified expenditures > $500,000** in a tax year; allows aggregation across multiple projects. (§1803A(b))
- Makes credits **transferable** and **carryforwardable for 10 years**; credits are **not refundable**. (§1803A(c)-(d); §1804A)
- Imposes **6% withholding** on payments to **loan-out companies** for services performed in Delaware, with penalties/interest aligned to employee withholding tax enforcement. (§1805A)
- Requires application to the Commission and a **Certificate of Completion** plus **audit** before claiming the credit. (§1806A(d)-(f); §1808A)
- Establishes an appeal route for noncertification decisions: appeal to the **Secretary of State** (or designee) within **60 days**. (§1807A(a))
- Requires **biennial reporting** beginning **2 years after the effective date of the Act**, and every 2 years thereafter. (§1809A)
- Caps total credit awards at **$25,000,000 per fiscal year**. (§1810A)

## Who Benefits
- **Qualified companies** engaged in eligible film/TV/esports/videogame production with **> $500,000** annual in-state qualified expenditures. (§1803A(b); §1802A(4)-(5))
- **Taxpayers who acquire credits** (transferees/purchasers/assignees) who can use them to offset Delaware income or franchise tax liabilities. (§1804A(c))
- **Delaware residents** receiving internship opportunities tied to qualified productions. (§1803A(e))

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **State General Fund revenues**, due to credits awarded up to **$25,000,000 per fiscal year** (estimated revenue loss shown beginning FY2028). (§1810A; fiscal note)
- **Production companies** that use loan-out companies, due to mandatory **6% withholding**, associated compliance steps, and exposure to penalties/interest for failures. (§1805A)
- **Applicants** facing costs and administrative requirements for application materials and required audits. (§1806A(d)-(e); §1808A)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective upon signature by the Governor**, but **provisions take effect July 1, 2026** (per fiscal note assumptions). (Fiscal note Assumption #1)
- Bill text states: **“This Act takes effect on July 1, 2026.”** (Section 2)
- **Appeal deadline:** appeal a noncertification decision within **60 days** of issuance. (§1807A(a))
- **Reporting:** first report **2 years after the effective date of the Act**, and **every 2 years thereafter**. (§1809A)
- Regulation deadline appears in text: Division of Revenue “shall also by **January 1, 2023** establish regulations...” (date as written). (§1806A(c))

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Application and certification required** through the Commission; claiming the credit requires attaching a **Certificate of Completion** to the Delaware tax return. (§1806A(d)-(f))
- **Audit requirement:** Department must conduct or cause a **full audit** of each tax credit (Department or certified third party). (§1808A)
- **Loan-out withholding requirement:** production company must withhold **6%** Delaware income tax on payments to loan-out companies for Delaware services; withholding liability is subject to **penalties and interest** like employee withholding taxes; collection/assessment to be set by regulation. (§1805A)
- **Transfer documentation requirement:** a transferor/seller/assignor must obtain and provide a **certificate** stating the amount of unused credit to the transferee/purchaser/assignee. (§1804A(a))
- **Cap compliance:** awards may not exceed **$25,000,000** in any fiscal year. (§1810A)
