## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 20 (Public Health Emergencies) to (1) rename the Public Health Emergency Planning Commission as the **Public Health Emergency Planning Council**, (2) update statutory references from a single “plan” to the **Public Health Emergency Operations Coordination Plan**, and (3) set clearer **meeting and review requirements** for the Council during and outside of declared public health emergencies, plus technical wording corrections.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Renames** the “Public Health Emergency Planning Commission” to the **Public Health Emergency Planning Council** and updates related definitions and references.  
- Requires the Council to meet **at least 1 time per year** when no public health emergency is active.  
- During a state of emergency due to a public health emergency, requires the Council to meet **within 15 days** of initiation and **at least every 30 days thereafter** until termination.  
- Requires the Council to **review** the **Public Health Emergency Operations Coordination Plan** (“Plan”) **every 2 years**.  
- Maintains/clarifies the Council’s **advisory role to the Governor** during a declared public health emergency.  
- Keeps isolation/quarantine hearing rights and standards, including: a continued-isolation/quarantine contest hearing **within 72 hours** of request (excluding weekends/holidays) and a remedies hearing **within 10 days** of court receipt.  
- Retains vaccination and treatment emergency powers, including limits that vaccination/treatment **must not be reasonably likely to lead to serious harm**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More frequent emergency governance:** Moves the initial required Council meeting during a public health emergency to **within 15 days** (rather than the earlier draft’s 30 days), potentially enabling faster advisory review during emergencies.  
- **Ongoing oversight outside emergencies:** Requires at least **annual** Council meetings, supporting continuity and readiness.  
- **Aligns statute with current planning structure:** Updates law to reflect the existing **Public Health Emergency Operations Coordination Plan** and its annexes (as described in the synopsis), improving consistency between statute and practice.  
- **Regular plan review cadence:** Mandates **biennial (every 2 years)** review of the Plan.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increased administrative burden:** Required meeting frequency—**within 15 days**, then **every 30 days** during an emergency, plus **at least annually** otherwise—may require additional staff time and coordination.  
- **No added operational detail in statute:** The bill updates terminology and governance mechanics but does not specify new substantive response measures beyond meeting/review requirements.

## Health Impact
- **Public health emergency governance:** Establishes the Public Health Emergency Planning **Council** and sets required meeting schedules during emergencies and non-emergencies.  
- **Vaccination/treatment authorities retained:** During a state of emergency, the public safety health authority may direct **vaccination** and **treatment**, with the constraint that they must not be “reasonably likely to lead to serious harm.”  
- **Isolation/quarantine due process retained/clarified:**  
  - Continued isolation/quarantine contest hearing: request allowed **on or after 10 days following a hearing** referenced in §3136(5)e; hearing must occur **within 72 hours** of request (excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays).  
  - Remedies hearing (treatment/terms/conditions): hearing must take place **within 10 days** of court receipt of request.  
  - At continued-isolation/quarantine hearing, the authority must prove by **clear and convincing evidence** that continued isolation/quarantine is warranted due to significant transmission risk with serious consequences.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Renaming/structural update:** “Public Health Emergency Planning Commission” renamed to **Public Health Emergency Planning Council**; “Council” defined in §3132.  
- **Council meetings (no emergency):** Must meet **at least 1 time per year** (§3141(f)).  
- **Council meetings (during emergency):** Must meet **within 15 days** of initiation of a state of emergency due to a public health emergency, and **at least every 30 days thereafter** until termination (§3141(g)).  
- **Plan terminology and review:** Updates from an older single plan concept to the **Public Health Emergency Operations Coordination Plan** and requires Council review **every 2 years** (§3142(a)-(b)).  
- **Subcommittee requirement:** Council must establish a **hospital biopreparedness planning subcommittee** including representation from **DEMA**, **DHSS**, the **medical community**, and **local emergency medical services** (§3142(e)).  
- **Technical corrections:** Updates terminology (e.g., “his or her” to “the person’s”) and internal cross-references (including transferring prior meeting provisions to §3141).
