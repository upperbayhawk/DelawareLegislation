## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 13 of the Delaware Code to create/clarify a right to **free legal counsel** for certain **relatives** seeking to adopt a child who is **in Department custody** after **parental rights have been terminated** and the child is **legally free for adoption**, including for related appeals.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a statutory right to **free legal counsel** for eligible **relatives** pursuing an adoption of a child who is **in Department custody** and **free for adoption** after **termination of parental rights (TPR)**. (§ 933)
- Applies **regardless of indigency status**—i.e., no income threshold is required. (§ 933(c))
- Requires the Court to **appoint a Delaware-authorized attorney** to represent the petitioner **for the duration of the case**, including **any appeal to the Delaware Supreme Court**. (§ 933(c))
- Allows a petitioner to **waive** appointed counsel; the Court must **document the waiver in an order**. (§ 933(d))
- Conforms appeal statutes so that qualifying appellants have a right to free counsel under **§ 933**. (§§ 905(b), 917(b))
- Retains/clarifies that an appeal from a Department/licensed agency refusal or termination must be filed **within 30 days after notice**. (§ 905(a))
- States that the free-counsel right in adoption proceedings **does not include** representation in a **TPR petition** filed before or along with the adoption petition. (§ 933(b))

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves access to counsel for family adoptions:** Eligible relatives seeking to adopt children in Department custody must receive counsel **even if they are not indigent**. (§ 933(c))
- **May strengthen procedural fairness:** Representation is provided **at all stages of the adoption proceedings** (excluding TPR petitions), which may help relatives navigate court requirements. (§ 933(b), (c))
- **Continuity of representation through appeals:** Counsel continues through the case, including **appeals to the Supreme Court**. (§ 933(c))
- **Clarifies eligibility and covered relatives:** The statute lists covered family relationships, reducing uncertainty about who qualifies. (§ 933(a); §§ 905(b)(3), 917(b)(3))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Limits coverage by excluding TPR petitions:** Free counsel expressly **does not cover** a termination of parental rights petition filed before or along with the adoption petition. (§ 933(b))
- **Narrow eligibility conditions:** Counsel is only guaranteed if the child is **in Department custody** and **TPR has occurred** and the child is **free for adoption**. (§ 933(b)(1)-(2))
- **Potential increased workload for courts and appointed counsel systems:** The Court “**shall ensure**” appointment for qualifying petitioners, regardless of indigency, which may increase appointments. (§ 933(c)) *(No fiscal estimate provided in bill text.)*

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates/expands right to free legal counsel** for qualifying relatives in adoption proceedings involving a child:
  - **in Department custody**; and
  - **parental rights terminated** and child **free for adoption**; and
  - petitioner/appellant was a listed **relative prior to TPR**. (§ 933(b); §§ 905(b), 917(b))
- **Defines “relative”** to include: sibling, grandparent, uncle, aunt, first cousin, first cousin once removed, great-grandparent, grandaunt/granduncle, half sibling, stepparent, stepsibling, stepaunt/stepuncle, or stepgrandparent. (§ 933(a); mirrored in §§ 905(b)(3), 917(b)(3))
- **Mandates appointment regardless of indigency** and provides representation through **any Supreme Court appeal**. (§ 933(c))
- **Allows waiver** of counsel, requiring the waiver be placed in a court order. (§ 933(d))
- **Appeal deadline remains 30 days after notice** for covered § 905(a) appeals. (§ 905(a))

## Who Benefits
- **Relatives (as specifically listed in the statute)** seeking to adopt a child in Department custody after TPR, who will receive **free appointed counsel regardless of income**. (§ 933(a)-(c))
- **Qualifying appellants** in appeals covered by §§ 905 and 917 who meet the same criteria for counsel under § 933. (§§ 905(b), 917(b))
