## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To correct a drafting error in Delaware’s Uniform Health-Care Decisions Act by deleting the word “other” from 16 Del. C. § 2527(e) so that advance health-care directives are interpreted “in accordance with law of this State” (excluding Delaware’s choice-of-law rules) when implemented.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **16 Del. C. § 2527(e)** (Construction) within the Uniform Health-Care Decisions Act framework.
- Makes a **technical correction**: deletes the word **“other”** that the Uniform Law Commission found was added in error.
- Clarifies that an advance health-care directive must be interpreted under **Delaware law**, **excluding** Delaware **choice-of-law rules**, at the time the directive is implemented.
- Applies to advance health-care directives created **before, on, or after September 30, 2025**.
- The amended language is tied to the moment the directive is **implemented** (“at the time the directive is implemented”).
- **Effective date:** **September 30, 2025** (aligned with the effective date of § 2527 itself).
- The enrolled/approved act indicates: **Approved July 21, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Textual clarity and uniformity:** Conforms Delaware’s statute to the Uniform Law Commission’s intended language by removing an erroneous word (“other”). (Synopsis)
- **Reduced interpretive confusion:** Removing “other” may reduce disputes about what body of law governs interpretation when an advance directive is implemented (Delaware law, excluding choice-of-law rules). (§ 2527(e))
