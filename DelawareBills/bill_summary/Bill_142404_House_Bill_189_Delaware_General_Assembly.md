## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29 of the Delaware Code to designate the Red Knot as Delaware’s official State migratory bird.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Designates the **Red Knot** as the **official migratory bird of Delaware**.  
- Amends **Title 29, Chapter 3, § 334** (“State migratory bird”).  
- Legislative findings note Red Knots migrate up to **9,000 miles per year** and rely on Delaware Bay stopovers.  
- Findings state **up to 90%** of the **Rufa** subspecies may be present along Delaware bays **in a single day** during spring migration.  
- Notes **horseshoe crab eggs** are an important food source for Red Knots.  
- Enacted as **Chapter 86, Volume 85, Laws of Delaware** (formerly HB 189).  
- **Approved July 16, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **State recognition/education value:** Official designation may support public awareness of Delaware’s bays and wetlands and the Red Knot’s role in state ecology (supported by the bill’s findings describing ecological importance).  
- **Civic/heritage significance:** Aligns state symbols with a species described as “an important part of Delaware’s history and ecology.”

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Primarily symbolic change:** The bill only designates an official bird and does not create conservation programs, funding, or regulatory measures (no operational provisions beyond the designation).
