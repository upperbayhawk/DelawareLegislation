## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Authorize and fund Delaware’s Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 capital projects and related financing/administration, including issuance of State general obligation (GO) bonds, Transportation Trust Fund spending, Delaware Transportation Authority revenue bond authority, General Fund capital appropriations, and fund reprogramming; and amend certain statutes/requirements related to capital spending and tax credits. (FY ending **June 30, 2026**)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes a **FY2026 capital program total of $977,310,591**, including **$339,145,000** in GO bond authorizations, **$368,340,039** in General Funds, **$212,616,463** in Transportation Trust Funds (state share), and **$57,209,088** in reauthorizations/reprogramming.  
- Authorizes issuance of up to **$339,145,000** in State **20-year GO bonds** (maturing no later than 20 years from issuance).  
- Recognizes local share for school construction bonds of **$87,115,316**, issued at the **school district’s discretion**.  
- Appropriates General Funds for capital purposes totaling **$368,340,039** across multiple agencies (including **$38,000,000** to DSHA and **$59,284,377** to DNREC).  
- Authorizes Transportation Trust Fund program withdrawals “not to exceed” **$653,705,105** (State **$212,616,463**; Federal **$397,793,988**; Other **$43,294,654**), and Delaware Transportation Authority revenue bond issuance up to **$26,741,437** (including up to **$25,000,000** for projects and up to **$1,741,437** for issuance costs/reserves).  
- Establishes multiple reporting/oversight requirements, including **quarterly bond bill expenditure reports** and specific annual/dated reporting (e.g., DSHA by **April 30**, DNREC Inland Bays report by **September 1** annually, Marijuana Commissioner annual report beginning **July 1, 2025**).  
- Makes policy changes beyond appropriations, including increasing the **annual cap on new Historic Preservation Tax Credit awards to $8,000,000** (from **$5,000,000**) and setting a new cap period (**FY 2026–2031**).  
- Sets reversion rules: many non-transportation authorizations revert if unexpended/unencumbered by **June 30, 2028** (Transportation Trust Fund balances excluded from Section 1 reversion clause).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- Funds major capital investments statewide (e.g., **ERP Cloud Migration $50,000,000**, school projects, state facility maintenance, housing programs totaling **$38,000,000** through DSHA).  
- Expands affordable housing production tools by funding DSHA programs and allowing transfers among certain DSHA appropriations with approval (supports “maximize housing opportunities”).  
- Increases availability of Historic Preservation Tax Credits by raising annual cap to **$8,000,000**, potentially enabling more preservation projects to receive credits.  
- Requires regular financial/expenditure reporting (e.g., **quarterly** capital expenditure status reports; DSHA reporting by **April 30**; agency-specific reporting), supporting oversight of capital spending.  
- Supports water quality and infrastructure financing via Clean Water and Drinking Water revolving fund appropriations and minimum set-aside (**at least $4,600,000**) for a clean water affordability financing program.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Increases reliance on long-term debt financing by authorizing **$339,145,000** in 20-year GO bonds and additional transportation revenue bond authority (up to **$26,741,437**).  
- Waives/limits certain procurement statutes for specified delivery methods (e.g., design-build contracts: “all other provisions of 29 Del. C. c. 69 shall not be applicable” to those projects, except listed requirements), reducing standard procedural applicability for those projects.  
- Imposes compliance conditions that may affect local entities’ eligibility for funds (e.g., LEA constable training/list requirements for School Safety & Security funding; failure renders LEA ineligible).  
- Preempts local zoning/permits in several contexts (e.g., certain DOT facilities, Auburn Valley Master Plan elements, 312 Cherry Lane MAT facility, Red Clay bus storage parcel if permits not issued by **October 1, 2025**), potentially limiting local control as stated in the bill text.

## Fiscal or Administrative Impact
- **Reversion deadlines**:
  - Section 1 authorizations (excluding Transportation Trust Fund balances): unexpended/unencumbered by **June 30, 2028** → subject to reversion or reauthorization.
  - General Fund appropriations: unexpended/unencumbered by **June 30, 2028** → revert to General Fund.
- **Quarterly reporting**: all state agencies and public school districts receiving bond bill funds must submit **quarterly expenditure status reports** (format set by OMB).  
- **Construction management authority**: OMB responsible for design/construction for OMB-listed projects; must agree/transfer equipment purchase funding to agencies by **September 1 of each fiscal year**.  
- **Appeals Board**: created (Lt. Governor, OMB Director, Controller General) to approve use of unencumbered monies after “substantially complete” (occupied by **75%** of planned tenants or deemed complete). One year after substantial completion, unencumbered balance reverts.  
- **DSHA administrative caps**:
  - Urban Redevelopment: DSHA admin functions capped at no more than the greater of **10%** or **$350,000**.
  - Strong Neighborhoods Housing Fund: capped at no more than the greater of **10%** or **$150,000**.
  - Workforce Housing Program: capped at no more than **10%**.
- **Studies/reports**:
  - DHSS Stockley Campus feasibility study report due **September 15, 2025**.
  - Department of Labor paid family medical leave actuarial study due **April 1, 2026**.
  - DOE/OMB/Controller General revised framework for CN prioritization due **December 1, 2025**.
  - DNREC Auburn Valley transition plan report due **April 1, 2026**.
  - DNREC Atlas Point air monitoring recommendations due **April 1, 2026**.
  - DNREC Inland Bays dredging/channel-marking report due **September 1** each year.
  - DNREC/DSWA yard waste status report due **December 1** each fiscal year.
  - OMB and DOT Community Workforce Agreement pilot assessments due **January 1** annually (OMB and DOT each have January 1 reporting requirements).
  - DSHA Housing Development Fund expenditure report due **April 30**.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Historic Preservation Tax Credits (30 Del. C. §1816)**:
  - Raises annual cap on new credit awards from **$5,000,000** to **$8,000,000**.
  - Sets overall cap statement: maximum new Historic Preservation Tax Credits “shall not exceed **$8,000,000**” for **FY 2026–2031**.
  - Adds/clarifies provisions on awarding unused credits and begins awarding unused credits if insufficient credits available “beginning on **June 30, 2025**” (as stated in amended text).
- **Procurement/project delivery adjustments**:
  - OMB design-build authority for up to **12** construction projects; 704 N. King Street renovation designated as design-build.
  - DOT CM/GC authority for up to **20** transportation projects.
  - OMB pilot program for Community Workforce Agreements/DBE best-value procurement for no more than **4** projects; annual report due **January 1**.
  - DOT authorized to use Community Workforce Agreements for no more than **2** large public works projects; annual report due **January 1**.
- **Electronic speed enforcement authorizations (time-limited)**:
  - Main Street Newark business district: tickets for **6 mph or more** over limit; authority expires after **1 year** unless reauthorized; cannot extend past **June 30, 2028**.
  - McKennans Church Road and Milltown Road (New Castle County): same **6 mph** threshold; same expiration rules; quarterly reporting to DelDOT.
- **School facility/operations requirements**:
  - Water bottle filling station minimums required for DOE plan approval (ratios and locations).
  - HVAC upgrades/new schools require SEU energy audit.
  - Enhanced MCI funds not subject to **$1,000,000** minor capital restriction; no local match required; district/charter plans report due **January 1, 2026**.
- **Tax Ditch authority**: DNREC Division of Watershed Stewardship may act as tax ditch officer for “inactive tax ditches” defined as having **zero officers for at least 1 year**, with access to inactive tax ditch funds (authority expires upon fulfillment of officer vacancy).
- **Property deed restriction removal**: terminates and rescinds deed restrictions on property deeded to **Faithful Friends** (removing use restriction and transfer/reversion condition); new deed may be recorded at Faithful Friends’ cost with OMB signature prior to recordation.
