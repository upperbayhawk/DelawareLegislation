## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Update the Delaware Revised Uniform Partnership Act (Title 6, Chapter 15) to clarify filing correction procedures, partnership ratification/waiver rules, merger/consolidation filing requirements, foreign LLP registration contents, registered-agent standards, and annual tax timing—aimed at keeping the statute “current” and maintaining Delaware’s “national preeminence.” (Synopsis)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits registered agents from performing duties **solely** via a **virtual office** and/or **mail forwarding service**; defines “virtual office” as duties performed solely through the internet or other remote communication.  
- Clarifies that a **statement/certificate of correction** may **correct or nullify** a previously filed statement/certificate, and sets effectiveness rules for those “substantially and adversely affected.”  
- Expands partnership **ratification/waiver** rules to cover acts/transactions taken **“by or in respect of”** a partnership, including acts by **partners or other persons**; allows ratification/waiver to be **express or implied** (including by action/inaction/acquiescence).  
- Specifies that when notice of ratification/waiver is required, **giving notice is not a condition** of effectiveness (though notice must be given after ratification/waiver to certain entitled persons).  
- Requires that, in a **consolidation** where the **resulting entity is a domestic partnership**, the **statement of partnership existence** must be included **as an attachment** to the certificate of consolidation.  
- Requires foreign LLP statements of qualification to include: **jurisdiction of formation, date of formation, and a partner statement** that the foreign LLP validly exists as an LLP **as of the filing date**; clarifies the “number of partners” reported is as of the statement’s **effective time**.  
- Confirms that when cancelling a statement of partnership existence via a filing, the **full annual tax** for that calendar year must be **paid before** the cancellation filing is made; unpaid tax bears **1.5% interest per month (or portion)** after the due date.  
- Act takes effect **August 1, 2025**; bill was **approved June 30, 2025** (as enacted Chapter 45, Volume 85).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves reliability of service of process and compliance communications** by requiring registered agents not to operate solely through remote/virtual arrangements (registered-agent duty changes in § 15-111(e)).  
- **Greater clarity and flexibility in fixing filing errors** by expressly allowing correction filings to **nullify** prior filings and specifying when corrections are retroactive vs. effective upon filing for persons “substantially and adversely affected” (§ 15-118).  
- **Reduces uncertainty around defective partnership actions** by expanding and clarifying ratification/waiver rules, including acceptance of **implied** ratification/waiver through conduct, inaction, or acquiescence (§ 15-202(g)).  
- **Clearer consolidation filings** by requiring the statement of partnership existence to be attached when a consolidation results in a domestic partnership (§ 15-902(c)).  
- **More complete foreign LLP registration information** (jurisdiction, formation date, and validity statement) to support the Secretary of State’s records and verification needs (§ 15-1102(a)).  
- **Clarifies annual tax timing at cancellation** to ensure the State receives the **full annual tax** before a cancellation filing becomes effective (§ 15-1208(b)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Operational burden for registered agents** who previously relied solely on virtual offices and/or mail forwarding; they must maintain a Delaware business office generally open (entity) or be generally present at a designated Delaware location (individual), and cannot fulfill duties solely virtually (§ 15-111(e)).  
- **Additional filing/administrative steps** for consolidations resulting in domestic partnerships due to the attachment requirement (§ 15-902(c)).  
- **Added information requirements** for foreign LLPs registering in Delaware (jurisdiction, formation date, partner validity statement; partner count measured at effectiveness) (§ 15-1102(a)).  
- **Cash-flow/timing impact at cancellation**: partnerships cancelling a statement of partnership existence via a filing must pay the **full annual tax for that calendar year before filing** (§ 15-1208(b)).  
- **Potential dispute complexity**: the statute explicitly authorizes Court of Chancery review of ratification/waiver validity upon application by certain parties, which may formalize litigation pathways (§ 15-202(g)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- What specific enforcement mechanism or consequence applies if a registered agent violates the “no solely virtual office/mail forwarding” rule, beyond the statutory prohibition (§ 15-111(e)).  
- The bill defines “virtual office” for § 15-111(e)(2), but does not further specify what minimum in-person presence/operations are required beyond “generally open” or “generally present” language already in statute (§ 15-111(e)(1)).  
- The text states corrections/nullifications are retroactive except for persons “substantially and adversely affected,” but does not define what qualifies as “substantially and adversely affected” (§ 15-118(a)).  
- For ratification/waiver notice: notice must be given after ratification/waiver to certain persons, but the statute says notice is not a condition to effectiveness; the bill does not specify remedies for failure to provide notice (§ 15-202(g)).

## Key Policy Changes
1. **Registered agents (§ 15-111(e))**
   - Adds prohibition: a registered agent may not perform duties/functions **solely** through a **virtual office**, a **mail forwarding service**, or both.  
   - Defines **“virtual office”** as performing duties/functions solely through the internet or other remote communication.  
   - Retains and enumerates duties (accepting/forwarding process and communications; forwarding annual tax statement or electronic notice; adhering to Secretary of State identity verification regulations; maintaining a generally open office/presence).

2. **Correction or nullification of filed records (§ 15-118(a))**
   - Expressly allows a statement/certificate of correction to **correct or nullify** a previously filed statement/certificate.  
   - Sets effectiveness: generally effective as of the **original filing date**, except for persons “substantially and adversely affected,” for whom it is effective from the **correction filing date**.

3. **Ratification/waiver of void or voidable acts (§ 15-202(g))**
   - Applies to acts/transactions taken **“by or in respect of”** a partnership, including those by any **partner or other person**, in any capacity.  
   - Allows ratification/waiver to be **express or implied**, including by statements, action, inaction, or acquiescence.  
   - Clarifies that required notice after ratification/waiver is **not a condition** of effectiveness.  
   - Court of Chancery may determine validity/effectiveness upon application by the partnership, any partner, or certain substantially/adversely affected persons (excluding harm that would have occurred if the act had been valid when taken).

4. **Merger/consolidation filings (§ 15-902(c))**
   - For a consolidation where the **resulting entity is a domestic partnership**, requires the **statement of partnership existence** to be included **as an attachment** to the certificate of consolidation.

5. **Foreign LLP registration contents (§ 15-1102(a))**
   - Requires statement of foreign qualification to include:
     - **Jurisdiction where formed** (state/territory/possession/other jurisdiction/country),
     - **Date of formation**, and
     - **Partner statement** that, as of the filing date, the foreign LLP validly exists as an LLP under its formation jurisdiction’s laws.  
   - Clarifies the “number of partners” reported is the number **at the time the statement becomes effective**.

6. **Annual tax timing upon cancellation (§ 15-1208(b))**
   - Annual tax is due **June 1** following the close of the calendar year or upon cancellation.  
   - If cancellation occurs via filing a statement/certificate under the chapter, the **full annual tax for the calendar year** in which that filing becomes effective is due **before filing**.  
   - Unpaid tax bears interest at **1 1/2% per month or portion** after the due date established by § 15-1208(d).

## Who Benefits
- **Delaware partnerships and their partners** seeking clearer statutory mechanisms to ratify/waive void or voidable acts and to correct or nullify filings (§§ 15-118, 15-202).  
- **Parties relying on registered-agent availability** for service of process and official communications, due to limits on “solely” virtual/mail-forwarding operations (§ 15-111).  
- **State of Delaware (tax administration)** through clarified timing requiring annual tax payment before cancellation filings and interest accrual rules (§ 15-1208(b)).  
- **Foreign limited liability partnerships** that benefit from clearer registration content requirements (even though it also imposes added disclosures) (§ 15-1102).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Registered agents** operating primarily through virtual offices and/or mail forwarding services, who must change operations to comply with the “not solely” prohibition (§ 15-111(e)(2)).  
- **Partnerships consolidating into a domestic partnership**, which must include an attachment (statement of partnership existence) with the certificate of consolidation (§ 15-902(c)).  
- **Foreign LLPs registering in Delaware**, which must provide additional formation/validity information and clarify partner count at effectiveness (§ 15-1102(a)).  
- **Partnerships seeking to cancel a statement of partnership existence** that would otherwise file before paying that year’s full annual tax (§ 15-1208(b)).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date of the Act:** **August 1, 2025** (Section 7).  
- **Approval/enactment date (as published):** **June 30, 2025**.  
- **Annual tax due date:** **June 1** following the close of the calendar year (§ 15-1208(b), as amended).  
- **Cancellation-related tax deadline:** if cancellation will occur via a filing under the chapter, the **full annual tax for that calendar year must be paid before the filing** that cancels the statement of partnership existence (§ 15-1208(b)).  
- **Interest on unpaid annual tax:** **1.5% per month or portion thereof** after the due date established by § 15-1208(d) (§ 15-1208(b)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Registered-agent compliance requirement:** may not perform duties solely through a virtual office and/or mail forwarding; must meet statutory duties and adhere to Secretary of State identity-verification regulations (§ 15-111(e)).  
- **Tax penalty (interest):** unpaid annual tax bears interest at **1 1/2% per month or portion** after the applicable due date (§ 15-1208(b)).  
- **Filing compliance requirements:**  
  - Correction filings may correct or nullify prior filings and must include specified information (§ 15-118(a)).  
  - Consolidation filings must attach the statement of partnership existence when resulting entity is a domestic partnership (§ 15-902(c)).  
  - Foreign LLP qualification filings must include jurisdiction/date/partner validity statement and partner count at effectiveness (§ 15-1102(a)).  
- **Judicial review mechanism:** Court of Chancery may determine validity/effectiveness of ratification/waiver upon application by specified parties (§ 15-202(g)).
