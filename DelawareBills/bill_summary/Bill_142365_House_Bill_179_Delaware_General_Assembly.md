## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 19, §3701 (Definitions) for Delaware’s Family and Medical Leave Insurance Program to change which employers are covered, by excluding employers with fewer than **25 employees in Delaware** during the previous **12 months**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes the definition of “employer” under **19 Del. C. §3701** for the Family and Medical Leave Insurance Program.
- **Excludes** from the program anyone who employs **less than 25 employees in Delaware** during the previous **12 months**.
- Removes the existing framework that treated **10–24 employee** employers as covered for **parental leave only**.
- Leaves intact language addressing **professional employer organizations (PEOs)**, stating the **client company** is treated as the employer for purposes of the chapter.
- The bill text provided does **not** state a new effective date or implementation timeline.
- The synopsis confirms the core change: coverage exclusion for employers with **<25 employees**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces regulatory/program participation obligations** for employers with **10–24 employees**, because the bill excludes employers with **fewer than 25 employees** from the program (previously, 10–24 employee employers were subject to parental leave provisions).
- **Simplifies coverage threshold** by using a single cutoff (**25 employees**) for employer inclusion/exclusion (as reflected in the amended exclusion language).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduces program coverage for workers at smaller employers (10–24 employees)** because those employers would no longer be subject to even the **parental leave provisions** previously applicable under §3701(7)a.1.
- Could **narrow access to benefits** under the program for employees working for employers with **fewer than 25 employees** (the bill excludes those employers from the definition of employer).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Employer coverage threshold change:** Amends §3701(7)b.1 to exclude “anyone who employs less than **10**” and replace it with “less than **25** employees” in Delaware during the previous **12 months**.
- **Eliminates partial coverage tier:** The prior rule that **employers with 10–24 employees** are subject **only to parental leave provisions** is no longer consistent with the new exclusion of employers with **<25 employees**.
- **PEO clarification retained:** Keeps language stating that where a company contracts with a **professional employer organization**, the **client company** is treated as the employer for purposes of the chapter.

## Who Benefits
- **Employers with 10–24 employees in Delaware** during the previous **12 months**, who would be excluded from the program under the revised definition.
- **Employers with fewer than 25 employees** generally, due to exclusion from coverage.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Employees working for employers with 10–24 employees**, who may lose access to program-related **parental leave** coverage that previously applied to those employers.
- **Employees at all employers with fewer than 25 employees**, to the extent program availability depends on employer coverage under the chapter.
