## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To formally recognize **January 26 through February 1, 2026** as **Catholic Schools Week in Delaware** by House Resolution.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Recognizes **January 26–February 1, 2026** as **Catholic Schools Week in Delaware**.
- Cites the first Catholic school in Delaware opening in **1830** and notes growth in Catholic education.
- States Delaware Catholic schools serve **over 8,800 students** across **21 schools**.
- Describes Catholic education as grounded in the Gospel of Jesus and a message of **peace, justice, and concern for humanity**.
- Notes curriculum emphasis on **academic excellence** and development of students **spiritually, mentally, and physically**.
- States Catholic school values, moral teachings, and discipline help students pursue opportunities and become productive members of society.
- Directs that a copy of the resolution be presented to **The Most Reverend William E. Koenig**, Bishop of the **Catholic Diocese of Wilmington**.
