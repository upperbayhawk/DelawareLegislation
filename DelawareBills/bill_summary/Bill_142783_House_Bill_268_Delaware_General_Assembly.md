## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s assault statute (11 Del. C. § 612) to make assaulting a postal worker acting in the lawful performance of duty a higher-level offense (assault in the second degree), rather than assault in the third degree.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds “postal worker” to the list of protected professions covered under **assault in the second degree** when the victim is acting in the lawful performance of duty.  
- The bill’s synopsis states this **elevates assaults on postal workers** from **assault in the third degree** to **assault in the second degree**.  
- The change is made in **Title 11, § 612 (Assault in the second degree)**.  
- Covered conduct: a person **intentionally causes physical injury** to a postal worker acting lawfully in duty.  
- Assault in the second degree is classified as a **Class C or Class D felony** (as stated in § 612 heading).  
- No funding, appropriations, or implementation timelines are included in the bill text.  
- No new enforcement agency or reporting requirement is specified.
