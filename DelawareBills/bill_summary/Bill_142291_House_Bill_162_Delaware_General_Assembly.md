## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 6, Chapter 25, Subchapter VI (Pyramid or Chain Distribution Schemes) to (1) define “multilevel distribution company” and “multilevel marketing program,” (2) require specified pre-sale disclosures to prospective purchasers/participants, and (3) require a buyback/repurchase policy (including cancellation and minimum repurchase price) when a multilevel distribution company requires purchases or other consideration to participate.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Defines **“multilevel distribution company”** and **“multilevel marketing program”** for purposes of Delaware’s pyramid/chain distribution scheme subchapter, with explicit exclusions for certain **licensed** insurance, real estate, and securities professionals.  
- Requires a multilevel distribution company to provide a **written disclosure document at least 48 hours** before the purchaser signs a business opportunity contract **or** before the seller receives **any consideration**—whichever occurs first.  
- Mandates a standardized disclosure first page stating Delaware **does not review/endorse** multilevel marketing programs, with required **font sizes** (16-point and 12-point bold caps).  
- Requires disclosures about the **compensation plan**, **inventory purchase requirements**, and details of services/training, plus specific earnings-related information (including **median and typical earnings**).  
- If earnings claims are made, the disclosure must include: **“No guarantee of earning or range of earning can be made.”**  
- Adds disclosure items requiring counts and percentages, including **participants in last 3 years**, **purchasers in last 3 years**, **how many other purchasers are selling in each county**, and **percentage of buyers who earn more than the initial payment and all inventory purchase requirements**.  
- Prohibits requiring a participant to buy goods/services or pay other consideration to participate **unless** the company agrees to **repurchase resalable goods** and contractually provides cancellation and repurchase terms.  
- Creates a **private right of action** for repurchase violations with **treble damages (3× actual damages)** plus **court costs and reasonable attorney’s fees**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved consumer information before commitment:** Requires a detailed written disclosure **48 hours** before contract/consideration, including compensation plan, inventory requirements, and earnings-related data (e.g., **median and typical earnings**).  
- **Stronger protections against inventory loading:** If participation requires purchases/consideration, the company must agree to repurchase goods in **resalable condition**, and must offer at least **90%** repurchase of the original price upon cancellation.  
- **Clear cancellation right:** Requires contracts to state participants may cancel **within 3 months** after receipt of first-ordered goods/services, with notice allowed by **email or first-class mail**.  
- **Enforcement leverage for harmed participants:** Provides a private right of action for repurchase violations with **3× actual damages** plus **attorney’s fees and costs**, which may encourage compliance.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Compliance and recordkeeping burden for companies:** Disclosures require multi-year counts and earnings information (e.g., totals over the **last 3 years**, county-by-county seller counts, and **median and typical earnings**), which may be administratively intensive.  
- **Increased litigation exposure:** Repurchase violations allow plaintiffs to recover **treble damages** plus **attorney’s fees and costs**, increasing financial risk for noncompliant companies.  
- **Potential constraints on participation models:** Companies that require purchases/fees to participate must implement a repurchase program meeting statutory terms (including **≥ 90%** repurchase), which could change or limit existing program structures.

## Key Policy Changes
- **New/clarified definitions (Title 6, Ch. 25, Subch. VI, § 2561):**
  - “Consideration” includes finder’s fee, bonus, refund, override, commission, cross-commission, dividend, and any financial remuneration.
  - “Multilevel distribution company” defined as selling goods/services through independent agents at different levels and paying consideration based on sales and/or recruitment/action/performance of additional participants.
  - Excludes certain **licensed** insurance, real estate, and securities actors from the definition.
  - Defines “multilevel marketing program” as the marketing program/plan of a multilevel distribution company.
- **Mandatory disclosure regime (§ 2565):**
  - Delivery deadline: **≥ 48 hours** before signing a business opportunity contract **or** before receipt of **any consideration** (whichever occurs first).
  - Required first page standardized warnings with **16-point** and **12-point** bold capital letters.
  - Requires specified business, compensation, service/training, buy-back details, and earnings-related metrics (including 3-year counts, county-by-county seller counts, percentages, and **median and typical earnings**).
  - Disclosure violations are deemed violations of **Subchapter II of Chapter 25**.
- **Repurchase/cancellation requirements (§ 2566):**
  - Cannot require purchases/consideration to participate unless the company agrees to repurchase goods in **resalable condition**.
  - Contracts must state: cancellation allowed **within 3 months** after receipt of first-ordered goods/services; notice by **email or first-class mail**; company will repurchase goods; repurchase price **≥ 90%** of original price.
  - Private right of action for violations: **3× actual damages** + **court costs** + **reasonable attorney’s fees**.

## Who Benefits
- **Prospective participants/purchasers** in multilevel marketing programs receiving mandated disclosures and a cancellation/repurchase right with a minimum **90%** repurchase price.  
- **Participants harmed by repurchase violations**, who gain an express cause of action and potential recovery of **treble damages** plus **fees and costs**.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Multilevel distribution companies** operating in Delaware that must comply with detailed disclosures, timing requirements (**48 hours**), contract language mandates, and repurchase obligations (**≥ 90%** buyback upon qualifying cancellation).  
- Companies facing increased potential liability due to **treble damages** and fee-shifting for repurchase violations.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Disclosure violations (§ 2565):** “Deemed a violation of subchapter II of Chapter 25 of this title.” Specific penalty amounts/procedures are not stated in the provided text.  
- **Repurchase violations (§ 2566):** Private right of action for anyone damaged or injured; recover **3 times actual damages**, **court costs**, and **reasonable attorney’s fees**; treble damages are **in addition to** other potential damages under common law or other Delaware Code provisions.  
- **Contract compliance:** Participation contracts must include specified cancellation and repurchase terms (including **≥ 90%** repurchase price) and allow notice by **electronic mail or first-class mail**.
