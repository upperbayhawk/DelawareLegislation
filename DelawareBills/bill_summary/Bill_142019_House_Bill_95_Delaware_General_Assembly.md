## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To restrict disclosure of student records to immigration enforcement by requiring prior authorization from the Delaware Attorney General (AG), unless disclosure is pursuant to a judicial warrant, and to provide a private right of action for violations.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires a **school** or the **Delaware Department of Education (DOE)** to obtain **authorization from the Delaware AG** before disclosing **student records** to an **immigration enforcement agency/agent**, **unless** the disclosure is pursuant to a **judicial warrant**.  
- Applies broadly to “**school**” entities including **school districts, charter schools, vocational-technical schools, and other schools/programs primarily funded with state or local funds**.  
- Creates a parallel requirement for **operators of companies that hold digital student data** (under Title 14, Chapter 81A) before disclosing student records to immigration enforcement, unless pursuant to a judicial warrant.  
- Sets procedural requirements for authorization requests: must be **in writing** and provide the AG **at least 72 hours notice**.  
- Requires the request to identify the **enforcement agency**, **reason** for the request, and a **detailed scope** (purpose, records sought, impacted individuals, and privacy/data-handling protections).  
- Provides a remedy: a **student or guardian** whose information is disclosed in violation may seek **injunctive relief** and **actual damages**, including **reasonable attorney’s fees**.  
- Defines key terms including **“guardian,” “student,” and “student record”** (student record includes information maintained by an academic institution containing personally identifiable information).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Strengthens privacy protections** by limiting when student records can be shared with immigration enforcement (requires **AG authorization** unless a **judicial warrant** exists).  
- **Creates a standardized process** for disclosure requests (written request, **≥72 hours notice**, specified content requirements), potentially improving accountability and documentation.  
- **Provides enforcement leverage** through a private cause of action for **injunctive relief** and **actual damages** plus **reasonable attorney’s fees** if records are disclosed in violation.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **May delay disclosures** due to the requirement to provide the AG **at least 72 hours notice** before authorization (except when disclosure is pursuant to a judicial warrant).  
- **Adds administrative steps** for schools/DOE and digital data operators (must prepare detailed written requests including lists of records and impacted individuals, and privacy-handling descriptions).  
- **Increases litigation exposure** for entities handling student records because violations can lead to lawsuits seeking **actual damages** and **attorney’s fees**.

## Key Policy Changes

- **Authorization request requirements (Title 14, § 4111A(c)):**
