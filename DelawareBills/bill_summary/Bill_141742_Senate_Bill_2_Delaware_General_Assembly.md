## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Propose a constitutional amendment to Article V, §1 of the Delaware Constitution to **specifically authorize early, in-person voting** and to require that it be available for certain elections.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **Article V, §1** of the Delaware Constitution to add a constitutional requirement for **early, in-person voting**.
- Requires early, in-person voting to occur on **10 calendar days before** the election date for covered elections.
- The 10-day period must **include the Saturday and Sunday immediately before** the election.
- Applies to: **general elections**, **primary elections** for offices decided at the general election, and **special elections** to fill a **General Assembly vacancy**.
- This bill is the **first leg** of a Delaware constitutional amendment; it must pass again in the next General Assembly **after the next general election**.
- Passage requires a **two-thirds vote of all members elected to each house** (not a simple majority), per **Article XVI, §1**.
- Does not specify funding, staffing levels, or operational rules beyond the 10-day requirement.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded access to voting** by requiring **10 days** of early, in-person voting (including the weekend immediately before Election Day). (Art. V, §1(b))
- **Constitutional authorization** may provide stronger legal footing for early in-person voting than statute alone. (Synopsis; Art. V, §1 amendments)
- Applies not only to general elections but also to **primaries** and certain **special elections**, potentially increasing access in more election contexts. (Art. V, §1(b)(1)-(3))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Creates a **mandatory 10-day early voting period** (including weekend staffing), which may increase administrative and operational demands on election officials. (Art. V, §1(b))
- **Constitutional entrenchment** reduces flexibility to adjust the early voting window through ordinary legislation without another constitutional amendment. (Amends Constitution; Synopsis)

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds a new constitutional subsection requiring:  
  - **Early, in-person voting must occur on 10 calendar days before** the election date, **including the Saturday and Sunday immediately before** the election. (Art. V, §1(b))
- Specifies the elections covered:
  1) **General election** (biennial Tuesday after the first Monday in November). (Art. V, §1(a); §1(b)(1))

- Confirms the General Assembly may prescribe voting “means, methods, and instruments” to secure secrecy, preserve election freedom and purity, and prevent fraud/corruption/intimidation (existing constitutional language, with minor edits shown). (Art. V, §1(a))
