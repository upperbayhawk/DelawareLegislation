## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 9 of the Delaware Code to change how often counties must reassess real property values.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes the mandatory countywide real property reassessment frequency from **at least once every 5 years** to **at least once every 10 years**.  
- Applies to **all real property** and places the duty on the **county in which the property is located**.  
- Updates the definition of the reassessment cycle so each **10-year period** begins when a county board of assessment adopts a **certification under 9 Del. C. § 8314** reflecting completion of a general reassessment.  
- Reduces the minimum reassessment cadence required by state law (fewer required reassessments over time).  
- Does not specify any new funding, fees, penalties, or enforcement mechanisms in the bill text.  
- No explicit effective date is provided in the bill text excerpt.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduced administrative frequency:** Counties are required to conduct general reassessments **half as often** (from every **5** years to every **10** years), which may reduce how often counties must undertake large reassessment efforts.  
  - Evidence: 9 Del. C. § 8306(b)(1) is amended from “**5** years” to “**10** years.”

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Less frequent updating of assessed values:** With reassessments required only every **10 years**, assessed values may be updated less often.  
  - Evidence: Statutory minimum changes from **5-year** to **10-year** reassessment requirement.
