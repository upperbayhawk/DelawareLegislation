## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Create a Transportation Appeals Board within the Delaware Department of Transportation (DelDOT) to provide a quasi-judicial process for appealing certain DelDOT decisions (including transportation-related permit grants/denials) and make the Board subject to Delaware Administrative Procedures Act requirements.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Establishes a **7-member Transportation Appeals Board** to hear appeals of DelDOT decisions.  
- Members are **Delaware residents appointed by the Governor with Senate confirmation**; **2 from each county** plus a **Chair** (who may reside in any county).  
- Sets **political balance limit**: **no more than a simple majority** of members may be from one political party.  
- Creates an appeals process with firm timelines: **appeal within 20 days** of receipt/publication; hearing **scheduled within 30 days**; hearing **held within 180 days**; written decision **mailed within 90 days** after hearing completion.  
- If the Board misses the hearing or written-decision deadlines, the **DelDOT decision becomes final for purposes of appeal**.  
- Allows further appeal to **Superior Court within 30 days** after receipt of the Board’s written decision.  
- Specifies that an appeal **does not automatically stay** DelDOT action; a stay may be granted **for good cause** by DelDOT or the **Court of Chancery** (and in later-stage appeals, by the Board or Court of Chancery).  
- Fiscal note anticipates new DelDOT staffing and support costs funded by the **Transportation Trust Fund (TTF)**, including **$155,445 (FY2026 ongoing)** and **$21,000 one-time** startup costs.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Creates a defined, quasi-judicial appeal pathway** for persons “substantially affected” by DelDOT actions, including permit decisions (Synopsis; §8424(a)).  
- **Provides procedural structure and timelines** (20/30/180/90 days), which may improve predictability for appellants and DelDOT (§8424(a)-(b)).  
- **Ensures administrative due process framework** by requiring public hearings under **Chapter 101, Title 29** and adding the Board to the agencies covered by that chapter (§8424(a); §10161(a)(53)).  
- **Gives Board fact-finding tools** (subpoenas, oaths, prehearing conferences) to develop a record (§8423(j)-(k)).  
- **Creates a conflict-of-interest disqualification rule** requiring disclosure on the hearing record (§8423(h)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **New administrative costs** to DelDOT to implement and staff the Board (Fiscal Note: recurring personnel costs and one-time equipment/furnishing costs).  
- **Short appeal window**: appellants must file within **20 days** of receipt/publication of DelDOT’s decision (§8424(a)).  
- **Limited appeal rights for certain state-owned land permit denials**: no appeal except claims solely alleging discriminatory treatment compared to similarly situated applicants (§8424(g)).  
- **No automatic stay** of DelDOT actions during appeal, potentially allowing projects/actions to proceed unless a stay is granted for good cause (§8424(h); §8425(c)).  
- **Board deadline failure defaults to DelDOT finality**, potentially disadvantaging appellants if Board scheduling/decision deadlines are missed (§8424(b)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates Transportation Appeals Board** as a quasi-judicial body to hear appeals of DelDOT decisions (§8423(a)).  
- **Board composition and appointment**: 7 Delaware residents; Governor-appointed with Senate consent; 2 per county plus Chair; party-balance limit (§8423(b)).  
- **Terms and limits**: up to **3-year terms**; max **2 consecutive full terms**; initial shorter terms permitted to stagger expirations (§8423(d)).  
- **Removal/attendance rules**: Governor may remove for cause; “neglect of duty” includes **3 consecutive absences without good cause** or attending **less than 50%** of regular meetings in a calendar year; neglect deemed resignation (§8423(f)).  
- **Ethics/conflict rule**: members with a financial interest must recuse and disclose (§8423(h)).  
- **Board powers**: subpoenas, sworn testimony, prehearing conferences, rulemaking authority (§8423(j)-(l)).  
- **Appeal process and deadlines** (§8424):
  - Appeal filed within **20 days** of receipt/publication.
  - Hearing scheduled within **30 days** of receiving appeal.
  - Hearing held within **180 days** of receiving appeal.
  - Written decision mailed by certified mail within **90 days** after hearing completion.
  - If deadlines missed, **DelDOT decision becomes final** for appeal purposes.
- **Standards of review/burdens**:
  - For case decisions: appellant must show decision **not supported by evidence on the record** (§8424(d)).
  - For regulations: regulation presumed valid; appellant must show **arbitrary and capricious** or adopted without reasonable basis (§8424(e)).
- **Limits on appeals for state-owned land permit denials** (§8424(g)).  
- **No automatic stay**; stay possible for good cause (§8424(h); §8425(c)).  
- **Option to proceed directly to Superior Court by stipulation** after filing with the Board (§8424(i)).  
- **Adds Board to Chapter 101 covered agencies** (§10161(a)(53)).  
- **Court appeal**: Superior Court appeal within **30 days** of receipt of Board written decision; court review limits on fact findings (“substantial evidence” standard) (§8425).

## Who Benefits
- **Businesses and members of the public** whose interests are “substantially affected” by DelDOT actions and who want an administrative appeal mechanism (Synopsis; §8424(a)).  
- Parties seeking a structured process with defined timelines for hearings and written decisions (§8424(b)).  

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- DelDOT (and indirectly the Transportation Trust Fund) due to **added staffing and operational costs** (Fiscal Note; §8423(m)).  
- Applicants denied permits involving **state-owned land (including subaqueous lands)** who are barred from appealing except on a narrow discrimination ground (§8424(g)).  
- Parties wanting an automatic pause of DelDOT actions during appeal (the bill expressly provides **no automatic stay**) (§8424(h); §8425(c)).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date:** “**effective upon signature of the Governor**” (Fiscal Note assumption).  
- **Appeal to Board:** within **20 days** after receipt or publication of DelDOT decision (§8424(a)).  
- **Hearing scheduling:** within **30 days** after Board receives appeal (§8424(b)).  
- **Hearing deadline:** within **180 days** after Board receives appeal (§8424(b)).  
- **Written decision deadline:** mailed by certified mail within **90 days** after completion of hearing (§8424(b)).  
- **Appeal to Superior Court:** within **30 days** of receipt of Board’s written decision (§8425(a)).  
- **Board member attendance threshold:** neglect of duty if absent **3 consecutive meetings** without good cause or attends **<50%** of regular meetings in a calendar year (§8423(f)(1)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Subpoena authority** and compelled testimony/documents (§8423(j)).  
- **Testimony under oath**; testimony under subpoena is subject to **penalties of perjury** (§8423(j)).  
- **Conflict-of-interest compliance**: disqualification required for members with a personal/private financial interest; must inform Chair for record notation (§8423(h)).  
- **Procedural compliance**: hearings are public and must follow **Chapter 101 of Title 29**, with deliberations allowed in executive session (§8424(a)).  
- **Failure-to-meet-Board-deadlines consequence**: if the Board fails to hold hearing or issue written decision as required, **DelDOT’s decision is treated as final for appeal purposes** (§8424(b)).
