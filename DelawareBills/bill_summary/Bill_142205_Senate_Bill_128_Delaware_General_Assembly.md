## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware Medicaid law to allow **comprehensive dental benefits for Medicaid-eligible adults** by (1) expanding the statutory definition of covered dental care, (2) **removing annual monetary limits** on adult dental coverage, and (3) requiring DHSS to seek federal approval via a **Medicaid State Plan Amendment (SPA)**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands the definition of “dental care” to include **diagnostic, preventive, restorative, and periodontal services** (31 Del. C. § 502).
- Removes current annual dollar caps on adult Medicaid dental benefits by prohibiting DHSS from imposing an **annual monetary limit** on adult dental care (31 Del. C. § 503(b)(2)).
- Current law cited in the bill previously limited adult dental benefits to **$1,000 per year**, plus up to **$1,500 additional** for emergency dental care via a review process; the bill eliminates the annual cap framework.
- Keeps a **$3.00 copay** for adult recipients for dental care treatments (31 Del. C. § 503(b)(2)c).
- Requires the DHSS Secretary to submit a **Medicaid SPA to CMS within 90 days of enactment**.
- The Act is **effective upon enactment**, but implementation occurs only after **publication in the Register of Regulations** of a DHSS notice that **CMS approved the SPA**.
- Fiscal note estimates **FY 2026 ongoing state Medicaid cost: ~$1,055,727**, plus a **one-time system state share: $25,000** (with federal matching assumptions).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Broader covered service categories**: Explicitly includes **diagnostic and periodontal** services (in addition to preventive and restorative), supporting more comprehensive adult dental coverage (31 Del. C. § 502).
- **Eliminates annual dollar caps**: By barring annual monetary limits on adult dental care, the bill can allow coverage to better match clinical need rather than an annual spending ceiling (31 Del. C. § 503(b)(2)b).
- **Potential to draw federal dollars**: Fiscal note estimates **$2,463,363 in additional federal funding** leveraged by state appropriations (assumes **70% blended federal match rate**).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher state costs**: Fiscal note estimates increased/ongoing **state share Medicaid costs** of **$1,055,727 (FY 2026)**, **$1,092,677 (FY 2027)**, and **$1,130,921 (FY 2028)**.
- **Administrative/system changes required**: Implementation requires a system modification for “tracking and utilization,” with a **one-time state share cost of $25,000** (assuming **75% federal match**).
- **Recipient cost-sharing remains**: All dental payments remain subject to a **$3.00 copay** for adults (31 Del. C. § 503(b)(2)c).

- The bill ties implementation to publication of a notice after CMS approval, but does not specify a **timeframe for CMS approval** or the expected implementation date.
