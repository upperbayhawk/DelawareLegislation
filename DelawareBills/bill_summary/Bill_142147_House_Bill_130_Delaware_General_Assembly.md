## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Titles 14 (public school employees) and 19 (other public employees) of the Delaware Code to require public employers to provide bargaining-unit employee contact information to the exclusive representative on set schedules, and to require employer access for union communications and meetings (with specified limits).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires public employers to provide specified bargaining-unit employee contact information to the exclusive representative **within 14 calendar days** of an employee’s **date of hire**.  
- Requires public employers to provide updated/complete bargaining-unit employee information **twice per year (January and October)**, **beginning in January 2026**.  
- The required information includes: **name; position; worksite location (including within-facility location when appropriate); employee ID number; home address; home and personal cell phone numbers on file; date of hire; work email; personal email on file**.  
- Requires the information be provided **“in a secure format agreed to by the exclusive representative.”**  
- Requires public employers to allow exclusive representatives to communicate with members using **employer-issued email addresses** for specified labor-relations and internal union governance/business purposes, consistent with **generally applicable technology use policies**.  
- Requires public employers to allow exclusive representatives to meet with members in **facilities owned or leased by the public employer**, so long as meetings **do not interfere with governmental operations**.  
- Prohibits meetings in government buildings (under this authority) from being used to **support or oppose any candidate for partisan political office** or to distribute materials about **partisan elections**.  
- Allows the government entity to **charge** the exclusive representative for **maintenance, security, and other costs** related to facility use that **would not otherwise be incurred**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved union-member communication and administration:** Explicitly authorizes union communication via **employer-issued email** for collective bargaining, contract administration, grievances, workplace complaints/issues, and internal union governance/business.  
- **More timely and complete member information:** Mandates delivery of contact details **within 14 days of hire** plus **January/October** recurring updates starting **January 2026**.  
- **Clarifies facility access for labor purposes:** Requires allowing meetings in employer-owned/leased facilities for labor-relations and internal union matters, subject to non-interference with operations.  
- **Cost recovery option for employers:** Allows charging for incremental **maintenance/security/other** costs incurred due to meetings.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative burden on public employers:** Requires collection, formatting, and transmission of detailed contact data on a **14-day** post-hire schedule plus **twice-yearly** full-unit reporting.  
- **Privacy/sensitivity concerns for employee data:** Requires sharing **home address**, **home and personal cell phone numbers**, and **personal email addresses on file** with the exclusive representative (in a “secure format”).  
- **Potential costs for unions to use facilities:** Meetings may be charged for **maintenance, security, and other** incremental costs.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- What qualifies as a **“secure format”** and what happens if the employer and exclusive representative **cannot agree** on the format.  
- Whether and how employees can **limit** disclosure of personal contact information beyond what is “on file” (e.g., opting out) is **not addressed**.  
- Scope and enforcement of “consistent with the employer’s **generally applicable technology use policies**” is not further defined (e.g., time-of-day limits, spam filters, content restrictions).  
- Standards for determining whether meetings “**interfere with governmental operations**” are not specified.  
- The bill allows charging for certain facility-use costs, but does not define how to calculate **“costs… that would not otherwise be incurred.”**

## Key Policy Changes
- **Title 14 (Public School Employment Relations Act) – new/updated § 4020:**  
  - Provide specified bargaining-unit employee contact information to the exclusive representative:
    - **Within 14 calendar days** of hire; and
    - **January and October** annually, **beginning January 2026**.  
  - Allow union communications to members via **employer-issued email** for enumerated labor and internal union purposes, consistent with general tech policies.  
  - Allow union meetings with members in employer-owned/leased facilities for enumerated purposes, as long as operations are not disrupted; bar partisan-election/candidate advocacy in government buildings under this meeting authority; allow charging for certain incremental facility-use costs.
- **Title 19 (Public Employment Relations Act) – new/updated § 1320:**  
  - Mirrors the same requirements and permissions described above for public employment bargaining units covered by Title 19.

## Who Benefits
- **Exclusive representatives (unions)** for public employee bargaining units (Titles 14 and 19), through mandated access to contact information, employer email communications, and meeting space.  
- **Bargaining-unit employees**, through facilitated communications related to collective bargaining, contract administration, grievances, and workplace complaints/issues (as described in the bill).
