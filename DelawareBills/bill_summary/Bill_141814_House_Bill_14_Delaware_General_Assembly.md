## Purpose
To propose the first-leg amendment to Article I of the Delaware Constitution to add an explicit constitutional **“Right of individual privacy”** stating it **“may not be infringed without a showing of a compelling state interest.”**

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds a new provision to **Article I** of the Delaware Constitution: **§ 22. Right of individual privacy.**
