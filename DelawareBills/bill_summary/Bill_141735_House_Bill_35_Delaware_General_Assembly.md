## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Propose the first-leg amendment to Article I of the Delaware Constitution to (1) constitutionally prohibit the imposition of the death penalty and (2) remove “capital offenses” language in the bail provision that would conflict with a constitutional ban on the death penalty.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends Article I, § 11 to state that “the death penalty [shall not be] imposed,” adding a constitutional prohibition on the death penalty.
- Amends Article I, § 12 to remove the “unless for capital offenses” exception to bail, because it is inconsistent with a constitutional prohibition of the death penalty.
- Makes technical edits striking the words “Section 11” and “Section 12” to align with prior constitutional technical corrections referenced as HB430 (152nd General Assembly) and expected second-leg passage in the 153rd.
- This bill is explicitly described as the “first leg” of a constitutional amendment; the same amendment must pass again after the next general election by the next General Assembly to become part of the Constitution.
- Requires a supermajority vote: two-thirds of the members elected to each house, as required by Article XVI, § 1 of the Delaware Constitution.
