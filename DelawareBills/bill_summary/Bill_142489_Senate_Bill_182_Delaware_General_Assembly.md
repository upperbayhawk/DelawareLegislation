## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s pharmacy law (Title 24, § 2523) to expressly allow manufacturers, manufacturer agents, or wholesalers to distribute FDA-approved dialysate drugs/devices for **home dialysis** directly to **end-stage renal disease (ESRD)** patients (or specified recipients) **without requiring a pharmacy license**, as long as specified conditions are met.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates/clarifies an **exemption** in **24 Del. C. § 2523(10)** allowing certain direct distribution of **dialysate drugs or devices** needed for home dialysis for **ESRD** patients.
- Distribution is allowed only if the dialysate drugs/devices are **FDA-approved** “as required by federal law.”
- Products must be **lawfully held** by a **manufacturer or manufacturer’s agent** that is **properly registered with the Board** as a **manufacturer or wholesaler**.
- Products must be held and delivered in **original, sealed label packaging** from the **manufacturing facility**.
- Delivery may occur **only upon receipt of a prescription from a practitioner**.
- Delivery may be made **directly** to (1) an **ESRD patient** or the patient’s **designee** for self-administration, or (2) a **health care provider or institution** for administration/delivery to an ESRD patient.
- The act was **approved July 21, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves access/logistics for home dialysis supplies**: The law explicitly allows manufacturers/wholesalers to ship dialysate drugs/devices directly to eligible recipients for home dialysis (Synopsis; § 2523(10)(e)).
- **Maintains safety and chain-of-custody conditions**: Requires **FDA approval**, Board registration of the distributing entity, **original sealed packaging**, and delivery only on a **practitioner prescription** (§ 2523(10)(a)-(d)).
- **Clarifies legal pathway without pharmacy licensure**: Synopsis states manufacturers/agents/wholesalers may ship directly to home dialysis patients **without a pharmacy license**.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced pharmacy involvement in dispensing**: By allowing distribution “without a pharmacy license” (Synopsis), pharmacies may be bypassed for these items, potentially reducing pharmacist-led dispensing touchpoints for these products (as reflected by the exemption structure in § 2523(10)).
- **Narrow scope/eligibility may limit impact**: The exemption applies only to **dialysate drugs or devices** for **home dialysis** in **ESRD** patients and only under specific delivery conditions (§ 2523(10)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Amends 24 Del. C. § 2523(10)** to allow a **manufacturer or wholesaler** (including a **manufacturer’s agent**) to distribute **dialysate drugs/devices** necessary for **home dialysis** for **ESRD** patients, provided:
  - **FDA approval** requirement is met (§ 2523(10)(a)).
  - Product is **lawfully held** by a properly **Board-registered** manufacturer/wholesaler (§ 2523(10)(b)).
  - Product remains in **original, sealed label packaging** from the manufacturing facility (§ 2523(10)(c)).
  - Delivery is **only by the manufacturer or manufacturer’s agent** and **only upon receipt of a practitioner prescription** (§ 2523(10)(d)).
  - Delivery is made directly to:
    1) the **ESRD patient** or the patient’s **designee** for self-administration, or  
    2) a **health care provider or institution** for administration/delivery to an ESRD patient (§ 2523(10)(e)1–2).
- Clarifies that this distribution can occur **without a pharmacy license** (Synopsis).

## Who Benefits
- **ESRD patients** using or seeking **home dialysis**, and their **designees** eligible to receive shipments (§ 2523(10)(e)1).
- **Health care providers and institutions** administering or delivering dialysis therapy to ESRD patients (§ 2523(10)(e)2).
- **Manufacturers, manufacturer agents, and wholesalers** distributing eligible dialysate drugs/devices under the exemption (Synopsis; § 2523(10)).
