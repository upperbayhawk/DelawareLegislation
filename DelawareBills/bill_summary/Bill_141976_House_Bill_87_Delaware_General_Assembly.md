## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend **Title 16, § 6201(c)** of the Delaware Code to **expand the membership** of the **Delaware Suicide Prevention Coalition** by adding **2 Governor-appointed members** with lived experience related to suicide.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds **2 new members** to the Delaware Suicide Prevention Coalition, both **appointed by the Governor**.  
- New required member categories: **(1)** a person who has **experienced suicide ideation or survived a suicide attempt**, and **(2)** a person who has **lost a loved one to suicide**.  
- Updates the Coalition’s statutory membership list in **16 Del. C. § 6201(c)** by adding new subsections **(11)** and **(12)**.  
- Leaves existing agency/organization member seats intact; does not remove or change current listed members beyond adding the two new positions.  
- The bill states the change is **requested by the Coalition**.  
- No funding, reporting, programmatic duties, or operational changes are specified beyond membership composition.  
- Enacted as **Chapter 129, Volume 85, Laws of Delaware**, and **approved August 12, 2025**.
