## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 7, § 6010 of the Delaware Code to limit the Department of Natural Resources and Environmental Control’s (DNREC’s) rulemaking authority by prohibiting DNREC from adopting rules/regulations or plans that are part of “Regulatory Initiatives” that **limit sales of any motor vehicle currently permitted under the Advanced Clean Car I regulations (adopted in 2014)**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits DNREC’s Secretary from adopting/amending/modifying/repealing rules, regulations, or plans (after public hearing) **when those actions are associated with “Regulatory Initiatives” that limit sales** of certain vehicles.
- The prohibition applies to **sales of any motor vehicle currently permitted under Delaware’s Advanced Clean Car I regulations adopted in 2014**.
- The bill’s synopsis states it targets **restrictions on the sale of fuel-powered cars, trucks, and SUVs**.
- The bill’s synopsis states it **does not change existing emission standards** that fuel-powered vehicles must meet.
- Operates as a constraint on DNREC’s rulemaking under **7 Del. C. § 6010(a)**, while leaving in place the general rule that DNREC rules cannot extend, modify, or conflict with state law.
- The synopsis states the bill is **retroactive to March 1, 2023**.
- No dollar amounts, fees, or appropriations are specified in the provided text.

## Key Policy Changes
