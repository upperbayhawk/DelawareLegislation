## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 18, Delaware Code to require payment of a fixed **$7,000** funeral benefit for eligible deceased members of volunteer fire companies, volunteer fire company ladies auxiliaries, and volunteer ambulance and rescue companies, and to clarify eligibility and funding source rules, including when the deceased was also a state employee eligible for certain burial benefits.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Replaces “reasonable funeral expenses… not to exceed $7,000” with a requirement that funeral expenses **must be paid in the amount of $7,000** for eligible deceased volunteer members.  
- Establishes/clarifies that an individual is eligible if they served in Delaware as an **active member for at least 10 years**, including combined service across **1 or more** eligible volunteer organizations.  
- Clarifies that eligibility under this chapter can apply **even if** the individual was also a state employee entitled to certain burial benefits (specifically citing **§ 8331, § 8395, § 8846 of Title 11; § 5316, § 5546 of Title 29**).  
- Specifies that payments under this chapter **must be made from the fund established under Subchapter III of Chapter 65, Title 18**, with legislative responsibility to provide adequate funding when necessary.  
- Fiscal note estimates the policy change will increase costs because each claim becomes a **$7,000 payment**, with an estimated **annual cost increase of $95,477** (based on FY2020–FY2024 data).  
- Estimated appropriations impact: **FY2026: $72,658 (prorated due to 10/1/2025 effective date); FY2027: $96,877; FY2028: $96,877**.  
- Effective date is **October 1, 2025**; the act was **approved September 9, 2025** (per enacted chapter text).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Predictable benefit amount:** Requires a uniform **$7,000** payment, reducing variability tied to what is deemed “reasonable” (text changes “reasonable… not to exceed” to “must be paid… $7,000”).  
- **Clearer eligibility standard:** Codifies a **10-year active service** requirement and allows aggregation across multiple Delaware volunteer organizations, which can simplify determinations.  
- **Clarifies interaction with state employee burial benefits:** Explicitly states that individuals eligible for certain state employee burial benefits may **also** be eligible for this chapter’s benefit (lists specific statutory benefits), which can reduce disputes over dual eligibility.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher state/fund cost per claim:** Fiscal note indicates removing the “reasonable expenses” limitation results in **$7,000 payments for each claim** and an estimated **annual cost increase of $95,477**.  
- **Potential overpayment relative to actual costs:** Because payment is fixed at **$7,000**, it may exceed actual funeral expenses in some cases (implied by replacing “reasonable funeral expenses” with a mandated amount).  
- **Funding pressure on designated fund:** Payments must come from the Title 18 Chapter 65 Subchapter III fund, and the General Assembly must provide adequate funding “when necessary,” potentially requiring added appropriations.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Benefit amount change:** From “reasonable funeral expenses… not to exceed **$7,000**” to funeral expenses **must be paid in the amount of $7,000**.  
- **Eligibility requirement clarified:** Eligible individual must have served in Delaware as an active member for **at least 10 years**, including service with **1 or more** eligible volunteer organizations in Delaware.  
- **State employee dual-benefit clarification:** If eligible under this chapter and also entitled to burial benefits under **11 Del. C. § 8331, § 8395, § 8846** or **29 Del. C. § 5316, § 5546**, the individual is **also eligible** for this chapter’s benefit.  
- **Source of payments:** Payments **must** be made from the fund established under **Subchapter III of Chapter 65, Title 18**; General Assembly to provide adequate funding when necessary.
