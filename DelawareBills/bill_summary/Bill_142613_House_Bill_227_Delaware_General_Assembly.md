## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 10 of the Delaware Code to require the Court of Chancery to audio record all **public proceedings** and make the recordings **publicly accessible online**, with limited exceptions.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires the Court of Chancery to **audio record all public proceedings** starting **January 1, 2027**.  
- Requires audio recordings to be posted on a **publicly accessible online platform** maintained by the **Administrative Office of the Courts**.  
- Recordings must be made available **“within a reasonable time”** after the proceeding concludes (no specific number of days stated).  
- Each recording must include a **disclaimer** that it **is not an official court transcript**.  
- Allows the presiding judicial officer to **restrict public access** to a recording **in whole or in part** for **good cause shown**.  
- Provides example reasons for restriction, including protecting **confidential business/personal information**, **safeguarding witnesses**, or **ensuring a fair trial**.  
- Any decision to withhold/redact must be documented in a **written order**, generally made part of the **public record** unless confidentiality requires otherwise.  
- Directs the Court of Chancery to adopt **administrative rules** to implement the law by **December 3, 2026**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Greater public transparency and access**: requires public online access to recordings of public proceedings (new §332(a)-(b)).  
- **Improved public understanding and accountability**: disclaimer clarifies recordings are informational and not a transcript (§332(b)(3)).  
- **Structured process for limiting access when necessary**: requires a **written order** for withholding/redaction and contemplates public filing unless confidentiality requires otherwise (§332(c)(3)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Unclear turnaround expectations**: recordings must be posted within a **“reasonable time”** but no defined deadline, which may lead to inconsistent availability (§332(b)(2)).  
- **Administrative workload and operational demands**: mandates recording of **all** public proceedings and online posting, plus rulemaking by a fixed date (§332(a), §332(b)(1), §332(d)).  
- **Potential disputes over access restrictions**: broad “good cause” standard may prompt litigation or contested motions about withholding/redaction (§332(c)(1)-(2)).
