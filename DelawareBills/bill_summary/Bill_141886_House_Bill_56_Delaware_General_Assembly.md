## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Require Delaware-regulated health coverage—including individual and group private insurance, the State employee health plan, and Medicaid health benefit plans—to cover **medically necessary removal of excess skin and subcutaneous tissue**, including **panniculectomy**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Mandates coverage in **individual health insurance** policies delivered/issued/renewed/extended/modified in Delaware for **medically necessary** removal of excess skin and subcutaneous tissue, including **panniculectomy** (Title 18, Ch. 33).
- Mandates the same coverage in **group and blanket health insurance** policies delivered/issued/renewed/extended/modified in Delaware (Title 18, Ch. 35).
- Requires the **State employee health insurance plan** to cover medically necessary removal of excess skin and subcutaneous tissue, including panniculectomy (Title 29, Ch. 52).
- Requires **carriers** to cover the same service in **health benefit plans delivered or issued under 31 Del. C. § 503(3)** (State Medicaid-related health benefit plans) (Title 31, Ch. 5).
- Defines **“panniculectomy”** as an operative procedure to contour the abdominal wall by removing **significant excess skin and subcutaneous adipose tissue**.
- Uses existing statutory definitions of **“medically necessary”** (Title 18 § 3371(8) for individual plans; Title 18 § 3581(8) for group/blanket, State employee plan, and Medicaid plans).
- Allows standard plan features (e.g., **deductibles, coinsurance, allowable charge limits, coordination of benefits**, and provider/facility network restrictions).
- Applicability is delayed: applies to policies/contracts/certificates **issued/renewed/modified/altered/amended/reissued after December 31, 2026**.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Title 18 (Individual insurance):** Requires all individual health insurance policies/contracts/certificates delivered/issued for delivery/renewed/extended/modified in Delaware to cover **medically necessary removal of excess skin and subcutaneous tissue**, including **panniculectomy** (new § 3370M).
- **Title 18 (Group & blanket insurance):** Requires all group and blanket health insurance policies/contracts/certificates delivered/issued for delivery/renewed/extended/modified in Delaware to cover the same (new § 3571EE).
- **Title 29 (State employee plan):** Requires the State employee health insurance plan to cover medically necessary removal of excess skin and subcutaneous tissue, including panniculectomy (§ 5223).
- **Title 31 (Medicaid-related health benefit plans):** Requires carriers to provide this coverage in all health benefit plans delivered or issued under **31 Del. C. § 503(3)** (§ 538).
- **Plan design preserved:** Permits deductibles, coinsurance, allowable charge limitations, coordination of benefits, and provider/facility restrictions (for Title 18 individual and group/blanket sections).
- **Exclusions:** Requirement does **not** apply to accident-only, specified disease, hospital indemnity, Medicare supplement, long-term care, disability income, or other limited benefit health insurance policies (Title 18 sections).
