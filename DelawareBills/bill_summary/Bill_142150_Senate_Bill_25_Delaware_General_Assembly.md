## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the Public School Employment Relations Act (Title 14) and public employment relations provisions (Title 19) to allow an employee organization to be certified as an exclusive bargaining representative through a “majority verification” process (signature verification), without holding an election, when **over 50%** of employees in the proposed appropriate bargaining unit authorize representation.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new **majority verification procedure** in **Title 14 (§ 4011A)** for public school employees and **Title 19 (§ 1311B)** for public employees.
- Allows certification based on a showing that **over 50%** of employees in the proposed unit provided **dated representation authorization signatures**.
- Requires the Board to **investigate** the petition and signatures.
- If the Board determines the **over-50%** threshold is met, the Board **may not order an election** and **shall certify** the employee organization.
- Leaves in place the existing petition-and-election framework requiring **30%** signature support to trigger unit determination/certification processes, and **10%** support for other organizations to appear on an election ballot.
- Retains existing election-related timing rules, including that **no election** may be held within **12 months** of a preceding valid election.
- Includes “technical corrections” stated to align with the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual (no specific substantive examples listed beyond the shown edits).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Faster certification where majority support is demonstrated:** If **over 50%** sign authorizations, the Board must certify and **cannot order an election** (Titles 14/19), potentially shortening time to representation compared to running an election.
- **Clear threshold for non-election certification:** Establishes a defined standard—**over 50%**—for certification via verification (Titles 14/19).
- **Applies to both school and other public employees:** Parallel provisions in **Title 14** and **Title 19** standardize the approach across these covered groups.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Election may be bypassed even when some employees may prefer a secret-ballot process:** The bill states the Board **may not order an election** if the **over-50%** signature threshold is met (Titles 14/19).
- **Administrative verification burden:** The Board must collect, review, and **investigate** dated authorization signatures to confirm the **over-50%** showing (Titles 14/19), which may increase workload compared with relying solely on elections in some cases.

## Key Policy Changes
- **New Title 14 § 4011A (Public School Employment Relations Act):**
  - Employee organization may petition for certification based on verification that **over 50%** of employees in the proposed unit want representation.
  - Requires **dated** authorization signatures.
  - Board must **investigate**.
  - If Board finds **over 50%** authorization signatures, Board **may not order an election** and **shall certify** the organization.
- **New Title 19 § 1311B (Public Employees):** Mirrors the Title 14 majority verification procedure with the same **over-50%** threshold, dated signatures, investigation, and mandatory certification without election.
- **Existing thresholds retained (as shown/confirmed in amended text):**
  - **30%** signatures to support petitions for unit determination/certification and for decertification petitions (Titles 14/19).
  - **10%** showing for other organizations to be listed on an election ballot (Titles 14/19).
  - Signatures for certain petitions are invalid if signed more than **12 months** before filing (Titles 14/19 provisions shown in §§ 4011(a) and 1311(a), not in the new sections).

## Who Benefits
- **Employee organizations** seeking certification as exclusive representative, particularly where they can secure **over 50%** authorization signatures and avoid an election.
- **Employees favoring representation** who want certification based on majority sign-up rather than waiting for an election process.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Employees who prefer a secret-ballot election** may have less opportunity for an election if the Board verifies **over 50%** authorizations (Titles 14/19).
- **Public employers** may face potentially faster union certification timelines where majority authorization is achieved (process change reflected in Titles 14/19).
