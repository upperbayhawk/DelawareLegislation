## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend 29 Del. C. § 2553 to require the Department of Justice’s Division of Civil Rights and Public Trust (DCRPT) to provide **quarterly reports** to the General Assembly, the Governor, and the Office of Legislative Services about **federal requests for assistance** to state law enforcement agencies related to specified matters involving **undocumented residents**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires DOJ’s **DCRPT** to submit a **quarterly report** to: the **Chief Clerk of the House** (for distribution to all members), the **Secretary of the Senate** (for distribution to all members), the **Governor**, and the **Director and Librarian of the Office of Legislative Services**.
- Reports must detail **any request** from a **federal agency/federal entity** (or an entity “**holding itself out**” as one) for assistance from **any State law enforcement agency** related to specified topics.
- Covered topics include requests involving: **driving privilege cards**, the **migrant education program and related funding contingency**, **stops based purely on suspicion of undocumented status**, operations in **schools or churches**, **SRO/constable participation** in federal immigration activity, sharing information about an **undocumented student**, and release of information about an **undocumented resident** from finance/revenue agencies.
- Fiscal note anticipates DCRPT will need **1 additional Legal Assistant III** to coordinate multi-agency data collection and reporting.
- Estimated **FY 2026 personnel cost** (including **OECs at 33.34%**) is **$75,413**, plus **one-time** startup costs of **$6,670** for technology equipment and furniture.
- FY 2026 funding is estimated for **nine months**, with annualization of the remaining **three months** in later years.
- Out-year estimated personnel costs: **FY 2027: $102,561**; **FY 2028: $104,613**, including a **2% inflation** factor.
