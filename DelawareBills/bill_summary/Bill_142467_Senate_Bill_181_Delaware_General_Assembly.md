## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 6 of the Delaware Code to regulate the secondary ticketing market for events in Delaware by establishing pricing and fee transparency, restricting presale and speculative ticket practices, banning certain deceptive and bot-driven conduct, and creating registration/bonding/reporting, audit, and enforcement mechanisms administered by the Department of Justice’s Division of Consumer Protection (“Division”).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **clear, conspicuous “all-in” ticket price disclosure** (total price including **all fees and taxes**) and an **itemized breakdown** before a buyer must enter payment information. (§ 2502N)
- **Prohibits speculative ticket sales** (listing/selling tickets not in the reseller’s actual/constructive possession, ownership, or contract to be transferred at time of listing/sale/advertisement). (§ 2503N)
- Restricts resale **before public on-sale**: resellers/secondary exchanges (and affiliates) generally **may not resell until tickets are placed on sale to the general public**; also bars access/resale via **fan club presales** before public on-sale (with a season/subscription holder exception). (§ 2504N)
- Establishes **refund requirements** (full refund including fees/taxes) for counterfeit tickets, cancelled events, and nonconforming tickets; refunds due **within 10 days** of cancellation or discovery of nonconformance. (§ 2506N)
- Imposes **anti-bot and anti-circumvention rules**, including a limit on using multiple IPs/accounts/emails to exceed **8 tickets (or the posted limit if less)** for a single internet ticket sale; requires platforms to report known circumvention to the Division **within 48 hours** and retain records **1 year**. (§ 2508N)
- Sets pricing limits: reseller’s total price **may not exceed the initial total ticket price (including fees and taxes)**; secondary exchange service fee **may not exceed 10%** of the initial total ticket price. (§ 2509N)
- Requires annual **registration** for resellers who resell **100+ tickets per year above a ticket’s original total price** (with an exclusion for certain sports season ticket package resales); requires **biannual reports**, a **minimum $10,000 surety bond**, and allows audits and registration revocation. (§§ 2510N–2516N)
- Establishes significant **civil penalties**, including **at least $15,000 per day** plus additional per-ticket penalties, and requires a public reporting website and annual enforcement reporting. (§ 2517N)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved price transparency for consumers** by requiring disclosure of the **total price** (fees/taxes included) and **itemization** before payment information is requested. (§ 2502N)
- **Consumer financial protection** through mandatory **full refunds** in defined situations and a set **10-day** refund timeline. (§ 2506N)
- **Reduced deceptive marketplace practices** by banning deceptive URLs/unauthorized use of artists’/venues’/organizers’ intellectual property and misleading “official” claims without express written consent. (§ 2507N)
- **Reduced ticket purchase circumvention** through explicit restrictions on bots, queue/security circumvention, and multi-account/IP tactics beyond **8 tickets (or lower posted limit)**, plus mandatory reporting to the Division. (§ 2508N)
- **Compensation mechanism for nondelivery** via a required **surety bond (≥ $10,000)** that can cover consumer losses including certain **travel expenses**; exchanges must coordinate and ensure bond funds are released within **60 days of the event date**. (§§ 2511N, 2513N)
- **Increased accountability** through registration, reseller reporting, exchange recordkeeping/reporting, and Division audit authority. (§§ 2510N, 2512N, 2514N)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Limits on resale pricing and exchange fees**: resellers may not exceed the **initial total ticket price** and secondary exchange fees are capped at **10%** of the initial total price, potentially reducing reseller/exchange revenue. (§ 2509N)
- **Compliance burden** for resellers and exchanges due to annual registration, biannual reporting, bonding, recordkeeping, and audit cooperation requirements. (§§ 2510N–2514N)
- **Operational and reporting obligations** on primary ticketing platforms (e.g., reporting circumvention within **48 hours**, retaining records for **1 year**). (§ 2508N)
- **Potential barriers to entry** for higher-volume resellers due to the **minimum $10,000 bond** requirement and Division-set registration fees. (§§ 2510N, 2511N)
- **High civil penalty exposure** (including **≥ $15,000 per day** plus per-ticket penalties and enhanced penalties for intentional violations). (§ 2517N)

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The bill states the Division will set a **registration fee** “to cover the costs of compliance and enforcement,” but the **amount and structure** are **not specified** in the statutory text. (§ 2510N(d))
- The text caps reseller price at the **initial total ticket price**, while the synopsis states a cap of **10% of the initial ticket price**; the operative statutory provision differs from the synopsis. (Compare § 2509N(a) vs. Synopsis)
- Definitions and application details for “**affiliate**” (used in presale restrictions) are **not specified** in source text. (§ 2504N)
- “**Reasonable travel expenses**” are listed (airfare, hotel, meals), but standards/limits for reasonableness are **not specified**. (§ 2513N)
- The Division “shall periodically review and adjust” bond amounts based on sales volume/complaint history, but **frequency, methodology, and thresholds** are **not specified**. (§ 2511N(c))
- The meaning of “**original total price**” in the 100-ticket registration threshold (tickets resold “above a ticket’s original total price”) could require regulatory clarification on calculation and documentation. (§ 2510N(a))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Mandatory upfront pricing disclosures** (total price including fees/taxes; itemization; seat/section disclosure where applicable). (§ 2502N)
- **Ban on speculative ticket listings/sales.** (§ 2503N)
- **Presale resale restrictions** tied to the general public on-sale date/time; prohibits fan-club access/resale before general on-sale (with a season/subscription holder exception). (§ 2504N)
- **Transferability restrictions allowed only with pre-purchase disclosure and consumer acknowledgement**; clarifies tickets are a “license” and venues may enforce conduct/safety/age/quantity policies. (§ 2505N)
- **Refund rules** with a **10-day** deadline for full refunds under enumerated circumstances. (§ 2506N)
- **Deceptive marketing/URL and IP misuse prohibitions** without authorization/consent. (§ 2507N)
- **Anti-bot / anti-circumvention rules**, including an **8-ticket** (or lower posted limit) anti-circumvention provision; **48-hour** reporting to the Division; **1-year** record retention. (§ 2508N)
- **Resale and exchange fee caps**: resale total price capped at the initial total price; exchange fee capped at **10%** of the initial total price. (§ 2509N)
- **Reseller registration threshold**: **100+ tickets/year** resold above original total price triggers annual registration; requires identifying info, **biannual reporting**, confidentiality of reports, and a Division-set registration fee. (§ 2510N)
- **Surety bond requirement**: **≥ $10,000**, adjustable by the Division; proof required before listing on exchanges. (§ 2511N)
- **Exchange duties**: verify reseller registration/bonding, report unregistered resellers, maintain and submit **biannual reports**. (§ 2512N)
- **Consumer compensation** via bond; exchange must ensure release of bond funds **within 60 days of the event date** for nondelivery. (§ 2513N)
- **Audits, public registry, and registration revocation** authority (**1 to 5 years** revocation period). (§§ 2514N–2516N)
- **Civil penalties** for violations, including per-day and per-ticket enhancements; violations deemed an unlawful practice under § 2513 of Title 6. (§ 2517N)

## Who Benefits
- **Ticket purchasers/consumers** (price transparency, refund rights, bond-backed compensation for nondelivery). (§§ 2502N, 2506N, 2511N, 2513N)
- **Artists, venues, event organizers** (restrictions on deceptive “official” claims and unauthorized IP use). (§ 2507N)
- **Compliant primary ticket sellers/platforms and legitimate resellers** (clearer rules and enforcement against bots/speculative/deceptive practices). (§§ 2503N, 2508N, 2517N)
- **State enforcement agencies (Division)** through explicit authority and reporting/audit tools. (§§ 2514N–2517N)

- **Secondary ticket exchanges** (fee cap at **10%**; verification/reporting/recordkeeping and biannual reporting duties). (§§ 2509N(b), 2512N)
- **Primary ticketing platforms** (48-hour circumvention reporting and 1-year record retention obligations). (§ 2508N)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective immediately upon enactment** (and fiscal note assumes upon Governor’s signature). (Section 2; Fiscal Note Assumption 1)
- **Implementation timing:** earlier of:
  1) **1 year from the date of enactment**, or  
  2) the date the Division publishes notice in the **Register of Regulations** that final implementing regulations have been promulgated. (Section 2)
- **Refund deadline:** within **10 days** of event cancellation or discovery of nonconformance. (§ 2506N(b))
- **Platform reporting deadline:** report known circumvention/attempted circumvention **within 48 hours of discovery**. (§ 2508N(b))
- **Record retention:** retain relevant circumvention records for **1 year**. (§ 2508N(c))
- **Public reseller list update frequency:** at least once every **30 days**. (§ 2515N(b))
- **Bond compensation timing:** exchange must ensure release of bond funds **within 60 days of the event date** (for nondelivery cases). (§ 2513N(b))
- **Reseller reporting cadence:** **biannual** reports to the Division. (§ 2510N(e))
- **Exchange reporting cadence:** **biannual** reports to the Division. (§ 2512N(c))
- **Annual enforcement report:** prepared annually and made available to the public. (§ 2517N(e))
- **Registration:** resellers meeting the threshold must **register annually** before conducting resale activity. (§ 2510N(a))

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Enforcement authority:** Division may implement/enforce via regulations with the **force and effect of law**; may audit resellers/exchanges and require cooperation and documentation. (§§ 2514N, 2517N(f))
- **Civil penalties (violations are an unlawful practice under § 2513 of Title 6):**
  - **Base penalty:** at least **$15,000 for each day** the violation occurs or continues. (§ 2517N(b)(1))
  - **Additional penalty:** greater of **$1,000 per ticket** listed/advertised/sold/resold in violation, **or** **5 times the total ticket price** of each violating ticket. (§ 2517N(b)(2))
  - **Intentional violation:** additional civil penalty of at least **$10,000 per ticket** sold or resold in violation. (§ 2517N(c))
- **Registration revocation:** Division may revoke reseller registration for **at least 1 year and not more than 5 years** for violations. (§ 2516N)
- **Core compliance requirements include:**
  - Pricing disclosures before payment info required and before final purchase. (§ 2502N)
  - No speculative ticket sales. (§ 2503N)
  - Presale resale restrictions and fan-club access restrictions. (§ 2504N)
  - Refund processing within statutory timelines. (§ 2506N)
  - No deceptive URLs/unauthorized IP use/false affiliation claims. (§ 2507N)
  - No bot/circumvention conduct; platform reporting and retention. (§ 2508N)
  - Adherence to resale and fee caps. (§ 2509N)
  - Annual registration (threshold-based), **biannual** reporting, confidentiality of reseller reports, surety bond **≥ $10,000**, exchange verification/reporting duties, and audit cooperation. (§§ 2510N–2514N)
