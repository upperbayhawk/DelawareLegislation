## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware election law (Title 15) to allow registered voters who are **unaffiliated with any political party** to vote in a political party’s **primary election**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Allows a duly registered voter who is **unaffiliated** (as shown on the voter’s “original permanent registration record”) to vote in a political party’s **primary election**.  
- Keeps the existing rule that an elector may vote in a party primary if the elector’s **party affiliation matches** the party holding the primary.  
- Updates primary-election voting procedures so election officers must check the voter’s registration record and deny voting only if the voter is **neither affiliated with that party nor unaffiliated**.  
- Provides that an unaffiliated voter who votes a party’s primary will be issued the **party ballot of their choice**.  
- States that the voter’s **preference becomes public record**, but the voter **does not become automatically affiliated** with that party.  
- Notes in the bill text that there are **approximately 170,000** registered Delaware voters who are unaffiliated.  
- Confirms that **party members still may not “crossover”** to vote in another party’s primary (per the bill’s synopsis text).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expands primary participation** to unaffiliated voters: the bill explicitly allows unaffiliated registered voters to vote in a party primary.  
- **Maintains anti-crossover rule for party members**: the bill and synopsis preserve that party-affiliated voters cannot vote in a different party’s primary unless qualified (i.e., unaffiliated).  
- **Clarifies administrative handling** for unaffiliated voters at the polls by directing issuance of a **party ballot of the voter’s choice** and specifying the recordkeeping outcome.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Public-record disclosure of primary choice for unaffiliated voters:** the bill states the voter’s “preference will become public record,” which may reduce privacy for unaffiliated voters who participate in a primary.  
- **Potential additional poll-work steps:** election officers must apply revised qualification checks and ballot issuance rules for unaffiliated voters (administrative complexity is implied by the procedural changes in §3161).

## Key Policy Changes
- **15 Del. C. §3110 (Qualifications of voters):**
  - Adds/clarifies that an elector may vote in a party primary if the elector is a duly registered voter and the registration record shows the elector is **unaffiliated with a party**.
- **15 Del. C. §3161 (Voting procedure):**
  - Updates the primary voting procedure to deny a ballot only if the voter’s affiliation does not match the party holding the primary **and** the voter is **not** qualified as unaffiliated under §3110(b).
  - Provides that qualified unaffiliated voters will be issued the **party ballot of their choice**; their **preference becomes public record**; and they **do not become automatically affiliated** with that party.
