## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14 to establish a **Teacher Academy Scholarship** for students who completed a Delaware Teacher Academy and enroll **full-time** in a **Delaware educator preparation program**, providing a **$2,500, one-year** award beginning with the **2025–2026 school year**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new **Teacher Academy scholarship** in Delaware law for eligible students enrolled in a **Delaware college or university** and an approved **educator preparation program** (as defined in **14 Del. C. § 1280**).
- Sets scholarship amount at **$2,500** for **1 year only**, payable in **2 equal parts** (one each semester) (optional payment structure).
- Limits awards to **no more than 35 scholarships per year**.
- Requires enrollment **as a full-time student** intending to earn a **bachelor’s degree**, starting the **fall semester directly after high school graduation**.
- Allows applicants for the **Educator Support Scholarship (14 Del. C. § 3438)** to be considered for this scholarship; **a separate application may not be required**.
- Requires applicants to apply for and accept all eligible financial aid **except loans** (must apply for Pell, Delaware Higher Education Office aid, and institutional aid).
- If applications exceed funding, Department of Education must develop **competitive selection criteria**.
- Fiscal note estimates state-funded costs of **up to $87,500 per fiscal year** (FY26–FY28), consistent with **35 x $2,500 = $87,500**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Targets teacher pipeline**: Supports students who completed a **Delaware Teacher Academy** and immediately enter a Delaware educator prep program (eligibility requirements in § 3438A(d)).
- **Defined, predictable award**: Fixed scholarship amount (**$2,500**) and duration (**1 year**) provides clear benefit to recipients (§ 3438A(c)).
- **Administrative simplification for some applicants**: Students applying for the **Educator Support Scholarship** must also be considered, and **a separate application may not be required** (§ 3438A(e)).
- **Leverages other aid first**: Requirement to apply for and accept non-loan aid may reduce duplicative support and coordinate funding (§ 3438A(f)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Limited reach due to cap**: No more than **35 scholarships per year**, even though the fiscal note cites approximately **930 senior students** enrolled in a Teacher Academy as of **March 2024** (fiscal note; Section 2 cap).
- **Narrow timing window**: Applicant must begin the educator preparation program **the fall semester directly after high school graduation**, excluding delayed entrants (§ 3438A(d)).
- **Only one-year support**: Scholarship is **1 year only**, which may not assist beyond the first year (§ 3438A(c)).
- **Competitive awards possible**: If applicants exceed funding, awards may become competitive based on DOE criteria, which may reduce predictability for applicants (§ 3438A(c)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Selection criteria not defined**: DOE must develop criteria if oversubscribed, but the bill does not specify what factors must be used (§ 3438A(c)).
- **“May be paid in 2 equal parts”**: The bill allows (but does not require) semester-split payment; exact payment schedule could vary (§ 3438A(c)).
- **Interaction with other scholarships**: The bill says it “supplements” the Educator Support Scholarship (Synopsis), but does not specify how awards coordinate if a student receives multiple awards beyond the aid-acceptance rules.
- **Eligibility breadth vs. fiscal note assumption**: Fiscal note assumes “all enrolled students who attend a Delaware Educator Preparation Program will be eligible,” but statutory eligibility includes Teacher Academy completion and immediate fall enrollment after graduation (§ 3438A(d); fiscal note Assumption #3).

## Budget Impact
- **State funds**: Scholarship “shall be paid through state funds” (fiscal note Assumption #3).
- Estimated maximum costs (fiscal note):

  - **Fiscal Year 2027: up to $87,500**
  - **Fiscal Year 2028: up to $87,500**
- Cost aligns with statutory cap: **35 awards/year x $2,500 = $87,500**.

  - Administer scholarship and verify eligibility (Teacher Academy completion; full-time Delaware educator prep enrollment; immediate fall start) (§ 3438A(d)).

  - Ensure applicants have applied for and accepted required non-loan aid (§ 3438A(f)).
  - Coordinate consideration with **Educator Support Scholarship** applications; may not require a separate application (§ 3438A(e)).

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds **14 Del. C. § 3438A** establishing the **Teacher Academy scholarship**.
- Defines:
  - “Career and Technical Education programs”
  - “Educator preparation program” (approved under **§ 1280**)
  - “Teacher Academy” (CTE program in Delaware public, charter, and vo-tech schools) (§ 3438A(a)).
- Sets scholarship amount/duration: **$2,500**, **1 year only**, may be paid in **two equal semester payments** (§ 3438A(c)).
- Establishes eligibility conditions: Teacher Academy completion; Delaware educator prep program enrollment; **full-time**; intent for **bachelor’s degree**; start **fall directly after high school graduation** (§ 3438A(d)).
- Requires consideration alongside **Educator Support Scholarship** applications; separate application may not be required (§ 3438A(e)).
- Requires applicants to apply for/accept non-loan aid (Pell, Delaware Higher Education Office aid, institutional aid); loans excluded (§ 3438A(f)).
- Caps awards at **35 per year**; expansion contingent on interest and funding; continues as funding is available (Section 2).

## Who Benefits
- Students who **successfully completed a Delaware Teacher Academy** and enroll **full-time** in a **Delaware** educator preparation program beginning the **fall semester immediately after graduation** (§ 3438A(d)).
- Applicants to the **Educator Support Scholarship** who may be automatically considered without a separate application (§ 3438A(e)).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Eligible students who apply but are not selected when applications exceed available funding (competitive criteria may be used) (§ 3438A(c)).
- Students who completed a Teacher Academy but:
  - enroll **part-time**, or
  - enroll **outside Delaware**, or
  - do not start the program **the fall directly after graduation** (§ 3438A(d)).
- Potential applicants beyond the **35-per-year** cap (Section 2).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective upon signature of the Governor** (fiscal note Assumption #1).
- Scholarship availability: beginning in the **2025–2026 school year** for students “entering or enrolled in an educator preparation program” (Section 2).
- Eligibility timing requirement: must begin the educator preparation program **the fall semester directly after high school graduation** (§ 3438A(d)).
- Annual award limit: **no more than 35 scholarships may be awarded per year** (Section 2).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Compliance requirements for applicants**:
  - Must apply for all appropriate financial aid, including **Federal Pell Grant**, Delaware Higher Education Office programs, and institutional aid (§ 3438A(f)).
  - Must accept all awarded/offered financial assistance **except loans** (§ 3438A(f)).
  - Must meet enrollment and timing conditions (full-time; Delaware educator prep; immediate fall start) (§ 3438A(d)).
- **No penalties specified** for noncompliance in the source text.
