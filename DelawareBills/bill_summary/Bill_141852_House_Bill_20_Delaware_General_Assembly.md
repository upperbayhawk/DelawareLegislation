## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To expand and formalize criminal background check and fingerprinting requirements for Delaware Department of Labor (DOL) employees, contractors, subcontractors, and volunteers who (1) access federal tax information (FTI) and other sensitive/confidential information, (2) access certain DOL-maintained funds, or (3) have one-on-one contact with vulnerable vocational rehabilitation clients—consistent with **26 U.S.C. § 6103(p)(4)** and **IRS Publication 1075**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands background check requirements beyond the Division of Unemployment Insurance FTI roles to include additional DOL divisions/roles: **Unemployment Insurance, Paid Leave, DOL IT staff, Industrial Affairs roles with access to the Workers’ Compensation Second Injury Fund, and Vocational Rehabilitation Counselors**.
- Requires certain applicants to undergo a criminal background check **after the completion of the first interview** for covered positions/roles.
- Requires covered individuals to provide **fingerprints** so DOL can obtain **both state and federal criminal history record information** (SBI + FBI).
- Creates ongoing monitoring: the State Bureau of Identification (SBI) must provide **subsequent criminal history record information** to DOL, including **Rap Back System** information **if available**.
- Requires individuals with access to **FTI** to have a **subsequent criminal background check at least every 5 years** (or earlier as needed to comply with IRS Publication 1075).
- Requires certain **current employees** (as of the Act’s effective date) to submit fingerprints to enable ongoing notifications of criminal history changes.
- Authorizes DOL to adopt standards for screening background checks and determining **disqualifying criminal convictions**, and to make qualification determinations.
- Reorganizes statute by moving the broader background-check provision into **Title 19, Chapter 1 (§118)** and conforming related language in **§3307**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved compliance with federal requirements**: Explicitly ties checks to **26 U.S.C. § 6103(p)(4)** and **IRS Publication 1075**, including periodic rechecks **not less than every 5 years** for those with FTI access.
- **Enhanced protection of sensitive/confidential information**: Covers personally identifiable information, confidential unemployment compensation information, and confidential paid leave information (family and medical leave benefits referenced in **§ 3701**).
- **Stronger safeguards for public funds**: Targets roles with access to the **Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund**, **Family and Medical Leave Insurance Account Fund**, and **Workers’ Compensation Second Injury Fund**.
- **Ongoing criminal-history monitoring**: Requires SBI to provide **subsequent** criminal history information (including Rap Back if available), enabling DOL awareness after initial screening.
- **Protection for vulnerable clients**: Applies to **Vocational Rehabilitation Counselors** who have direct one-on-one contact with vulnerable individuals.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Broader screening may create hiring/retention barriers**: More categories of workers (including **volunteers**, **contractors**, and **subcontractors**) must undergo fingerprint-based background checks for covered roles.
- **Disqualification criteria not defined in statute**: DOL is authorized to decide what qualifies as a **disqualifying criminal conviction**, which may create uncertainty for applicants and workers (standards are not specified in the text).
- **Administrative burden and processing time**: Fingerprinting and state/federal checks (SBI + FBI) and ongoing monitoring may increase onboarding and management workload for DOL and SBI (specific workload estimates not provided).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates/expands Title 19 §118** to require criminal background checks and fingerprinting for DOL employees/contractors/subcontractors/volunteers with:
  - access to **federal tax information** (per IRS Pub. 1075),
  - access to sensitive/confidential information (including confidential unemployment compensation and paid leave information),
  - access to specified funds (UI Trust Fund; Family and Medical Leave Insurance Account Fund; Workers’ Compensation Second Injury Fund),
  - or one-on-one contact with vulnerable clients as **Vocational Rehabilitation Counselors**.
- **Trigger for applicants**: Covered applicants must submit to a background check **after the completion of the first interview** (for listed divisions/roles).
- **Promotion/transfer trigger**: After the Act’s effective date, an employee seeking a promotion/transfer into a role that requires checks must submit after the first interview for that promotion/transfer.
- **Current workforce fingerprinting** (as of the Act’s effective date): Requires fingerprints to enable ongoing receipt of subsequent criminal history info for:
  - all employees of **Unemployment Insurance**,
  - all employees of **Paid Leave**,
  - all **DOL IT staff**,
  - Industrial Affairs employees with access to **Second Injury Fund**,
  - **Vocational Rehabilitation Counselors**.
- **Scope of check**: Requires both:
  - entire **state criminal history record information** (SBI), and
  - entire **federal criminal history record information** (FBI).
- **Ongoing monitoring**: SBI must provide DOL any **subsequent** state criminal history and, if available, criminal history via the **Rap Back System** (§ 8502 of Title 11).
- **FTI rechecks**: Individuals with access to FTI must have a subsequent background check **not less than every 5 years** (or earlier as needed for IRS compliance).
- **Agency discretion**: DOL may adopt screening standards and define disqualifying convictions; DOL reviews checks and determines qualification for employment/access/contact.
- **Conforming amendment to §3307 (Title 19, Ch. 33)** retains FTI-related background check language and reflects the broader approach now housed in §118.

## Who Benefits
- Individuals and employers whose **personally identifiable information** and other confidential DOL-held data are protected by expanded screening and monitoring.
- Beneficiaries of safeguarded funds: **Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund**, **Family and Medical Leave Insurance Account Fund**, and **Workers’ Compensation Second Injury Fund**.
- Vulnerable clients served by **Vocational Rehabilitation Counselors** due to required checks and ongoing monitoring for those roles.
- DOL, by strengthening compliance with **IRS Publication 1075** and **26 U.S.C. § 6103(p)(4)**.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Applicants, employees, contractors, subcontractors, and volunteers in covered roles who must undergo fingerprinting and background checks, including periodic rechecks for FTI access (**at least every 5 years**).
- Current covered employees (as of the effective date) required to submit fingerprints for ongoing monitoring.
