## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 4 of the Delaware Code to authorize and regulate third-party delivery of alcoholic liquors from off-premise licensees, establish related compliance requirements, set a license fee, and sunset the authorization after 3 years unless renewed.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes off-premise alcohol sales for **curbside service** and expands **delivery service** using **licensed third-party delivery vendors**.  
- Allows delivery from a **store** licensed under **§ 516** within a **5-mile radius** of the store.  
- Creates a **third-party delivery license**; applicants must be **registered to do business in Delaware** and **cannot also hold an off-premise license**.  
- Requires delivery personnel to be **at least 21**, **certified responsible alcoholic beverage servers** under **§ 1205**, and trained under a **Commissioner-approved responsible delivery program**.  
- Imposes delivery safeguards: **ID/age and sobriety verification**, **2-year record retention** (name, DOB, delivery address), and **return requirement** if verification fails.  
- Prohibits deliveries to specific destinations (including **out-of-state**, **state-operated facilities**, **correctional facilities**, **hospitals**, **schools**, and certain delivery/locker locations).  
- Sets a **$1,000 biennial license fee** for third-party delivery services.  
- Act is **effective upon Governor’s signature** and **expires 3 years after enactment unless renewed**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded consumer access/convenience**: Enables delivery of alcoholic liquors using licensed third parties (bill synopsis; § 516 amendments).  
- **Structured safety controls**: Requires **21+** delivery personnel, **server certification**, Commissioner-approved training, and **ID/sobriety checks** before handoff (§ 516(c)(1)–(3) as amended).  
- **Clear prohibited delivery locations**: Explicit restrictions reduce deliveries to sensitive locations (e.g., **schools**, **correctional facilities**, **hospitals**, **undergraduate housing**) (§ 516(c)(7)).  
- **Operational clarity for retailers**: Retailer responsibilities are deemed satisfied upon transfer to the licensed third-party vendor, and delivery acts are **not attributable to the off-premise licensee** (§ 516(d); Synopsis).  
- **State oversight mechanism funded by fees**: Establishes a dedicated **third-party delivery license** with a **$1,000 biennial fee** (§ 554(ss)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increased state enforcement costs**: Fiscal note estimates need for **2.0 FTE Alcohol & Tobacco Police Officer III** plus equipment and operating costs to oversee compliance (Fiscal Note Assumption #4).  
- **Administrative burden on vendors**: Requires Commissioner-approved training content, age/ID verification processes (including ID scanning), and **2-year recordkeeping** of recipient data (§ 516(c)(1)–(3)).  
- **Delivery limitations may restrict service**: Delivery is limited to **within a 5-mile radius** for stores under § 516 and barred to many location types (§ 516(a)(2); § 516(c)(7)).  
- **Business model restrictions**: Third-party delivery license cannot be granted to an entity whose business is **primarily the interstate shipment of goods** and cannot be held by an entity that also holds an off-premise license (§ 516(a)(2), lines 24–30).

## Key Policy Changes
- Authorizes a **third-party delivery license** for alcohol delivery and requires delivery providers to be **registered to do business in Delaware** (§ 516(a)(2), lines 21–25).  
- Prohibits issuing a third-party delivery license to an entity that **also holds an off-premise license** (§ 516(a)(2), lines 24–26).  
- Requires a **written agreement** between the off-premise licensee and the licensed third-party delivery vendor for delivery authority (§ 516(a)(2), lines 26–28).  
- Establishes delivery compliance rules, including:  
  - Delivery by personnel **21+**, **responsible server certified** (§ 1205), and trained in Commissioner-approved content (§ 516(c)(1)).  
  - Alcohol placement in vehicle area not readily accessible to driver (e.g., trunk/back seat) when delivered by motor vehicle (§ 516(c)(2)).  
  - Mandatory **recipient ID + sobriety verification**; **2-year record retention** (name, DOB, delivery address); return-to-store requirement if verification fails (§ 516(c)(3), lines 54–62).  
  - Delivery only during lawful sales hours (§ 516(c)(4)).  
  - Online/app orders must be transactions between customer and off-premise licensee; licensee is **merchant of record** (§ 516(c)(5)).  
  - Delivery limited to alcohol obtained directly from the off-premise licensee taking the order (§ 516(c)(6)).  
  - Prohibits delivery to listed facilities/locations and **outside Delaware** (§ 516(c)(7)).  
  - Third-party vendor must supply delivery equipment **at no charge to the store** (§ 516(c)(8)).  
  - Vendor must provide contracts to Commissioner upon request (§ 516(c)(9)).  
- Limits store delivery radius under § 516 to **within 5 miles** (§ 516(a)(2), line 19).  
- Sets third-party delivery license **biennial fee at $1,000** (§ 554(ss)).  
- Specifies allocation of responsibility: off-premise licensee’s delivery responsibilities satisfied upon transfer to licensed vendor; vendor acts not attributable to licensee (§ 516(d), lines 81–86; Synopsis).  
- Adds a **sunset**: Act **expires 3 years after enactment** unless renewed (Section 3).

## Who Benefits
- **Consumers in Delaware** seeking alcohol delivery/curbside options (Synopsis; § 516).  
- **Off-premise licensees** (including stores and other entities with valid off-premise licenses) that can offer curbside and, where authorized, delivery through licensed vendors (§ 516(a)(2); Synopsis).  
- **Third-party delivery vendors** that obtain the new license category and contract with off-premise licensees (§ 516(a)(2); § 554(ss)).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **DATE/state government** due to increased enforcement workload and costs (Fiscal Note #3–#4).  
- **Delivery businesses** that are primarily engaged in **interstate shipment of goods**, which are excluded from being third-party delivery vendors (§ 516(a)(2), lines 29–30).  
- **Off-premise licensees and vendors** facing compliance burdens (training, verification, recordkeeping, contract availability to Commissioner) (§ 516(c)(1)–(3), (9)).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective:** “effective upon signature by the Governor” (Fiscal Note Assumption #1).  
- **Sunset/Expiration:** **Expires 3 years after enactment into law**, unless renewed by subsequent act (Section 3; Fiscal Note Assumption #1).  
- **Record retention deadline:** Third-party delivery vendor must retain recipient records for **2 years** (§ 516(c)(3), lines 58–60).  
- **License fee timing:** **$1,000 biennial** third-party delivery license fee (§ 554(ss)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Compliance requirements (explicit):**
  - Must comply with **Chapter 7**, including **§ 706** (no sales to intoxicated individuals) and **§ 708** (no sales to persons under **21**) (§ 516(c)(a)).  
  - Delivery personnel must be **21+**, **§ 1205 certified**, and trained via a **Commissioner-approved** program covering specified topics including ID scanning (§ 516(c)(1)(A)–(G)).  
  - Motor-vehicle delivery: alcohol must be stored where **not readily accessible to driver** (§ 516(c)(2)).  
  - Must verify recipient is **not intoxicated** and **21+** with photo ID reasonably matching appearance; if not verified, alcohol must be **returned to the premises of the licensee** (§ 516(c)(3), lines 54–62).  
  - Must maintain delivery recipient records (name, DOB, delivery address) for **2 years** (§ 516(c)(3), lines 58–60).  
  - Delivery only during hours the off-premise licensee may lawfully sell (§ 516(c)(4)).  
  - Prohibited destinations include (non-exhaustive): **out-of-state**, **state-operated facility**, **correctional facility**, **hospital**, **locker mailbox**, **post office box**, **package shipping or storage facility**, **retail licensee**, **public/charter/grade/middle/high school**, **undergraduate housing**, and **outside Delaware** (§ 516(c)(7)).  
  - Vendor must provide delivery equipment **at no charge to the store** (§ 516(c)(8)).  
  - Vendor must provide contracts to Commissioner upon request (§ 516(c)(9)).
