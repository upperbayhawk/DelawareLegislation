## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To repeal and recodify Delaware’s sentencing commission provisions in Title 11, modernize the Commission’s mission and structure, place it within the Criminal Justice Council (for administrative and budgetary purposes), and require an annual report on substantive guideline changes.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Repeals **Subchapter X, Chapter 65, Title 11** (the prior establishment of the Delaware Sentencing Accountability Commission).  
- Recodifies the entity as the **Delaware Sentencing Guidelines and Accountability Commission** in **Chapter 89A, Title 11** (replacing a repealed pension-plan chapter heading).  
- Establishes a **mission** focused on **evidence-based sentencing practices**, updating guidelines to **hold individuals accountable** and **minimize repeat offenses**.  
- Sets **9-member commission composition**: **3 judiciary** (appointed by Chief Justice), **3 DOJ Criminal Division** (appointed by Attorney General), **2 Office of Defense Services** (appointed by Chief Defender), and **1 private criminal defense attorney** (appointed by Chief Defender in consultation with Chief of Conflicts Counsel).  
- Makes the sentencing guidelines the **“presumption” in sentencing** and directs the Commission to address **selection and severity of sanctions**.  
- Requires **public comment at meetings** consistent with **29 Del. C. § 10004**.  
- Requires an **annual report** of any **substantive guideline changes**, due **no later than October 1 of each year**, to specified state officials.  
- Fiscal Note anticipates additional staffing and funding within the **Criminal Justice Council**: **FY 2026 recurring $187,291** plus **$5,000 one-time**, with projected **FY 2027 $251,881** and **FY 2028 $256,761**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clearer governance and accountability:** Membership, quorum rules, leadership (Chair/Vice Chair), and duties are explicitly codified (e.g., **9 members**; quorum must include representation from judiciary, DOJ, and defense).  
- **Regular transparency to policymakers:** Requires an annual report describing **substantive changes** to sentencing guidelines (**due by October 1** each year).  
- **Evidence-based focus:** Mission and duties explicitly reference **evidence-based sentencing practices** and reducing **recidivism**.  
- **Administrative support structure:** Placement within the **Criminal Justice Council** for administrative/budgetary purposes and creation of an **Executive Director** role.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Added costs and staffing needs:** Fiscal Note identifies new recurring costs (**$187,291 in FY 2026**) and one-time startup costs (**$5,000 in FY 2026**), plus higher out-year costs (**$251,881 FY 2027; $256,761 FY 2028**).  
- **Guidelines as “presumption” may reduce flexibility:** The bill states sentencing guidelines “**shall be the presumption** in the sentencing of individuals,” which could constrain departures relative to a purely advisory approach (bill does not further define how departures work).  
- **Data collaboration contingent on funding:** Collaboration with the Statistical Analysis Center is only authorized **“if funding is available.”**

## Key Policy Changes
- **Repeal:** Repeals **Subchapter X, Chapter 65, Title 11**.  
- **Recodification:** Establishes/recodifies Commission under **Chapter 89A, Title 11** (renaming chapter to the Commission and repealing prior pension-plan provisions listed in that chapter heading).  
- **Mission statement added/updated:** Evidence-based sentencing practices; accountability; reduce repeat offenses.  
- **Membership fixed at 9**, appointment authorities defined; members serve **at the pleasure** of the appointing authority; members serve **without compensation** but may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses.  
- **Quorum requirement:** Majority of members and must include at least one member from judiciary, DOJ, and defense (ODS or private defense attorney).  
- **Guidelines role:** Sentencing guidelines “**shall be the presumption**” and must address sanctions selection/severity.  
- **Public comment requirement:** Public comment at meetings consistent with **29 Del. C. § 10004**.  
- **Annual report requirement:** Report of substantive changes due **by October 1** annually to specified recipients.  
- **Conditional data collaboration:** Authorized to collaborate with the Statistical Analysis Center **if funding is available**.
