## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14, § 1902 to require that, after a county-wide reassessment of real property under **9 Del. C. § 8306(b)**, a county **modify (by subsequent county ordinance)** the **school property tax exemption amounts** that were established under a **county ordinance adopted on or before January 1, 1998**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates **14 Del. C. § 1902(a)(3)** (school district tax levy authority and exemptions) regarding certain **property tax exemptions**.
- Applies to **real estate exempt from taxation under a county ordinance adopted on or before January 1, 1998** that granted/clarified/expanded an exemption from **county taxation**.
- Provides that **upon a county-wide reassessment** under **9 Del. C. § 8306(b)**, a county **shall** adopt a **subsequent county ordinance** to **modify these exemption amounts**.
- The bill’s stated intent is to **protect seniors and individuals with disabilities** from **significant school tax increases** tied to reassessment-driven property value changes (per Synopsis).
- Shifts action to the **county ordinance process** (rather than school districts) for adjusting exemption amounts after reassessment.
- **No dollar amounts, percentages, or exemption thresholds** are specified in the bill text itself.
- The act was **approved June 13, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **May reduce reassessment-driven school tax increases** for properties receiving the covered exemptions, because counties must update (“modify”) exemption amounts after reassessment. (Text: requirement to modify exemptions upon reassessment; Synopsis: protection from significant school tax increases.)
- Creates a **clear mandatory trigger (“shall”)** for county action after a county-wide reassessment under **§ 8306(b)**, potentially improving consistency in how older exemption amounts are handled.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential reduction in the school district tax base** for properties covered by the exemptions if modified exemption amounts increase or otherwise expand exempt value. (Text: exemptions reduce taxable assessed value subject to district tax levy; modification could maintain/expand exempt amounts.)
- Requires counties to enact a **new ordinance after each county-wide reassessment**, adding procedural steps and potential administrative workload. (Text: “shall, by subsequent county ordinance, modify these exemption amounts.”)
