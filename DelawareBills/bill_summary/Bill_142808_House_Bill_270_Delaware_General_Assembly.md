## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Delaware’s Fiscal Year 2026 Bond and Capital Improvements Act and related authorities (FY ending **June 30, 2026**) to (1) revise bond reversion transfers and project reallocations, (2) expand/clarify certain committee and agency authorities, (3) adjust certain statutory provisions (fees, tax credit cap, school bus lighting rule, port governance), and (4) require a port-area truck-routing study with a reporting deadline.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Increases** the amount transferred to/from the State Treasurer’s **Agency Bond Reversion Account** from **$8,592,759.96** to **$15,592,759.96**, including adding **$7,000,000** for a **Department of Labor Sussex Facility** to the “transfer to” list.  
- Directs transferred bond reversion funds to specified projects, including **$7,000,000** for **Delaware Veterans Cemetery Expansion** and multiple Department of Correction facility power/HVAC/roof projects (including **$2,500,000** and **$5,886,560** line-items).  
- Expands the **Legislative Building Committee** scope to develop **design standards** and **plans** for Legislative Hall needs (public meeting space, parking, offices, Capitol Police space), and clarifies its liaison/recommendation role.  
- Authorizes DNREC park infrastructure funding uses, including up to **$600,000** (additional) for **Brandywine Zoo** accreditation efforts via a DNREC–Delaware Zoological Society agreement, for a **new mixed-species exhibit**.  
- Authorizes **one-time reimbursements** from **DelDOT Community Transportation Funds** for 18 listed local projects with caps “up to” stated amounts (largest: **$276,790** for Peoples Settlement Association playground).  
- Reallocates Community Redevelopment/Reinvestment Fund amounts to several Milford-area and other projects (e.g., **$98,000**, **$160,000**, **$227,846**, **$177,254**).  
- Changes DNREC extremely hazardous substance fee language and sets/retains fee schedules including **$900/year minimum** (standard) and a reduced-fee category capped at **$500/year** for certain propane or ammonium nitrate reporting sources.  
- Requires a **Hay Road truck-routing feasibility/cost/impact study** and a written report by **January 1, 2027**; increases the **Historic Tax Credit** annual cap from **$5,000,000** to **$8,000,000** and revises Diamond State Port Corporation Executive Director selection to require a **majority vote of the Board**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More capital dollars redirected to active needs:** The bill increases bond reversion transfers to **$15,592,759.96** and assigns funds to specific projects (e.g., **$7,000,000** cemetery expansion; DOC emergency generator/HVAC/roof work), potentially improving facility reliability and safety as described by project titles.  
- **Supports accessibility and recreation upgrades:** DNREC funds are specified for **ADA compliance needs at Bellevue State Park ($776,277)** and recreational enhancements like the **Trap Pond State Park splash pad ($1,000,000)**.  
- **Local project support via reimbursements:** DelDOT is authorized to issue capped, **one-time reimbursements** for community projects (e.g., sidewalk/curbing/drainage in Smyrna **up to $30,000**; drainage work; security cameras/equipment).  
- **Increased availability of Historic Tax Credits:** Raises the annual credit limitation from **$5,000,000** to **$8,000,000**, while retaining chronological awarding and carryover priority rules.  
- **Truck traffic mitigation planning:** Requires a study to examine redirecting/limiting truck traffic to reduce routing near residential areas by evaluating an alternative route (Hay Road to 12th Street) and reporting findings.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced funds available for other uses:** Increasing the transfer from the bond reversion account to **$15,592,759.96** commits more reverted bond funds to the listed projects, leaving fewer reverted funds for other potential priorities (the bill specifies the reallocation but does not describe alternatives).  
- **Potentially lower fee collections for certain facilities:** DNREC fee provision includes a reduced maximum fee schedule (**$500** for first unit; **$25** each additional up to **300 units**) for stationary sources reporting **propane or ammonium nitrate** meeting the stated risk management plan threshold, which could reduce receipts compared to the general **$900** minimum fee category.  
- **Governance change may affect executive hiring dynamics at the port:** Executive Director selection now requires a **majority vote of the Board** (in addition to chair appointment subject to Governor approval), which could add procedural steps (the bill does not quantify any delay).  
- **Red Clay school bus amber-light timing exemption:** Exempts certain Red Clay school buses (those equipped with and operating stop arm cameras) from the requirement to activate amber warning lamps approximately **10 seconds** prior to red stop lamps; the bill does not specify safety outcomes.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The bill states the Treasurer transfers funds “**as funds become available**” but does not define timing, prioritization, or schedule.  
- The DNREC/Delaware Zoological Society agreement for up to **$600,000** is required and subject to **29 Del. C. § 6960**, but the bill text does not summarize key agreement terms (deliverables, oversight, repayment, etc.).  
- The term “**sufficient quantity**” in the DNREC fee provision is referenced as part of the risk management plan threshold but is not defined in the bill text.  
- For DelDOT Community Transportation Funds reimbursements, the bill authorizes “**up to**” amounts but does not specify eligibility documentation, reimbursement mechanics, or audit requirements in the bill text.  
- The Hay Road study must evaluate “feasibility, costs, and impacts” but the bill does not specify methodology, scope boundaries, or whether public input is required.

## Key Policy Changes
1. **Bond Reversion Account transfers increased/edited**
   - Adds **DOL – Sussex Facility** (**$7,000,000.00**) to amounts transferred **to** the State Treasurer’s Agency Bond Reversion Account.  
   - Increases total transfer amount from **$8,592,759.96** to **$15,592,759.96** (both “to” and “from” sections).  
   - Allocates “from” transfers to:  
     - OMB Minor Capital Improvement and Equipment **$61,060.60**  
     - Delaware Veterans Cemetery Expansion **$7,000,000.00**  
     - DOC projects including **$2,500,000.00**, **$5,886,560.00**, and **$98,035.61**  
     - DHSS Maintenance and Restoration **$47,103.75**
2. **Legislative Building Committee authority expanded**
   - Authorized to develop **design standards** and **plans** for Legislative Hall needs; clarifies roles and committee actions (decisions vs recommendations).
3. **Residual fund authorizations**
   - OMB/Facilities may use remaining funds from New Troop 6 appropriation also for **Delaware State Police Firing Range Improvements**.  
   - OMB/Facilities may use remaining **Ferris Windows** project funds for the **Cleveland White** project.  
   - OMB/Facilities may use remaining DOC project funds for **JTVCC W1 Building HVAC** project.
4. **DNREC parks/zoo funding and agreement**
   - Specifies park allocations: **$750,000** Brandywine Zoo improvements; **$776,277** Bellevue ADA/improvements; **$1,000,000** Trap Pond splash pad/enhancements.  
   - Allows **up to $600,000** additional for Brandywine Zoo accreditation efforts via agreement with Delaware Zoological Society for a **mixed-species exhibit**.
5. **DelDOT Community Transportation Funds reimbursements (one-time)**
   - Authorizes capped reimbursements (“up to”): **$32,000; $7,000; $50,300; $44,000; $12,000; $25,863; $2,805; $41,350; $10,000; $14,000; $9,930; $5,100; $6,000; $276,790; $10,000; $10,000; $7,000; $30,000** for the listed recipients/projects.
6. **Community Redevelopment/Reinvestment Fund reallocation**
   - Reallocates: **$98,000; $160,000; $40,000; $177,255; $227,846; $177,254** to specified entities/projects, including a reallocation from Downtown Milford, Inc. to the City of Milford Police Department (pole barn enclosure).
7. **DNREC extremely hazardous substance fees (statutory language)**
   - Retains general fee minimum **$900/year** first unit and **$50** per additional unit up to **300 units**.  
   - Provides reduced maximum fee category for certain stationary sources reporting **propane or ammonium nitrate** with potential release ≥ “sufficient quantity”: **$500/year** first unit and **$25** per additional unit up to **300 units**.
8. **Hay Road study requirement**
   - Requires written report by **January 1, 2027** to Controller General, OMB Director, and Co-Chairs of Joint Committee on Capital Improvement.
9. **Historic Tax Credit cap increased**
   - Changes annual limitation from **$5,000,000** to **$8,000,000**; maintains chronological award based on **Stage II approval** date/time and carryover rules.
10. **Red Clay school bus amber lamp timing exemption**
   - Amber activation ~**10 seconds** before red stop lamps requirement does not apply to Red Clay buses with operating stop arm cameras.
11. **Johnson’s School property transfer with reverter/DelDOT clause**
   - Two-step conveyance; automatic reversion to State if no longer needed; land portion must be conveyed to DelDOT at no cost if needed for transportation projects.
12. **Diamond State Port Corporation Executive Director selection**
   - Requires Executive Director be selected by a **majority vote of the Board** (chair appointment remains subject to Governor approval).
