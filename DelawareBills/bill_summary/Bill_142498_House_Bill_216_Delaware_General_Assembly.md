## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 15, Chapter 80 (campaign finance) to expand disclosure and reporting requirements—especially for third-party advertising and certain out-of-state political committees—to increase transparency about the underlying sources of funding in Delaware elections.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates new statutory definitions, including **“affiliated controlling entity,” “conduit,” “donor,” “out-of-state political action committee,”** and **“underlying funding source.”**
- Requires **out-of-state political committees** to file a statement of organization **within 24 hours** after Delaware-related activity exceeds **$2,000** during an election period (with specified exemptions).
- Requires (in the statement of organization) disclosure of **all affiliated controlling entities** of a political committee.
- Expands “paid for by” disclaimer rules for **third-party advertisements** costing **$500 or more** to include a **“learn more” link** to the Commissioner’s website.
- If the third-party advertiser is **not an individual**, the advertisement must disclose specified governance/ownership/donor information, including **50%+ owners** (if any) and the **top 5 transferors in the last 12 months** (if applicable).
- Requires a link to an entity-maintained website listing certain contributors: **all contributions > $100 in the last 12 months**, and for non-individual contributions **> $100**, the **underlying funding source**; the website must use **white background** and **black 12-point font** and include a prescribed header statement.
- Tightens/clarifies third-party advertiser reporting and directs the Commissioner to issue regulations **with illustrative examples**, including how to identify **underlying funding sources** when funds are not from an individual.
- Prohibits reports from showing a **negative ending balance**; a negative balance makes the report **incomplete** and it **may be considered tardy** under existing law.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More transparency for voters**: third-party ads (≥ **$500** FMV) must provide substantially more identifying and funding information when the payer is not an individual, including **responsible party**, **affiliated controlling entities**, **50%+ owners (if any)**, and **top 5 transferors in last 12 months (if applicable)**. (15 Del. C. § 8021(c))
- **Improved traceability of non-individual money**: for contributions **> $100** that are not from an individual, the linked website must disclose the **underlying funding source**. (15 Del. C. § 8021(c)(5))
- **Enhanced oversight of out-of-state activity**: out-of-state political committees crossing **$2,000** in Delaware-related contributions/expenditures (as defined) must register quickly (**within 24 hours**). (15 Del. C. § 8005(a)(2), (d))
- **Reporting completeness incentives**: disallowing negative ending balances is designed to ensure reports reflect the **source of all funds**, including loans, rather than missing amounts. (15 Del. C. § 8031A; Synopsis #4)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher compliance burden for third-party advertisers (non-individuals)**: ads must carry multiple disclosures and a link to an entity website meeting specific formatting requirements (**white background**, **black 12-point font**) and contributor listing rules. (15 Del. C. § 8021(c)(1)–(5))
- **Increased reporting/registration workload for out-of-state committees**: crossing the **$2,000** threshold triggers registration **within 24 hours**, and the bill broadens what counts toward that threshold (including certain contributions/expenditures tied to Delaware elections). (15 Del. C. § 8005(a)(2), (d))
- **Potential criminal exposure**: knowing violations of specified sections (including new **§ 8031A**) are made **Class B misdemeanors**. (15 Del. C. § 8043(a) as amended)

## Key Policy Changes
- **New/expanded definitions** (Title 15, § 8002), including:
  - **Affiliated controlling entity** (criteria include governance instrument binding, board composition tied to controlling entity, or charter/sponsorship). (§ 8002(1))
  - **Conduit** (entity mainly acting as intermediary/pass-through). (§ 8002(9))
  - **Donor** (person contributing to an entity that directly/indirectly contributes to a political committee or third-party ad). (§ 8002(11))
  - **Out-of-state political action committee** (FEC-registered or registered with another state’s reporting agency; accepts contributions from or makes expenditures to a Delaware election). (§ 8002(20))
  - **Underlying funding source** rules differing by entity type (non-PAC/nonprofit; PAC; nonprofit including 501(c)(4)), including **50%+ ownership** and **top 5 transferors in past 12 months**, and special rules when acting as a **conduit for earmarked funds from a single donor**. (§ 8002(33))
- **Political committee definition updated** to include:
  - Threshold **> $500** aggregate contributions/expenditures during an election period (not including independent expenditures), and excludes “out-of-state political committee” in clause (a). (§ 8002(23)a.1)
  - Out-of-state political committee that contributes **more than $2,000** to a Delaware election (with specified exemption). (§ 8002(23)a.3)
- **Political committee registration deadlines**:
  - In-state committees: file statement of organization **within 24 hours** after exceeding **$500** during an election period. (§ 8005(a)(1))
  - Out-of-state political committees: file statement of organization **within 24 hours** after exceeding **$2,000** Delaware-related threshold during an election period, **exempting contributions to candidate committees and political parties**. (§ 8005(a)(2))
- **Statement of organization must include affiliated controlling entities**. (§ 8005(b)(6))
- **Third-party ad disclaimers expanded** for ads with **fair market value ≥ $500** (with exception for printed items **< 9 square inches**):
  - Must include “Paid for by … Learn more … at [Commissioner web address].” (§ 8021(b))
  - If payer is not an individual, must also disclose responsible party, affiliated controlling entities, 50%+ owners (if any), and top 5 transferors (if applicable), plus a link to the entity’s contributor website with specified listing and formatting requirements. (§ 8021(c))
- **Third-party advertiser reporting**:
  - Trigger remains: aggregate third-party ad expenditures **exceed $500** during an election period. (§ 8031(a))
  - Filing deadline: **within 48 hours** if expenditure is made **more than 30 days** before a primary/special election or **more than 60 days** before a general election; **within 24 hours** if made **30 days or less** before a primary/special or **60 days or less** before a general election. (§ 8031(d))
  - Requires Commissioner regulations with illustrative examples and posting online summary. (§ 8031(e))
  - Records retention: **3 years following the election**. (§ 8031(f))
- **Negative ending balance prohibited** in reports under §§ 8030 and 8031; negative balance report is **incomplete** and **may be considered tardy** under § 8044. (§ 8031A)
- **Penalty change/clarification**: knowing violations of **§ 8004, § 8005, or § 8031A** are **Class B misdemeanors** (as stated in amended § 8043(a)). (§ 8043(a))

## Who Benefits
- **Voters and the public**, through increased disclosure about who funds third-party election advertising and the underlying sources of non-individual contributions. (§ 8021; § 8002(33); Synopsis)
- **Election administrators and enforcement bodies**, via clearer registration triggers for out-of-state committees and standardized disclosure elements. (§ 8005; § 8031(e))

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Non-individual third-party advertisers** (corporations, nonprofits, PACs, etc.) facing expanded ad-disclaimer and website-disclosure obligations, plus reporting and recordkeeping requirements. (§ 8021(c); § 8031; § 8031(f))
- **Out-of-state political committees** engaging in Delaware elections that cross the **$2,000** threshold (as defined) and must register quickly (**24 hours**). (§ 8005(a)(2), (d))
- **Political committees** required to identify **affiliated controlling entities** and maintain compliance with additional disclosure rules. (§ 8005(b)(6))

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date**: The Act takes effect **6 months after enactment**. (Section 8)
- **Registration deadline (political committees)**:
  - In-state: **within 24 hours** after exceeding **$500** aggregate contributions/expenditures during an election period. (§ 8005(a)(1))
  - Out-of-state: **within 24 hours** after exceeding **$2,000** Delaware-related threshold during an election period (with exemptions). (§ 8005(a)(2))
- **Third-party advertisement report filing deadline**:
  - **48 hours** after expenditure if made **more than 30 days** before a primary/special or **more than 60 days** before a general election.
  - **24 hours** after expenditure if made **30 days or less** before a primary/special or **60 days or less** before a general election. (§ 8031(d))
- **Political committee officer-change reporting**: report changes **within 7 days** after change becomes effective. (§ 8005(c)(1))
- **Record retention**:
  - Political committees: **3 full years following the election**. (§ 8005(c)(2))
  - Third-party advertisers filing under § 8031: **3 years following the election**. (§ 8031(f))

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Class B misdemeanor**: any person who **knowingly violates** **§ 8004, § 8005, or § 8031A** is guilty of a **Class B misdemeanor** (except as otherwise set forth in § 8044). (§ 8043(a) as amended)
- **Reports must not show a negative ending balance**; a negative balance renders the report **incomplete** and it **may be considered tardy** under § 8044. (§ 8031A)
- **Statements/reports under penalty of perjury**:
  - Statement of organization must be filed **under penalty of perjury**. (§ 8005(b))
  - Third-party advertisement report must be filed **under penalty of perjury**. (§ 8031(a))
- **Ad disclaimer and website compliance** for third-party ads ≥ **$500** (with exception for printed items **< 9 square inches**), including mandatory text and disclosure elements and website formatting requirements. (§ 8021(b)–(c))
