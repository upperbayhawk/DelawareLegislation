## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Require counties and school districts to recalculate and reissue **2025-2026** property and local school tax bills using **2024-2025 assessed values** (except for certain new construction), extend the payment deadline to **October 30, 2025**, delay interest/penalties until after that date, and require refunds/credits for overpayments.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires each school district to issue a **new tax collection warrant** to the county for **tax year 2025-2026** using the **“previously assessed value”** (the assessed value used for **2024-2025**).  
- School districts must deliver the new warrant **no later than 10 business days after the effective date of the Act**.  
- Upon receiving the warrant, the county must **void all 2025-2026 tax bills already issued** and **reissue** bills totaling: (1) county property tax calculated on **previously assessed value**, plus (2) local school tax per the new warrant.  
- The void-and-reissue requirement **does not apply to tax bills for “new construction”** (defined as property where a building’s completion during **2024-2025** required a supplemental assessment and roll update).  
- Extends the deadline for payment of reissued bills (including any higher balance due after adjustment) to **October 30, 2025**.  
- **No interest or penalties** for unpaid taxes due under this section may be charged **until after October 30, 2025**.  
- Counties must **refund or credit** any **overpayment** of **2025-2026** taxes to taxpayers who paid amounts due under a bill later adjusted under this section.  
- School districts may **request and borrow from State Division I funds** if there is a **cash flow shortfall** of local school funds due to the payment-deadline extension for the **2026 fiscal year**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Stabilizes 2025-2026 tax calculations** by requiring use of **2024-2025 assessed values** for county and local school taxes (explicitly required by Section 1(2) and 1(3)).  
- **Provides taxpayers more time to pay** by extending the payment deadline to **October 30, 2025** (Section 1(4)a.).  
- **Avoids near-term penalties/interest** by prohibiting interest and penalties until **after October 30, 2025** (Section 1(4)b.).  
- **Protects taxpayers from overpayment** by requiring counties to **refund or credit** overpayments when bills are later adjusted (Section 1(5)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative burden for counties**: must **void** already-issued **2025-2026** bills and **reissue** new bills (Section 1(3)a.).  
- **Administrative burden for school districts**: must issue and deliver a **new tax collection warrant** within **10 business days after the Act’s effective date** (Section 1(2)).  
- **Potential cash flow shortfalls for school districts** due to the extended payment deadline, acknowledged by allowing borrowing from **State Division I funds** (Section 1(6)).  
- **Different treatment of certain properties**: the reissued-bill process **does not apply to “new construction”** tax bills (Section 1(3)b.), meaning not all taxpayers are treated the same under the recalculation/reissue requirement.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The bill extends interest/penalties timing “for unpaid taxes due under this section,” but does not specify how this interacts with any other delinquency provisions outside this section.  
- The bill states the reissue requirement does not apply to “tax bills for new construction,” while “new construction” is defined as completion during the **2024-2025** tax year requiring supplemental assessment; the synopsis also references “new construction completed during the 2025-2026 tax year,” creating potential inconsistency.  
- The bill allows school districts to “request and borrow from State Division I funds” but does not specify **limits, interest, repayment terms, approval criteria, or process**.

## Key Policy Changes
- Defines:

- Requires counties, upon receipt, to **void and reissue 2025-2026 tax bills** using previously assessed values for county tax, plus the new local school tax amount (Section 1(3)a.).

- Extends payment deadline for reissued bills to **October 30, 2025**, and bars interest/penalties until after that date (Section 1(4)).  
- Requires counties to **refund or credit** overpayments for **2025-2026** taxes when a taxpayer paid an amount later adjusted (Section 1(5)).
