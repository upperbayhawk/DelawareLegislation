## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 24, Chapter 17 of the Delaware Code to update the statutory scope of practice for licensed respiratory care practitioners (respiratory therapists), including explicitly authorizing “all forms of extracorporeal life support” (e.g., ECMO and ECCO2R) and recognizing telemedicine/telehealth participation, while making technical drafting corrections.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands the listed duties within “respiratory care” to include **all forms of extracorporeal life support**, specifically **extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO)** and **extracorporeal carbon dioxide removal (ECCO2R)**.  
- The bill’s synopsis states current law allows respiratory therapists to **assist** with extracorporeal life support but not **administer medication during the process**, which can delay care; the bill is intended to remove that delay.  
- Adds/clarifies that respiratory care includes **telemedicine** (as defined in the chapter) and, as further described in regulation, participation in **telehealth**.  
- Confirms respiratory care practitioners work under **general supervision** of a person certified to practice medicine (including via protocols/orders).  
- Maintains that respiratory care practitioners may evaluate patients and make decisions **within parameters** defined by a physician and the **Board of Medical Licensure and Discipline**.  
- Makes **technical corrections** to align with the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual (per synopsis).  
- Bill was **released 04/08/2025 at 10:16 AM** (as shown on the bill text).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduced delays in patient care**: The synopsis states that under current law therapists may have to wait for another licensed professional to administer medication during extracorporeal life support; the bill is intended to eliminate that delay.  
- **Clearer scope for advanced respiratory support**: By explicitly listing **ECMO** and **ECCO2R** and “all forms of extracorporeal life support,” the bill clarifies that these activities are within respiratory care duties when performed under required supervision/parameters.  
- **Supports modern care delivery**: Explicitly includes **telemedicine** and participation in **telehealth** as part of respiratory care practice (subject to regulation).
