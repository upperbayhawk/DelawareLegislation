## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends 18 Del. C. § 708(c) to allow additional distributions from an existing special fund used to provide post-retirement increases/distributions for eligible retired county and municipal police officers and firefighters (and certain survivors and disability recipients), including by expanding eligibility and enabling payouts upon enactment rather than waiting for a pre-set date.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Raises the pension-income cap for receiving a distribution from the special fund from **$35,000** to **$55,000** in annual pension.  
- Updates the definition of “eligible retiree” to include individuals who retired before **June 30, 2025** (previously **June 30, 2018**), plus **surviving beneficiaries** and **disability pension** recipients.  
- Changes the schedule trigger for biennial distributions to start from **[enactment date]** (instead of **September 1, 2019**) and continue every two years thereafter.  
- Maintains the requirement that **at least $500,000** remain in the special fund after distributions.  
- Maintains the cap that **Category 3** payments may not exceed **$3,000 per individual**, with **Category 1 = 3× Category 3** and **Category 2 = 2× Category 3**.  
- Requires eligible retirees to be **alive on July 1** of each biennial payout year to receive payment.  
- Keeps governance/controls: the **State Board of Pension Trustees** manages the fund and **no disbursements** occur without its prior approval; counties/municipalities must **disperse payments within 30 days** of receiving funds.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More retirees become eligible** due to the increased pension cap (**$35,000 → $55,000**) and later retirement cutoff date (**June 30, 2018 → June 30, 2025**).  
- **Earlier access to distributions** because biennial availability is tied to **[enactment date]** rather than waiting for a date specified in prior law (**September 1, 2019**).  
- **Predictable distribution structure** is retained (category-based multipliers; **$3,000** cap for Category 3; minimum **$500,000** remaining in the fund).  
- **Administrative oversight remains** through required approval by the State Board of Pension Trustees before funds are disbursed.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Fewer funds may be available per person** (or fewer years of payments) because eligibility expands (higher pension cap and more retirement cohorts), while the bill retains the requirement that **$500,000 must remain** in the fund after distributions.  
- **Excludes higher-pension retirees**: individuals with annual pensions **over $55,000** remain ineligible for distributions from this special fund.  
- **Potential reversion to General Fund remains**: any funds not used for a post-retirement increase within **10 years from deposit** revert to the **General Fund**, which may limit long-term availability for intended beneficiaries.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Eligibility expansion (retirement date cutoff):** “Eligible retiree” changes from retired before **June 30, 2018** to retired before **June 30, 2025** (plus surviving beneficiary or disability pension recipient).  
- **Eligibility expansion (pension cap):** ineligible threshold changes from annual pension **more than $35,000** to **more than $55,000**.  
- **Distribution timing trigger:** biennial distribution schedule changes to start from **[enactment date]** rather than **September 1, 2019**, and continues every two years.

## Who Benefits
- Retired county and municipal police officers and firefighters who:
  - retired before **June 30, 2025**, and
  - have annual pension **$55,000 or less**, and
  - are alive on **July 1** of a biennial payout year.  
- **Surviving beneficiaries** and individuals receiving **disability pensions** meeting applicable eligibility rules.  
- Counties/municipalities with eligible retirees that receive and pass through distributions under § 708(c)(4).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Retirees with annual pensions **over $55,000** (remain ineligible for distributions).  
- Counties/municipalities may face administrative burden to categorize retirees, transmit information by **July 1**, and disburse within **30 days**.  
- The special fund’s available balance for future distributions may be strained by an increased eligible population (eligibility expanded by date and income cap).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Approved July 1, 2025** (act approval date).  
- Biennial distributions: on each biennial anniversary starting from **[enactment date]** and each biennial anniversary thereafter.  
- Eligibility “alive” requirement: retiree must be alive on **July 1** of each biennial payout year.  
- Board determination deadline: State Board of Pension Trustees determines total amount available for distribution by **July 1** of the distribution year, based on category information received **before July 1**.  
- County/municipality payment deadline: must disperse payments to eligible retirees within **30 days** of receiving funds.  
- Reversion deadline: funds not utilized for a post-retirement increase revert to the **General Fund** if not used within **10 years from the date of deposit** into the special fund.

- **Distribution constraints:** must keep **≥ $500,000** in the fund after distributions; **Category 3 ≤ $3,000** per person; Category multipliers (**3×** and **2×**); and no payments to individuals with annual pensions **> $55,000**.
