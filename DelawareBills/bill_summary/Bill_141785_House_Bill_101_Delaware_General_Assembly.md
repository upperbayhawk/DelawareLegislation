## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Make a **one-time supplemental appropriation** of **$17,561,900 in General Funds** for the **fiscal year ending June 30, 2026** to the **Office of Management and Budget (OMB), Contingencies and One-Time Items (10-02-11)** and specified related purposes.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Appropriates **$17,561,900 (General Funds)** for **one-time** projects and contingencies for **FY 2026 (ends June 30, 2026)**.
- Funds include **$2,000,000** for **ERP Cloud Migration** and **$432,200** for **Technology**.
- Provides **$3,500,000** for **health programs funded by the Tobacco Settlement Fund** to supplement **FY 2026 reductions** to state-operated health programs.
- Adds **$1,700,000** for a **Trauma Informed Services Contingency**.
- Appropriates **$3,000,000** for **first-year lease and moving expenses** for the **Department of Justice**, **contingent** on separate fee-legislation changes to **6 Del. C. § 73** and **Title 6 of the Administrative Code**.
- Includes **$3,000,000** for an **Insurance Revenue Shortfall** item.
- Includes multiple smaller allocations for **equipment** and **permit-to-purchase** items across agencies, some tied to implementing prior legislation (e.g., **SS 1 for SB 2** (152nd GA) and **HB 86** (152nd GA)).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Supports continuity of certain state health programs in FY 2026** by providing **$3,500,000** to supplement **FY 2026 reductions** to state-operated health programs (Section 4).
- **Modernizes state systems** by funding **ERP Cloud Migration ($2,000,000)** and additional **Technology ($432,200)**.
- **Enables implementation of previously enacted reforms** by funding permit-to-purchase/equipment tied to full implementation of **Senate Substitute 1 for Senate Bill 2 (152nd GA)** and **House Bill 86 (152nd GA)** (Sections 2, 3, 6, 7).
- **Addresses facility needs for the Department of Justice** via **$3,000,000** for first-year lease and moving expenses (Section 5).
- **Covers specific shortfalls/contingencies**, including **Insurance Revenue Shortfall ($3,000,000)** and **Hospital Provider Contingency ($1,500,000)**.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- The health-program funding is explicitly **one-time** and intended to allow agencies to find **alternative state, federal, or private funding sources** later; this indicates potential **future funding cliff** if replacement funding is not secured (Section 4).
- The **$3,000,000 Department of Justice lease/moving appropriation cannot be spent unless separate fee legislation passes** (Section 5), which may delay or prevent use of funds.
- Multiple line items are labeled as **contingencies** (e.g., health care services, trauma-informed services, hospital provider), which may reduce specificity on exact programmatic allocation within those categories (as described in Section 1 line items).
