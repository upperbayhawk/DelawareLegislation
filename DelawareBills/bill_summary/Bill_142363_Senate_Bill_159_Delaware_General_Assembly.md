## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To require counties to approve (i.e., not deny) certain conditional use permits for electrical substations and directly related project infrastructure on unincorporated land in heavy industrial zones when the substation supports large renewable energy generation projects, and to retroactively validate certain prior applications. The Act also includes a sunset date.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits any county from denying a conditional use permit for an electrical substation (and directly related project infrastructure) on unincorporated land if specified conditions are met.  
- Applies only where the substation supports a proposed renewable energy generation project **of 250 MW or greater**.  
- Requires the substation to be located in a **heavy industrial zone**, and the local code must already treat an electrical substation as an **allowed conditional use** in that zone.  
- Limits eligibility to zoning districts that already had an electric substation **rated 230 kV or greater as of August 3, 2023**.  
- Makes the law **retroactive**: qualifying county applications filed **on or after August 3, 2023** are **deemed approved**, even if previously denied.  
- Prohibits counties from changing zoning classifications or zoning codes to make a previously filed qualifying application no longer qualify.  
- The Act **sunsets after December 31, 2026**, but permits granted/deemed approved before then remain effective, and qualifying pending applications continue to be governed by the Act.  
- Approved **June 30, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Greater permitting certainty for qualifying renewable-energy-related substations:** Counties “shall [not] deny” qualifying conditional use permits, reducing local denial risk for projects meeting the statute’s criteria.  
- **Retroactive relief for previously denied projects:** Any qualifying application filed **on or after August 3, 2023** is “**deemed to be approved**” despite prior adverse county action.  
- **Prevents post-filing zoning maneuvers that could block projects:** Counties are prohibited from altering zoning classifications or zoning codes to render a previously filed application unqualifying.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced county discretion over land-use decisions:** The bill removes a county’s ability to deny qualifying conditional use permits for these substations in covered circumstances.  
- **Retroactive override of county actions:** Prior denials or adverse actions on qualifying applications filed **on or after August 3, 2023** are negated by deeming the applications approved.  
- **Limits local zoning changes affecting pending/previously filed applications:** Counties are barred from changing zoning classifications/codes in ways that would disqualify a previously filed application.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Mandatory approval standard:** Adds/establishes rule that **no county shall deny** a conditional use permit for a qualifying electrical substation and directly related infrastructure on unincorporated land when all conditions below are met:
  1) Supports a proposed renewable energy generation project **≥ 250 MW**.  
  2) Located in a **heavy industrial zone**.  
  3) Electrical substations are an **allowed conditional use** in that zone.  
  4) The zoning district already had an electric substation **≥ 230 kV as of August 3, 2023**.  
- **Retroactive approval:** Any qualifying application filed **on or after August 3, 2023** and before enactment is **deemed approved**, regardless of prior county adverse action.  
- **Anti-circumvention provision:** Counties are prohibited from changing zoning classification or zoning code (or otherwise) to make a previously filed application unqualifying.  
- **Sunset:** Act expires **after December 31, 2026**, with continuation rules for permits already granted/deemed approved and for qualifying pending applications.

## Who Benefits
- Developers/sponsors of **renewable energy generation projects ≥ 250 MW** needing substations in qualifying heavy industrial zones on unincorporated county land.  
- Applicants with qualifying substation conditional use applications filed **on or after August 3, 2023**, including those previously denied.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Counties** (local governments) whose discretion to deny certain conditional use permits is restricted and whose prior denials may be overridden.  
- Stakeholders relying on county zoning changes to affect pending/previously filed qualifying applications (the bill prohibits such changes for that purpose).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
