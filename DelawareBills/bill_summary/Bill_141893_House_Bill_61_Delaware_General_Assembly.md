## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 16 of the Delaware Code to prohibit Delaware-organized organizations from selling, distributing, or dispensing drugs when their leadership knows or should know the drugs will be used in any capacity in a government-sanctioned execution, and to impose criminal, civil, and (for corporations) charter-revocation consequences.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits an organization organized under Delaware law from selling, distributing, or dispensing a drug if the organization’s “officer” **knows or should know** it will be used **in any capacity** in a government-sanctioned execution.  
- Requires anyone who sells/distributes/dispenses a drug containing **any quantity of pentobarbital** to obtain a **signed purchaser attestation** that it will not be used (or re-sold for use) in a government-sanctioned execution.  
- Makes the organization’s highest-ranking leader (defined as an “officer”) **civilly and criminally liable** if they **knew or should have known** an agent/employee was violating the prohibition.  
- Sets a **criminal penalty: Class A felony** for a person who violates the section.  
- Sets a **civil penalty: up to $50,000** per violation.  
- If the violator is a **Delaware-organized corporation**, its **corporate charter will be revoked** upon a finding of criminal or civil liability.  
- Applies to “organizations” (entities) organized under Delaware law; “organization” excludes individuals.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Creates strong deterrence** against Delaware-organized businesses supplying execution-related drugs by pairing a **Class A felony** with a **civil penalty up to $50,000** and potential **corporate charter revocation** for corporations.  
- **Adds a compliance checkpoint for pentobarbital** by requiring a **signed attestation** from purchasers for drugs containing **any quantity** of pentobarbital.  
- **Extends accountability to leadership** by making an “officer” liable when they **knew or should have known** of employee/agent violations.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **High liability exposure for leadership** due to a **“knows or should know”** standard and officer liability for employee/agent conduct, combined with **Class A felony** consequences.  
- **Potential business risk for Delaware corporations** because a corporation’s **charter will be revoked** upon a finding of criminal or civil liability for a violation.  
- **Administrative burden** on sellers of drugs containing **any quantity of pentobarbital** to obtain and manage purchaser attestations.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
