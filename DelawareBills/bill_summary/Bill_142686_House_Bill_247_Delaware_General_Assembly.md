## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Provide short-term relief to New Castle County homeowners facing substantially increased **2025–2026** residential property and school tax bills by requiring the County to offer payment plans and, for compliant taxpayers, temporarily limiting enforcement actions and prohibiting added fees, penalties, and interest.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **New Castle County** tax collecting authority to **offer all taxpayers** a **payment plan** for **county property taxes and school taxes** assessed to **residential properties** for the **2025–2026 tax year**.  
- Each payment plan must consider the **difference in taxes owed between 2024–2025 and 2025–2026** tax years.  
- After offering a payment plan, the County must give taxpayers **at least 30 days** from the offer date to enter the plan.  
- If a taxpayer enters and stays compliant with the plan, the County **may not collect the taxes out of the taxpayer’s real or personal property** through specified legal/collection actions.  
- If compliant, the County **may not charge any fees, penalties, or interest** on the taxes due.  
- Applies only to **New Castle County residential property and school taxes** assessed for **2025–2026**.  
- The Act **expires December 31, 2026**, but the collection/fee prohibitions continue for **payment plans that extend beyond** that date as long as the taxpayer remains compliant.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Immediate payment flexibility:** Requires payment plans be offered to **all taxpayers** with residential property and school taxes in New Castle County for **2025–2026**.  
- **Protection from major enforcement actions while compliant:** Bars actions such as **civil suits, wage garnishment, monitions, sheriff’s sales, and tax-lien collection actions** while the taxpayer pays under the plan.  
- **Cost containment for compliant taxpayers:** Prohibits **fees, penalties, and interest** on taxes due for those who comply with the payment plan.  
- **Time to decide:** Guarantees at least **30 days** after the offer to enter the payment plan.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced enforcement leverage for the County:** While a taxpayer is compliant, the County cannot use many standard collection tools (e.g., **monition**, **writ of venditioni exponas**, **sheriff’s sale**, **tax lien collection action**), potentially slowing collections.  
- **Potential delayed revenue timing:** Payment plans and prohibition on penalties/interest may reduce or delay near-term cash flow from **2025–2026** taxes (no alternative revenue provisions stated).  
- **Limited scope:** Relief applies only to **New Castle County** and only to **residential** property and school taxes for **2025–2026**, not other counties, other property types, or other tax years.
