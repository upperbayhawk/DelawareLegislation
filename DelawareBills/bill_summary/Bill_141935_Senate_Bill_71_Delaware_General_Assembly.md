## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 18 (insurance) to (1) create an annual, birthday-based open enrollment period for people already enrolled in Medicare supplement (“Medigap”) policies/certificates to switch to another Medigap policy with the same or lesser benefits without medical underwriting, and (2) allow people enrolled in Medicare Advantage (Part C) to move to a Medigap policy during Medicare open enrollment periods with specified insurer rules.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates an **annual Medigap open enrollment window** for current Medigap enrollees that starts **30 days before** the person’s birthday and remains open **at least 30 days after** the birthday. (§ 3411(a))
- During that birthday open enrollment, Medigap issuers **cannot deny coverage**, **cannot condition issuance/effectiveness**, **cannot price-discriminate** based on health factors, and **cannot impose preexisting-condition exclusions**. (§ 3411(b))
- Limits the birthday switching right to moving to a Medigap policy/certificate with the **“same or lesser benefits.”** (§ 3411(a))
- Requires Medigap issuers to send notice to affected insureds **no less than 30 days** and **no more than 60 days** before the birthday open enrollment begins, including dates/rights and **benefit modifications or premium adjustments**. (§ 3411(e))
- Allows an individual enrolled in a **Medicare Advantage plan (Part C)** to cancel it and enroll in a Medigap policy/certificate during **Medicare open enrollment periods**. (§ 3411(c))
- For Medicare Advantage → Medigap applicants, issuers **may not deny or condition effectiveness**, but may **individually rate** and may apply a **preexisting condition limitation** consistent with **§ 3403**. (§ 3411(d))
- Adds a statutory definition of **“Medicare Advantage plan”** tied to **42 U.S.C. § 1395w-28(b)(1)** and enumerates included plan types. (§ 3401)
- **Effective date:** **January 1 following enactment**; the act was **approved September 3, 2025**, so it takes effect **January 1, 2026**. (Section 3; approval date)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved switching flexibility for current Medigap enrollees:** Annual opportunity around birthdays to change Medigap coverage without denial, health-based pricing discrimination, or preexisting-condition exclusions. (§ 3411(a)-(b))
- **Consumer notice requirement:** Mandatory advance notice **30–60 days** before the open enrollment begins, including changes to benefits or premium adjustments, may improve transparency and planning. (§ 3411(e))
- **Pathway from Medicare Advantage to Medigap:** Explicitly permits Medicare Advantage enrollees to move into Medigap during Medicare open enrollment periods, with protections against outright denial. (§ 3411(c)-(d))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Restricted upgrade options for current Medigap enrollees:** The birthday open enrollment only allows switching to a policy with the **“same or lesser benefits,”** not higher benefits. (§ 3411(a))
- **Weaker protections for Medicare Advantage → Medigap switchers:** Issuers may **individually rate** applicants and may apply a **preexisting condition limitation** (per § 3403), which could increase premiums or limit coverage for some individuals. (§ 3411(d))
- **New administrative burden on issuers:** Issuers must provide individualized notices within a specific **30–60 day** timing window each year for eligible insureds. (§ 3411(e))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- What constitutes **“same or lesser benefits”** for Medigap policy comparisons is not further defined in the bill text. (§ 3411(a))
- The bill references **“Medicare open enrollment periods”** but does not specify which federal open enrollment periods apply (e.g., annual election period vs. other periods). (§ 3411(c))
- The scope and details of the permitted **preexisting condition limitation** are referenced by cross-citation to **§ 3403**, but § 3403’s content is not included in the provided text. (§ 3411(d))

## Health Impact
- Affects access to and terms of **Medicare supplement (Medigap) coverage** by prohibiting denial and preexisting-condition exclusions for certain switchers (current Medigap enrollees using the birthday window). (§ 3411(b))
- For Medicare Advantage enrollees switching to Medigap, maintains access by prohibiting denial/conditioning effectiveness, but allows **individual rating** and **preexisting condition limitations** consistent with § 3403. (§ 3411(d))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Adds definition:** “Medicare Advantage plan” defined by reference to **42 U.S.C. § 1395w-28(b)(1)** and includes coordinated care plans (including HMOs/PPOs/provider-sponsored orgs), MSA plans, and private fee-for-service plans. (§ 3401)
- **Creates annual birthday open enrollment for current Medigap enrollees:** Begins **30 days before** birthday and stays open **at least 30 days after**. (§ 3411(a))
- **Guarantees issue/price protections during birthday open enrollment:** No denial, no conditioning issuance/effectiveness, no health-based pricing discrimination, and no preexisting-condition exclusions. (§ 3411(b))
- **Permits Medicare Advantage → Medigap switching during Medicare open enrollment:** Explicit authorization to cancel Medicare Advantage and enroll in Medigap. (§ 3411(c))
- **Sets rules for Medicare Advantage → Medigap applications:** No denial/conditioning effectiveness; individual rating and § 3403 preexisting-condition limits permitted. (§ 3411(d))
- **Imposes notice requirement:** **30–60 days** before birthday open enrollment begins, issuers must notify eligible insureds about dates/rights and benefit/premium changes. (§ 3411(e))
- **Clarifies relationship to other enrollment periods:** The open enrollment periods created are **in addition to and independent of** other federal/state open enrollment periods. (§ 3411(f))

## Who Benefits
- **Current Medigap enrollees in Delaware** who want to switch to another Medigap policy/certificate with the same or lesser benefits, especially those with health conditions, because denial, health-based pricing discrimination, and preexisting-condition exclusions are prohibited during the birthday window. (§ 3411(a)-(b))
- **Medicare Advantage enrollees** seeking a route into Medigap during Medicare open enrollment periods, because issuers may not deny or condition effectiveness. (§ 3411(c)-(d))

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Medigap issuers** due to required annual notifications (timed **30–60 days** before open enrollment begins) and limits on underwriting/pricing during the birthday open enrollment period. (§ 3411(b), (e))
- **Medicare Advantage enrollees switching to Medigap** who may face **individual rating** and **preexisting condition limitations** (per § 3403). (§ 3411(d))
