## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s summary possession (eviction) statute (25 Del. C. § 5705) to increase the maximum time window for serving the notice of hearing and complaint before the hearing.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes 25 Del. C. § 5705(a) governing **service timing** in summary possession actions.
- Keeps the **minimum** service requirement the same: **at least 5 days** before the hearing.
- Increases the **maximum** allowable service window from **not more than 30 days** to **not more than 90 days** before the hearing.
- Applies to the **notice of hearing and the complaint** in a summary possession action.
- The bill text does **not** specify an effective date.
- No appropriations, fees, or funding mechanisms are included in the bill text.
- No new penalties or enforcement mechanisms are added; it adjusts an existing procedural timeframe.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Greater scheduling flexibility for courts and parties:** Expanding the maximum service window to **90 days** may allow earlier service and more flexibility in calendaring hearings (based on the change from 30 to 90 days in § 5705(a)).
- **More time between service and hearing (when served early):** Parties may have more lead time if service occurs well in advance, since service can occur up to **90 days** prior to the hearing (per amended § 5705(a)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Longer potential period of uncertainty before a hearing:** Because service may occur up to **90 days** before the hearing (instead of 30), defendants/tenants could receive notice substantially earlier and remain in a prolonged pre-hearing period (grounded in the extended maximum timeframe).
- **Potential for older service relative to hearing date:** Allowing service up to **90 days** before the hearing could mean notices/complaints are served much earlier relative to the hearing date than under prior law (30-day cap).
