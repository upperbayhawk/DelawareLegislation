## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s “Delaware Volunteerism Act” (Title 14, Chapter 89A) to (1) expressly allow high school students to earn up to **1 elective credit** for volunteering with **local fire departments**, (2) require certain schools to notify students about the elective-credit option **at or before course selection**, and (3) authorize the Department of Education to issue implementing regulations.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Allows volunteer service performed **with a local fire department** to qualify toward the **Delaware Volunteer credit** (up to **1 elective credit**).
- Requires **high schools that offer the volunteer credit** to inform students of the option to earn **up to 1 elective credit** through volunteer work **at or before** the time students select classes.
- Keeps the existing hour requirement: **45 hours per semester for 2 semesters** (total **90 hours**) for students in **grades 9–12** to receive **1 Delaware Volunteer credit**.
- Maintains key guardrails: service **cannot be performed during required school attendance hours** and **cannot be political or advocacy** in nature.
- Continues transcript-related requirements: the credit must appear prominently on the transcript, and DOE must include a **statement of explanation** with official transcript responses.
- Continues verification requirements: volunteer time must be verified by the organization’s supervisor and certified by school personnel using DOE/Office of Volunteerism forms.
- Authorizes the Department of Education to **promulgate regulations** needed to implement the chapter.
- The enrolled act was **Approved September 9, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded qualifying placements:** Students may use **local fire department** volunteering to meet requirements for the **Delaware Volunteer credit** (explicitly added in § 8902A(c)(2)b).
- **Clearer student notice:** Schools that offer the credit must notify students **at or before course selection**, improving awareness and access to the elective-credit option (§ 8902A(b)).
- **Potential workforce/community engagement alignment:** The statute continues to emphasize promoting community service and its benefits for employment and postsecondary applications (§ 8902A(b)); the bill adds fire departments as a named option.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative workload may increase:** Schools that offer the credit must provide timely notice and continue to certify hours; DOE and the Office of Volunteerism continue to maintain information and forms, and DOE may issue regulations (§ 8902A(a), (b), (c)(3); § 8903A).
- **Time burden on students remains substantial:** The requirement remains **45 hours/semester for 2 semesters** (**90 total hours**) to earn **1 credit** (§ 8902A(c)(1)).
- **Uneven availability based on school practice:** The notice requirement applies to **“High schools that offer the volunteer credit”** (§ 8902A(b)), implying some schools may not offer it (the bill does not state that all schools must offer it).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Which “Department” has regulatory authority?** § 8903A says “The Department may promulgate regulations…”—the synopsis indicates this is the **Department of Education**, but the section text uses “Department” without restating “of Education.”
- **Scope of “volunteer” roles at fire departments:** The bill does not define what activities at a local fire department qualify, beyond being “voluntary community service.”
- **Whether all high schools must offer the credit:** The bill requires notice only for “high schools that offer the volunteer credit,” but does not specify whether offering the credit is mandatory statewide.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Explicit eligibility expansion:** Volunteer community service may be performed “**…with a local fire department**” in addition to organizations/projects submitted to a guidance counselor by the Office of Volunteerism or DOE (§ 8902A(c)(2)b).
- **Mandatory notice timing (for schools that offer the credit):** Such schools must inform students of the option to earn **up to 1 elective credit** through volunteer work **at or before** course selection; notice must include that students may be able to volunteer for a local fire department (§ 8902A(b)).
- **Regulatory authority added/clarified:** DOE is authorized to promulgate regulations necessary to implement Chapter 89A (§ 8903A).
