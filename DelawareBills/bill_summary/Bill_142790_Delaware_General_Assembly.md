## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Designate the month of **February 2026** as **“Career and Technical Education Month”** in the State of Delaware and affirm the importance of Career and Technical Education (CTE).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Designates **February 2026** as **“Career and Technical Education Month”** in Delaware (House Concurrent Resolution No. 89).
- Formally **affirms the importance** of CTE and encourages public awareness and participation in CTE programs.
- Cites that CTE programs are offered by **31 local education agencies** in Delaware, including **3 technical school districts**, **16 comprehensive/traditional districts**, **9 charter school districts**, and **2 state institutions serving at-risk youth**.
- Notes that in **academic year 2024–2025**, Delaware high schools offered **120+ CTE programs of study** across **16 Career Clusters**, and middle schools offered **16 CTE programs of study**.
- States that in **2024–2025**, **over 60%** of Delaware middle and high school students participated in **at least one** CTE program.
- Reports a **duplicative total course enrollment of over 90,000** in CTE courses in **2024–2025**.
- Identifies **837 Division 1 CTE teaching units** generated in **2024–2025**, with **30%** in vocational technical districts and **70%** in K–12 districts.

- **Raises awareness and encourages participation** in CTE programs by urging citizens to become familiar with and support CTE services and benefits (synopsis).
- Highlights CTE’s role in **career readiness** by emphasizing academic, technical, and employability skills (WHEREAS clauses).
