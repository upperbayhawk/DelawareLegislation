## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To revise operating requirements for the Delaware Anti-Trafficking Action Council in Title 11, including quorum, voting, membership size, meeting format rules, and financial reporting requirements.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Reduces the Council’s quorum requirement from **13 members to 10 members** for meetings and action-taking.  
- Clarifies that, **except for Executive Director appointment**, the Council may act by **majority vote of members present** at a meeting.  
- Maintains a higher threshold for appointing an Executive Director: approval by a **quorum majority of the full membership** (with vacancies not counted toward “full membership” for this purpose).  
- Expands annual financial reporting to cover spending of **any state, federal, or other funds received**, rather than only “state funds” as previously framed.  
- **Repeals** the requirement to provide a funding-spend report **at each full council meeting**.  
- Allows virtual meetings compliant with **29 Del. C. § 10006A**, and permits a **staff member** (not necessarily a Council member) to be present at the public “anchor location.”  
- Increases Council membership from **24 to 25 members**, including adding a **retired judicial officer with knowledge of human trafficking** (appointed by the Speaker of the House).  
- Act was **approved May 7, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Easier to conduct business**: Lower quorum (from **13 to 10**) may reduce cancelled meetings and enable more consistent decision-making.  
- **More transparent annual reporting scope**: Annual report must explain spending of **state, federal, or other funds received**, expanding coverage beyond the prior “state funds” framing.  
- **Improved meeting accessibility and compliance flexibility**: Virtual meetings are expressly allowed with an anchor location staffed by a **staff member**, supporting compliance with open meeting rules without requiring a Council member to be physically present.  
- **Adds expertise**: Expands membership to **25** by adding a **retired judicial officer** with trafficking knowledge.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Less frequent financial updates to the public**: Repeals the requirement to report at **each full council meeting** how state funding was spent since the prior meeting; spending explanations are instead incorporated into the **annual report**.  
- **Quorum below a majority of membership**: With **25 members** and a **10-member quorum**, Council action (generally by majority of those present) can occur with fewer than a majority of total seats participating, as recognized in the bill synopsis.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Council membership**: increases from **24 to 25 members**; adds a **retired judicial officer with knowledge of human trafficking** appointed by the **Speaker of the House**.  
- **Quorum**: reduced from **13 to 10 members**.  
- **Voting standard (general)**: Council may generally take action with an affirmative vote of a **majority of Council members present** at a meeting (except as otherwise provided).  
- **Executive Director appointment threshold**: Executive Director appointed by the Chair with approval of a **quorum majority of the full membership**; serves at the pleasure of a **quorum majority of the full membership**; **vacancies are not counted** for “full membership” for this purpose.  
- **Staffing/contracts**: Executive Director may employ staff and contract for services **within limits of appropriations or available funding from another source**, subject to approval of a **quorum**.  
- **Financial reporting**: annual report must explain spending of **state, federal, or other funds received**; prior definition noted that “state funds” meant state money allocated other than staff-position funding.  
- **Repeal**: removes requirement to provide spending report at **each full council meeting**.  
- **Meetings (virtual/anchor location)**: permits virtual meetings compliant with **29 Del. C. § 10006A**, and allows a **staff member** to be present at the anchor location instead of (or in addition to) a Council member.
