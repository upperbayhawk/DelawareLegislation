## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Authorize New Castle County’s Office of Finance to conduct post–general reassessment “quality control” reviews of certain tax parcels’ new assessed values—especially potentially underassessed non-residential parcels—allowing revisions before the next tax year, and adjust related 2026 timing for tax billing and school district tax collection processes.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a New Castle County–specific “quality control review” process for a parcel’s *new* assessed value following a general reassessment.  
- Allows review when specific triggers apply, including certain large non-residential parcels (≥ **$300,000** assessed value) with unusual decreases/low increases, or sale-price discrepancies (≥ **25%** below sale price within **5 years**).  
- Requires the review to follow existing assessment standards in **9 Del. C. § 8306(a)** and makes any corrections subject to **notice and appeal** requirements under **§§ 8312 and 8321**.  
- Sets a hard deadline: any revisions/corrections under this authority must be made no later than **October 16, 2026**.  
- Changes the due date for affected New Castle County taxes to **November 9, 2026**, and requires mailing of tax statements **not more than 30 days after** taxes are due and payable (subject to § 8602(b)).  
- Adjusts school district warrant delivery deadlines in New Castle County for the **2026–2027** school tax year to **October 30, 2026** (instead of the second Thursday in July).  
- Permits districts to request, and the State to advance, **State Division I funds** if there is a local funds shortfall due to the delayed warrant timeline.  
- Act sunsets/terminates on **March 31, 2027**, unless extended by later legislation.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves assessment accuracy after reassessment:** Explicitly authorizes New Castle County to review and correct new assessed values when defined indicators suggest error or potential underassessment (e.g., assessed value ≥ $300,000 but decreased; assessed value ≥ $300,000 with increase ≤ 50% of median non-residential increase; assessed value ≥ 25% below recent sale price).  
- **Promotes tax fairness across taxpayers:** By addressing potential underassessment of some parcels, the bill aims to reduce unfair shifting of the tax burden caused by inaccurate assessments (as described in the bill’s findings).  
- **Maintains due process:** Corrections are explicitly subject to existing notice and appeal processes (§§ 8312 and 8321).  
- **Supports continuity of school funding:** Provides a mechanism for state advances of Division I funds if delayed warrant delivery causes a shortfall for the 2026–2027 school tax year.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increased exposure to higher assessments for some non-residential owners:** The review triggers explicitly focus on non-residential parcels meeting thresholds (e.g., ≥ $300,000; sale-price comparisons), which could lead to upward corrections and higher tax liabilities for affected parcels.  
- **Compressed timeline for changes:** Corrections must be completed by **October 16, 2026**, which may concentrate administrative activity and could create time pressure for notices/appeals.  
- **Delayed tax and school collection timing in 2026:** Taxes affected by this section are due **November 9, 2026**, and New Castle County school boards deliver warrants by **October 30, 2026**, which may shift cash-flow timing for governments and taxpayers.

## Key Policy Changes
- **New quality control review authority (New Castle County only):** Adds **9 Del. C. § 8345** allowing the Office of Finance to review a parcel’s new assessed value after a general reassessment when any of these apply:  
  1) **Clerical, mathematical, or factual mistake** in the reassessment process.  
  2) **Non-residential** parcel with new assessed value **≥ $300,000** that **decreased** from prior assessed value.  
  3) **Non-residential** parcel with new assessed value **≥ $300,000** whose percent change is **no greater than 50%** of the county median increase for non-residential properties.  
  4) **Non-residential** parcel with new assessed value **≥ 25% less** than the parcel’s most recent sale price within the prior **5 years**, when sale price is determinable from public records.
- **Standards and appeals:** Requires adherence to **§ 8306(a)** standards; corrections subject to **§§ 8312 and 8321** notice/appeal requirements.  
- **Deadline for corrections:** Must be completed by **October 16, 2026**.  
- **2026 tax due date adjustment:** Taxes assessed and levied “subject to this section” are due **November 9, 2026**; statements mailed **not more than 30 days after** taxes are due and payable (subject to § 8602(b)).  
- **Subpoenas:** Any subpoena of records issued by the Office of Finance under this context is subject to **§ 8304A**.  
- **No private right of action:** The section does **not** create a private cause of action to force the Office of Finance to conduct a review of a specific parcel.  
- **School warrant timing change (New Castle County):** Amends **14 Del. C. § 1916** to set a one-time deadline of **October 30, 2026** for New Castle County districts (instead of the second Thursday in July), with potential Division I advances if shortfalls occur.

## Who Benefits
- **Taxpayers generally in New Castle County** (including residential taxpayers), to the extent corrections address underassessment and reduce unfair tax burden shifts.  
- **New Castle County government** (Office of Finance) through explicit authority to conduct post-reassessment quality control reviews and corrections.  
- **New Castle County school districts**, which gain a statutory warrant deadline aligned with the 2026 correction timeline and access to potential State Division I fund advances in case of shortfalls.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Owners of certain non-residential parcels in New Castle County** meeting the triggers (e.g., ≥ **$300,000** assessed value; assessed ≥ **25%** below recent sale price) whose assessed values may be revised upward, increasing taxes.  
- **Taxpayers and entities sensitive to shifted billing timelines**, because taxes subject to the section become due **November 9, 2026** and statements may arrive up to 30 days afterward.

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **October 16, 2026:** Deadline for New Castle County Office of Finance to make revisions/corrections under **9 Del. C. § 8345**.  
- **November 9, 2026:** Due date for “all taxes assessed and levied subject to this section” (notwithstanding § 8601(1)).  
- **Within 30 days after taxes are due and payable:** Office of Finance must mail tax statements to taxables (subject to § 8602(b)).  
- **October 30, 2026:** Deadline for New Castle County school boards to deliver their warrant and duplicate assessment list for the **2026–2027** school tax year.  
- **March 31, 2027:** Act expires on this date unless extended by subsequent act.
