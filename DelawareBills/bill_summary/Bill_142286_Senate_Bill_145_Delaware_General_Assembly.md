## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Delaware’s Workers’ Compensation Act (Title 19) to (1) formally allow total/partial disability benefit payments by direct deposit (with claimant opt-in) and (2) increase the reimbursable share of certain Division of Industrial Affairs functions from **66.6% to 100%**, shifting full costs of inspection and safety functions to the workers’ compensation assessment structure. Also makes technical drafting corrections.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes workers’ compensation **total or partial disability** payments by **check or (with mutual consent) direct deposit**.  
- Requires claimants to **opt in** to direct deposit by signing and dating an **electronic or printed authorization form**.  
- Requires anti-fraud eligibility language to appear **on the back of checks** or **on the direct deposit authorization form** for all total/partial disability payments.  
- Clarifies that if an attorney negotiates a disability check for a client, that negotiation represents the attorney printed the required language on checks the attorney distributes to clients.  
- Makes the direct-deposit/fraud-warning requirements applicable to payments made by the **Workers’ Compensation Fund** under **§ 2347**.  
- Increases reimbursement/assessment coverage for Division of Industrial Affairs functions: **inspection** and **safety** functions move from **66.6% to 100%** reimbursement.  
- Redirects certain self-insurer tax receipts to the **Department of Labor, Division of Industrial Affairs** (instead of the Secretary of Finance), while stating the sums remain part of the **General Fund**.  
- Bill is **approved June 30, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Modernizes payment methods:** Expressly permits **direct deposit** for total/partial disability payments, aligning statute with “current practices and technology” (Synopsis; § 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Claimant choice preserved:** Direct deposit requires **mutual consent** and **claimant opt-in authorization** (electronically or printed) (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Anti-fraud notice standardized across payment types:** Requires a uniform warning statement on checks or direct-deposit authorization forms for **all** total/partial disability payments (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Full funding for inspection and safety functions:** Raises reimbursement from **66.6% to 100%**, stated to ensure inspection/safety functions are “fully covered” (§ 2392(c); Synopsis).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Added administrative steps for direct deposit:** Requires claimants to sign/date an authorization form and requires inclusion of mandated language on the authorization form (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Higher assessment burden on insurance carriers (cost shift):** The bill increases reimbursable expenses for inspection and safety functions from **66.6% to 100%**, and the Synopsis states “Insurance carriers pay the assessment imposed by this section” (§ 2392(c); Synopsis).  
- **Potential compliance burden on attorneys distributing checks:** Attorney negotiation of a check is treated as a representation regarding printing the required anti-fraud language on attorney-distributed checks (§ 2344(b)(2)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The bill requires direct deposit “**with mutual consent**” but does not define how consent is documented beyond claimant authorization (e.g., whether carrier signature is required in all cases) (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- The bill references an “**electronic authorization form**” and “electronically signing,” but does not specify technical standards for e-signatures or record retention (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- Not specified in source text whether the shift to **100%** reimbursement changes assessment rates, only that costs are intended to be fully covered (§ 2392(c); Synopsis).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Direct deposit authorization (new/clarified):** Total/partial disability payments may be made by **check** or **with mutual consent by direct deposit**; claimants may **opt in** via signed/dated electronic or printed authorization (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Mandatory anti-fraud language placement:** For **all** total/partial disability payments, carriers/self-insureds must print the specified warning language:
  - on the reverse side of the check above endorsement; **or**
  - on the direct deposit authorization form (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Applicability expanded to Workers’ Compensation Fund payments:** The subsection’s provisions also apply to payments made by the **Workers’ Compensation Fund** pursuant to **§ 2347** (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Reimbursement percentage increase:** § 2392(c) increases reimbursement for inspection and safety functions from **66.6% to 100%**.  
- **Administrative payment routing change:** § 2391(b) changes where self-insurer tax funds are paid (to Department of Labor, Division of Industrial Affairs).

## Who Benefits
- **Injured workers/claimants** who prefer electronic payments, through availability of **direct deposit** (opt-in) (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Office of Workers’ Compensation / Division of Industrial Affairs** functions (inspection and safety) through **full (100%) reimbursement** of those functions’ expenses (§ 2392(c); Synopsis).  
- **State administration** by clarifying procedures and updating statute to current practices (Synopsis).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Insurance carriers** (and potentially employers through premiums), because inspection and safety expenses reimbursed through assessments increase from **66.6% to 100%** (§ 2392(c); Synopsis notes carriers pay the assessment).  
- **Self-insured employers** may face administrative compliance tasks related to updated payment methods and continued reporting/tax obligations (e.g., payroll reporting and tax payment as condition of continuing self-insurer status) (§ 2391(b); § 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Attorneys who distribute negotiated disability checks** may face added compliance expectations tied to the mandated printed warning language (§ 2344(b)(2)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Mandatory notice language requirement:** Carriers/self-insureds must include the specified anti-fraud statement on checks or direct deposit authorization forms for all total/partial disability payments (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Direct deposit authorization requirements:** Claimants must **sign and date** electronic or printed authorization forms to opt in; direct deposit requires **mutual consent** (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Attorney representation rule:** Negotiation of a disability check by an attorney (or agent) is a representation that the attorney printed the required language on checks the attorney distributes to clients (§ 2344(b)(2)).  
- **Penalties (existing section, technically corrected/clarified):**  
  - Insurers/self-insurers violating or refusing to comply with the chapter, or willfully making false/fraudulent statements/returns: **fine $100 to $1,000 per offense**; assessed by the **Industrial Accident Board** after notice and hearing (§ 2386(a), (c)).  
  - Other violations listed in § 2386(b): **fine $100 to $1,000** or **imprisonment up to 90 days**, or both (§ 2386(b)).  
  - Section does not limit penalties under **§ 2374** (§ 2386(d)).
