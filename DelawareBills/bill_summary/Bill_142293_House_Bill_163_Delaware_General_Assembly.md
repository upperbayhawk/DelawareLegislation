## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 29 to establish and run the Delaware Diabetic Wellness Pilot Program within the Department of Human Resources to study whether proactive “well care” for prediabetic and diabetic patients (using physician-led care teams, laboratory testing, and technology such as continuous glucose monitoring) can improve health outcomes and reduce long-term medical costs.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Establishes the **Delaware Diabetic Wellness Pilot Program** administered by the **Secretary of the Department of Human Resources** in partnership with a **Delaware healthcare system**, **physicians**, and a **technology company** selected by the Secretary.  
- Targets a **voluntary** group of **State of Delaware Group Health Insurance Plan (GHIP)** covered patients who are **prediabetic or diabetic**, with **at least 400 and no more than 500 participants**.  
- Requires an **RFP** process from GHIP providers and coordination with the **Delaware Health Information Network (DHIN)** to recruit participants and support data collection/reporting.  
- Uses **baseline physician-led laboratory testing**, **real-time glucose monitoring/technology**, and **regular comparisons** to evaluate participant progress (baseline data compared **every 3 months**; lab testing initially **every 6 months**).  
- Requires individualized care plans assessing specified metabolic/cardiovascular metrics (e.g., **Hemoglobin A1C, lipid panel, blood pressure, waist circumference**, etc.) and tracking healthcare utilization (e.g., ED/urgent care visits, hospitalizations).  
- DHIN must issue **summary results every 6 months** after implementation until a final report is submitted.  
- Implementation occurs **90 days after** (1) the technology company is selected and (2) **400–500 volunteers** are recruited; the Act is **effective immediately**.  
- Program **sunsets 3 years after the implementation date** unless extended; **final analytic report due within 6 months after** the pilot ends.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Structured measurement and monitoring:** Requires baseline labs and ongoing comparisons (**every 3 months**; labs initially **every 6 months**) to evaluate changes in metabolic markers and health utilization.  
- **Personalized, physician-led plans:** Mandates individualized healthcare management plans with specified metrics (e.g., **A1C, lipids, HS-CRP, Vitamin D, magnesium**), which may improve care consistency across participants.  
- **Regular public-sector oversight:** DHIN reporting **every 6 months** to the Secretary, State Benefits leadership, Governor, and General Assembly increases visibility into results.  
- **Potential cost-management focus:** Bill intent explicitly targets controlling and lowering long-term medical costs related to prediabetes/diabetes by shifting from “sick care” to “well care.”  
- **Food access/navigation support:** Incorporates Delaware Department of Agriculture’s **Delaware Grown** initiative to help participants source **fresh whole foods** when seasonally available.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Limited scope/population:** Participation is limited to **400–500 volunteers**, initially within **GHIP** (state employee plan) populations; this may limit generalizability and reach.  
- **Implementation contingent on recruitment threshold:** The pilot cannot implement until **at least 400** volunteers are recruited, potentially delaying start.  
- **Added provider/team requirements:** Requires physician-led teams, trained dieticians/care coordinators, and lifestyle/functional **CME participation** for team members—additional administrative burden is implied by these requirements.  
- **Data-sharing complexity:** Requires coordination with DHIN and technology-based monitoring while maintaining HIPAA compliance, increasing operational complexity.

- Requires monitoring of key health indicators including: **waist circumference, blood pressure, Hemoglobin A1C, lipid panel (Total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, triglycerides/HDL ratio), high-sensitivity C-reactive protein, Vitamin D, magnesium**, and tracking healthcare utilization (ED/urgent care visits, hospitalizations).

## Key Policy Changes

- Assigns administration to the **Secretary of the Department of Human Resources** working with a **Delaware healthcare system**, physicians, and a **technology company** selected by the Secretary.  
- Requires a participant cohort size of **at least 400 but no more than 500** **volunteer** GHIP-covered prediabetic/diabetic patients; allows opening to other health plans if GHIP recruitment does not reach **400**.
