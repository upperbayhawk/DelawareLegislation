## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29 to require criminal background checks for Delaware Department of Finance employees (and certain others) and to provide for ongoing/continuous criminal history monitoring through subsequent state updates and, if available, the FBI “Rap Back System.”

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **current Department of Finance employees** to submit to a **criminal background check**.  
- Requires individuals **conditionally offered employment** with the Department of Finance to submit to a **criminal background check**.  
- Authorizes the Secretary of Finance to require **current or prospective contractors/third parties** who **require or may require access** to Department **systems, facilities, or data** to submit to a background check.  
- Requires fingerprinting and submission of necessary information to the **State Bureau of Identification (SBI)** to obtain **entire state and federal criminal history record information** (or statements of no record).  
- Requires SBI to **forward** state and federal criminal history information to the Department.  
- Establishes **continuous criminal history monitoring** for current employees/contractors/third parties by requiring SBI to provide **subsequent state criminal history** and, if available, **Rap Back** updates.  
- Gives the Department the right to **review and use** obtained criminal background information (as permitted by law) for **employment qualification** and **continued access** decisions.  
- Sets a compliance deadline: current employees and contractors must submit required information to SBI **no later than 30 days after** receiving the Department’s **written request**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved security for sensitive systems/data:** The bill explicitly covers individuals with access (or potential access) to Department **systems, facilities, or data** and allows screening/monitoring for those roles.  
- **Ongoing monitoring, not just a one-time check:** Requires SBI to provide **subsequent state criminal history** and, if available, **Rap Back** updates for continuous monitoring.  
- **Clear compliance timeframe for current staff/contractors:** Establishes a concrete deadline—**30 days after receipt of a written request**—for submitting fingerprints/information.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Added administrative burden for affected individuals and agencies:** Requires fingerprint submission and processing through **SBI** and collection/forwarding of state and federal history information.  
- **Potential privacy and employment-access implications:** The Department may use background information (as permitted by law) to determine **qualification for employment** or **continued access**, which may affect current employees/contractors if new information arises.  
- **Discretionary application to contractors/third parties:** Contractors/third parties “**may be required**” to submit to checks depending on the Secretary’s determination, which could create inconsistent application across vendors.
