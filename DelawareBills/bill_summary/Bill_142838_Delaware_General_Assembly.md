## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Designate **February 2026** as **“Turner Syndrome Awareness Month”** in the State of Delaware and affirm the General Assembly’s view that awareness supports effective **screening and treatment** for Turner Syndrome.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Designates **February 2026** as **“Turner Syndrome Awareness Month”** in Delaware.  
- States Turner Syndrome is a **random chromosomal disorder** that **exclusively affects females**, involving a **missing or incomplete X chromosome**.  
- Cites the Turner Syndrome Foundation: Turner Syndrome affects **1 in every 2,000 females** and is the **second-most common genetic disorder**.  
- Notes babies are **not routinely screened at birth**, and **more than 50%** of babies with Turner Syndrome are **diagnosed later in life**.  
- Lists potential lifelong complications, including **cardiovascular disease**, **learning disabilities**, and increased risk for **ovarian failure**, **Type II diabetes**, and **hypothyroidism**.  
- Asserts complications **may be mitigated** with **early diagnosis and treatment**.  
- Resolution is a **designation/awareness measure** and does not set out a new screening mandate in the text.
