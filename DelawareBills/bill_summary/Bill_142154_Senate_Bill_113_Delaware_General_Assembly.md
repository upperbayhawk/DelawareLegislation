## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To codify in Title 29 the formal existence of (1) the **Division of Legislative Services** and (2) the **Office of the Controller General**, which are referenced elsewhere in Chapter 11 but were not explicitly created in the Delaware Code.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Creates in statute** a **Division of Legislative Services** within Subchapter I, Chapter 11, Title 29 (new § 1106A).
- States the Division exists to **assist the Director of the Division of Legislative Services** in carrying out functions assigned under **§ 1107** and other state law.
- Amends **§ 1110** to state that **an Office of the Controller General is created**.
- States the Office exists to **assist the Controller General** in carrying out functions assigned under **§ 1110** and other state law.
- Does **not** change who appoints the Controller General: the **Legislative Council** continues to appoint the Controller General (§ 1110(a)(1)).
- Reaffirms the Controller General’s primary role is to assist the **Joint Finance Committee** and **Joint Committee on Capital Improvement** (§ 1110(a)(1)).
- The act is primarily a **codification/organizational clarification** rather than a program expansion described in detail.
- **Approved July 30, 2025** (enacted as Chapter 120, Volume 85).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clarifies legal status**: Explicitly creates the Division of Legislative Services and the Office of the Controller General in the Code, aligning statute with existing referenced structure (Synopsis; § 1106A; § 1110(a)(3)).
- **Improves statutory consistency**: Chapter 11 already describes the Director organizing/supervising the Division and the Controller General having staff support, and this act adds the missing “created” language (Synopsis).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **No operational detail provided**: The bill creates the entities but does not specify staffing levels, governance details, reporting requirements, or duties beyond general assistance language (§ 1106A; § 1110(a)(3)).
- **No fiscal detail provided**: The act does not state whether codification affects appropriations, staffing, or costs.

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds **§ 1106A** to Title 29: “There is created a **Division of Legislative Services** to assist the Director… in performing functions assigned under **§ 1107** and other State law.”
- Amends **§ 1110(a)(3)** to state: “There is created an **Office of the Controller General** to assist the Controller General in performing functions assigned under this section and other State law.”
- Leaves intact that the **Legislative Council appoints** the Controller General and that the Controller General primarily assists the **Joint Finance Committee** and **Joint Committee on Capital Improvement** (§ 1110(a)(1)).

## Who Benefits
- **Legislative Council, Director of the Division of Legislative Services, and Controller General**: benefit from clearer statutory grounding for the offices they lead/oversee (§ 1106A; § 1110(a)(3); Synopsis).
- **General Assembly finance and capital budgeting committees** (Joint Finance Committee; Joint Committee on Capital Improvement): indirectly benefit from clarified institutional support structure (§ 1110(a)(1)).
