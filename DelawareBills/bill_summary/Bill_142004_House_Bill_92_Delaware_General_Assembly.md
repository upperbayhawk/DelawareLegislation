## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Repeal Delaware’s “Advanced Clean Car Program” changes to 7 DE Admin. Code Reg. 1140 (effective **December 11, 2023**) and reinstate the prior version of Reg. 1140 (the “Delaware Low Emission Vehicle Program”).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Repeals amendments to **Regulation 1140** adopted by DNREC in **27 DE Reg 433** and effective **December 11, 2023**, identified in the bill as the **Delaware Advanced Clean Car Program**.  
- Revives the **pre–December 11, 2023** version of **Regulation 1140**, identified as the **Delaware Low Emission Vehicle Program**.  
- Directs the **Registrar of Regulations** to publish the revived pre-12/11/2023 version of Regulation 1140 as a **final regulation** in the **next issue** of the Delaware Register of Regulations published after enactment.  
- Requires the Registrar to publish an accompanying **order** stating the Act is the basis for that publication.  
- Overrides normal Delaware administrative procedures (“**Notwithstanding Subchapter II of Chapter 101 of Title 29** or any other law … to the contrary”) for purposes of this publication.  
- Establishes a delayed effective date for the substantive repeal/revival: **Section 1** takes effect **10 days after** the required publication occurs.  
- The bill synopsis states the change “thereby terminat[es] the **Electric Vehicle Mandate**.”
