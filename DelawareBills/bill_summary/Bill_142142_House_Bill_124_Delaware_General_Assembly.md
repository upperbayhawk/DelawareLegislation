## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend 16 Del. C. § 6612 to raise the **maximum** fees the State Fire Marshal may charge (with State Fire Prevention Commission approval) for (1) licenses to service portable unit fire suppression appliances and (2) all other permits, licenses, and certifications required under State Fire Prevention Regulations.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases the **maximum fee** for a license to service **portable unit fire suppression appliances** from **$50 to $100**.  
- Increases the **maximum fee** for **all other permits, licenses, and certifications** required by State Fire Prevention Regulations from **$25 to $100**.  
- Fee schedules continue to require **approval of the State Fire Prevention Commission**.  
- Fiscal note projects the proposed fee adjustments would generate a **minimum of $13,300** in revenue.  
- Fiscal note lists many specific fees that the State Fire Marshal anticipates increasing to **$100** upon enactment (e.g., covered malls, places of assembly, public accommodations, fireworks-related permits).  
- Intended use of additional revenue is to support operations of the **State Fire Marshal** and the **State Fire Prevention Commission**.  
- Bill requires a **three-fifths vote** in each chamber under **Delaware Constitution Article VIII, § 10** (license fee increase).  
- Act was **approved September 9, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Increased operational funding**: Additional revenue is intended “to support the operations for the State Fire Marshal and the State Fire Prevention Commission.”
- **Updates outdated caps**: Synopsis states the fee cap changes reflect “increasing cost of providing these services since the maximum fees were last set several decades ago.”
- **Maintains oversight**: Fees remain subject to **State Fire Prevention Commission approval**.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher costs for regulated entities**: Raises maximums from **$25 → $100** (a potential **$75** increase per fee) and **$50 → $100** (a potential **$50** increase), and the fiscal note indicates many specific licenses/permits are anticipated to be set at **$100**.
- **Higher single-event permitting costs**: Fiscal note shows single-event permits (e.g., burning, exhibits/trade shows, fireworks, impairment of fire protection, special events) anticipated to increase to **$100**.
- **Higher explosives/blasting-related licensing costs**: Fiscal note indicates items like **Explosives** and **Blaster’s License** anticipated to move to **$100** (from **$10** in listed examples).

## Key Policy Changes
- Amends **16 Del. C. § 6612(5)b.** to increase the maximum fee for a license to service **portable unit fire suppression appliances** from **$50** to **$100**.
