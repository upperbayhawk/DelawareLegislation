## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the Town of Selbyville charter to change how Town Council elections are structured by creating **4 election districts**, electing **one councilmember per district**, setting **districting and redistricting deadlines**, and adding/clarifying **candidate residency qualifications**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires Selbyville to be divided into **4 election districts**; **initial districts must be established no later than January 1, 2026**.  
- Requires districts to be **contiguous** and, where possible and practicable, **nearly equal in population** using the **most recent federal decennial census** data.  
- Requires redistricting after each federal decennial census: boundaries must be adjusted **no later than January 1 of the second year following the census**.  
- Changes Town Council composition to **4 members total**, with **1 member from each district**, nominated and elected **only by registered voters of that district**.  
- Keeps the Mayor elected by **registered voters of the Town at large** (townwide).  
- Adds/clarifies candidate qualifications: Mayor and council candidates must be **at least 21 years old** and a **U.S. citizen**, **Delaware citizen**, and **bona fide resident of Selbyville for at least 2 years**; council candidates must also reside in the **district** they seek to represent.  
- Provides transition timing: new district boundaries **supersede prior boundaries upon enactment for the next Annual Municipal Election (including nominations)**, and for other purposes when the newly elected council takes office at the **Annual Meeting**.  
- Updates staggered election language so council seats from **districts 1 & 3** are elected in **odd-numbered years**, and **districts 2 & 4** in **even-numbered years**, with **2-year terms**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More geographically based representation:** Each of the **4 districts** elects its own councilmember (text: “**1 member from each of the 4 districts**” elected by that district’s voters).  
- **Population balancing requirement:** Districts must be “**nearly equal in population**” where practicable, based on decennial census data, which can support more even representation.  
- **Regular redistricting schedule:** Requires updates by **January 1 of the second year after each federal decennial census**, creating a defined timeline.  
- **Clearer eligibility rules:** Explicit **2-year Selbyville residency** and **district residency** requirements for council candidates.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Limits who can vote for each council seat:** Voters may no longer vote townwide for all councilmembers; councilmembers are elected only by **registered voters of the district**.  
- **May restrict candidate pool:** Requires candidates to have lived in Selbyville for **at least 2 years**, and council candidates must also live in the relevant district.  
- **Administrative complexity:** Creating and periodically adjusting **4 district boundaries** (including by a fixed deadline) adds procedural work for the Town Council.

## Key Policy Changes
- Establishes **4 election districts** for Selbyville and requires:
  - **Contiguous territory** for each district.
  - Districts to be **nearly equal in population** where possible/practicable (based on latest federal decennial census).
  - **Initial districts by January 1, 2026**.
  - Post-census adjustment by **January 1 of the second year following the federal decennial census**.
- Changes Town Council structure/elections:
  - Town Council is **4 members**, **1 from each district**.
  - Each councilmember nominated and elected by **registered voters in the member’s district**.
- Candidate qualifications:
  - Must be **21**.
  - Must be a **citizen of the United States** and **of the State of Delaware**.
  - Must be a **bona fide resident of Selbyville for at least 2 years**.
  - Town Council candidates must reside in the **district** they seek to represent.
- Staggered elections and terms:
  - **Districts 1 & 3** seats elected in **odd-numbered years**.
  - **Districts 2 & 4** seats elected in **even-numbered years**.
  - Mayor and council terms stated as **2 years**, effective with the **Annual Meeting** under Section 7.1.

## Who Benefits
- **Registered voters within each district**, who gain the ability to elect a councilmember specifically representing their district.  
- **Residents seeking more population-balanced representation**, due to the “nearly equal in population” districting requirement.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Voters who prefer at-large council elections**, because council elections become district-based (only district voters elect that district’s seat).  
- **Potential candidates** who have lived in Selbyville for **less than 2 years**, and/or (for council) who do not live in the district they wish to represent.
