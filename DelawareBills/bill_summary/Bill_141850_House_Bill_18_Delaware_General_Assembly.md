## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Increase specified insurance-professional licensing fees and redirect a portion of those fees to support Delaware Department of Insurance (DOI) operations by expanding funding deposited into the Insurance Commissioner Regulatory Revolving Fund and raising the fund’s allowable year-end balance cap.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases multiple DOI licensing fees in **18 Del. C. § 701**—generally by **$25** per affected license type (initial and/or renewal), including insurance producers, adjusters, appraisers, business entities (agencies), and others.
- Sets **license amendment** fees under Chapter 17 to increase from **$25 to $50**.
- Requires **15% of licensing fees** collected under **§ 701(23)–(31), (35)–(37), (41), and (42)** to be deposited into the **Insurance Commissioner Regulatory Revolving Fund** to support DOI operations.
- Raises the Revolving Fund’s maximum **unencumbered** year-end balance to **$5,000,000** (from **$1,400,000** set effective **June 30, 2005**).
- Requires any **unencumbered balance over $5,000,000** at the end of each fiscal year to be **transferred to the General Fund**.
- Centralizes certain licensing fee provisions by tying **rental company limited lines** and **portable electronics vendor** fees to the amounts in **§ 701** (instead of separate provisions in §§ 2006 and 2057).
- Fiscal estimates in the fee impact statement project additional revenues in **FY 2026–FY 2028**, with amounts split between **Appropriated Special Fund (ASF)** and **General Fund (GF)**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More dedicated operational funding for DOI:** Depositing **15%** of specified licensing fees into the Regulatory Revolving Fund is expressly to support operations of the Insurance Commissioner’s Office (18 Del. C. § 305(c)(1)d).
- **Greater budget stability/working capital for DOI:** Increasing the allowable unencumbered balance to **$5,000,000** may allow the agency to carry higher reserves for operations (18 Del. C. § 305(e)).
- **Clear rule for excess funds:** Requires year-end transfers of any unencumbered balance **above $5,000,000** to the **General Fund** (18 Del. C. § 305(e)).
- **Fee schedule consolidation:** Rental company limited lines and portable electronics vendor fees are standardized by referencing **§ 701**, potentially simplifying administration (Sections 3–4).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher costs for licensees:** Multiple initial and renewal fees rise by **$25**, including (examples) insurance producers **$100→$125**, adjusters **$100→$125**, surplus lines broker initial **$250→$275**, and bail agent **$200→$225** (18 Del. C. § 701).
- **Higher transaction cost for changes to licenses:** Chapter 17 license amendment fee increases **$25→$50** (18 Del. C. § 701(24)).
- **Higher fees for certain business models:** Travel insurance producers and portable electronics vendors increase to **$1,025** initial and **$525** renewal (18 Del. C. § 701(37) and (42)); discount medical plan organizations increase to **$525** initial and **$275** renewal (18 Del. C. § 701(36)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates a new earmark:** **15%** of licensing fees collected under **§ 701(23)–(31), (35)–(37), (41), (42)** must be deposited in the **Insurance Commissioner Regulatory Revolving Fund** (18 Del. C. § 305(c)(1)d).
- **Raises Revolving Fund cap and changes handling of excess:**
  - Sets max unencumbered year-end balance at **$5,000,000**.
  - Requires **transfer to General Fund** of any unencumbered balance **over $5,000,000** at fiscal year end (18 Del. C. § 305(e)).
- **Increases licensing fees (selected examples from § 701):**
  - Insurance producer: **$100→$125** (initial and biennial renewal)
  - Adjuster: **$100→$125** (initial and biennial renewal)
  - Motor vehicle appraiser: **$100→$125** (initial and biennial renewal)
  - Apprentice/temporary producer (initial): **$50→$75**
  - Surplus lines broker: initial **$250→$275**; renewal **$200→$225**
  - Business entity (insurance agency): **$100→$125** (initial and biennial renewal)
  - Bail agent: **$200→$225**
  - Viatical settlement provider/broker: **$250→$275** (initial and renewal as stated in § 701(30))
  - Discount medical plan organization: initial **$500→$525**; renewal **$250→$275**
  - Limited lines travel insurance producer: initial **$1,000→$1,025**; renewal **$500→$525**
  - Rental company limited lines producer: **$325 per year** (set in § 701(41))
  - Portable electronics vendor limited lines: initial **$1,025**; renewal **$525** (set in § 701(42))
  - Amendments to license under Chapter 17: **$25→$50**
- **Centralizes fees:**
  - **18 Del. C. § 2006** changes rental company limited lines fee from **$300 per year, per company** to “the fee set forth in § 701.”
  - **18 Del. C. § 2057(e)** removes prior caps (**not to exceed $1,000 initial / $500 renewal**) and instead sets fee as “the fee set forth in § 701.”

## Who Benefits
- **Delaware Department of Insurance / Insurance Commissioner’s Office:** Gains a dedicated share (**15%**) of specified licensing fees for operations and may retain up to **$5,000,000** unencumbered in the Revolving Fund.
- **General Fund (in some years and in excess-balance transfers):** Receives transfers of any Revolving Fund unencumbered balance **over $5,000,000** at fiscal year-end (and projected **GF +$1.67 million in FY 2027** per fee impact statement).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Insurance professionals and entities subject to the fee increases** under § 701, including producers, adjusters, appraisers, surplus lines brokers, bail agents, viatical settlement providers/brokers, discount medical plan organizations, travel insurance producers, rental companies, and portable electronics vendors.
