## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Chapter 70, Title 25 (Manufactured Homes and Manufactured Home Communities Act) to (1) clarify how time periods are computed, (2) уточнить/expand landlord maintenance duties in manufactured home communities (including drainage, utilities, and tree care), and (3) clarify that “business invitees” are not treated as a tenant/resident’s “guest” or “visitor.”

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes time-counting rules under Chapter 70 so that **Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays are excluded when the prescribed period is “12 days or less”** (previously tied to a shorter period under existing law, per synopsis).
- Defines “legal holiday” to include **Delaware legal holidays under 1 Del. C. § 501** **or a federal holiday**.
- Clarifies that a tenant’s “guest”/“visitor” **does not include a business invitee**.
- Expands/clarifies landlord maintenance obligations in rental agreements for manufactured home community lots, including **bulkheads, streets, and other grounds** to prevent standing water (with specified exemptions).
- Requires landlord to maintain **utilities and services provided by the landlord up to the physical connection to the home**, with a repair timing requirement: **within 48 hours after written notification**, or **as soon thereafter as practicable** if 48 hours is not practicable.
- Updates “tree” definition and tree-care obligations: applies to trees **at least 25 feet high** or with trunk/stem **at least 6 inches in diameter**, and explicitly includes **root system, limbs, and trunk/stem**.
- Updates the referenced tree-care standard from the (defunct) American Association of Nurserymen to **ANSI A300 Tree Care standards**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More consistent/clearer deadlines**: Excluding weekends/holidays for periods **12 days or less** may reduce disputes about when notices, cure periods, or other Chapter 70 deadlines expire.
- **Improved habitability and safety expectations**: Expressly requires landlord maintenance of **bulkheads, streets, and grounds** to prevent standing water, potentially reducing water-related damage or hazards (as permitted by law).
- **Faster utility repairs**: Sets an explicit expectation to repair landlord-provided utilities/services **within 48 hours after written notice**, improving service reliability for residents.
- **Clearer responsibility for significant trees**: Requires care/removal of qualifying trees (≥ **25 feet** or ≥ **6 inches** diameter) including **roots/limbs/trunk**, which may reduce risk from neglected trees in common areas/lots.
- **Modernized tree-care benchmark**: Shifts to **ANSI A300** standards, aligning obligations with a current, recognized industry standard.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increased landlord compliance burden/costs**: Expanded/clarified duties (standing-water prevention in more areas, utility repair timelines, tree maintenance per ANSI A300) may increase operating and maintenance costs.
- **Potential for longer effective time windows in practice**: Excluding weekends/holidays for periods **up to 12 days** can make some processes take longer in calendar days (even if the “counted” days remain the same).
- **Ambiguity around “as soon thereafter as practicable”**: The 48-hour utility repair rule includes an exception that could be contested and may lead to disputes about what is “practicable.”

## Key Policy Changes
1. **Time computation rule added/clarified (§ 7002A)**:
   - Do not count the day of the act/event/default that starts the period.
   - If the period is **12 days or less**, do **not** count **Saturdays, Sundays, or legal holidays**.
   - If the last day falls on a **Saturday/Sunday/legal holiday**, deadline moves to the **next non-holiday weekday**.
2. **Definitions updated (§ 7003)**:
   - “Guest”/“visitor” **does not include a business invitee**.
   - “Legal holiday” includes holidays under **1 Del. C. § 501** **or a federal holiday**.
   - “Tree” defined as woody perennial **≥ 25 feet** or trunk/stem **≥ 6 inches diameter**, and includes **root system, limbs, and trunk/stem**.
3. **Rental agreement required provisions expanded/clarified (§ 7008(a)(13))**:
   - Landlord must maintain/regrade **lot areas, bulkheads, streets, and other grounds** to prevent standing water/moving-water harms (with listed exemptions).
   - Landlord must maintain landlord-provided **utilities/services up to the home’s physical connection**, and repair within **48 hours** after **written notification** (or as soon as practicable if not possible within 48 hours).
   - Landlord must maintain/care for/remove qualifying trees; tree-care standard updated to **ANSI A300 Tree Care standards** (instead of American Association of Nurserymen standards).

## Who Benefits
