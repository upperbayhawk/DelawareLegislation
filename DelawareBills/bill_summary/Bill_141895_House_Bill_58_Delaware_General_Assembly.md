## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 11 of the Delaware Code to prohibit Delaware law-enforcement officers from engaging in certain stops, questioning, arrests, searches, detentions, or inquiries based on actual or suspected citizenship/immigration status or federal **civil** immigration matters, including acting on **civil immigration warrants** or **immigration detainers**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new prohibition in **11 Del. C. § 1903A** titled **“Civil immigration actions prohibited.”**
- Bars law-enforcement officers from **stopping, questioning, arresting, searching, or detaining** someone based on actual or suspected **citizenship/immigration status** or suspected **violations of federal civil immigration law**.
- Prohibits officers from **inquiring** about an individual’s **immigration status**, **citizenship**, **place of birth**, or **eligibility for a Social Security number**.
- Prohibits officers from **arresting, detaining, or prolonging detention** based on **civil immigration warrants** or **immigration detainers**.
- The bill text does not specify exceptions, enforcement mechanisms, penalties, training requirements, or an effective date.
- The bill text does not provide fiscal estimates or implementation details.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- Limits state and local law-enforcement actions tied to **federal civil immigration law**, by explicitly prohibiting reliance on suspected civil immigration violations and civil immigration warrants/detainers (11 Del. C. § 1903A(1), (3)).
- Restricts collection of sensitive personal information (immigration status, citizenship, place of birth, SSN eligibility) by prohibiting related inquiries (11 Del. C. § 1903A(2)).
- Prevents **prolonged detention** based on civil immigration warrants or detainers (11 Del. C. § 1903A(3)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Reduces the actions Delaware law-enforcement officers may take in response to **civil immigration warrants** and **immigration detainers** by prohibiting arrest, detention, or prolonging detention on those bases (11 Del. C. § 1903A(3)).
- Limits officer questioning by prohibiting inquiries into immigration status/citizenship/place of birth/SSN eligibility (11 Del. C. § 1903A(2)), which may constrain information-gathering in contexts where such questions were previously asked.

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds **11 Del. C. § 1903A** to prohibit a Delaware law-enforcement officer from:
  1. **Stopping, questioning, arresting, searching, or detaining** any individual based on actual or suspected **citizenship/immigration status** or actual or suspected violations of **federal civil immigration law**.
  2. **Inquiring** about an individual’s **immigration status**, **citizenship**, **place of birth**, or **eligibility for a Social Security number**.
  3. **Arresting, detaining, or prolonging detention** based on **civil immigration warrants** or **immigration detainers**.
