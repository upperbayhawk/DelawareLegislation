## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Rename the Delaware State Police Training Academy to honor Colonel James L. Ford, Jr., and require signage reflecting the new name.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Renames the “Delaware State Police Training Academy” as the **“Colonel James L. Ford, Jr. State Police Training Academy.”**
- Requires **appropriate signs** be placed on the academy grounds reflecting the new name.
- The act is honorary and commemorative; it does not create new programs or alter training standards in the bill text.
- Legislative findings cite Colonel Ford’s death on **March 1, 2025** (age **93**) and **51 years** of public service.
- References Colonel Ford’s Delaware law enforcement and public safety leadership, including State Police Superintendent (retired **1976**) and Cabinet Secretary of Public Safety and Homeland Security (**2001–2004**).
- The enrolled act indicates it was **Approved August 20, 2025**.
