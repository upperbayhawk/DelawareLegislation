## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To update and modernize Delaware’s Self-Service Storage Facilities Act (Title 25, Chapter 49) by clarifying electronic rental agreements and notices, refining lien enforcement and sale advertising rules (including online advertising/auctions), establishing rules for towing vehicles/watercraft after extended default, and setting clearer procedures for property removal and disposal after termination/nonrenewal.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Allows self-storage **rental agreements to be delivered and accepted electronically**.  
- Clarifies “**last known address**” includes the address in the latest rental agreement, or (subject to rental-agreement requirements) a later written change-of-address notice.  
- Makes an occupant **bound by a written rental agreement even if unsigned** if the occupant **pays, rents, or uses** the space **more than 30 days after delivery** of the written agreement.  
- Updates lien-sale advertising: requires publication **once a week for 2 consecutive weeks** in a newspaper (print or electronic) **or** on a **publicly accessible independent website** that regularly advertises or conducts **online auctions/sales** of personal property.  
- Requires the lien-sale/disposition occur **not sooner than 30 days after the first publication** of the advertisement.  
- Treats online auction/sale as a “public sale” and states **no license is required** because the sale is incidental to the self-storage business.  
- Authorizes towing of a stored **motor vehicle, trailer, or watercraft** if the occupant is in default **for 60 consecutive days**, with specific notice requirements (electronic or verified mail with fallback to verified mail if no electronic receipt).  
- After termination/nonrenewal notice, occupant must receive **not less than 35 days** to remove property; owners may restrict access (upon notice) and may dispose of remaining property after the deadline.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Modernized transactions and communication:** Explicitly permits **electronic delivery/acceptance** of rental agreements and use of **electronic mail** for certain notices, potentially reducing delays and paperwork.  
- **Clearer enforceability of agreements:** The **30-day “use/pay/rent after delivery”** rule clarifies when an **unsigned** written rental agreement is still binding.  
- **Expanded advertising options for lien sales:** Allowing advertisements on qualifying **publicly accessible independent websites** (in addition to newspapers) may increase visibility for sales and standardize practice with online auctions.  
- **More structured vehicle/watercraft handling:** Provides a defined **60 consecutive day** default threshold for towing motor vehicles/trailers/watercraft and requires notice with towing company and redemption location details.  
- **Clearer post-termination timeline:** Requires at least **35 days** for removal after termination/nonrenewal notice, and clarifies owner authority to restrict access and dispose of remaining items after the deadline.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Occupants can be bound without signing:** An occupant may be legally bound by a **written** rental agreement even if **unsigned** if they continue use/payment beyond **30 days**, which could disadvantage occupants who did not review or affirmatively sign.  
- **Risk of missed electronic notice:** The bill allows notices by **electronic mail** in several contexts; although towing notice requires a fallback to **verified mail** if no electronic delivery receipt, the bill still relies on electronic communications for key steps.  
- **Towing authority after 60-day default:** Authorizes towing of motor vehicles/trailers/watercraft after **60 consecutive days** of default, which could impose additional costs and burdens on delinquent occupants (towing/recovery).  
- **Owner disposal authority after termination deadline:** Allows owners to **dispose** of property remaining after the post-termination deadline, increasing the consequences of non-removal.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities

## Key Policy Changes
- **Electronic agreements:** Rental agreements “may be delivered and accepted electronically.”  
- **“Last known address” clarified:** Includes address from latest rental agreement or a subsequent written change-of-address notice (subject to rental agreement requirements).  
- **Unsigned agreement enforceability:** Occupant bound even if unsigned if occupant “**pays, rents, or uses**” the space **more than 30 days** after delivery of the written rental agreement.  
- **Required rental agreement disclosures:** Must advise occupants of (1) **existence of the lien** and (2) that stored property **may be sold** to satisfy the lien if occupant is in default.  
- **Lien-sale advertising expanded:** Publication **once a week for 2 consecutive weeks** in print/electronic newspaper **or** qualifying independent website; sale **not sooner than 30 days** after first publication; ad must include specified details (description, facility/unit, occupant name, time/place/manner, names of occupants).  
- **Online auctions clarified:** A “public sale” includes offering property on a **publicly accessible website** that regularly conducts online auctions/sales; **no license required** for such sale as incidental to the self-storage business.  
- **Towing after extended default:** If the liened property is a **motor vehicle, trailer, or watercraft** and occupant is in default **for 60 consecutive days**, owner may have it towed; owner must provide towing/redeeming information by **verified or electronic mail**, with fallback to **verified mail** if no electronic delivery receipt.  
- **Termination/nonrenewal removal period and disposal:** After written notice of termination/nonrenewal (in person, electronic mail, or verified mail), occupant may not continue using the facility and must be given **not less than 35 days** after delivery to remove property; owner may restrict access (upon notice) and may dispose of remaining property after the deadline.

## Who Benefits
- **Self-service storage facility owners/operators:** Clearer enforceability of rental agreements, expanded advertising methods (including online), explicit authority for online public sales without a license, and defined towing authority after **60 consecutive days** of default.  
- **Potential buyers at lien sales:** More sales may be advertised online, increasing access to sale information and participation.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Storage occupants/tenants in default or post-termination:** Increased enforceability of **unsigned** agreements after **30 days**, potential for towing of vehicles/watercraft after **60 consecutive days** of default, and disposal of remaining property after the **35-day** removal window following termination/nonrenewal notice.  

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Approved:** **July 30, 2025**.  
- **Deadlines/thresholds created or revised by the Act:**
  - Occupant bound by unsigned written rental agreement if use/payment continues **more than 30 days** after delivery.  
  - Lien-sale ad must run **once a week for 2 consecutive weeks**; sale/disposition **not sooner than 30 days** after first publication.  
  - Vehicle/trailer/watercraft towing authorized after **60 consecutive days** of default.  
  - Termination/nonrenewal notice must give **not less than 35 days** after delivery for occupant to remove all property.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Mandatory rental agreement disclosures:** Rental agreement must include statements advising occupants of the **lien** and potential **sale** of stored property upon default.  
- **Lien-sale process requirements:** Advertising frequency (**2 consecutive weeks**) and timing (sale **≥ 30 days** after first publication) and required ad contents (property description, facility/unit, occupant name(s), and sale time/place/manner).  
- **Towing notice requirements:** If towing a motor vehicle/trailer/watercraft after **60 consecutive days** default, owner must send occupant the towing company’s **name/address/telephone** and the **street address** for redemption by **verified or electronic mail**; if emailed and no electronic receipt establishing delivery, owner must send via **verified mail**.  
- **Post-termination/nonrenewal compliance:** Occupant may not use the facility after notice; must have at least **35 days** to remove property; owner may impose reasonable access restrictions (upon notice) and may dispose of remaining property after the stated date.
