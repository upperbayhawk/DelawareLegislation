## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 7 of the Delaware Code to revise definitions of “game animals,” update permissible hunting weapons/ammunition for deer and primitive weapon seasons, expand and clarify when Sunday hunting is allowed, and adjust certain raccoon/opossum hunting rules.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates the statutory list of “game animals” in **7 Del. C. § 701**, including adding **skunk** and **weasel** (and the synopsis states removing **European and snowshoe hares**).
- Lowers the minimum muzzleloader bore size from **0.42 inches (10.67 mm)** to **0.40 inches (10.16 mm)** for primitive weapon season in **7 Del. C. § 704(f)** and makes related caliber conforming changes.
- Adjusts deer handgun ammunition rules in **7 Del. C. § 704(g)**, including changing the straight-wall handgun ammo range from **.357–.50** to **.30–.50** caliber and changing minimum case length from **1.25 inches** to **1.30 inches** (with **.38 caliber minimum 1.29 inches** retained).
- Changes the special January handgun/rifle season timing from **“second Saturday”** to **“second Sunday”** following the first Saturday in January (**7-day season**) in **7 Del. C. § 704(g)(4)a**.
- Rewrites the Sunday hunting prohibition in **7 Del. C. § 712** to generally **permit Sunday hunting for game birds and game animals** during Department-established seasons, subject to private landowner discretion and public-land designation by the applicable agency.
- Continues/retains prohibition on “remote-controlled” hunting weapons via computer software/services in **7 Del. C. § 704(h)**.
- Keeps/clarifies certain raccoon and opossum rules, including box-trap maximum opening dimension of **195 square inches** and allowing a **.22 caliber rimfire pistol** for raccoon/opossum hunting (**7 Del. C. § 786**).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More hunting opportunity on Sundays:** § 712 is rewritten to allow Sunday hunting for game birds and game animals **within DNREC-established seasons**, expanding opportunities beyond the prior deer-focused exception.
- **Expanded legal equipment options:** Lowering minimum muzzleloader bore to **.40 caliber** in § 704(f) can allow more firearms to qualify for primitive weapon season.
- **Potentially clearer framework for Sunday hunting:** The new § 712 structure lists explicit conditions under which Sunday hunting is allowed (e.g., within DNREC seasons; on private lands at landowner discretion; on designated public lands).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced default limitation on Sunday hunting:** Replacing a broad Sunday prohibition with broad permission (subject to conditions) may increase hunting activity on Sundays compared to prior law (§ 712 rewrite).
- **Public notice/comment process appears reduced/removed:** Prior § 712(d) required agencies to provide “adequate opportunity for public comment” before designating/changing Sunday deer hunting on public lands; the rewritten § 712 language does not include that explicit public-comment requirement.
- **More complex ammunition specifics remain:** Although the synopsis states “simplifies” caliber rules, § 704(g)(1) still contains multiple caliber/case-length constraints (e.g., **barrel length 5.75"–12.5"**, **case length 1.30" min**, **1.82" max**, special **.38** rule).

## Key Policy Changes
- **7 Del. C. § 701 (Game animals list)**
  - Adds **skunk** and **weasel** to the list of “game animals” (as reflected in the amended list).
  - Synopsis indicates **European and snowshoe hares** are removed as game animals (not clearly visible as explicit strike/insert in the excerpted § 701 text).
- **7 Del. C. § 704(f) (Primitive weapon season muzzleloader specs)**
  - Minimum bore reduced from **0.42"** to **0.40"**.
  - Prohibits balls smaller than **.40 caliber** (changed from **.42**).
  - Conforming change: DNREC may permit a single-shot muzzle-loading pistol minimum reduced from **.42** to **.40**.
- **7 Del. C. § 704(g) (Deer hunting with handgun/rifle in certain areas)**
  - Handgun ammo caliber range changes from **.357–.50** to **.30–.50** (straight-wall handgun ammunition).
  - Minimum cartridge case length changes from **1.25"** to **1.30"**; **maximum case length remains 1.82"**; special rule remains that **.38 caliber must have minimum case length 1.29"**.
  - January handgun/rifle season remains **7 days**, but end date changes to the **second Sunday** following the first Saturday in January (was second Saturday).
- **7 Del. C. § 704(h)**
  - Continues to prohibit software/services enabling a person **not physically present** to remotely control a weapon to take live animals/birds.
- **7 Del. C. § 712 (Sunday hunting)**
  - Replaces a general Sunday prohibition (with enumerated exceptions) with a general rule that Sunday hunting of **game birds and game animals** is permitted if listed conditions apply (including being within DNREC seasons; private landowner discretion; and designated public lands).
  - Creates a fallback: if § 712(a) does not apply, Sunday hunting is **not permitted**.
- **7 Del. C. § 786 (Raccoon and opossum)**
  - Retains/sets box-trap maximum opening dimension of **195 square inches**.
  - Allows use of a **.22 caliber rimfire pistol** to hunt raccoons and opossums.
