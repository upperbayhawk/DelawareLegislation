## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s unfair insurance practices law (Title 18, §2322) to strengthen protections for **unrepresented** individuals who settle **automobile-accident personal injury** claims shortly after injury by (1) clarifying and extending how a settlement can be rescinded and (2) requiring clearer notice of the right to rescind.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Allows an injured person to **rescind** a release/settlement related to an automobile accident within **60 days** if they signed/cashed settlement while **unrepresented** and within **30 days** of injury/incident.
- Applies when a release is **signed within 30 days of the infliction of injuries** (and the person had **no attorney guidance**, **no power of attorney**, and **no contract of employment** with an attorney).
- If **no release is signed**, rescission can occur within **60 days** of **cashing a settlement check** or a **transfer of electronic funds**.
- To rescind effectively, the injured person must provide **written notice** and **return the check/proceeds**.
- The rescission notice and returned proceeds must be sent **no later than 10 days after** the **60-day rescission period** ends.
- Requires releases executed within **30 days of the incident** to include a **conspicuous, separate notice** of the right to rescind (on the release or settlement check), or via **written communication** if neither exists.
- Effective date: **6 months after enactment into law**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves informed consent for unrepresented claimants:** Requires conspicuous, separate notice of the right to rescind for releases executed within **30 days** of the incident (or written notice if no release/check exists). (§2322(c); Synopsis)
- **Creates a clear 60-day reconsideration window:** Permits rescission within **60 days** of signing the release (or within **60 days** of cashing/receiving funds if no release). (§2322(a))
- **Adds clarity for electronic payments:** Explicitly covers rescission timing tied to **transfer of electronic funds**, not just checks. (§2322(a))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **May delay finality of settlements:** Settlements with unrepresented claimants may remain subject to rescission for **60 days**, plus up to an additional **10 days** for sending notice/returning proceeds. (§2322(a), (b)(3))
- **Administrative burden for insurers/claims handlers:** Requires ensuring proper rescission notice language is provided conspicuously and separately on releases/settlement checks (or via written communication). (§2322(c))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Exact wording of required rescission notice:** The bill requires a notice stated “conspicuously and separately” but does not specify the exact language. (§2322(c))
- **What qualifies as “written communication” when no release/check exists:** The bill requires written communication but does not define acceptable forms (mail, email, etc.). (§2322(c))
- **Interaction between “injuries” timing vs. “incident” timing:** Subsection (a) references signing within **30 days of infliction of the injuries**, while subsection (c) references execution within **30 days of the incident**. The bill does not explain how to resolve differences if these dates diverge. (§2322(a), (c))
- **Scope of “unrepresented” status:** The bill lists “without assistance or guidance,” “without any power of attorney,” and “without any contract of employment,” but does not specify how disputes over these facts are resolved. (§2322(a))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Rescission right tied to representation status and timing:**
  - A release signed within **30 days** of injury, by an individual **without attorney assistance/guidance** and with **no power of attorney** or **contract of employment** with an attorney, **may be rescinded within 60 days** of signing. (§2322(a))
  - If **no release is signed**, rescission may occur within **60 days** of **cashing a settlement check** or **transfer of electronic funds**. (§2322(a))
- **Conditions for an effective rescission:**
  1. **Written notice** to the releasee, the releasee’s legal representative, or insurance carrier. (§2322(b)(1))
  2. **Return** of any check or settlement proceeds received. (§2322(b)(2))
  3. Both items must be **sent no later than 10 days after** the **60-day** rescission period expires. (§2322(b)(3))
- **Mandatory notice in settlement documents/communications:**
  - A release of liability executed within **30 days of the incident** must include conspicuous, separate notice of the right to rescind on the release or settlement check; if neither exists, provide the language via **written communication**. (§2322(c))
- **Effective date:** The Act takes effect **6 months after enactment**. (Section 2)

## Who Benefits
- **Unrepresented individuals** settling **automobile-accident personal injury** claims within **30 days** of the injury/incident, who gain a **60-day** right to rescind and must receive conspicuous notice of that right. (§2322(a), (c); Synopsis)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Insurers/claims administrators and releasees** seeking prompt, final settlement closure, due to the **60-day rescission window** and notice/processing requirements (including potential rescission after payment). (§2322(a)-(c))

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date:** **6 months after enactment into law.** (Section 2)
- **Eligibility trigger for rescission (release):** Release signed within **30 days** of infliction of injuries (and claimant unrepresented as specified). (§2322(a))
- **Rescission period:** **60 days** after signing the release; or if no release, **60 days** from **cashing a settlement check** or **transfer of electronic funds**. (§2322(a))
- **Rescission mailing/sending deadline:** Written notice and returned proceeds must be sent **no later than 10 days after** the end of the **60-day** rescission period. (§2322(b)(3))
- **Notice requirement applicability:** Release executed within **30 days** of the incident. (§2322(c))
