## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To prohibit the operation of “private detention facilities” in Delaware by amending Title 11, Chapter 65, including updating definitions and adding an express prohibition.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits any person from operating a **private detention facility** within Delaware.  
- Defines “private detention facility” as a detention facility operated by a **private, nongovernmental, for-profit entity** under a **contract or agreement with a governmental entity**.  
- Retains/clarifies the broader definition of “detention facility” as facilities confining persons for **punitive sentences** or **pretrial/other proceedings**.  
- Explicitly **excludes** several types of facilities from the “private detention facility” definition (e.g., certain juvenile services, civil commitment evaluation/treatment, residential care, school disciplinary detention, public health quarantine/isolation).  
- Applies as a statewide prohibition (“within the State”).  
- No penalties, enforcement mechanism, funding, or implementation dates are stated in the provided text.  
- Synopsis states the bill’s intent: **ban operation of private detention facilities** in Delaware.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Amends 11 Del. C. § 6503 (Definitions):**
  - Defines “private detention facility” as a detention facility **operated by a private, nongovernmental, for-profit entity** under **contract/agreement with a governmental entity**.
  - Creates explicit exclusions from “private detention facility,” including:
    - Juvenile rehabilitative/counseling/treatment/mental health/educational/medical service facilities under **Family Court jurisdiction (Title 10, Ch. 9)**.
    - Facilities providing evaluation/treatment for persons detained or committed under **Title 16, Ch. 50**.
    - Facilities providing ancillary services (educational/vocational/medical/other) to inmates under **direct supervision** of DOC or other law enforcement.
    - Residential care facilities licensed under **Title 16, Ch. 22**.
    - School facilities used for **disciplinary detention** of a pupil.
    - Facilities used for **quarantine or isolation** for public health reasons under **Title 20, § 3136**.
- **Adds/Amends 11 Del. C. Subchapter XI, § 6593 (Private detention facilities; prohibited):**
  - “**A person may not operate a private detention facility within the State.**”
