## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To require annual disclosure of recorded votes cast at meetings of, or on matters before, the PJM Interconnection Regional Transmission Organization (PJM) by specified Delaware entities.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **annual reporting** of **each “recorded vote”** cast at PJM during the **immediately preceding calendar year**.  
- Applies (as enacted) to **DP&L** and the **Division of the Public Advocate** **when they have voted** at PJM.  
- Reports must be submitted to the **Delaware Public Service Commission (“the Commission”)**, the **Governor**, the **General Assembly**, and the **Director and Librarian of the Division of Legislative Services**.  
- “Recorded vote” is broadly defined to include any tabulated vote (individually or by sector), **regardless of whether it is a final position** or whether the voters have decision-making authority.  
- “Meeting” is broadly defined to include any PJM committee, user group, task force, or other part of PJM where votes are taken.  
- A utility **may** include this PJM voting report within its **annual renewable energy portfolio standards (REPS)** compliance report (Title 26, Chapter 1, Subchapter III-A).  
- The enacted text requires inclusion of **all reported votes cast**, even if otherwise disclosed elsewhere.  
- **Approved July 16, 2025.**

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Greater transparency and accountability:** Mandates disclosure of recorded PJM votes, making voting activity visible to state leadership and the legislature (Commission, Governor, General Assembly, Division of Legislative Services).
- **Broad coverage of vote types:** Because “recorded vote” includes sector-tabulated votes and non-final positions, the reporting captures more PJM decision points than only final votes.
- **Administrative convenience option:** Allows DP&L to bundle the report with its annual **REPS compliance** reporting, potentially reducing duplicative filings.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative burden:** Requires compiling and filing an annual report covering each recorded PJM vote cast in the prior calendar year.
- **Narrower scope than earlier bill text:** The enacted version no longer lists additional entities and affiliates (e.g., DEC, Delaware Municipal Cooperative, and “affiliates”) that appeared in the earlier bill text provided—potentially reducing the total transparency achieved compared to that earlier draft.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **No stated filing deadline:** The bill requires a report “each year” for the “immediately preceding calendar year,” but does not specify *by what date* the report must be submitted.
- **Report contents beyond vote list:** The earlier draft required “a brief description explaining how each vote…is in the interest of the public,” but the enacted text provided does **not** include that requirement; it is unclear whether any narrative explanation is expected.
- **Definition of which votes must be included:** The enacted text says the report must include “all reported votes cast,” which could be read as circular; it does not explicitly restate “each recorded vote cast” in the must-include clause the way the earlier draft did.
- **Format and public availability:** The bill does not specify the report format, whether it must be posted publicly, or how it will be stored/accessed.

## Key Policy Changes
