## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 16 (Pure Food and Drugs) to prohibit the manufacture and sale (and related commercial handling) of human food containing Red Dye 3 (CAS No. 16423-68-0) in Delaware, and to establish civil penalties for violations.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits a person from **manufacturing, selling, delivering, distributing, holding with intent to sell, or offering for sale** in Delaware **human food** that contains **Red Dye 3 (CAS No. 16423-68-0)**.  
- Applies **notwithstanding** existing Title 16 provisions **§§ 3311, 3313, and 3314** (i.e., the ban overrides those sections to the extent they would allow the conduct).  
- Defines “food” for this section as the Title 16 definition, but **limited to articles for consumption by humans**.  
- Establishes civil penalties: **up to $5,000** (plus costs) for a **first occurrence**; **up to $10,000** (plus costs) for a **subsequent occurrence**.  
- Treats **each day** a violation occurs as a **separate occurrence**, increasing potential cumulative penalties.  
- Effective date is **October 1, 2027**.  
- Bill synopsis states food includes **drink, ice, confectionary, and condiments** (context for what “food” covers).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces consumer exposure** to Red Dye 3 in foods sold in Delaware by banning specified commercial activities involving foods containing the additive. (Ban in § 3325(b))  
- Creates a **deterrent** through monetary penalties and **per-day counting** of violations, encouraging compliance and faster removal from shelves. (§ 3325(c)-(d))  
- Provides a **clear statewide rule** for products containing Red Dye 3, rather than relying on voluntary reformulation. (§ 3325(b))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Compliance and reformulation costs** for manufacturers/retailers/distributors handling products containing Red Dye 3, because covered foods could no longer be manufactured or sold in Delaware after the effective date. (§ 3325(b))  
- Potential for **civil penalty exposure** that can compound daily (up to **$5,000** first occurrence and **$10,000** subsequent, **per day**), which may be significant for small businesses with inventory already in supply chains. (§ 3325(c)-(d))  
- The bill sets a state-specific prohibition that may require **Delaware-specific inventory management** for multi-state sellers. (§ 3325(b))

## Health Impact
- The synopsis states Red Dye 3 “has been **linked to behavioral concerns in children and cancer**” and is used in many foods, including those marketed to children; the bill’s policy response is to **ban foods containing Red Dye 3** in Delaware commerce. (Synopsis; § 3325(b))

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds **§ 3325. Red dye 3 prohibited in food** to Chapter 33, Title 16.  
- Prohibits, in Delaware, the following activities involving **human food** containing **Red Dye 3 (CAS No. 16423-68-0)**: **manufacture, sale, delivery, distribution, holding with intent to sell, and offering for sale**. (§ 3325(b))  
- Establishes civil penalties:  
  - **First occurrence:** up to **$5,000** **plus costs**. (§ 3325(c)(1))  
  - **Subsequent occurrence:** up to **$10,000** **plus costs**. (§ 3325(c)(2))  
  - **Each day** of violation is a **separate occurrence**. (§ 3325(d))  
- Makes the prohibition apply **notwithstanding §§ 3311, 3313, or 3314**. (§ 3325(b))  
- Effective date: **October 1, 2027**. (Section 2)
