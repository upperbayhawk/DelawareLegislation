## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends 29 Del. C. § 7907 to rename and update the Delaware Nursing Home Residents Quality Assurance Commission, expand its scope to all “long-term care facilities” (nursing facilities, assisted living facilities, and secured memory care units), clarify staffing and appointments, streamline operations, and add/clarify duties (including forming subcommittees, proposing policy changes, and seeking grants).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Renames** the body to the **“Delaware Residents’ Protection Commission”** (from the Delaware Nursing Home Resident’s Quality Assurance Commission).  
- **Expands coverage** from nursing homes to **“long-term care facilities,”** defined to include **nursing facilities, assisted living facilities, and secured memory care units** (by cross-reference to **16 Del. C. § 1102**).  
- **Clarifies staffing/hiring:** the **Executive Director and staff are selected by a majority of Commission members**, then become **employees of the Administrative Office of the Courts (AOC)**, subject to AOC policies, and **serve at the pleasure of the Commission**.  
- **Revises membership and appointments:** sets specific appointing authorities, and **shifts appointment of 4 members** to be chosen by a **majority vote** of certain sitting/appointed members (rather than the Governor), as described in § 7907(c)(5).  
- **Adds operational rules:** quorum is **a majority of members present** (vacancies not counted), and Commission actions pass by **majority of members present**.  
- **Adds attendance-based removal:** a member is **deemed resigned** after **3 consecutive** absences or **4 of 12 consecutive** meeting absences.  
- **Allows subcommittees** that may act on behalf of the Commission consistent with its purpose, and permits receiving testimony from nonmembers.  
- **Effective date:** the Act takes effect **90 days after enactment**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Broader resident protection scope:** By defining “long-term care facility” to include **assisted living** and **secured memory care units** (not just nursing facilities), the Commission’s monitoring and recommendations apply to more settings where vulnerable adults receive care.  
- **Clearer accountability for staffing:** Selection by the Commission with employment through the **AOC** may clarify lines of supervision and employment policies for the Executive Director and staff (§ 7907(b)).  
- **Faster filling of seats (as stated in synopsis):** Moving **4 appointments** to a majority vote of specified members (§ 7907(c)(5)) is intended to “allow for faster appointment of members” (Synopsis).  
- **Operational continuity:** The “deemed resigned” rule for repeated absences (3 consecutive or 4 of 12) may reduce persistent vacancies-in-practice and improve meeting functionality (§ 7907(f)(3)).  
- **Additional capacity tools:** Authority to **form subcommittees** and **seek grant funding** may help the Commission carry out duties within appropriations (§ 7907(j), (n)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential added workload and complexity:** Expanding the Commission’s focus from nursing homes to all “long-term care facilities” may increase the volume and variety of issues the Commission must monitor and report on (§ 7907(a), (m), (o)).  
- **Reduced gubernatorial appointment role:** The Governor no longer appoints all positions previously contemplated; **4 members** are appointed by a vote of certain Commission-related appointees/members (§ 7907(c)(5); Synopsis).  
- **Strict attendance rule may increase turnover:** Automatic “deemed resigned” after **3 consecutive** or **4 of 12** absences could result in more frequent membership changes (§ 7907(f)(3)).  
- **Uncompensated service remains:** Members serve **without compensation** (only expense reimbursement), which can limit who is able to participate and sustain attendance (§ 7907(p)).

## Health Impact

- The expanded definition of covered facilities (including assisted living and secured memory care units) extends the Commission’s health/safety monitoring and recommendations to additional care settings (§ 7907(a)(2)b).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Name change:** “Delaware Nursing Home Resident’s Quality Assurance Commission” updated to “**Delaware Residents’ Protection Commission**” (§ 7907(a)(1)-(2)).  
- **Scope expansion:** Commission purpose and duties shift from nursing homes to **all “long-term care facilities”** (nursing facility, assisted living facility, secured memory care unit) (§ 7907(a)(1)-(2); § 7907(m)).  
- **Staffing/hiring:** Executive Director may speak on behalf of the Commission; selection by majority of members; staff become **AOC employees** serving at the pleasure of the Commission (§ 7907(b)).  
- **Membership and appointments revised:** Specific appointment pathways; **4 members appointed by majority vote** of certain members/appointees (§ 7907(c)(5)).  
- **Geographic distribution requirement:** Membership must include representation from **each of the 3 counties** (§ 7907(e)).  
- **Term/attendance rules:** 3-year terms for certain appointees, with ability to shorten terms to stagger; “deemed resigned” after **3 consecutive** or **4 of 12 consecutive** meeting absences (§ 7907(f)(2)-(3)).  
- **Governance rules:** Chair elected by members; quorum is **majority present**; actions approved by **majority of members present**; ability to adopt rules/bylaws (§ 7907(g)-(i)).  
- **Virtual meeting rule:** May hold virtual meetings under FOIA with an “anchor location” in jurisdiction open to the public where **1+ member or staff** attend (§ 7907(l)).  
- **Subcommittees:** May form subcommittees; subcommittees may act on behalf of Commission consistent with purpose (§ 7907(n)).  
- **Annual report content expanded/clarified:** Must include trend data, visit findings/recommendations, summaries of recommendations/correspondence (with FOIA-related redactions), legislative review/positions, advocacy goals, and Executive Director duty time allocations (including **percentage of time devoted to each duty**) (§ 7907(o)).

## Who Benefits
- **Residents of long-term care facilities** (including nursing facilities, assisted living facilities, and secured memory care units) through expanded monitoring and policy advocacy (§ 7907(a)(1)-(2); § 7907(m)).  
- **Families and advocates** seeking timely oversight responses to allegations of abuse/neglect/mistreatment/financial exploitation (§ 7907(a)(1)).  
- **State oversight ecosystem** (DHSS, DOJ, law enforcement, providers) via a formal body tasked with coordination, analysis of trends, and recommendations (§ 7907(m)(1), (5), (6)).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Commission members** who have difficulty attending meetings may lose their seat due to the “deemed resigned” attendance thresholds (**3 consecutive** or **4 of 12**) (§ 7907(f)(3)).  
- **Long-term care facility providers** may face increased scrutiny and recommendations affecting operations because the Commission’s monitoring and reporting explicitly extends to long-term care facilities broadly (§ 7907(a), (m), (o)).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date:** **90 days after enactment into law** (Section 2).  
- **Meeting frequency:** Commission must meet **at least quarterly** (§ 7907(l)).  
- **Attendance removal thresholds:** “Deemed resigned” after **3 consecutive** meetings absent or **4 out of 12 consecutive** meetings absent (§ 7907(f)(3)).  
- **Annual report:** Required **annually** (no specific due date stated) (§ 7907(o)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **No criminal/civil penalties specified.**  
- **Compliance mechanisms within the Commission:**  
  - **Automatic resignation standard** for attendance: absent **3 consecutive** meetings or **4 of 12 consecutive** meetings → deemed resigned; appointing authority may accept resignation (§ 7907(f)(3)).  
  - **Quorum and voting rule:** official business requires **majority present**; actions pass by **majority of members present** (§ 7907(h)).
