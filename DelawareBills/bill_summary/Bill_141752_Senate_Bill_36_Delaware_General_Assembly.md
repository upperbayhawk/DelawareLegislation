## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 21 to require Delaware motor vehicle dealers to **offer** retail car buyers the option to **purchase a contract cancellation option agreement** that allows the buyer to **return the vehicle without cause** within a minimum cancellation window, subject to specified fees and conditions.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires dealers selling a **new or used** motor vehicle at retail to an individual for **personal, family, or household use** to **offer** a contract cancellation option agreement (with limited exceptions).  
- Establishes the **price** of the cancellation option using **cash sale price** tiers: **$75, $150, $250, or 1%/3%/5%** depending on price thresholds (e.g., **5%** if **$100,000+**).  
- Requires a **minimum cancellation period** through the dealer’s **close of business on the third business day** after delivery.  
- Permits a **restocking fee** capped by cash sale price (up to **$1,000** for vehicles **$20,000+**), with special rules for lease buyouts; the option price must be **credited toward** the restocking fee.  
- Sets a minimum mileage floor if the dealer imposes a mileage limit: cannot be **fewer than 250 miles**.  
- Requires the dealer to provide a **full refund** (minus allowed fees) within **3 business days** after a compliant cancellation; trade-in return rules depend on whether the option was charged.  
- Allows a dealer to refuse to offer the option to a consumer who cancelled previously for **30 days** after that cancellation.  
- Fiscal note: DMV anticipates expanded oversight/auditing responsibilities and estimates **FY2026: $248,403 ongoing + $134,000 one-time**, funded from the **Transportation Trust Fund**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Adds a guaranteed short “return window” option**: If purchased, buyers can cancel “without cause” for at least **3 business days** after delivery.  
- **Standardizes and caps fees**: Restocking fees are limited (e.g., **$175** for ≤ **$5,000**; **$1,000** for **$20,000+**), and the cancellation option price must be **credited toward** the restocking fee.  
- **Improves clarity/documentation**: Requires a **separate document** with specified disclosures (identity, VIN, cancellation period, fees, mileage limit, refund conditions, signature form).  
- **Protects buyers’ trade-in value** in certain situations: If a trade-in is sold/transferred and buyer cancels, refund must include **fair market value** (or contract value, whichever is greater) or **retail market value** (or contract value, whichever is greater), depending on circumstances.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Adds direct consumer cost to obtain the right**: Cancellation is only available under this section if the buyer **purchases** the option; the option cost ranges from **$75** up to **5% of cash sale price** for **$100,000+** vehicles, and is **not otherwise subject to setoff or refund**.  
- **Restocking fees can still be substantial**: Up to **$1,000** for vehicles **$20,000+**, plus potential additional amounts tied to a prior lease (excess mileage/damage/wear) in lease buyout situations.  
- **Administrative burden for dealers**: Must offer the option, prepare a separate agreement with required terms, manage trade-ins during the cancellation period, and process refunds within prescribed timelines.  
- **State administrative cost**: DMV anticipates needing **3.0 FTE Investigator III** and **1.0 FTE Investigative Supervisor** plus vehicles and startup equipment (see Budget Impact).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Mandatory offer by dealers**: Dealers must offer buyers the chance to purchase a cancellation option agreement for **new or used** retail sales to individuals for personal/family/household use.  
- **Option pricing schedule (cash sale price):**
  - **$75** if **$5,000 or less**
  - **$150** if **more than $5,000 but less than $10,000**
  - **$250** if **$10,000 or more but less than $30,000**
  - **1%** if **$30,000 or more but less than $50,000**
  - **3%** if **$50,000 or more but less than $100,000**
  - **5%** if **$100,000 or more**
- **Separate agreement document** required with minimum disclosures/terms, including cancellation window, restocking fee, mileage limit (if any), and cancellation procedure.  
- **Minimum cancellation period**: no less than **close of business on the third business day** following delivery.  
- **Mileage limit floor**: If imposed, cannot be fewer than **250 miles**.  
- **Restocking fee caps (cash sale price), with crediting rule**:
  - Max **$175** if **$5,000 or less**
  - Max **$350** if **more than $5,000 but less than $10,000**
  - Max **$500** if **$10,000 or more but less than $20,000**
  - Max **$1,000** if **$20,000 or more**
  - Dealer must **credit** the option price **toward** the restocking fee; the option price is **not otherwise refundable**.  
- **Lease buyout add-on to restocking fee**: If the buyer leased the same vehicle immediately prior to purchase, dealer may add amounts due under the lease for **excess mileage**, **unrepaired damage**, and **excess wear and tear**, if specified in the lease.  
- **Refund timing**: Dealer must refund within **3 business days** after compliant cancellation; trade-in return generally due **no later than the day after** cancellation (with different handling depending on whether the option was charged).  
- **Repeat-cancellation limitation**: Dealer not required to offer the option again to the same individual until **30 days** after a prior cancellation.  
- **No required notice to subsequent buyers** that a vehicle had been returned under this section; does not limit other disclosure duties under other laws.

## Who Benefits
- **Consumers** buying new or used vehicles for **personal, family, or household use** who want a defined, time-limited ability to cancel a purchase “without cause” (if they purchase the option).  
- Consumers with trade-ins may benefit from **required return** of the trade-in vehicle (or compensation at specified value standards if the trade-in was sold/transferred and cancellation occurs).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Dealers**: compliance, documentation, refund processing, and trade-in holding requirements; additional exposure to oversight/auditing.  
- **Consumers who purchase the option**: nonrefundable option cost (except as a credit against restocking fee) and potential restocking fees and lease-related added charges.

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date:** The Act takes effect **90 days after enactment into law** (Section 2).  
- **Minimum buyer cancellation window:** Through **close of business on the third business day** after delivery.  
- **Dealer refund deadline:** **No later than 3 business days** after buyer exercises cancellation in compliance with the agreement.  
- **Trade-in return deadline:** Generally **no later than the day after** the buyer cancels (specifics vary by whether the option was charged).  
- **Dealer may refuse repeat option sales:** For **30 days** after the individual cancelled a prior purchase under the option.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Compliance requirements specified**:  
  - Dealers must **offer** the option (subject to the 30-day repeat-cancellation exception).  
  - The option agreement must be **separate** and contain required information and disclosures.  
  - Mileage limits, if used, must be at least **250 miles**.  
  - Refunds and trade-in handling must follow specified **timelines** and **valuation rules**.
