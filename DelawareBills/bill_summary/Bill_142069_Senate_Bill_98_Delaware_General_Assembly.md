## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Update and clarify multiple provisions of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act (Title 6, Chapter 18) to address registered agent practices, ratification/waiver of void or voidable acts, filing corrections (including nullification), consolidation filing requirements, LLC agreement amendment cross-references, and timing of annual tax payments when an entity/series will cease.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits Delaware LLC registered agents from performing duties **solely** via a **“virtual office”** and/or **mail forwarding service**; defines “virtual office” as duties performed solely via the internet or other remote communication.  
- Expands and clarifies § 18-106(e) so ratification/waiver rules apply to void/voidable acts “**by or in respect of**” an LLC, including acts by a **member, manager, or other person** (in an individual capacity or on behalf of the LLC).  
- Specifies that ratification/waiver under § 18-106(e) may be **express or implied**, including through **statements, action, inaction, or acquiescence**.  
- Clarifies that when § 18-106(e) requires notice after a ratification/waiver, **giving notice is not a condition** to the ratification/waiver being effective.  
- Confirms a **certificate of correction** may **correct or nullify** an erroneously executed **certificate of cancellation**.  
- Requires that, in certain consolidations where the resulting entity is a **domestic LLC**, the **certificate of formation** must be attached to the **certificate of consolidation**.  
- Confirms a certificate of correction may **nullify** a previously filed certificate and addresses effective timing: generally relates back to the original filing date, except for persons “substantially and adversely affected.”  
- Confirms the annual tax is due **before filing** a certificate that will cause an LLC/registered series/foreign LLC registration to cease, and restates interest on late tax at **1.5% per month (or portion)**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves service-of-process reliability** by requiring registered agents to have more than purely virtual/mail-forwarding operations (registered agents may not perform duties solely through those means).  
- **Greater legal clarity and flexibility** for LLCs and stakeholders by expressly allowing **implied** ratification/waiver (including by inaction or acquiescence) for void/voidable acts “in respect of” the LLC.  
- **Reduces transactional uncertainty** by clarifying that required notice after ratification/waiver is **not a condition** to effectiveness (ratification/waiver can still be effective even if notice is not given).  
- **Corrects filing mistakes more completely** by allowing certificates of correction to **nullify** erroneous filings, including certificates of cancellation.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Compliance burden on registered agents**: agents that currently operate **solely** via virtual office and/or mail-forwarding must change operations to comply (prohibition is explicit).  
- **Potential reduced transparency for some stakeholders**: although notice may be required after ratification/waiver in certain cases, the bill states notice is **not a condition** to effectiveness, meaning actions may become effective before notice is given.  
- **Upfront tax payment requirement at dissolution/cessation**: if an LLC/series/foreign registration will cease by filing a certificate, the **full annual tax for that calendar year must be paid before filing**, potentially accelerating cash outlays.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- What specific operational setup satisfies the requirement that a registered agent not perform duties **solely** via a virtual office and/or mail-forwarding (e.g., minimum staffing/hours beyond “generally open” / “generally present”)?  
- The bill references “regulations established by the Secretary” regarding identity verification for registered agents; the content/timing of those regulations is **not specified in source text**.  
- The bill does not specify any new penalty structure for violating the “no solely virtual office/mail forwarding” rule (enforcement mechanism beyond general registered agent requirements is not detailed).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Registered agents (§ 18-104(e))**
  - Adds explicit rule: a registered agent **may not perform duties solely** through a **virtual office** and/or **mail forwarding service**.  
  - Defines **“virtual office”** as performing duties solely through the internet or other remote communication.
- **Ratification/waiver of void or voidable acts (§ 18-106(e))**
  - Applies to acts/transactions “**by or in respect of**” an LLC, including acts by a **member, manager, or other person** (individual capacity or on behalf of LLC).  
  - Allows ratification/waiver to be **express or implied**, including by **statement, action, inaction, or acquiescence**.  
  - Clarifies that required notice following ratification/waiver is **not a condition** to effectiveness.  
  - Court of Chancery may determine validity/effectiveness upon application by the LLC, a member, a manager, or a person claiming substantial adverse effect (excluding harm that would have resulted if the act had been valid when taken).
- **Cancellation corrections (§ 18-203(b))**
  - Confirms a certificate of cancellation filed before dissolution/completion of winding up may be **corrected or nullified** via certificate of correction.
- **Merger/consolidation filings (§ 18-209(c))**
  - Requires attachment: for a **consolidation** where the resulting entity is a **domestic LLC**, the **certificate of formation** must be attached to the certificate of consolidation.
  - Continues to allow service of process on the Secretary of State for certain non-Delaware surviving/resulting entities; permits **electronic transmission** service only as prescribed by the Secretary of State.
- **Certificates of correction (§ 18-211(a))**
  - Explicitly allows certificates of correction to **correct or nullify** a previously filed certificate.  
  - Effectiveness: generally effective **as of the original filing date**, except for persons “substantially and adversely affected,” for whom it is effective from the **filing date** of the correction.
- **LLC agreement amendment cross-references (§ 18-302(e), (f))**
  - Confirms amendments may be permitted “as otherwise permitted by law,” including under **§§ 18-209(f), 18-217(f), and 18-221(e)** (division and registered series merger contexts referenced in synopsis).
  - Subsection (f) continues to apply only to LLCs whose original certificate of formation was filed **on or after January 1, 2012**.
- **Annual LLC/series tax timing (§ 18-1107(c))**
  - Restates annual tax due date: **first day of June** following close of calendar year (or upon cancellation of certificate).  
  - If existence/registration will cease by filing a certificate, the **full annual tax for that calendar year is due and payable before filing**.  
  - Late tax interest: **1.5% per month (or portion)** until paid.

## Who Benefits
- Delaware LLCs, members, managers, and other stakeholders who need clearer statutory rules for **ratifying/waiving** void or voidable acts and for **correcting/nullifying** erroneous filings.  
- Parties relying on reliable service of process and communications through registered agents (registered agent operations cannot be solely virtual/mail-forwarding).  
- The State’s tax administration, by clarifying pre-filing payment of annual tax when an entity/series/registration will cease.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Registered agent businesses that operate **only** as virtual offices and/or mail-forwarding services (must change operations to comply).  
- LLCs/series/foreign LLCs planning to cease existence/registration that must pay the **full annual tax before filing** the cessation-related certificate.

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date of the Act:** **August 1, 2025**.  
- **Approval date (enacted chapter):** **June 30, 2025**.  
- **Annual tax due date (existing rule reiterated):** **first day of June** following the close of the calendar year (for domestic LLCs, registered series, and foreign LLCs).  
- **Pre-filing tax requirement:** when existence/registration **will cease by filing a certificate**, the **full annual tax for the calendar year** in which the certificate becomes effective must be paid **before the filing**.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Registered agent compliance requirement:** may not perform duties/functions **solely** through a virtual office and/or mail-forwarding service; must also satisfy Secretary of State regulations regarding **identity verification** (regulations referenced but not detailed).  
- **Tax penalty/interest:** unpaid annual tax after the due date bears interest at **1.5% per month (or portion thereof)** until fully paid.  
- **Service of process rules (merger/consolidation context):** for certain non-Delaware surviving/resulting entities, service may be made on the Secretary of State; **electronic transmission** permitted only as prescribed by the Secretary of State.
