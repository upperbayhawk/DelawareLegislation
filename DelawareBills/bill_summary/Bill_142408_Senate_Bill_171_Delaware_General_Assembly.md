## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends the Town of Houston charter to update municipal governance, elections, contracting, tax and budget procedures, enforcement authority, and borrowing powers; and to make various technical and drafting-standard corrections (including gender-neutral language and corrected Delaware Code citations).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Elections timing changes:** Moves the **annual municipal election to the last Saturday in February** (from March) with polls **3 p.m.–7 p.m.**; nomination day shifts to the **last Saturday in December** (from February), **6 p.m.–8 p.m.**, with **14 days’ notice** required.  
- **Candidate/officeholder qualifications updated:** Raises minimum age for Mayor/Town Councilmember to **21**; adds continuing disqualification if delinquent on **town taxes, utilities, or any other debt owed to the Town**; adds a **2-year lookback** bar for certain prior resignations.  
- **Annexation voting clarified for multi-owner properties:** Each property owner’s vote is proportional to ownership share, but **collective property-owner vote per property may not exceed 1 vote**; **husband and wife jointly** each have **1/2 vote**; tax-exempt or non-assessed property owners **cannot vote** in annexation special elections.  
- **Contracting rules adjusted:** Competitive bidding threshold exemption increases to **$5,000** (from $500) and keeps specified exceptions (e.g., professional services, emergency). Contracts over **$500** involving Town officials remain unlawful **unless unanimously approved**; otherwise **null and void**.  
- **Tax collection rules clarified/adjusted:** Keeps **3% abatement** for timely payment but specifies it applies to taxes paid **July 1 through August 31**; taxes unpaid after **October 1** are delinquent with **1.5% interest per month** penalty; adds allowable **collection charge up to 18%** of delinquent tax (plus interest/penalty).  
- **Enforcement authority increased:** Maximum penalty for ordinance violations increases to **$2,500** (from $500) and/or **60 days** imprisonment.  
- **Borrowing authority increased:** Revenue-anticipation borrowing increases to **$500,000** per year and **$500,000** max outstanding at any one time (from $100,000), and repayment period extends to **20 fiscal years** (from 10).  
- **Effective/approval:** Act approved **June 30, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More flexible purchasing:** Raising the competitive-bid exemption threshold to **$5,000** may reduce administrative burden for smaller purchases (Section 14(b)(1)).  
- **Clearer annexation election rules for shared ownership:** Explicit proportional voting and a **1-vote cap per property** may reduce disputes over who may vote and how votes are counted (Section 3(f)(6); Section 34(a)(5) similarly for borrowing elections).  
- **Stronger enforcement tools:** Increasing maximum ordinance fines to **$2,500** may improve compliance leverage (Section 30(a)(27)/(26)).  
- **Improved short-term financing flexibility:** Increasing revenue-anticipation borrowing limits to **$500,000** and allowing up to **20 fiscal years** for repayment can increase cash-flow capacity (Section 30(a)(42)/(41)).  
- **Transparency around budgets/audits:** Requires Town Budget posted in **2 public places** and on the Town website within **1 week** of adoption (Section 29(e)); audit report must be available to any resident/taxpayer (Section 24).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher penalties on residents/businesses:** Ordinance violations may result in fines up to **$2,500** (previously $500), increasing potential financial exposure (Section 30(a)(27)/(26)).  
- **Greater municipal debt/cash-flow exposure:** Higher borrowing limits (**$500,000**) and longer repayment window (**20 fiscal years**) could increase the Town’s long-term financial obligations (Section 30(a)(42)/(41)).  
- **Reduced competitive bidding for mid-sized purchases:** Allowing non-bid procurements up to **$5,000** may reduce market testing compared with the prior $500 threshold (Section 14(b)(1)).  
- **Candidate eligibility tightened:** Raising minimum age for Mayor/Councilmember to **21** and adding disqualifications (e.g., delinquency on utilities/other debts; certain prior resignations within **2 years**) may reduce the eligible candidate pool (Section 5).  
- **Earlier election date:** Moving the annual municipal election to the **last Saturday in February** may compress campaign/administrative timelines (Section 7(a)(1); Section 6 nomination date).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **“Other debt owed to the Town” definition:** The charter bars candidates/officeholders delinquent on “**taxes, utilities, or any other debt owed to the Town of Houston**,” but does not define what counts as “other debt” or “delinquent” (Section 5(a)(4)).  
- **Resignation disqualification scope:** The 2-year prior-resignation disqualification applies unless resignation was for specified reasons (health, family health, work, loss of residency), but the charter does not define documentation or decision process (Section 5(a)(7)).  
- **Removal of “Sabbath Day” power:** Synopsis states the act removes regulation/control of observance of the Sabbath Day; text shows renumbering and edits but may require careful comparison to confirm complete removal from the powers list (Section 30).  
- **Town Budget posting count changed:** The budget posting requirement changes from **4 public places to 2**, but the charter does not specify which locations qualify (Section 29(e)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Election calendar**
  - Annual municipal election: **last Saturday in February**, **3 p.m.–7 p.m.** (Section 7(a)(1)).  
  - Candidate nominations: **last Saturday in December** (or different date set by Town Council), **6 p.m.–8 p.m.**; notice posted at least **14 days** prior (Section 6(a)).  
  - Absentee-type voting: ballot and reasons must be **in writing and notarized**; delivered by **3 p.m.** on election day (Section 7(e)(5)).  
- **Candidate/office qualifications**
  - Mayor/Councilmember minimum age becomes **21** (Section 5(a)(2)).  
  - Must not be delinquent on **taxes, utilities, or any other Town debt** (Section 5(a)(4)).  
  - Disqualifies candidates who resigned as Mayor/Councilmember within prior **2 years** unless for specified reasons (Section 5(a)(7)).  
- **Annexation**
  - Committee report due within **90 days** of appointment (Section 3).  
  - Special election: public hearing; election held **30–60 days** after hearing (Section 3(d)(4)); polls **1 p.m.–6 p.m.** (Section 3(i)(9)).  
  - Voting for multi-owner properties: proportional voting with **1-vote cap per property**; joint spouses **1/2 vote each** (Section 3(f)(6)).  
  - If annexation vote/resolution fails, territory cannot be reconsidered for **1 year** (e.g., Sections 3(c)(3), 3(k)(11), 3(l)(12)).  
  - Recordation of annexation plot/description must be within **90 days** of special election (or resolution for exempt/non-assessed territory), though late recordation does not invalidate annexation; effectiveness is at recordation or at expiration of the **90-day** period (Sections 3(k)(11), 3(l)(12)).  
- **Contracts/procurement**
  - Competitive bidding exemption threshold increased to **$5,000** (Section 14(b)(1)).  
  - Conflicted contracts over **$500** require **unanimous vote** of all elected Town Council members or are **null and void** (Section 14(a)).  
- **Taxes**
  - **3% abatement** if paid **July 1–August 31** (Section 28(d)).  
  - After **October 1**: **1.5% interest per month** penalty; delinquency after **October 1** (Section 28(d)).  
  - Collection charge for delinquent tax may be up to **18%** of tax + interest/penalty (Section 28(d)).  
  - Optional senior exemption: Town may exempt Town property tax for residents **65+** who have applied for Kent County exemption, up to **$4,000** in assessments (Section 28(e)).  
- **Penalties**
  - Ordinance violations: maximum fine increased to **$2,500** (from $500) and/or **60 days** imprisonment (Section 30(a)(27)/(26)).  
  - Removed Alderman/Assistant Alderman failure-to-turn-over-records misdemeanor includes fines **$25–$100 per day** for noncompliance (Section 18(g)).  
- **Borrowing**
  - Revenue anticipation borrowing: up to **$500,000** in any one year and **$500,000** outstanding at one time; must be fully paid by end of **20 fiscal years** following the first borrowing fiscal year (Section 30(a)(42)/(41)).  
  - Bonded indebtedness remains capped at **25%** of assessed real property value (Sections 30(a)(36)/(35) and 34(b)).  
- **Budget posting**
  - Town Budget must be posted in **2** public places and on the Town website within **1 week** after adoption (Section 29(e)).  
- **Banking flexibility**
  - Street-appeal compensation deposits may be made in the **bank used by the Town of Houston** (replacing a specific named bank) (Section 31(b)(4)).  

## Who Benefits
- **Town government/administration:** Greater flexibility for procurement up to **$5,000** without competitive bidding (Section 14(b)(1)); increased short-term borrowing authority up to **$500,000** (Section 30(a)(42)/(41)).  
- **Voters/property owners in annexation and special elections:** Clearer multi-owner voting rules (Sections 3(f)(6) and 34(a)(5)).  
- **Residents seeking budget/audit information:** Budget must be posted and placed on website within **1 week**; audit report must be available to residents/taxpayers (Sections 29(e), 24).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Individuals cited for ordinance violations:** Exposure to higher fines up to **$2,500** (Section 30(a)(27)/(26)).  
- **Younger potential candidates (18–20):** No longer eligible for Mayor/Town Councilmember positions due to **21-year minimum** (Section 5(a)(2)).  
- **Property owners with delinquent taxes/charges:** Subject to **1.5% per month** penalty after **October 1** and possible collection charge up to **18%** (Section 28(d)).  
- **Vendors/contractors:** More Town purchases may fall below the competitive-bidding requirement due to the **$5,000** threshold (Section 14(b)(1)).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Act approval:** **Approved June 30, 2025** (Chapter 52, Volume 85).  
- **Nominations:** Last Saturday in **December** (or other date set by Town Council), **6 p.m.–8 p.m.**; notice posted **≥ 14 days** before (Section 6(a)).  
- **Annual municipal election:** Last Saturday in **February**, polls **3 p.m.–7 p.m.** (Section 7(a)(1)).  
- **Absentee submission deadline:** Delivered to Election Inspector **no later than 3 p.m. on election day** (Section 7(e)(5)).  
- **Annexation committee report:** Within **90 days** of committee appointment (Section 3).  
- **Annexation election timing:** Special election held **30–60 days** after public hearing (Section 3(d)(4)); annexation election polls **1 p.m.–6 p.m.** (Section 3(i)(9)).  
- **Annexation recordation:** Must be completed within **90 days** after special election (or resolution), though annexation still becomes effective at recordation or at expiration of the **90-day** period if not recorded (Sections 3(k)(11), 3(l)(12)).  
- **Tax collection start:** Town Tax Collector to proceed **on or before July 1** (Section 28(a)).  
- **Tax discount window:** **July 1–August 31** for **3% abatement** (Section 28(d)).  
- **Tax delinquency:** Unpaid after **October 1** is delinquent; **1.5% per month** penalty begins for taxes paid after **October 1** (Section 28(d)).  
- **Budget adoption/posting:** Mayor presents draft at regular **May** meeting; Council adopts final budget at regular **June** meeting; must post within **1 week** after adoption (Section 29(b), 29(e)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Ordinance violations:** Punishable by fine up to **$2,500** and/or imprisonment up to **60 days** (Section 30(a)(27)/(26)).  
- **Tax penalties:**  
  - **1.5% interest per month** after **October 1** on unpaid taxes (Section 28(d)).  
  - Collection charge up to **18%** of delinquent tax plus interest/penalty (Section 28(d)).  
- **Tax sale redemption:** After tax sale, deed not prepared until **1 year** after sale; redemption requires paying purchase price + costs + **20% interest** plus deed-preparation expense (Section 28(g)(6)).  
- **Conflicts of interest in contracts:** Any contract over **$500** with Town officials requires **unanimous** vote of all elected Town Council members or is **absolutely null and void** (Section 14(a)).  
- **Alderman/Assistant Alderman turnover:** Upon removal, must deliver books/papers within **2 days** and pay over moneys within **5 days**; violation is a **misdemeanor** with **$25–$100 per day** fine for each day of noncompliance (Section 18(g)).  
- **Claims against Town:** Written notice of claim required within **90 days** of injury/damage; must be sent to Mayor by **certified mail, return receipt requested** (Section 35).
