## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Adopts the **Social Work Licensure Compact** in Delaware (new **Title 24, Chapter 34**) to **facilitate interstate practice** of regulated social workers, **increase public access** to competent social work services, reduce duplicative multi-state licensing burdens, support telehealth, and preserve state authority to protect public health and safety. (§ 3401)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Enacts the **Social Work Licensure Compact** into Delaware law and joins Delaware to the multistate compact framework. (§ 3400)
- Creates/joins the **Social Work Licensure Compact Commission**, with **1 Delaware delegate**, and requires Delaware participation in the Commission’s **data system** and information-sharing. (§§ 3403(b)(2), 3410(b))
- Authorizes a **Multistate License** (issued by a social worker’s **home state**) allowing practice in **all compact member states** via a “Multistate Authorization to Practice.” (§§ 3402(18)-(19), 3405)
- Sets multistate eligibility requirements, including: **fingerprints/biometrics for FBI and state criminal history checks**, continuing competence, and compliance with the **remote state’s laws where the client is located**. (§ 3404(a))
- Adds category-specific requirements:
  - **Clinical**: master’s degree + supervised practice (**minimum 3,000 hours** or **2 years full-time**), plus clinical competency pathway. (§ 3404(b))
  - **Master’s** and **Bachelor’s**: degree + national exam/alternative competency pathways. (§ 3404(c)-(d))
- Establishes discipline/enforcement framework: **remote states** can restrict practice privilege in their state; **home state** controls adverse action against the **Multistate License**; encumbrances can **deactivate multistate privileges across states**. (§§ 3404(f)-(h), 3409)
- Provides special rules for **military members and spouses** to retain home state designation during active duty. (§ 3408)
- Compact effective date is tied to adoption by the **7th member state** (already met per synopsis); synopsis notes Delaware applications **“could start in late 2025.”** (§ 3414(a); Synopsis)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded access to services**: the stated purpose is to “increase public access to social work services,” including via **telehealth**. (§ 3401(1), (9))
- **Reduced duplicative licensing**: aims to reduce “overly burdensome and duplicative requirements” tied to holding multiple licenses. (§ 3401(2))
- **Workforce mobility / shortages**: explicitly intends to “promote mobility and address workforce shortages” through mutual recognition across member states. (§ 3401(5))
- **Stronger cross-state disciplinary visibility**: requires participation in a Commission **data system** and sharing of **adverse action** and certain investigative information among states. (§§ 3403(b)(2)-(3), 3411)
- **Support for military families**: allows active-duty members/spouses to keep a designated home state while on active duty. (§ 3408)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Added compliance steps for applicants**: multistate applicants must submit **fingerprints/biometric data** for **FBI and state** criminal history checks. (§ 3404(a)(3))
- **New reporting duty with deadline**: licensees must report adverse actions/encumbrances/restrictions from any state **within 30 days**. (§ 3404(a)(4))
- **Potential for multi-state practice disruption**: if a Multistate License is **encumbered**, the authorization to practice is **deactivated in all remote states** until cleared. (§ 3404(g); § 3409(g))
- **Ongoing state obligations to the Commission**: Delaware must fully participate in the data system, designate a delegate, and comply with Commission rules that have force of law unless conflicting with state practice-law standards as determined by a court. (§§ 3403(b)(2), (5), (8); 3412(b))
- **Possible fees/assessments**: Commission may levy **annual assessments** on member states and **fees on licensees** to fund operations (amounts not specified). (§ 3410(g)(3))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Delaware-specific fee amounts**: Delaware “may charge a fee” for granting the Multistate License, but no dollar values are provided. (§ 3403(d))
- **Commission assessments/fees**: Commission may levy assessments and licensee fees, but the **formula and amounts** are to be set by Commission rule. (§ 3410(g)(3))
- **Timing for Delaware implementation**: the bill ties compact operation to the 7th member state enactment; synopsis says applications “could start in late 2025,” but Delaware’s operational start date is not fixed in the statutory text. (§ 3414(a); Synopsis)
- **Definition thresholds set by Commission rule**: key concepts (e.g., “minor infraction,” “immediate threat”) are partly “as may be defined by the Commission.” (§ 3402(6))

## Health Impact
- States the compact’s purpose includes protecting **public health and safety** while facilitating access and telehealth. (§ 3401; § 3404(f); § 3409; § 3412(l))

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds a new **Title 24, Chapter 34** adopting the Social Work Licensure Compact. (§ 3400)
- Establishes a system for **Multistate Licenses** (bachelor’s, master’s, clinical) issued by the **home state** and recognized by all member states. (§§ 3402(18), 3405)
- Requires **criminal background check submissions** (fingerprints/biometrics) for multistate applicants. (§ 3404(a)(3); § 3403(b)(4))
- Imposes a **30-day reporting requirement** for licensees to report adverse actions/encumbrances/restrictions taken by any state. (§ 3404(a)(4))
- Confirms the **remote state’s authority** over practice in that state and allows remote states to remove the privilege to practice, impose fines, and take protective actions under due process. (§ 3404(f))
- Establishes Commission governance, rulemaking, financing (assessments/fees), and a multi-state **data system** for licensure and discipline information. (§§ 3410-3412; § 3411)

## Who Benefits
- **Delaware-licensed social workers** seeking to practice in other compact states without obtaining multiple single-state licenses. (§ 3401(2), (5); § 3405)
- **Delaware residents/clients** who may gain increased access to social work services, including via **telehealth** and broader provider availability. (§ 3401(1), (9))
- **Military members and spouses** who can retain a home state designation during active duty. (§ 3408)
- **State regulators** through structured cross-state disciplinary data sharing and coordinated enforcement mechanisms. (§§ 3411; 3409)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Applicants/licensees** who must meet added multistate compliance requirements (fingerprints/biometrics; reporting within 30 days; compliance with remote-state laws). (§ 3404(a)(3)-(6))
- **Licensees with disciplinary encumbrances**: an encumbered Multistate License can result in **deactivation of practice privileges across all remote states**. (§ 3404(g); § 3409(g))
- **Delaware licensing authority** may incur added administrative workload (data system participation, reporting, delegate participation, background-check processes). (§ 3403(b); § 3411)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Compact effective date (general)**: takes effect when the compact statute is enacted into law in the **7th member state**. (§ 3414(a))
- **Rule effective date**: Commission rules bind member states **the day after adoption** or later date specified; generally not sooner than **30 days** after notice of adoption/amendment (except emergency rules). (§ 3412(c), (k)(3))
- **Licensee reporting deadline**: report adverse action/encumbrance/restriction taken by any state **within 30 days** of the action. (§ 3404(a)(4))
- **Delegate vacancy deadline**: must be filled within **60 days** of the vacancy. (§ 3410(b)(5))
- **State withdrawal effective date**: **180 days** after enactment of repealing statute. (§ 3414(b)(1))
- **Post-termination recognition period**: if a state is terminated, it must continue recognizing compact licenses for at least **6 months** after notice of termination. (§ 3413(f))
- **Post-withdrawal recognition period**: withdrawing state must recognize compact licenses for at least **180 days** after notice of withdrawal. (§ 3414(b)(3))
- **Synopsis implementation note**: applications “could start in **late 2025**.” (Synopsis) *(This timing is not a statutory deadline in the compact text.)*

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Remote state enforcement**: may remove a social worker’s multistate authorization to practice in that state for a period of time, **impose fines**, and take other actions to protect health and safety, consistent with due process and its laws. (§ 3404(f))
- **Home state discipline authority**: only the **home state** can take adverse action against a Multistate License; home-state adverse action deactivates multistate privileges in all other member states until encumbrances removed. (§ 3409(a)(2), (g))
- **Subpoena enforcement across states**: subpoenas issued in one member state for witnesses/evidence in another must be enforced by courts of competent jurisdiction; issuing authority pays witness fees/travel/mileage as required by the state where the witness/evidence is located. (§ 3409(a)(1))
- **Cost recovery**: a member state may recover from the regulated social worker the **costs of investigations and case dispositions** resulting from adverse action, if permitted by state law. (§ 3409(d))
- **State default/termination**: Commission can issue notice of default, provide technical assistance, and ultimately **terminate** a noncompliant state by majority vote of delegates; terminated state remains responsible for obligations incurred through termination. (§ 3413(b)-(f))
- **Commission legal enforcement**: Commission may sue a defaulting state in federal court (D.C. or where Commission principal offices are located) seeking injunctive relief/damages; **prevailing party awarded costs and reasonable attorneys’ fees**. (§ 3413(j)(1))
- **Member state obligations** include data reporting, background-check procedures, and compliance with Commission rules. (§ 3403(b); § 3411)
