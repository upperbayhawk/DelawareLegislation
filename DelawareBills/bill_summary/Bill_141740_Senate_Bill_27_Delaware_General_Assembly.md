## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Establish the **Office of New Americans** within Delaware’s **Department of State** to improve the lives and economic prosperity of immigrants (“new Americans”) and Delawareans generally, including by coordinating immigration-related information, policy analysis, and language-access strategies. (Title 29, new Subchapter I-A)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Creates the Office of New Americans** in the **Department of State** to address immigration-related inquiries, coordination, and policy support.  
- Requires the **Secretary of State to appoint a Director** who **serves at the Secretary’s pleasure** and is **exempt from the classified/state service** under Title 29, Chapter 59.  
- Assigns the Office duties including: responding to inquiries; serving as an information clearinghouse; analyzing economic/demographic trends; disseminating information on services, naturalization pathways, English instruction; and developing strategies for **meaningful access regardless of English proficiency**.  
- Requires coordination “as necessary” with the **Office of Immigration Assistance** in the **Department of Justice**.  
- Establishes an **Advisory Committee** housed in and administered by the Office, with specified agency leaders and **six Governor-appointed members** (including language access, healthcare access, refugee resettlement, higher education, and public representation).  
- The Advisory Committee must hold **at least 1 public hearing each year** and may conduct studies and issue reports.  
- Fiscal note projects **FY2027** costs of **$239,359 (operating/recurring)** plus **$30,000 (one-time)**, with recurring costs rising to **$244,197 (FY2028)** and **$249,132 (FY2029)**; includes **OECs at 33.41%** and **2% inflation** in FY2028–FY2029.  
- Effective **upon signature by the Governor** (per fiscal note assumption).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Centralized coordination and expertise:** Creates a dedicated Office to respond to inquiries and act as an immigration-policy clearinghouse for state agencies. (§ 8754(1)-(2))  
- **Improved access to state resources:** Requires strategies to provide immigrants “meaningful access… regardless of their level of English proficiency.” (§ 8754(7))  
- **Better-informed policymaking:** Office must analyze trends and make policy recommendations, and advise executive and legislative bodies on impacts of proposed legislation on immigrants. (§ 8754(3), (9))  
- **Structured stakeholder input:** Advisory Committee includes multiple state agencies and community expertise, and must hold **at least one public hearing annually**. (§ 8756; § 8758(c))  
- **Potential economic integration support:** Office must disseminate information to immigrants wishing to invest, launch businesses, or apply skills in Delaware. (§ 8754(4))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **New recurring and one-time costs:** Fiscal note estimates **$239,359 recurring + $30,000 one-time in FY2027**, increasing to **$244,197 (FY2028)** and **$249,132 (FY2029)**.  
- **Leadership position is at-will and exempt from classified service:** Director serves at the Secretary’s pleasure and is exempt from classified/state service protections. (§ 8753(b))  
- **Administrative workload expansion:** Office must staff/administer the Advisory Committee and support at least one public hearing each year. (§ 8755(b); § 8758(c))  
- **Duties broad, performance measures limited:** While the Office must “evaluate the success of activities and adjust,” the bill does not specify metrics or reporting requirements. (§ 8754(8))
