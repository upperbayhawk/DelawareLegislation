## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 15 of the Delaware Code to move the date of primary elections (for statewide, county, and municipal offices) to the **fourth Tuesday in April** and to adjust related candidate filing/withdrawal, filing-fee notice, candidate qualification timing, criminal history background-check timing, and the voter party-switch “closed period” to align with the new primary schedule.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Moves primary elections for all political parties from the **second Tuesday after the first Monday in September** to the **fourth Tuesday in April**.  
- Moves the candidate **notification of candidacy** deadline from **12:00 noon on the second Tuesday in July** to **12:00 noon on the fourth Tuesday in February**.  
- Moves the candidate **withdrawal** deadline from **4:30 p.m. on the first Friday after the second Tuesday in July** to **4:30 p.m. on the first Friday after the fourth Tuesday in February** (with holiday/weekend adjustment retained).  
- Moves political party notice of filing-fee amounts to the State Election Commissioner and Department from **July 1** to **February 1** of each general election year.  
- Requires statewide/General Assembly/elected county candidates to request a **criminal history background check** **no earlier than the first day they may file** and **no later than the filing deadline**, and to provide proof it was requested **before** giving notice of candidacy.  
- Moves the Commissioner’s deadline to determine candidate constitutional qualification (Art. II, § 21) from **August 1** to **March 15** of each general election year.  
- Changes the party-affiliation change blackout period to **“the 59 days before the day of the primary election”** (replacing the prior general-election-year window tied to late May through the primary).  
- Applies to **all primary elections after December 31, 2026**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Alignment with presidential primary timing:** The bill states the new date (fourth Tuesday in April) matches the presidential primary date in presidential election years, potentially consolidating election administration timing.  
- **Earlier candidate-vetting timeline:** Moving the constitutional qualification determination deadline to **March 15** and tying background-check request timing to the filing window creates earlier verification steps before April primaries.  
- **Clear, uniform party-switch blackout rule:** Replacing a calendar-date window with a consistent **59-day-before-primary** standard creates a rule that automatically adjusts with the primary date.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Compressed general-election campaign period after primaries:** Moving primaries from September to April materially increases the time between primary and general election (the bill changes the date; consequences are not addressed in the text).  
- **Earlier deadlines for candidates and parties:** Candidates must file by **late February** and parties must report filing fees by **February 1**, shifting compliance and campaign planning earlier in the year.  
- **Party affiliation change restriction tied to earlier primary:** Because the blackout is “**59 days before the day of the primary election**,” an April primary moves the restriction earlier in the year than under a September primary.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Primary election date change (15 Del. C. § 3101(3)):** From the **second Tuesday after the first Monday in September** to the **fourth Tuesday in April**.  
- **Notification of candidacy deadline (15 Del. C. § 3101(1)):** From **12:00 noon, second Tuesday in July** to **12:00 noon, fourth Tuesday in February**.  
- **Withdrawal deadline (15 Del. C. § 3101(2)):** From **4:30 p.m., first Friday after the second Tuesday in July** to **4:30 p.m., first Friday after the fourth Tuesday in February**, with the same rule when that Friday is a legal holiday (move to next non-Sat/Sun/legal holiday).  
- **Filing-fee notice deadline (15 Del. C. § 3103(c)):** From **July 1** to **February 1** of each general election year.  
- **Criminal history background check timing (15 Del. C. § 3106(g)):** Candidates for statewide office, the General Assembly, or any elected county office must request a check **no earlier than the first filing date** and **no later than the filing deadline**, and must provide proof the check was requested **before** giving notice under § 3106(a).  
- **Candidate constitutional qualification determination deadline (15 Del. C. § 3106(g)(5)):** From **August 1** to **March 15** of each general election year.  
- **Party affiliation change blackout period (15 Del. C. § 2049(a)):** Replaced with a prohibition **“during the 59 days before the day of the primary election.”**  
- **Exception to blackout for prospective candidates (15 Del. C. § 2049(c)):** Conforming change replaces “through September 1” with “**59 days before the day of the primary election**,” while retaining affidavit requirements from party chairs and the individual (plus withdrawal form for other candidacies).

## Who Benefits
- **Election administrators (State Election Commissioner/Department):** Earlier and aligned deadlines for primaries, filing fees, candidate qualification determinations, and background-check requests.  
- **Voters and candidates seeking predictable rules:** A uniform **59-day** party-switch restriction tied directly to the primary date.  
- **Political parties:** A primary schedule synchronized with the presidential primary date (as stated in the synopsis).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Candidates needing more time to decide/run:** Earlier filing and withdrawal deadlines (February rather than July) reduce decision time.  
- **Voters who want to change party affiliation close to the primary:** The **59-day-before-primary** blackout applies and, with an April primary, restricts changes earlier in the year.

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Applicability:** “**Applicable to all primary elections after December 31, 2026**.” (Section 5)  
- **New recurring statutory deadlines (general election years):**
  - Filing-fee notification due **February 1**.  
  - Candidate notification of candidacy due **12:00 noon on the fourth Tuesday in February**.  
  - Candidate withdrawal due **4:30 p.m. on the first Friday after the fourth Tuesday in February** (or next eligible day if holiday/weekend rule applies).  
  - Commissioner qualification determination due **March 15**.  
  - Primary election held **fourth Tuesday in April**.  
- **Party affiliation change restriction:** No party-affiliation change permitted **during the 59 days before the day of the primary election** (with the existing candidate-related affidavit exception).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Candidate compliance (background checks):** Covered candidates must **request** a criminal history background check within the allowed window and must **provide proof of the request before** giving notice of candidacy under § 3106(a).  
- **Ballot access condition (qualification determination):** The Commissioner must determine by **March 15** that a candidate is qualified under **Article II, § 21 of the Delaware Constitution** before the candidate’s name may be listed on a primary ballot.  
- **Party compliance (filing-fee notice):** Party chairs must notify the State Election Commissioner and Department of filing-fee amounts by **February 1**.  
- **Voter registration processing:** Party-affiliation changes received during the prohibited period must be **held** and processed when the period reopens (retained rule in § 2049(b)).
