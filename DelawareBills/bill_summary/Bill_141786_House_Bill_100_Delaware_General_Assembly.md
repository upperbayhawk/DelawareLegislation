## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
House Bill 100 is Delaware’s FY2026 appropriations act, authorizing payment of state government expenses for the fiscal year ending **June 30, 2026**, and setting procedures, conditions, and limitations on spending (including pay/benefit rules, education salary formulas, procurement posting/aggregation, workforce reporting, DelDOT carryforward controls, and health data/claims database conditions). Unpaid and unencumbered appropriations generally **revert to the General Fund** (with an encumbrance exception under **29 Del. C. § 6521**).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Makes FY2026 line-item appropriations across the **Legislative, Judicial, and Executive** branches, including major agencies (e.g., **DHSS, DSCYF, DOC, DNREC, DelDOT, DOE**) and higher education (UD/DSU/DTCC).  
- Requires appropriations **not paid out by the last day of June 2026** to **revert to the General Fund**, except amounts properly **encumbered under 29 Del. C. § 6521**.  
- Provides covered employee compensation changes, including a **2.0%** general salary increase for covered employees in departments **01–11** and **DTCC Plan B** effective the **first day of the first full pay cycle of FY2026**, with exclusions (including **29 Del. C. § 5903(17)**) and non-retroactive delays for unsatisfactory performance.  
- Updates education compensation statutes in **14 Del. C. c. 13**, including raising **§ 1305(b)** base salary from **$34,266 to $36,760**, setting school lunch salary schedule cost shares (**73% / 62%** State share depending on subsection) with remaining costs paid from **local funds**, and providing paraprofessional supplements (**$1,000 / $2,000**).  
- Sets statewide benefit and payroll assumptions, including **Workers’ Compensation payroll recovery rate 1.40%** (unless an MOA applies) and a family caregiving leave contribution rate of **0.08% through 12/31/2025**, changing to **0.04% employee / 0.04% employer** starting **1/1/2026**; deferred compensation employer contributions remain suspended (since **7/1/2008**).  
- Imposes administrative/oversight requirements: **procurement bid posting** on a single public site and **quarterly procurement coordination/aggregation through OMB** (when conditions are met); **OMB workforce reports** to JFC due **Jan. 15, July 1, and Jan. 1**; **DelDOT quarterly** overtime reports; and **DHR exempt-position comparability list due on or before Aug. 15**.  
- Adds health-system data conditions: requires benchmark work to use **DHIN claims data “to the fullest extent practicable,”** and conditions certain **claims database appropriation (08912)** spending on DHIN deliverables; DHSS may require insurer claims submission under **16 Del. C. § 10312**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- Funds core state services and programs for FY2026 with detailed line-item appropriations (e.g., **DHSS Medicaid $1,079,935.4**; DOE **Public School Transportation $186,129.2**; DOE **Mental Health Services $72,722.2**).  
- Provides compensation increases and structured pay administration tools (e.g., **2.0%** general increase for covered groups; **Selective Market Variations** pay range **75%–125%** of midpoint with minimum/“+5%” adjustment rule after approval).  
- Raises statutory education compensation baselines and supplements (e.g., **§ 1305(b) base salary to $36,760**; paraprofessional supplements **$1,000/$2,000**; service Step 1 must be **≥85%** of instructional Step 1).  
- Adds transparency and oversight through recurring reporting and posting requirements (e.g., procurement posting; workforce reports due **Jan. 15 / July 1 / Jan. 1**; DelDOT overtime reporting **quarterly**; DHR comparability list due **Aug. 15**).  
- Conditions health claims database spending on defined DHIN deliverables, and requires disclosure when non-DHIN benchmark data is used (subject to confidentiality/proprietary limits), supporting accountability for cost benchmark work.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Requires local funding for portions of school lunch salary schedules: State share set at **73%** (for **§ 1322(a),(b)**) and **62%** (for **§ 1322(c)**), with remaining costs required from **local funds**, plus payroll charging compliance limits.  
- The **2.0%** general increase is **not retroactive** when delayed for unsatisfactory performance, and certain employees are excluded (including those covered by **29 Del. C. § 5903(17)**).  
- Deferred compensation employer contribution remains suspended (noted as suspended since **July 1, 2008**).  
- Adds administrative burden and potential process delays via additional reporting/approval gates (procurement coordination, workforce reporting, claims database deliverables, DelDOT carryforward approvals).  
- Notes reference sizable contingency/other statewide appropriations (e.g., **Salary/OEC Contingency $149,213.4**) without narrative explanation in the provided source notes.

## Key Policy Changes
- FY2026 appropriations and staffing authorizations across state government, with reversion of unpaid/unencumbered funds by end of FY (**June 2026**), except **29 Del. C. § 6521** encumbrances.  
- Compensation/benefits parameters: **2.0%** general salary increase for covered groups; **Workers’ Comp 1.40%** payroll recovery rate (absent MOA); family caregiving leave rate change on **1/1/2026** to **0.04% employee / 0.04% employer**; deferred compensation employer contribution remains suspended since **7/1/2008**.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- Spending must comply with this Act and referenced state budget controls (including **Title 29, Part VI** as cited in notes).  
- **Reversion rule:** appropriations not paid by end of **June 2026** must revert to the General Fund unless **encumbered under 29 Del. C. § 6521**.  
- Pay/benefit compliance: agencies must apply the **2.0%** increase rules (including exclusions and performance-based delay), implement pay plan/SMV approvals, and apply required benefit rates (Workers’ Comp **1.40%**; caregiving leave rate change **1/1/2026**).

- Procurement compliance: bid notices must be posted on a **single public-access site** for required duration; quarterly procurement coordination/aggregation through OMB required when conditions are met.  
- Reporting compliance: DHR comparability list by **Aug. 15**; OMB workforce reports by **Jan. 15 / July 1 / Jan. 1**; DelDOT overtime reports **quarterly**; Health Fund recommendations by **Nov. 15**; Innovation Fund report by **May 1**.
