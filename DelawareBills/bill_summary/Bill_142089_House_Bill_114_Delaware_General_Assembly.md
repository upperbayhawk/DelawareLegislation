## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To legalize and regulate **low speed motorized scooters** in Delaware by defining them, exempting them from the existing scooter/skateboard operating ban, and establishing operating, equipment, labeling, age, and helmet rules—while keeping higher-speed scooters prohibited under existing law.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Defines “**low speed motorized scooter**” as a motorized scooter that **cannot travel more than 15 miles per hour (mph)**.  
- Exempts **low speed motorized scooters** from the general prohibition in **21 Del. C. § 4198N** that otherwise bans operating motorized skateboards/scooters on **public highways, streets, sidewalks, or rights-of-way** (except under listed conditions).  
- Creates new **21 Del. C. § 4198Q**, generally treating low speed scooter operators like **bicyclists** (rights/duties), with specific exceptions.  
- Exempts low speed scooters from requirements related to **insurance/financial responsibility, driver’s licenses, registration, title, off-highway vehicle provisions, and license plates**.  
- Requires **manufacturer/distributor labeling** on and after **January 1, 2026**: permanently affixed label with **classification number, top assisted speed, motor power**, printed in **Arial, at least 9-point**.  
- Prohibits riding low speed scooters on **sidewalks** (including sidewalks where bicycles are permitted) and allows local/state agencies to prohibit use on certain paths/trails after specified procedures/criteria.  
- Requires **helmet use** for **all operators and passengers under age 18** (approved, properly fitted, fastened), by reference to **§ 4198K**.  
- Prohibits low speed scooters from being operated as part of a **rental service** and sets a minimum operator age of **14**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)

- **Safety-related consumer information:** The required **permanent label** (classification number, top assisted speed, motor power) beginning **1/1/2026** provides standardized information on the device.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Definition change/addition (Title 21 § 101):**  
  - Adds/sets “Low speed motorized scooter” as a motorized scooter that cannot travel more than **15 mph**.
- **Exemption from general scooter/skateboard operation prohibition (Title 21 § 4198N):**  
  - States § 4198N **does not apply** to any **low speed motorized scooter**.  
  - Retains penalties in § 4198N for covered violations:  
    - **1st offense:** **$25–$115** fine.  
    - **2nd/subsequent within 24 months:** **$57.50–$230** fine; device **may be forfeited** by court.
- **Creates new operating framework for low speed scooters (new § 4198Q):**  
  - **Bicycle-equivalency rule:** Rights/duties generally same as bicycles unless otherwise specified.  
  - **Exempts from** insurance/financial responsibility, licensing, registration, title, off-highway vehicle rules, license plates.  
  - **Labeling mandate (effective 1/1/2026):** permanent label; prominent location; includes **classification number, top assisted speed, motor power**; **Arial ≥ 9-point**.  
  - **Tampering/modification rule:** speed capability/engagement changes allowed only if required label is **replaced after modification**.  
  - **Product safety compliance:** must comply with **U.S. CPSC 16 C.F.R. Part 1505** (electrically operated toys/other electrically operated articles).  
  - **Where allowed:** anywhere bicycles are allowed (streets, highways, shoulders, bicycle lanes, bikeways, bicycle/multi-use paths), **except**:
    1) Path restrictions allowed by local/state authorities **after notice and public hearing** based on safety/legal compliance findings;  
    2) Trails designated **nonmotorized** with **natural surface tread** (cleared/graded native soil, no added surfacing) may prohibit;  
    3) **Sidewalks** (even if bicycles are permitted).  
  - **Helmet requirement:** under **18** must wear approved helmet.  
  - **No rental service operation.**  
  - **Minimum operator age:** must be at least **14**.

## Who Benefits
- **Individuals age 14+** who want to operate a scooter that qualifies as **≤ 15 mph**, because the bill authorizes use under a bicycle-like framework.  
- **Manufacturers/distributors** of compliant low speed scooters (≤ 15 mph) who can sell products with a defined legal category, subject to labeling requirements.  
- **Road/path users and local governments** seeking clearer rules (helmet rule for minors; ability to restrict use on certain paths/trails under defined conditions).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected

- **Manufacturers/distributors**: must meet labeling requirements starting **January 1, 2026**, and ensure compliance with **16 C.F.R. Part 1505**.
