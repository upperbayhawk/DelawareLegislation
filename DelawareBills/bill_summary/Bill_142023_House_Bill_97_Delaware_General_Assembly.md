## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Titles 14 and 31 of the Delaware Code to update the statewide educator professional standards system—covering licensure/certification/permits, professional development, evaluation, discipline, reporting, confidentiality, and due process—while strengthening student-safety credentialing for unsupervised student contact; creating a **no-tuition Special Institute for Teacher Licensure and Certification** with service/repayment obligations; revising state-funded salary supplement and mentoring stipend rules; expanding DOE reimbursement authority tied to credential noncompliance (including vendor accountability and limits on charging employees); increasing penalties for knowingly false background-check/criminal-history information; and standardizing education HR/payroll position codes.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Credentialing for unsupervised student contact:** Prohibits unsupervised work with students in public schools without a valid Title 14 license/certificate/permit or an approved Title 24 professional license; limited substitute exemption for service **< 12 consecutive weeks** in one school.  
- **Permit framework expanded:** Requires paraprofessionals and certain student support/classroom personnel to hold a Title 14 permit (or certain Title 24 licenses); permits may be valid **up to 5 years** (intern permits generally **not renewable**, but may be **extended** to complete an internship).  
- **Discipline and due process:** Establishes/clarifies denial and discipline procedures for **licenses, certificates, and permits**, including notice and hearing rights; in denial hearings, the **applicant bears the burden of proof** (§1217(d)).  
- **Temporary suspensions pending hearing:** (1) “Automatic” temporary suspension effective on the **date of arrest or grand jury indictment** for **violent felonies (11 Del. C. §4201(c))** or **any felony crime against a child**; and (2) a “**clear and immediate danger**” process with **≥72 hours** notice and expedited hearing timelines (generally **≤60 days** order duration).  
- **Mandatory reporting deadlines:** Credential holders must report specified events within **20 days** (certain convictions/arrests/indictments) and **30 days** (surrender/discipline in any jurisdiction); chief school officers/heads of school must report certain separations within **15 days**, and DOE must notify the credential holder within **15 days** after receiving the leader report.  
- **DOE reimbursement for credential noncompliance:** DOE may seek reimbursement from districts/charters for defined payroll costs tied to credential noncompliance, including intentional retention of uncredentialed staff **>90 calendar days** after hire (reimbursement limited to post-day-90 and not earlier than **30 days** after proper notice), and certain **expired-credential** situations after DOE notice; DOE-delay exception applies; appeals go to the **State Board of Education** (final).  
- **Special Institute (no tuition):** Establishes a **no-tuition** pathway for eligible bachelor’s degree holders in DOE-designated **critical needs areas**, with completion via student teaching or **1 year** supervised full-time teaching and a service obligation (**≥1 year teaching per year funded within 5 years**, or evidence of unsuccessful applications in **≥5** districts) or repayment equal to tuition that would have been charged.  
- **Criminal-history enforcement:** Knowingly false/incomplete/inaccurate background-check-related information (or knowingly violating §309) is a **Class G felony** (Title 31 §311(c)); **Superior Court** has exclusive jurisdiction.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Student safety strengthened:** Unsupervised-contact credential requirement; expedited temporary suspension authority for arrest/indictment involving **violent felonies** or **felony crimes against a child**, and for “clear and immediate danger” allegations (with defined timelines).  
- **Clearer compliance expectations:** Standardized permit/credential requirements across more roles; explicit reporting deadlines (**15/20/30 days**).  
- **Transparency of outcomes:** Investigations/reports are confidential, but **final orders are public**.  
- **Pipeline support:** **No-tuition** Special Institute targets DOE-designated **critical needs areas**, with defined completion and service/repayment terms.  
- **Employee protection on cost shifting:** Districts/charters generally may not charge employees for DOE reimbursement amounts except where the employee intentionally provided false information or failed to provide required documentation (as summarized from §1313).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative burden increases:** Expanded permitting, credential monitoring, reporting, and rule-implementation duties for DOE, districts/charters, and covered personnel.  
- **Staffing flexibility reduced:** Substitute teachers exempt from the unsupervised-contact credential rule only if serving **<12 consecutive weeks** in one school.  
- **Employment impacts before a hearing:** Temporary suspension can occur **without a prior hearing** in arrest/indictment cases and under the “clear and immediate danger” process (with short timelines such as **10 days** to request an expedited hearing).  
- **Financial exposure for districts/charters:** Potential DOE reimbursement if they intentionally retain uncredentialed staff **>90 days** or use staff with **expired credentials** after DOE notice (subject to notice limits and exceptions).  
- **Repayment risk for participants:** Special Institute participants may owe repayment equal to tuition that would have been charged if service/application conditions are not met.  
- **Confidentiality limits public visibility during investigations:** Certain reports/investigative records are confidential and not FOIA public records (while final orders are public).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Unsupervised-contact rule:** No unsupervised work with students without a valid Title 14 credential/permit or approved Title 24 license; substitute exemption only if **<12 consecutive weeks** in one school.  
- **Approved Title 24 license definition:** Enumerates covered professional licensing boards (e.g., OT; mental health; psychology; social work; SLP/audiology excluding hearing aid dispensers; PT/athletic training).  
- **Permits:** Creates/updates multiple permit categories; permits valid **up to 5 years**; **intern permits not renewable** but may be **extended** to complete internship (details largely by regulation).  
- **Quarterly reporting:** Professional Standards Board reports due **Jan 1 / Apr 1 / Jul 1 / Oct 1**.  
- **Denials:** Written notice and hearing right; **applicant bears burden of proof** (§1217(d)).  
- **Discipline:** Applies to licenses/certificates/permits; sanctions include reprimand, limitation, suspension (up to **5 years**), revocation; mandatory revocation for specified convictions/acts (including sexual offense against a child, per notes).  
- **Temporary suspension—arrest/indictment:** Effective on **date of arrest or grand jury indictment** for **violent felonies** or **any felony crime against a child**; expedited hearing option (request **within 20 days**; hearing **within 90 days** of Board receipt).  
- **Temporary suspension—clear and immediate danger:** **≥72 hours** notice; generally **≤60 days**; expedited hearing request **within 10 days**, hearing **within 15 days**, decision **within 20 days** (as summarized).  
- **Mandatory reporting:** Credential holder **20-day** and **30-day** reporting; school leader separation reporting **within 15 days**; DOE notice to credential holder **within 15 days** of receiving leader report.  
- **Salary supplements:** State-funded, **subject to annual appropriation**; minimum **$750**; maximum **unclear ($1,500 vs $5,000)**; limited to qualifying “academic” additional responsibilities (by regulation).  
- **Mentoring stipends:** Retired educator mentors eligible for same stipend as nonretired mentors; treated as “casual employee” for pension purposes (as summarized).  
- **DOE reimbursement (§1313):** Reimbursement may be sought for defined payroll costs tied to credential noncompliance, including intentional retention **>90 days** and certain expired-credential situations after DOE notice; reimbursement limited to post-**90 days** and **≥30 days** after proper notice; DOE-delay exception; employer appeal to State Board (final); employers generally cannot charge employees except for intentional falsity/documentation failure.  
- **Vendor/contractor accountability:** Knowing noncompliance may be referred for **business license revocation** consideration.  
- **Title 31 felony:** Knowingly false/incomplete/inaccurate background-check-related information (or knowingly violating §309) is a **Class G felony**; **Superior Court** exclusive jurisdiction.  
- **Position codes:** DOE/DHR-PHRST guidance by **end of FY 2026**; mandatory standardized codes by **start of 2026–2027 school year**.

- **Unsupervised-contact compliance:** Required Title 14 credential/permit or approved Title 24 license for unsupervised student contact, with stated exemption for certain short-term substitutes.  
- **Denial enforcement:** DOE must provide written denial notice and hearing opportunity; **applicant bears burden** by preponderance of evidence (as summarized).  
- **Discipline enforcement:** Secretary may reprimand, limit, suspend (up to **5 years**), revoke credentials/permits; mandatory revocation for specified convictions/acts (including sexual offense against a child, per notes).  
- **Temporary suspensions:**  
  - Arrest/indictment-based suspension without prior hearing for **violent felonies** or **felony crimes against a child**, effective on arrest/indictment date; expedited hearing rights with stated deadlines.  
  - “Clear and immediate danger” suspension with **≥72 hours** notice and expedited hearing/decision deadlines; generally **≤60 days** duration (as summarized).  
- **Mandatory reporting compliance:** Failure by credential holders (20/30-day deadlines) or school leaders (15-day separation reporting) is grounds for discipline, including against school leaders’ licenses (as summarized).  
- **Confidentiality/public access:** Certain reports and investigative information are confidential and not FOIA public records; **final orders are public** (as summarized).  
- **DOE reimbursement enforcement (§1313):** DOE may seek reimbursement under defined conditions; reimbursement timing constrained (post-**90 days**; **30+ days** after proper notice); DOE-delay exception; appeal to **State Board of Education** (final); employers generally barred from charging employees except for intentional falsity or missing documentation (as summarized).  
- **Vendor/contractor compliance:** Knowing noncompliance may be referred for possible **business license revocation**.
