## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the Town of Millsboro charter to (1) change how owner-initiated annexation petitions are processed and how the annexation committee is appointed, and (2) change how Town Council committees are appointed by requiring majority advice and consent and clarifying members’ ability to recommend committees and appointments.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires an annexation petition signed by **all property owners** to be **submitted to Town Hall** and **reviewed by Town staff for completeness** before it is presented to the Town Council.  
- Specifies the petition must include: **territory description**, **reasons for annexation**, and **requested zoning designation**.  
- Changes annexation committee appointment authority: the **Mayor and Town Council (by Resolution)** must appoint the committee (previously implied as solely the Mayor).  
- Requires the annexation committee to be composed of **at least 3 Town Council members**.  
- Keeps a firm deadline: the committee must submit a written report **no later than 90 days** after appointment.  
- Requires a **public hearing** (set by a second resolution) before a third resolution annexes the territory and assigns zoning.  
- Annexation resolution must pass by **affirmative vote of a majority of all Town Council members**.  
- For other Town committees, requires the Council President to appoint committees **with the advice and consent of a majority** of the Town Council; clarifies all Council members may recommend creation and membership/chair appointments and such recommendations must be **reasonably considered**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved completeness screening:** Staff review for completeness before Council consideration may reduce incomplete or missing-information petitions (petition must include description, reasons, and zoning designation).  
- **Shared governance for annexation review:** Requiring appointment **by the Mayor and Town Council by Resolution** increases Council involvement and formalizes the process.  
- **Predictable timeline:** The committee report is due **within 90 days** of appointment, which can create clearer expectations for petitioners and the Town.  
- **More inclusive committee-appointment process:** Committee appointments by the Council President now require **majority advice and consent**, and Council members have explicit authority to make recommendations.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Added procedural step before Council review:** Staff completeness determination is required before the petition reaches the Town Council, which could add an administrative gate to the process (no timeframe for staff review is specified).  
- **Potential for longer internal decision-making:** Requiring Council involvement in committee appointment (and majority advice/consent for other committees) may add steps compared with single-officer appointment.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Annexation petition intake:** Petition signed by **all property owners** must be submitted to **Town Hall**; must include **description**, **reasons**, and **zoning designation**; must be deemed complete by **Town staff** before being presented to **Town Council**.  
- **Annexation committee appointment:** **Mayor and Town Council** appoint the annexation committee **by Resolution**; committee must include **≥ 3 Town Council members**.  
- **Annexation committee deadline:** Written report due **within 90 days** of appointment; must address advantages/disadvantages to Town and territory, recommend whether to proceed, reasons, and zoning recommendation.  
- **Annexation approvals:** If advantageous, Council may set a public hearing by second resolution; after the hearing, Council may annex and set zoning by third resolution passed by **majority of all Town Council members**.  
- **Other committee appointments (Town governance):** Council President appoints committees **with advice and consent of a majority** of Town Council; all Council members may recommend creation of committees and appointments/chairs, and those recommendations must be **reasonably considered**.

## Who Benefits

- **Town Council members:** increased formal role in annexation committee appointment and explicit right to recommend committee creation and appointments.  
- **Town of Millsboro governance:** more structured committee appointment and annexation review procedures.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
