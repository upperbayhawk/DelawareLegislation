## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Create a special Delaware motor vehicle license plate for World War II veterans and establish eligibility, fees, issuance limits, plate numbering, and return requirements after a plateholder’s death.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds a new law section, **21 Del. C. § 2139H**, establishing **special “WWII” license plates** for eligible World War II veterans.
- Allows the **owner of an eligible vehicle** to apply to the Department (Division of Motor Vehicles) for assignment of a **special World War II veteran registration number** if the owner has **official documentation** showing WWII veteran status.
- Limits eligible vehicles to **(1) private passenger vehicles** and **(2) trucks with a three-fourth ton or less manufacturer’s rated capacity**.
- Requires **U.S. Department of Defense certification** that the applicant is a WWII veteran as a condition for issuance.
- Sets **no additional fee beyond the annual registration fee**, except a **$10 administrative fee** for an **original application**.
- Directs the **$10 administrative fee** into a **special fund** used by DMV **to administer the program and fund Department projects**, notwithstanding certain Title 2 provisions.
- Limits issuance to **only 1 plate per applicant** and requires DMV to reserve enough plates labeled **“WWII”**, **numbered consecutively beginning with 1**.
- After being informed the plateholder has died, DMV must request plate return from the estate representative **within 90 days**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Recognition of WWII veterans:** Establishes a dedicated **“WWII”** plate and registration number for verified WWII veterans (Synopsis; §2139H(a), (e)).
- **Low incremental cost to applicants:** No fee beyond normal annual registration, aside from a **one-time $10 administrative fee** on original application (§2139H(c)).
- **Program administration funding:** The **$10 fee** is earmarked to a **special fund** for DMV to administer the section and fund Department projects (§2139H(c)).
- **Clear eligibility limits:** Restricts to specific vehicle types (private passenger; trucks **≤ three-fourth ton**) (§2139H(a)(2)) and requires **Department of Defense certification** (§2139H(b)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Added applicant burden:** Requires **official documentation** and **Department of Defense certification** to prove eligibility (§2139H(a)(1), (b)).
- **Limited availability per person:** **Only 1 plate** may be issued per applicant, which may restrict multi-vehicle owners (§2139H(d)).
- **Administrative workload for DMV and estates:** DMV must manage plate reservation/numbering and must send return letters to estate representatives and track the **90-day** return window (§2139H(e)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates new §2139H** in Title 21, Chapter 21 establishing **special World War II veteran license plates**.
- **Eligibility and proof:**
  - Applicant must be a vehicle owner and possess **official documentation** indicating WWII veteran status (§2139H(a)(1)).
  - **U.S. Department of Defense certification** is required for issuance (§2139H(b)).
- **Vehicle eligibility:**
  - **Private passenger vehicle**, or
  - **Truck with three-fourth ton or less manufacturer’s rated capacity** (§2139H(a)(2)).
- **Fees:**
  - No fee beyond annual registration fee.
  - **$10 administrative fee** for an **original application**; deposited into a **special fund** for DMV administration and Department projects (§2139H(c)).
- **Issuance limit:** **Only 1 plate** per applicant (§2139H(d)).
- **Plate design/numbering and return:**
  - Plates must include designation **“WWII”** and be **numbered consecutively starting with 1** (§2139H(e)).
  - After notice of death, DMV must request return from the estate representative **within 90 days** (§2139H(e)).

## Who Benefits
- **World War II veterans** who own an eligible vehicle and can obtain Department of Defense certification (§2139H(a), (b)).
- **Delaware DMV/Department** through a dedicated **$10 per original application** administrative funding source (§2139H(c)).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Applicants** who cannot readily obtain **Department of Defense certification** or required documentation (§2139H(a)(1), (b)).
- **Estates/representatives** of deceased plateholders who must return the plate after DMV’s request within **90 days** (§2139H(e)).
