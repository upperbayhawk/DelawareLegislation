## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 14 (local school taxes) to (1) require county property tax exemption amounts to be updated by ordinance to reflect changes in property value and inflation, (2) require New Castle County school tax exemptions to match New Castle County’s county-tax eligibility criteria and exemption amounts as of **July 1, 2025**, and (3) set implementation steps for **2025–2026** tax bills in districts that issue a new tax warrant under **House Bill 242 (153rd GA)**, including a payment-deadline extension and refunds of overpayments.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires counties, after a **county-wide reassessment under 9 Del. C. § 8306(b)**, to modify exemption amounts by **subsequent county ordinance** to “reasonably reflect changes in property value and inflation.”  
- For **school tax exemption determinations**, **New Castle County** must use the **same eligibility criteria and exemption amount** used for **county property tax exemptions** as required **as of July 1, 2025** (based on county ordinances in effect on that date).  
- Implementation trigger: **On enactment of this Act and upon enactment of HB 242**, in any district that issues a **new tax warrant** under HB 242, **New Castle County** must supplement/adjust already-issued **2025–2026** tax bills to apply the expanded school tax exemption.  
- For any bill adjusted under the Act, New Castle County must extend the **2025–2026** payment deadline to **October 30, 2025**.  
- New Castle County must **refund any overpayment** in school taxes to taxpayers who paid based on a bill that was later adjusted under the Act.  
- Fiscal Note estimates potential reduction in local school revenue in New Castle County of **$11,497,652.60 per year** for **FY 2026, FY 2027, and FY 2028**.  
- District-level estimated revenue losses (FY2026) include: **Christina $3,370,920.23**, **Red Clay $2,800,217.83**, **Brandywine $1,978,096.52**, **Colonial $1,589,343.98**, **Appoquinimink $1,029,069.08** (plus smaller amounts for others).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Standardization/consistency (New Castle County):** Requires school tax exemptions to match county exemption eligibility criteria and amounts as of **July 1, 2025**, reducing differences between county-tax and school-tax exemption treatment (bill text §1902(a)(3)b).  
- **Automatic updating mechanism after reassessment:** Requires exemption amounts to be modified by ordinance to “reasonably reflect” **property value and inflation** following a **county-wide reassessment** (bill text §1902(a)(3)a).  
- **Taxpayer protection for mid-year billing changes:** Requires adjusted/supplemental bills, an extended deadline to **October 30, 2025**, and **refunds of overpayments** for affected taxpayers (Section 2).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced local school revenues (New Castle County):** Fiscal Note estimates potential local school revenue loss of **$11,497,652.60** annually (FY **2026–2028**), tied to applying larger senior exemptions for school taxes consistent with county exemptions.  
- **Administrative burden for re-billing/refunds:** If HB 242 is enacted and a district issues a new tax warrant, New Castle County must **supplement/adjust** issued bills, **extend deadlines**, and **process refunds** (Section 2), increasing administrative workload.  
- **Potential funding pressure on districts:** The Fiscal Note provides district-by-district revenue loss estimates (e.g., Christina **$3.37M**, Red Clay **$2.80M**), which could constrain locally raised school funding (Fiscal Note).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Definition of “reasonably reflect changes in property value and inflation”:** The bill requires updates to exemption amounts but does not define the methodology, index, or calculation standard.  
- **Scope of “eligibility criteria and exemption amount” snapshot:** The statute references what was required **as of July 1, 2025** (bill text) while the synopsis references **June 1, 2025**; the operative bill text states **July 1, 2025**.  
- **Applicability outside New Castle County:** The bill specifies New Castle County requirements for school tax exemption determinations, but does not spell out parallel school-tax conformity rules for Kent or Sussex beyond the general county ordinance updating language.  
- **Interaction details with HB 242:** Section 2 only applies “upon enactment of HB 242” and in districts issuing a “new tax warrant” under HB 242; the bill text here does not explain HB 242’s mechanics or which districts will qualify.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Title 14 §1902 exemption updating requirement:** After a county-wide reassessment under **9 Del. C. § 8306(b)**, a county must, by **subsequent county ordinance**, modify exemption amounts to “reasonably reflect changes in property value and inflation.”  
- **New Castle County school-tax exemption conformity (as-of date):** For school tax exemption determinations, New Castle County must use the **same eligibility criteria and exemption amount** used for county property taxes **as required as of July 1, 2025** (based on county ordinances in effect that date).
