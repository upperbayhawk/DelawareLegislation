## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Require Delaware public utilities to provide advance notice before (1) relocating a call center outside the utility’s service territory or (2) contracting call center work outside the utility’s service territory, with a limited disaster-related exemption.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates statutory definitions for **“call center”** and **“disaster”** in Title 26.  
- Requires a public utility to give **at least 120 days’ notice** before relocating a call center outside its **service territory** or contracting call center work outside its **service territory**.  
- Notice must be provided **in the manner and form determined by the Public Service Commission (PSC/Commission)**.  
- Requires notice to the **Commission** of the utility’s intent.  
- Requires notice to **all current and prospective call center employees**.  
- Requires notice to the **leadership of any labor unions** in which a current or prospective employee is a member.  
- Provides an exemption from the notice requirement when contracting work outside the service territory **due to a “disaster”** (as defined in **Title 20, Chapter 31**).  

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved transparency and planning:** The **120-day** advance notice requirement gives the Commission, employees, and unions time to prepare for call center relocation/outsourcing decisions.  
- **Workforce communication:** Mandated notification to **current and prospective employees** and **union leadership** formalizes early communication about potential job-location or outsourcing changes.  
- **Regulatory oversight support:** Required notification to the **PSC** may enhance the Commission’s awareness of service-related operational changes affecting customer assistance channels.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative burden on utilities:** Utilities must provide multiple notices (Commission, employees, unions) and comply with Commission-prescribed **manner and form**, at least **120 days** in advance.  
- **Reduced operational flexibility:** The **120-day** lead time may slow or constrain a utility’s ability to quickly relocate or outsource call center functions, except when the disaster exemption applies (for contracting).  
- **Limited exemption scope:** The exemption applies only when the utility **contracts call center work outside the service territory due to a disaster**; the text does not provide a parallel exemption for **relocating** a call center due to a disaster.

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds definition: **“Call center”** = a facility/operation where a public utility receives **incoming telephone calls, emails, or other electronic communication** to provide customer assistance or service.  
- Adds definition: **“Disaster”** = as defined in **Title 20, Chapter 31**.  
- Creates new **§ 226. Call Centers** requiring **≥ 120 days** advance notice before:  
  - **Relocating a call center outside** the utility’s service territory; or  
  - **Contracting call center work outside** the utility’s service territory.  
- Specifies three required notices (Commission, employees, unions) to be provided **in the manner and form determined by the Commission**.  
- Establishes an exemption from notice requirements when a utility **contracts call center work outside the service territory due to a disaster**.

## Who Benefits
- **Current call center employees** of public utilities (advance notice of relocation/outsourcing plans).  
- **Prospective call center employees** (as referenced in the text) who receive notice of intended changes.  
- **Labor unions and union leadership** representing affected employees (required notice).  
- **Public Service Commission** (formal notice of intended relocation/outsourcing actions).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected

- **Third-party call center contractors** outside a utility’s service territory (may face delayed contracting timelines due to notice lead time, except in disaster contracting situations).
