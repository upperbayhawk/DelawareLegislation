## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend 11 Del. C. § 4217 to change the Department of Correction’s role in issuing regulations for the sentence modification process by making regulations optional rather than mandatory, while prohibiting the Department from adding extra limitations on eligibility.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes 11 Del. C. § 4217(h) so the Department of Correction **may** (instead of **shall**) adopt regulations to implement the sentence modification process.
- Explicitly provides the Department **may not impose additional restrictions, exclusions, or eligibility requirements** on people seeking sentence modification under § 4217.
- The bill is framed as a technical adjustment to the Richard “Mouse” Smith Compassionate Release Act (Senate Substitute No. 1 for Senate Bill No. 10), which revised sentence modification procedures.
- The stated reason is that the Department of Correction “does not believe regulations are necessary” to successfully implement the revised process.
- Maintains legislative control over eligibility by limiting the Department’s ability to narrow access through regulation or policy.
- No specific funding, staffing, or implementation timelines are included in the bill text provided.
- The section heading references “**[Effective Jan. 10, 2026]**” in § 4217, but this bill text does not separately state its own effective date.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces mandatory rulemaking burden:** By changing “shall” to “may,” the Department is not required to undertake a formal regulatory process to implement § 4217. (Text: “The Department shall may adopt regulations…”)
