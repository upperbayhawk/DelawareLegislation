## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 25 to remove contradictory statutory language about the Delaware Manufactured Home Relocation Authority (DMHRA) Board’s authority to adjust the cap on the Delaware Manufactured Home Relocation Trust Fund (Trust Fund), including eliminating the fixed **$15 million** cap and clarifying the Board vote required to approve certain actions.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Removes the fixed statutory cap stating the Trust Fund “**is $15 million**” and instead allows the DMHRA Board to set and adjust the cap based on need.  
- Explicitly authorizes the DMHRA Board to **adjust, eliminate, or reinstate** the **cap on the Trust Fund**, in addition to payments and assessments.  
- Requires an **affirmative vote of at least 3 of the 5 Board members** to approve actions regarding payments, assessments, and the fund cap.  
- Addresses a constitutional constraint noted in the bill synopsis: a statutory dollar cap cannot be changed by the DMHRA (as a non-legislative entity), so the bill removes the dollar amount that caused the conflict.  
- Notes that the Trust Fund is the **only money available** to DMHRA to pay administrative costs and carry out responsibilities under **Chapters 70 and 71** (except appropriations for board meeting stipends).  
- Cites that DMHRA has **additional responsibilities** under **Subchapter VI of Chapter 70** related to community owner rent increase matters, without receiving appropriations to support that work.  
- Characterizes DMHRA as needing to remain **“fiscally revenue-neutral”** and responsible for operational costs under § 7042.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clarifies legal authority and removes internal contradiction:** Eliminates the conflicting provision that set a fixed **$15 million** cap while also claiming the Board could change it.  
- **Improves administrative flexibility:** Allows the Board to adjust the Trust Fund cap “**based on the costs reasonably necessary**” to carry out Trust Fund purposes (§ 7042(e) as amended).  
- **Supports DMHRA operational funding needs:** The synopsis states the Trust Fund is the only funding source for DMHRA administrative costs and responsibilities under **Chapters 70 and 71**, which may help DMHRA cover costs as duties expand.  
- **Adds procedural clarity:** Consolidates and clarifies that at least **3 of 5** Board members must approve covered actions (§ 7041(c)(3)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Removes an explicit numeric limit:** Eliminating the statutory **$15 million** cap removes a clear, legislatively-set upper bound from the statute text.  
- **Greater reliance on Board discretion:** The cap can be “**adjusted, eliminated, or reinstated by the Board at any time**” (subject to vote requirements), which may reduce predictability compared to a fixed cap.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **No new cap-setting methodology beyond general standard:** The bill states the cap is based on “costs reasonably necessary,” but does not specify a formula, reporting requirements, or external review process.  
- **No details on how cap adjustments affect assessments or stakeholders:** The bill clarifies authority but does not specify how changes to the cap would practically change assessment levels, collection, or disbursement timing.  
- **Interaction with existing sunset:** The Trust Fund section is labeled as expiring **July 1, 2029** (per § 7042 header reference), but the bill does not discuss whether any changes are needed because of that expiration.

## Key Policy Changes
- **25 Del. C. § 7041(c)(3):**  
  - Clarifies that with an affirmative vote of **at least 3 of the 5 Board members**, the Board may authorize:  
    1) **Payments**;  
    2) **Adjust, eliminate, or reinstate** the **Trust Fund assessment**; and  
    3) **Adjust, eliminate, or reinstate** the **cap on the Trust Fund**.
- **25 Del. C. § 7042(e):**  
  - Removes the sentence establishing that the cap “**is $15 million**.”  
  - Provides that the Board may establish and adjust the cap “**based on the costs reasonably necessary**” to carry out Trust Fund purposes, and that the cap may be “**adjusted, eliminated, or reinstated**” subject to the § 7041(c)(3) vote requirement.
