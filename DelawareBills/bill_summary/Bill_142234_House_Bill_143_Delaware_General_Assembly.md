## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s CPA licensure and interstate practice privilege rules in Title 24 to align with an updated approach to the Uniform Accountancy Act, including updated definitions, multiple education/experience pathways to a Delaware permit, and revised “practice privileges” (automatic mobility) standards for out-of-state CPAs.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates the definition of **“substantial equivalency”** and specifies that the Board must consider qualifications **without regard to the sequence** in which education, exam, and experience were completed.  
- Revises Delaware CPA **permit-to-practice** eligibility to recognize **three education pathways** (including a **baccalaureate-only** pathway) and ties required experience to the education path chosen.  
- Maintains the rule that an applicant may not sit for the CPA exam until completing at least **120 semester hours** including a **baccalaureate or higher degree** (with accounting concentration or equivalent).  
- Changes experience requirements: **1 year** of experience for applicants using certain education pathways, and **2 years** for the baccalaureate-only pathway.  
- Updates interstate **practice privileges** for out-of-state CPAs: in addition to a “substantial equivalency” presumption, the bill adds explicit minimums (degree, exam, and **1 year** of experience).  
- Provides a transition rule for certain out-of-state CPAs from **non-substantially equivalent** states who **held a license and had Delaware practice privileges as of December 31, 2024**, allowing continued privileges subject to specified conditions.  
- States that individuals practicing under the interstate privilege section **must be granted practice privileges** and **no notice or other submission** is required.  
- **Effective date:** **July 1, 2026**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More licensure pathways:** Adds/recognizes a **baccalaureate degree** pathway for a Delaware permit (with specified experience), potentially expanding eligibility for candidates who do not have 150 semester hours or a post-baccalaureate degree. (24 Del. C. § 107(c)(3), § 107(f))  
- **Clearer experience expectations by pathway:** Specifies **1 year** vs **2 years** of experience depending on the education route. (24 Del. C. § 107(f))  
- **Facilitates cross-state practice (automatic mobility):** Provides that out-of-state CPAs covered by § 109 are granted practice privileges **without notice or submission**, including when services are provided electronically. (24 Del. C. § 109(a)(1) and (a)(2))  
- **Flexibility on sequencing:** Allows the Board to evaluate “substantial equivalency” **without regard to the sequence** of education/exam/experience, potentially reducing technical barriers. (24 Del. C. § 102(23))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher experience requirement for baccalaureate-only applicants:** Requires **2 years** of experience if applying under the **baccalaureate-only** education pathway, compared to **1 year** for other pathways. (24 Del. C. § 107(f))  
- **Additional conditions for out-of-state automatic mobility (as written):** Even when a state is “substantially equivalent,” § 109(a)(1) adds explicit requirements (baccalaureate degree, exam passage, **1 year** of experience) to receive Delaware practice privileges. (24 Del. C. § 109(a)(1))  
- **Reduced state visibility into out-of-state practice:** The bill states **no notice or other submission** is required for practice privileges under § 109, which may limit Delaware’s advance awareness of who is practicing under mobility. (24 Del. C. § 109(a)(1)–(2))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **“Removes reference” vs text changes:** The synopsis says the bill “removes reference to Substantial Equivalency,” but the bill text continues to define and use **“substantial equivalency.”** (Synopsis; 24 Del. C. § 102(23); § 109)  
- **Meaning of “as of December 31, 2024, had practice privileges”:** The criteria for demonstrating that an individual “had practice privileges in this State under this section” on **December 31, 2024** is not elaborated in the bill text. (24 Del. C. § 109(a)(2))  
- **Board rule details not in bill:** Several standards depend on “accounting concentration or equivalent as determined by Board rule” and Board-prescribed supervision requirements; the bill does not include those rule specifics. (24 Del. C. § 107(c); § 107(f); § 109(a)(1))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Definition update (Substantial equivalency):** Requires the Board (or designee) to evaluate comparability and to consider qualifications **without regard to sequence** of education, exam, and experience. (§ 102(23))  
- **Delaware permit requirements (education options):** Clarifies/establishes three education routes, including **baccalaureate-only**. (§ 107(c)(1)–(3))  
- **CPA exam sitting requirement:** Applicant cannot sit until at least **120 semester hours** and a **baccalaureate or higher degree**, plus accounting concentration/equivalent. (§ 107(d))  
- **Experience requirements tied to education path:**
  - **1 year** experience if applying under § 107(c)(1) or § 107(c)(2)  
  - **2 years** experience if applying under § 107(c)(3)  
  - Experience must involve accounting/attest/compilation/advisory/tax/consulting skills and be supervised by a **U.S. CPA**, and may be gained in government, industry, academia, or public practice. (§ 107(f))  
- **Practice privileges—substantially equivalent states:** Presumption of substantial equivalency when verified by NASBA NQAS, but now conditioned on having:
  - **Baccalaureate or higher degree** (with accounting concentration/equivalent)  
  - **Passed the Uniform CPA Exam** (or successor)  
  - **1 year** of experience of the type described in § 107(f)  
  - No notice/submission requirement; applies to in-person, mail, telephone, or electronic services. (§ 109(a)(1))  
- **Practice privileges—non-substantially equivalent states (transition rule):** Individuals whose state is not verified as substantially equivalent, but who **as of December 31, 2024** both (i) held a valid CPA license and (ii) had Delaware practice privileges under this section, may continue privileges under the conditions stated, including potential **individual** substantial equivalency verification; and a possible education exemption for certain pre-2012 licensees. No notice/submission required. (§ 109(a)(2))

## Who Benefits
- Delaware CPA applicants who qualify under the **baccalaureate-only** education pathway (with 2 years’ experience). (§ 107(c)(3), § 107(f))  
- Out-of-state CPAs practicing across state lines under § 109 who can obtain Delaware practice privileges **without filing notice or submissions**. (§ 109(a)(1)–(2))  
- Out-of-state CPAs meeting the **December 31, 2024** transition criteria in § 109(a)(2).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Delaware permit applicants relying only on a baccalaureate degree who must meet a **2-year** experience requirement (higher than the **1-year** requirement in other pathways). (§ 107(f))  
- Regulators/administrators may have reduced advance information about who is practicing under mobility because **no notice or other submission** is required. (§ 109(a)(1)–(2))

## Effective Dates and Deadlines

- Key dates used in eligibility/transition provisions:

  - **January 1, 2012:** licenses issued prior to this date may qualify for an education exemption for purposes of § 109(a)(2).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
