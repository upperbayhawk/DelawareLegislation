## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Delaware income tax law (Title 30, §1106) to increase the amount of a “United States military pension” that can be excluded (subtracted) from Delaware taxable income, ultimately raising the military pension exclusion to **$25,000** for **all** military pensioners.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases the Delaware subtraction (exclusion) for **United States military pensions** from **$12,500** to **$25,000** through scheduled increases.
- Applies the increased military pension exclusion **regardless of age** by the time the phase-in is complete.
- For tax years beginning **before January 1, 2027**, the military pension exclusion remains **up to $12,500** (with special treatment for under-60 taxpayers).
- For the taxable year beginning **January 1, 2027**, the military pension exclusion increases to **$15,000** (with “greater of” rules for both under-60 and 60+ taxpayers).
- For the taxable year beginning **January 1, 2028**, the military pension exclusion increases to **$20,000**.
- For taxable years beginning **on or after January 1, 2029**, the military pension exclusion increases to **$25,000**.
- Does not change the general (non-military) pension exclusion thresholds of **$2,000** (under 60) and **$12,500** (60 or older) as stated in the amended section.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Tax relief for military retirees:** Allows a larger portion of military pension income to be subtracted from federal adjusted gross income for Delaware income tax purposes (increasing from **$12,500** to **$25,000** by **tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2029**).
- **Expanded benefit to age 60+ military pensioners:** The “greater of” structure for ages **60 or older** starting **January 1, 2027** allows choosing between **$12,500** (general pension/eligible retirement income) and the higher military pension exclusion (**$15,000** in 2027; **$20,000** in 2028; **$25,000** in 2029+).
- **Predictable phase-in schedule:** The bill specifies fixed annual thresholds (**$15,000**, **$20,000**, **$25,000**) tied to specific taxable years.

## Key Policy Changes
- Amends **30 Del. C. §1106(b)(3)** to increase the military pension subtraction as follows:
  - **Taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2022, and ending before January 1, 2027**
    - **Under age 60:** the greater of
      - up to **$2,000** (general pension), **or**
      - up to **$12,500** (United States military pension)
    - **Age 60 or older:** up to **$12,500** (general pension/eligible retirement income)
  - **Taxable year beginning on January 1, 2027**
    - **Under age 60:** greater of up to **$2,000** (general pension) or up to **$15,000** (military pension)
    - **Age 60 or older:** greater of up to **$12,500** (general/eligible retirement income) or up to **$15,000** (military pension)
  - **Taxable year beginning on January 1, 2028**
    - **Under age 60:** greater of up to **$2,000** or up to **$20,000** (military pension)
    - **Age 60 or older:** greater of up to **$12,500** or up to **$20,000** (military pension)
  - **Taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2029**
    - **Under age 60:** greater of up to **$2,000** or up to **$25,000** (military pension)
    - **Age 60 or older:** greater of up to **$12,500** or up to **$25,000** (military pension)
- Retains/recodifies the definition of **“United States military pension”** to include pensions resulting from service in:
  - **Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, Coast Guard**
  - **Commissioned corps of NOAA**
  - **Commissioned corps of the Public Health Service**
  - **National Guard**
