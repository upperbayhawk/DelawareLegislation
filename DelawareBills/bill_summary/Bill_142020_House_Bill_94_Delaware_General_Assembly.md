## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware law to restrict state/local law enforcement cooperation with federal immigration enforcement activities occurring **at schools or churches** unless **pre-authorized by the Delaware Attorney General**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits **sheriffs, deputies, constables** (Title 10) from cooperating with federal immigration enforcement activity **at schools or churches** without **Delaware Attorney General authorization**.  
- Prohibits **police officers** (Title 11, as defined by **11 Del. C. § 8401**) from the same cooperation absent **Attorney General approval**.  
- Requires authorization requests to be made by the officer’s **supervising authority**, not the individual officer.  
- Requires the request be **in writing** and submitted **at least 72 hours prior** to the activity date.  
- Requires the request to include: the **federal enforcement agency name**, **reason**, **dates and duration**, and a **detailed scope**.  
- Scope details must include: **purpose**, **records sought**, **names of individuals who may be impacted**, and **how the activity will be carried out**.  
- The bill text does not describe exceptions (e.g., emergencies) or penalties for noncompliance.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- Creates a clear **state-level oversight checkpoint** (Attorney General authorization) before cooperation with federal immigration enforcement at **schools or churches**.  
- Standardizes documentation by requiring specific information (including **records sought** and **individuals who may be impacted**) in a **written request**.  
- Establishes a **lead time requirement** (**72 hours**) that may reduce ad hoc or informal cooperation arrangements at these locations.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Could **delay** cooperation requests because they must be submitted **at least 72 hours** in advance, which may impede time-sensitive activities at schools or churches.  
- Adds administrative steps for agencies (supervisory submission; required content list), potentially increasing workload.  
- The bill provides restrictions only for activities **at schools or churches**, which may create uneven rules across other locations (no broader framework is provided in the text).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Title 10 (new/modified § 2115):**  
  - A **sheriff, deputy, or constable** may not cooperate with federal immigration enforcement activity **at schools or churches** unless the **Delaware Attorney General** first authorizes the specific cooperation.  
  - Authorization request must be submitted by the **supervising authority** and must be:

  - Applies parallel restrictions to **police officers** (as defined in **11 Del. C. § 8401**), requiring **Attorney General approval** and the same **written, 72-hour, detailed-content** request process.
