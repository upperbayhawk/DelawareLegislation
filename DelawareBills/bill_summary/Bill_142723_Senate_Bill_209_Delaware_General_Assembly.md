## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the City of Seaford municipal charter provisions related to the Seaford Police Department to (1) explicitly add and define the Chief of Police role, (2) clarify the organization, oversight, powers, and duties of the department and its members, and (3) remove an existing charter-level misdemeanor penalty for officers who neglect or refuse to perform required duties.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Establishes/clarifies in the Seaford Charter that the **Mayor appoints a Chief of Police**, **with advice and consent of City Council**.
- Specifies the **Chief of Police reports directly to the City Manager** and is subject to **state employment laws** and any **employment agreement**.
- Grants the Chief of Police authority to **set departmental directives/rules/policies** and to **appoint, assign, promote, suspend, or dismiss** department members, subject to applicable laws and Seaford personnel policies.
- Clarifies that Seaford police have powers within city limits and, **within 1 mile outside city limits**, have powers/authority under **Delaware Code Titles 11 & 21**; in **hot pursuit**, authority extends **anywhere in Delaware**.
- States that, as allowed by state and local law, department members may have police powers **outside Seaford’s corporate limits**.
- Requires police officers to submit a **consolidated arrests report monthly (or more often if the Mayor deems desirable)**.
- Removes a charter provision making an officer’s neglect/refusal of duties a **misdemeanor** with **$25–$500** fine, possible **imprisonment up to 1 year**, and **automatic forfeiture of office** upon conviction.
- Notes the bill requires a **two-thirds vote of each chamber** to pass because it amends a municipal charter (Delaware Constitution, Article IX, § 1).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clearer governance and accountability**: Explicitly defines appointment process and reporting line (Chief reports to **City Manager**), potentially reducing ambiguity in command structure.
- **Operational clarity and flexibility**: Express authority for the Chief to issue directives and manage personnel actions (appoint/assign/promote/suspend/dismiss) **subject to applicable laws and personnel policies**.
- **Clarified jurisdictional scope**: Restates and clarifies authority **within city limits**, **within 1 mile outside**, and statewide authority in **hot pursuit**, aligning charter language with policing realities and referenced state code powers (Titles **11 & 21**).
- **Eliminates duplicative or outdated criminal penalty in the charter**: Removing the charter misdemeanor may avoid conflict with modern employment law frameworks or other disciplinary mechanisms (the bill text expressly removes the charter penalty).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced charter-level deterrent for dereliction of duty**: Removing the explicit misdemeanor penalty eliminates a specific charter criminal consequence (**$25–$500 fine**, **up to 1 year imprisonment**, **automatic forfeiture**) for neglect/refusal of duty.
- **Expanded/affirmed policing reach beyond city limits**: The charter text affirms authority **within 1 mile outside city limits** and statewide in **hot pursuit**, which may raise concerns for some residents about extraterritorial enforcement (as described in the amended charter text).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Chief of Police created/explicitly added to the charter** and defined as head of the Seaford Police Department.
- **Appointment and oversight**: Mayor appoints Chief **with City Council advice and consent**; Chief **reports directly to City Manager**.
- **Chief’s powers**:
  - May **establish directives, rules, regulations, and policies** to operate the department.
  - May **appoint, assign, promote, suspend, or dismiss** members, subject to applicable laws and Seaford personnel policies.
- **Police powers/jurisdiction clarified**:
  - Police powers within Seaford limits; within **1 mile outside** city limits, vested with powers/authority under **Delaware Code Titles 11 & 21**.
  - In **hot pursuit**, authority extends to **any part of Delaware**.
  - Additional statement that, as allowed by state and local law, members may have police powers outside city limits.
- **Reporting requirement**: Officers must submit a consolidated report at least **monthly** (or more often if the Mayor deems desirable) including at minimum **number of arrests** in the preceding month.
- **Penalty removal**: Deletes charter misdemeanor provision for neglect/refusal of duty that previously allowed:
  - Misdemeanor charge,
  - Presentment to Sussex County Grand Jury,
  - **Fine: not less than $25 and not more than $500**,
  - Possible imprisonment up to **1 year**,
  - **Automatic forfeiture of office** upon conviction.
