## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Require Delaware school boards to provide a way for the public to **remotely attend** school board meetings and **provide public comment** remotely.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **Title 14** to mandate remote public access and remote public comment for school board meetings.
- Adds the same requirement to two separate code sections: **14 Del. C. § 504B(c)** and **14 Del. C. § 1048(f)**.
- Applies to school board meetings covered by the “Open meetings” provision (§ 504B) and “Regular and special meetings” provision (§ 1048).
- The bill requires **a method** for remote viewing/attendance and remote comment, but does not specify the technology or platform.
- Does not state minimum technical standards (e.g., recording, streaming quality, accessibility features).
- Does not specify enforcement mechanisms or penalties for noncompliance in the bill text.
- **Effective date: September 2, 2025** (also the approval date shown in the chapter law).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)

- **Uniform statewide expectation:** Places the requirement in two Title 14 sections, reinforcing consistent application across school board meeting contexts. (Based on amendments to § 504B and § 1048.)
