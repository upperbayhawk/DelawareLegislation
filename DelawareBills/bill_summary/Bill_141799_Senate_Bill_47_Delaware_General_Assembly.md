## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 1 of the Delaware Code to make a federal National Day of Mourning declared due to the death of a current or former President of the United States a **legal holiday in Delaware**, requiring **closure of State government offices**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Establishes a new provision in **Title 1, Chapter 6** creating a **State legal holiday** tied to a **federal National Day of Mourning** for a deceased current or former U.S. President.  
- Requires that **State government offices must be closed** on that National Day of Mourning.  
- Treats the day as a **legal holiday** for Delaware purposes.  
- Creates potential **holiday/overtime pay obligations** for applicable state employees working in **24/7 facilities/operations**.  
- Fiscal note estimates the **General Fund overtime cost** for a legal holiday at **$843,224** (based on January 2025 State holiday data; includes “other employment costs”).  
- Total fiscal impact is **indeterminable** because the timing/frequency of such National Days of Mourning is **unknown**.  
- Fiscal impact is listed as **Indeterminable** for **FY 2026, FY 2027, and FY 2028**.
