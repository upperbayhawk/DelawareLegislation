## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29, § 7907A of the Delaware Code to change how often the Distressed Cemetery Fund must be audited and to update the auditor office name.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes the audit requirement for the Distressed Cemetery Fund from **annual** audits to audits **at least once every 5 years**.  
- Replaces the reference to the **“State Auditor’s Office”** with the updated name **“Office of Auditor of Accounts.”**  
- Applies specifically to **29 Del. C. § 7907A(c)** (Distressed Cemetery Fund audit provision).  
- Originates from the **Joint Legislative Oversight and Sunset Committee** review of the **Delaware Cemetery Board** (per synopsis).  
- Establishes a clear minimum audit frequency threshold: **no less often than once every 5 years**.  
- **Approved August 21, 2025** (as enacted in the attached chapter law).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduced administrative/audit workload** compared to annual audits, because the statute now requires an audit only **at least once every 5 years** (instead of every year).
- **Clarifies/updates office naming** by using “Office of Auditor of Accounts,” aligning statutory text with the office’s current name.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Less frequent financial oversight** of the Distressed Cemetery Fund because audits are no longer required annually, but only **at least once every 5 years**.
