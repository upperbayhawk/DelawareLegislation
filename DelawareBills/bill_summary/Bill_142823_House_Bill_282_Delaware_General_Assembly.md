## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Reduce Delaware state spending in Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 by requiring agencies and certain offices to transfer at least 1% of their FY 2026 General Fund appropriations (with an exclusion for debt service) to the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) for reversion to the General Fund.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **each state agency, higher education institution, and other elective office** to identify and transfer certain funds.  
- Transfer amount must equal **at least 1%** of the entity’s **FY 2026 General Fund budget**.  
- **Debt service appropriations are excluded** from the 1% calculation/transfer requirement.  
- Transfers must occur **within 30 days of enactment** of the Act.  
- Funds are transferred to **OMB Contingencies and One-Time Items**.  
- Purpose of transfer is **reversion to the General Fund**.  
- Bill applies **“notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary,” except as provided** in the Act.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces FY 2026 spending by at least 1%** across covered entities (Section 1; Synopsis).  
- May help address a **budgetary shortfall** (Synopsis).  
- May reduce pressure to enact **tax law changes** referenced in the synopsis by lowering spending (Synopsis).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Creates an across-the-board reduction requirement of **at least 1%** of FY 2026 General Fund budgets for covered entities, which may require program reductions or delayed spending (Section 1).  
- Requires action **within 30 days of enactment**, which may compress internal budget planning and reallocation timelines (Section 1).  
- Transfers money to OMB for reversion, potentially reducing agencies’ flexibility to use those appropriations for FY 2026 needs (Section 1).
