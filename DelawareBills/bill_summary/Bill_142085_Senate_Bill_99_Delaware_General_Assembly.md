## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Create a William Penn High School background special license plate through the Delaware Division of Motor Vehicles (DMV), with proceeds supporting scholarships and community events sponsored by the high school via the William Penn Alumni Association Fund.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes DMV to issue a **William Penn High School special license plate** if it receives **at least 200 applications**.
- Eligible vehicles include **private passenger vehicles** and certain **motor vehicles/trailers with GVWR ≤ 26,000 pounds**, with exclusions.
- Vehicles that qualify for the **International Registration Plan (IRP)** (alone or in combination) **may not** be issued the plate.
- Requires a **one-time $50 initial application fee**, including a **$15 administrative fee**, **in addition to** annual registration fees.
- The **$15 administrative fee** is deposited into a **special fund** for DMV to **promote sales** of the plate and **administer** the program.
- Remaining proceeds from the $50 fee (after DMV administrative/promotion costs) are deposited with the **State Treasurer** and set aside in the **Scholarship Fund** for the **William Penn Alumni Association Fund**.
- Plate design must be submitted by the **William Penn Alumni Association** and approved by DMV; design constraints include: may substitute “**William Penn High School**” for “**The First State**,” cannot be **solely black and white**, and DMV may reject designs posing **public safety enforcement** problems.
- Enacted as **Chapter 109, Volume 85**, **approved July 30, 2025**; bill notes a **three-fifths vote requirement** due to imposition of a new license fee.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Dedicated scholarship/community funding stream:** Directs net proceeds (beyond DMV costs) into the **Scholarship Fund** for the **William Penn Alumni Association Fund** to support scholarships and community events.
- **Program cost recovery for DMV:** Earmarks a **$15 administrative fee** and permits a **replacement plate fee** set to cover plate cost, supporting administration without relying solely on general funds.
- **Public availability:** As a “background special license plate,” it is available to the **public at large**, not limited to alumni members (as described in the synopsis).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Additional cost to motorists:** Requires a **one-time $50 fee** (including **$15 administrative**) **in addition to** annual registration fees.
- **Implementation threshold may delay availability:** DMV may issue the plate **only after at least 200 applications** are received.
- **Eligibility limitations:** Excludes vehicles qualifying for **IRP**, which limits access for certain commercial/combination vehicle registrations.

## Key Policy Changes
- Creates **21 Del. C. § 2140T** establishing a **William Penn High School special license plate**.
- Sets issuance condition: DMV issues the plate **only if it receives at least 200 applications**.
- Defines eligible vehicles:
  - **Private passenger vehicles**; and
  - Motor vehicles/trailers with **GVWR ≤ 26,000 pounds**, owned by individuals or artificial entities,
  - **Excluding** IRP-qualifying vehicles (individually or in combination).
- Establishes fees:
  - **$50 one-time initial fee** (includes **$15 administrative fee**) plus annual registration.
  - **Replacement fee** set by DMV to cover cost.
  - Fee applies even to persons otherwise exempt from registration fees under **§ 2159**.
- Establishes revenue handling:
  - **$15 administrative fee** → special fund for DMV promotion/administration.
  - Remaining proceeds → State Treasurer → set aside in **Scholarship Fund** for **William Penn Alumni Association Fund**.
- Establishes design submission/approval rules and constraints, including prohibition on **solely black-and-white** plates and DMV authority to reject designs for enforcement/public safety concerns.
