## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To temporarily exempt food served in **Level IV and Level V Department of Correction (DOC) facilities** from Delaware’s restriction on using **polystyrene foam food service packaging**, delaying the restriction’s application to those facilities.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Existing law in **16 Del. C. § 3004Q** prohibits food establishments from providing **ready-to-eat food** in **polystyrene foam food service packaging**, with the restriction **effective July 1, 2025**.
- This bill changes an exception to allow continued use of polystyrene foam packaging for food provided in **Level IV and Level V DOC facilities**.
- As enacted (Chapter 66, Volume 85), the exemption is **temporary**: it applies **until July 1, 2026**.
- The stated rationale in the synopsis is to avoid “**significant hardships**” and “**increase costs**” for DOC.
- The synopsis cites DOC’s use of polystyrene packaging while providing **over 14,000 meals per day** to inmates.
- The Act was **approved July 1, 2025**.
- Material impact: DOC Level IV/V facilities may continue using polystyrene foam for ready-to-eat meals for **one additional year** beyond July 1, 2025.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Avoids immediate operational disruption for DOC (Level IV/V):** The synopsis states the prohibition would create “significant hardships” for DOC; the exemption prevents the restriction from applying to these facilities until **July 1, 2026**.
- **Cost containment for DOC (as stated):** The synopsis states the prohibition would “increase costs” for DOC; delaying applicability may avoid near-term cost increases tied to switching packaging.
- **Continuity of meal service scale:** The synopsis references DOC providing **over 14,000 meals per day**, implying the exemption supports continuity for high-volume meal operations in Level IV/V facilities.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Delays implementation of polystyrene restrictions in certain facilities:** The exemption allows continued use of polystyrene foam packaging in Level IV/V DOC facilities until **July 1, 2026**, limiting the reach of the July 1, 2025 restriction during that period.
- **Unequal application of the restriction:** Food establishments generally are prohibited from using polystyrene foam packaging for ready-to-eat food starting **July 1, 2025**, but Level IV/V DOC facilities receive a delayed application (until **July 1, 2026**).
