## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To finalize (as the second required passage) a constitutional amendment to Article III, § 10 of the Delaware Constitution governing **members of the Governor’s cabinet**, including **Senate confirmation**, **holdover limits after a Governor’s term ends**, and **Delaware residency requirements**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Concludes the **second leg** of a constitutional amendment first proposed in the **152nd General Assembly** (Chapter 278, Volume 84) and, upon concurrence by the **153rd General Assembly**, makes it part of the Delaware Constitution.
- Defines **“member of the Governor’s cabinet”** as the **principal officer of an executive department**, including those made subject to § 10 by act of the General Assembly.
- Requires cabinet members to be **appointed by the Governor with consent of a majority of all members elected to the Senate**.
- Limits post-term “holdover” service: cabinet members may hold over until confirmation and oath of office of the same or a new appointee, but **no longer than 90 days after the expiration of the Governor’s term**.
- Applies (by text change) **residency requirements** to **members of the Governor’s cabinet** (not just the Secretary of State): must become a **bona fide Delaware resident within 6 months** of appointment, with a possible **one-time additional 6-month extension** for good cause.
- Makes **failure to obtain or retain Delaware residency** an **automatic resignation** that **creates a vacancy**.
- Includes technical drafting corrections (e.g., gender-neutral references and conforming language).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Greater continuity and accountability at transitions:** The **90-day maximum holdover** after a Governor’s term ends creates a clear deadline to resolve cabinet leadership through confirmation and oath-taking. (Constitutional text: §10(c))
- **Clearer constitutional standards for cabinet positions:** Defines “member of the Governor’s cabinet” and ties coverage to principal officers of executive departments, including those designated by statute. (§10(a)(2))
- **Strengthens residency expectations:** Requires cabinet members to become Delaware residents within **6 months** (extendable by **6 more months** for good cause), and to remain residents during their term. (§10(e))
- **Clarifies vacancy consequences:** Residency noncompliance results in **automatic resignation** and a **vacancy**, providing an explicit enforcement mechanism. (§10(e)(3))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential operational disruption at gubernatorial transitions:** The **90-day holdover cap** could leave an office vacant if confirmations are delayed beyond 90 days after a Governor’s term expires. (§10(c))
- **May narrow the eligible candidate pool:** Cabinet members must become **bona fide residents within 6 months** (or up to **12 months** with extension) and remain residents during the term, which may deter out-of-state candidates. (§10(e)(1)-(2))
- **Automatic resignation is strict:** Failure to obtain or retain residency **automatically** causes resignation and vacancy, which could abruptly remove officials. (§10(e)(3))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **“Good cause” standard for residency extension:** The amendment allows an additional **6-month** extension “on good cause shown,” but does not define “good cause.” (§10(e)(1))
- **Meaning of “bona fide resident”:** The constitutional text requires becoming a “bona fide resident” but does not define the evidentiary or legal test in this amendment. (§10(e))
- **Scope of “executive department” coverage:** The definition includes principal officers of executive departments and those made subject by statute, but the constitutional text itself does not list departments. (§10(a)(2))
- **Process if Senate does not act within 90 days:** The amendment sets a holdover limit but does not specify a contingency process if confirmation does not occur before the 90-day cap. (§10(c))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Constitutional definition added:**  
  - “Governor’s term of office” defined as the entire term under Article III, § 5, whether or not fully served. (§10(a)(1))  
  - “Member of the Governor’s cabinet” defined as the principal officer of an executive department, including those made subject by statute. (§10(a)(2))
- **Confirmation requirement stated for cabinet members:** Governor appoints cabinet members **with consent of a majority of all members elected to the Senate**. (§10(b))
- **Holdover limitation created:** After expiration of the Governor’s term, a cabinet member may hold over pending confirmation and oath, but **not more than 90 days** after the Governor’s term expires. (§10(c))
- **Residency requirement applied to cabinet members (not just Secretary of State):**
  - Must become a Delaware bona fide resident within **6 months** of appointment. (§10(e))  
  - Governor may grant **one additional 6-month extension** for good cause. (§10(e)(1))  
  - Must remain a Delaware resident **during the member’s term of office**. (§10(e)(2))  
  - Noncompliance causes **automatic resignation** and a **vacancy**. (§10(e)(3))
- **Technical drafting corrections** (conforming to Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual). (Synopsis)

## Who Benefits
- **Senate and the confirmation process:** The amendment emphasizes the Senate’s role in confirming cabinet members and reconfirmation timing tied to gubernatorial terms (as described in the synopsis and implemented via §10(b) and §10(c)).
- **Delaware residents/general public:** May benefit from cabinet members being required to become and remain **Delaware residents** and from a defined transition timeline. (§10(c), §10(e))

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Current or prospective cabinet members who live out-of-state:** Must establish **bona fide Delaware residency within 6 months** (or **12 months** with extension) and maintain residency, or face **automatic resignation**. (§10(e))
- **Agencies during transition periods:** Leadership could be disrupted if Senate confirmations do not occur within the **90-day** holdover window after a Governor’s term expires. (§10(c))

## Effective Dates and Deadlines

- **Holdover deadline:** Cabinet holdover service may not exceed **90 days after the expiration of the Governor’s term of office**. (§10(c))

  - Governor may grant an additional **6 months** for good cause (total potential time: **up to 12 months**). (§10(e)(1))
- **Constitutional amendment becomes operative upon concurrence:** The bill states that when the **153rd General Assembly concurs**, the amendment becomes part of the Delaware Constitution. (Whereas clauses)

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Residency compliance requirement:** Cabinet members must obtain and retain Delaware residency per §10(e).
- **Automatic consequence for noncompliance:** Failure to obtain or retain residency **operates as an automatic resignation** and **results in a vacancy in the office**. (§10(e)(3))
- **Confirmation requirement:** Appointment of cabinet members requires **Senate consent by majority of all members elected to the Senate**. (§10(b))
- **Holdover compliance:** Holding over beyond **90 days after the Governor’s term expires** is not permitted. (§10(c))
