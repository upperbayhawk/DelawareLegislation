## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Authorize the Delaware Department of Agriculture to raise the **biennial pesticide and device registration fee cap** in Title 3 from **$140** to **$500**, and require annual recalculation of the fee to reflect program administration costs.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases the statutory maximum biennial registration fee for each pesticide or device from **$140** to **$500**.  
- The Department of Agriculture initially expects to raise the fee to **$280** (from **$140**).  
- The Department must **compute the appropriate registration fee at the beginning of each calendar year**.  
- Fee revenue must continue to be deposited into an **appropriated special fund (ASF)** account at the Department of Agriculture.  
- Revenue is intended to **defray the Department’s expenses associated with administration** under the pesticides chapter.  
- Estimated biennial revenue would increase from approximately **$1,820,000** to **$3,640,000** (an increase of **$1,820,000**).  
- The bill includes **technical drafting corrections** (non-substantive) to align with the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual.  
- Passage requires a **three-fifths vote** of the members elected to each house (Delaware Constitution, Art. VIII, §10) because it increases a license fee’s effective rate.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More program funding for administration:** The fee is intended to “approximate and reasonably reflect all costs necessary to defray” Department expenses under the chapter; increased revenue supports that cost recovery.  
- **Improved budget alignment:** Requiring calculation “at the beginning of each calendar year” can keep fees aligned with actual administrative costs.  
- **Dedicated use of funds:** Revenues are deposited into an **ASF** used to defray Department administrative expenses (reduces reliance on other funding sources for these activities).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher costs for registrants:** Pesticide and device companies would pay higher biennial registration fees (initially anticipated to be **$280**, with authority up to **$500**).  
- **Fee uncertainty year-to-year:** Annual computation at the start of each calendar year could lead to more frequent fee changes, affecting registrants’ budgeting.
