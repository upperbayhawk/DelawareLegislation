## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Create a temporary “Delaware 250” special background license plate to commemorate Delaware’s 250th anniversary of Separation Day (June 15, 1776) and America’s semiquincentennial (July 4, 2026), and to raise dedicated funds to support Delaware 250’s celebration activities.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Establishes a new special license plate in Title 21: **“Delaware 250”** (§ 2140T).
- DMV may issue the plate **only if it receives at least 200 applications**.
- Eligible vehicles include **private passenger vehicles** and certain vehicles/trailers with **GVWR ≤ 26,000 pounds**, but **IRP-qualified vehicles are excluded**.
- Sets a **one-time $50 initial application fee**, which includes a **$15 administrative fee**, **in addition to** normal annual registration fees.
- Requires the **$15 administrative portion** to be used by DMV for **promotion and administration** of the plate program; remaining net proceeds go to a dedicated **Delaware 250 Fund**.
- The **Delaware 250 Fund** is held by the State Treasurer and used by **Delaware 250**, which determines how funds are spent to support the celebration.
- Plate design must be submitted by **Delaware 250** and approved by DMV; DMV can reject designs posing a **public safety enforcement problem**; plate **cannot be solely black-and-white**.
- The authorizing section **expires December 31, 2026**; unencumbered funds **revert to the Transportation Trust Fund**; encumbered funds are **transferred to Delaware 250**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Dedicated fundraising mechanism:** Net proceeds from the **$50 one-time fee** (after DMV’s promotional/administrative costs from the **$15** component) are directed to the **Delaware 250 Fund** to support commemorative activities (§ 2140T(e)).
- **Administrative cost coverage:** The bill explicitly allocates a **$15 administrative fee** to DMV for **promoting sales and administering** the program (§ 2140T(d)).
- **Time-limited program:** Authority to issue the plate expires **December 31, 2026**, limiting long-term administrative commitment (Section 2).
- **Public safety guardrails for design:** DMV may refuse designs that create a **public safety enforcement problem** and requires DMV approval (§ 2140T(f)(3)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **New mandatory fee for participants:** Applicants must pay a **$50 one-time fee** even if they are otherwise exempt from registration fees under **§ 2159** (§ 2140T(c)(2)).
- **Plate availability contingent on demand:** The plate will not be issued unless DMV receives **at least 200 applications** (§ 2140T(a)), potentially limiting access.
- **Uncertain replacement cost:** Replacement plate fee is **set by DMV** and must cover plate cost, but no dollar amount is specified (§ 2140T(c)(3)).
- **Limited vehicle eligibility:** Vehicles eligible for the **International Registration Plan (IRP)** cannot receive the plate (§ 2140T(b)(2)b).

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds **21 Del. C. § 2140T** to authorize a “**Delaware 250**” special background license plate.
- Sets minimum issuance threshold: **DMV issues only after 200 applications** are received (§ 2140T(a)).
- Limits eligible vehicles to:
  - **Private passenger vehicles**; and
  - Certain vehicles/trailers with **GVWR ≤ 26,000 lbs**, including those owned by individuals or artificial entities; but **excludes IRP-qualified vehicles** (§ 2140T(b)).
- Establishes fees:
  - **$50 one-time initial fee**, including **$15 administrative fee**, plus regular annual registration (§ 2140T(c)(1)).
  - **$50 fee applies even to § 2159-exempt nonprofit members** (§ 2140T(c)(2)).
  - Replacement plate fee set by DMV to cover cost (§ 2140T(c)(3)).
- Creates a dedicated flow of funds:
  - **$15 administrative fee** → DMV special fund for promotion/administration (§ 2140T(d)).
  - Remaining proceeds → State Treasurer into **Delaware 250 Fund**, controlled by **Delaware 250** for celebration support (§ 2140T(e)).
- Plate design rules and approval:
  - Submitted by Delaware 250; DMV final color/design approval (§ 2140T(f)).
  - May replace “The First State” with “**Delaware 250**” (§ 2140T(f)(1)).
  - Cannot be solely **black and white** (§ 2140T(f)(2)).
  - DMV may refuse designs for public safety enforcement issues (§ 2140T(f)(3)).
- **Sunset and fund disposition:**
  - Authority **expires December 31, 2026** (Section 2).
  - Unencumbered Delaware 250 Fund money on **December 31, 2026** reverts to **Transportation Trust Fund**; encumbered money transfers to Delaware 250 (Section 3).

## Who Benefits
- **Delaware 250** (entity referenced in the bill) receives net proceeds via the **Delaware 250 Fund** to support celebration activities (§ 2140T(e)).
- **Residents and eligible vehicle owners** who want a commemorative plate honoring Delaware’s/USA’s 250th anniversaries (§ 2140T(a), (b)).
- **DMV** receives a dedicated **$15 administrative fee** per initial plate for program promotion/administration (§ 2140T(d)).
- **Transportation Trust Fund** may receive any **unencumbered** Delaware 250 Fund moneys as of **December 31, 2026** (Section 3).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Applicants/vehicle owners** who must pay the **$50 one-time fee** (including those otherwise exempt from registration fees under **§ 2159**) (§ 2140T(c)(2)).
- **Owners of IRP-eligible vehicles** who are ineligible for the plate (§ 2140T(b)(2)b).
- **Potential applicants** if the minimum threshold of **200 applications** is not met (plate not issued) (§ 2140T(a)).
