## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14, Chapter 39 of the Delaware Code to deny Teachers’ Retirement and Disability Pension benefits to individuals convicted of sexual abuse of a student, and to deny benefits to individuals indicted for such an offense who avoid prosecution by remaining outside the United States for more than 1 month.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Denies retirement or disability benefits under Chapter 39, Title 14 to an individual (and the individual’s survivor or beneficiary) after the individual’s **conviction** for **sexual abuse of a student**.  
- Extends/clarifies denial of benefits to an individual **under indictment** for sexual abuse of a student who **willfully remains outside the United States for more than 1 month** to avoid prosecution.
