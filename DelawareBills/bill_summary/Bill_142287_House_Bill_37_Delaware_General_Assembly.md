## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s Equal Accommodations Law (Title 6, Chapter 45) definitions to ensure government services—whether provided directly by government entities or by government-funded or government-supervised entities—are treated as “places of public accommodation” and thus subject to the same non-discrimination requirements as private businesses. Also aligns the definition of “person” with similar definitions elsewhere in the Delaware Code.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands/clarifies the definition of **“place of public accommodation”** to expressly cover **government services and entities**, including **state agencies, local government agencies, and public libraries**.  
- Brings within “public accommodation” certain **non-government entities** when they (a) **receive state funding to perform a public function** or (b) **perform a public function under government control and supervision**.  
- Keeps existing carve-outs: “place of public accommodation” **does not apply** to **sale/rental of dwellings** and **tourist homes with fewer than 10 rental units**.  
- Adds a broader definition of **“person”** to explicitly include **sole proprietorships, LLCs, trusts, governmental bodies, and any other legal/commercial/government entity**.  
- Non-discrimination protections referenced in the synopsis include protections based on **race, age, marital status, creed, religion, color, sex, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or national origin**.  
- Contains conditional effective-date instructions tied to **House Bill 36**, with key date **July 1, 2025**, and a fallback date of **November 1, 2025** in one scenario.  
- Bill chapter approval date: **Approved August 20, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clearer coverage of government services:** Explicitly includes **state agencies, local government agencies, and public libraries** as places of public accommodation, reinforcing that services offered to the public by government entities fall under non-discrimination requirements (per synopsis and revised definition).  
- **Extends protections to outsourced public functions:** Covers a **person receiving state funding to perform a public function** and a **person performing a public function under government control and supervision**, potentially reducing discrimination risks in contracted or delegated public services.  
- **Greater definitional consistency:** Updates “person” to match similar Code definitions by including additional entity types (e.g., **LLCs, trusts, governmental bodies**), which may reduce interpretive disputes about who is covered.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Broader compliance obligations for contractors/grantees:** Entities that **receive state funding to perform a public function** or operate **under government control/supervision** may newly be treated as “places of public accommodation,” which could increase compliance burden under the Equal Accommodations Law (as a direct result of the expanded definition).  
- **Potential for disputes over “public function” scope:** The added categories depend on whether an entity is performing a **“public function”** and/or is under **government “control and supervision,”** which may invite interpretive disagreements (terms not further defined in the provided text).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Redefines “person” (Title 6 § 4502(18))** to include:  
  - **Sole proprietorships, limited liability companies, trusts, governmental bodies**, and **any other legal, commercial, or government entity**, in addition to entities already referenced under § 302 of Title 1.
- **Revises “place of public accommodation” (Title 6 § 4502(19)/(20))** to include, among other items:  
  - Establishments offering goods/services/etc. to the general public;  
  - **All state agencies** and **local government agencies**;  
  - **Public libraries** and **state-funded agencies**;  
  - **A person that receives funding from this State to perform a public function**;  
  - **A person performing a public function under the control and supervision of a state or local government agency**;  
  - **Hotels and motels** catering to the transient public (retained/clarified).  
- **Retains exclusions** from “place of public accommodation”:  
  - **Sale or rental of dwellings** (houses, housing units, apartments, rooming houses, other dwellings) and **tourist dwellings**;  
  - **Tourist homes with less than 10 rental units** catering to the transient public (**threshold: < 10 rental units**).
- Adds technical/formatting corrections to conform to the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual (per synopsis).

## Who Benefits
- **Members of the public** using **government services**, including services delivered by:  
  - **State agencies, local government agencies, and public libraries**; and  
  - **State-funded or government-supervised entities performing public functions**, who are brought explicitly under public-accommodation non-discrimination requirements (per synopsis and definitions).
- **People in protected classes** under the Equal Accommodations Law referenced in the synopsis (race, age, marital status, creed, religion, color, sex, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Entities receiving state funding to perform a public function** and **entities performing a public function under government control/supervision**, to the extent they now must comply as “places of public accommodation” (expanded coverage in § 4502 definition).
