## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware Title 14 provisions governing the Sussex County Vocational-Technical High School District’s taxing authority and enrollment rules, including admissions and discipline-related enrollment protections.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Removes the statute’s specified school tax rate caps for Sussex County Vo-Tech that were set as cents per $100 of assessed real property value for tax years **2021–2024** and “**all years thereafter**.”  
- Under prior law, the “current expense” tax cap rose to **29.50 cents per $100** of real property value for **tax year 2024 and all years thereafter**; the bill deletes those specific limits.  
- Removes the statute’s specified enrollment caps that increased from **1,300 (SY 2021-2022)** to **1,450 (SY 2024-2025 and all years thereafter)** for grades **9–12**; the bill deletes those specific limits.  
- Keeps/continues a **random lottery** admissions process when applications exceed the district’s allowed enrollment capacity.  
- Keeps/continues a proportionality limit: admissions from any **one Sussex County school district** may not exceed that district’s share (as a percentage) of **Sussex County’s total eighth grade enrollment**.  
- Keeps/continues limited admissions preferences only for: (1) **siblings** of currently enrolled students; and (2) **children of employees** working **at least 30.0 hours/week** on a permanent basis during the school year.  
- Keeps/continues a prohibition on excluding applicants from the lottery based on being below the **70th percentile GPA** or having **failed any eighth grade course**, unless not academically eligible for promotion under Department of Education standards.  
- Keeps/continues limits on removing students for discipline without parent/guardian and home district consent, except for expulsions under **14 Del. C. § 4130**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Greater local flexibility on tax rates:** By deleting the fixed “cents per $100” schedule (including the **29.50 cents per $100** cap for **tax year 2024 and all years thereafter**), the district can set tax rates without those statutory figures.  
- **Potential to serve more students:** By deleting the fixed statutory caps (up to **1,450 students** for **SY 2024-2025 and all years thereafter**), the district is no longer bound to those numbers in statute.  
- **Maintains broad access safeguards in admissions:** The bill preserves the rule prohibiting exclusion from the lottery based on **below 70th percentile GPA** or **failed eighth grade course(s)** (subject to DOE promotion eligibility).  
- **Limits preferential treatment:** Preferences are restricted to two categories, including the **30.0 hours/week** employee threshold, which may support transparency and consistency.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Less statutory constraint on tax burden:** Removing the specified tax-rate schedule eliminates explicit statutory limits such as the **29.50 cents per $100** figure for later years, which could reduce predictability for taxpayers.  
- **Reduced statutory clarity on enrollment maximums:** Removing explicit caps (e.g., **1,450** for **SY 2024-2025 and all years thereafter**) means the maximum is no longer clearly stated in statute.  
- **Operational uncertainty tied to external determinations:** The synopsis indicates enrollment capacity will be established by a DOE **Certificate of Need**, rather than fixed statute, which may change over time (details not provided in bill text).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Deletes fixed tax-rate schedule** for Sussex County Vo-Tech “current expense” taxes that previously capped rates at:
  - **26.50 cents per $100** (tax year **2021**)
  - **27.50 cents per $100** (tax year **2022**)
  - **28.50 cents per $100** (tax year **2023**)
  - **29.50 cents per $100** (tax year **2024 and all years thereafter**)  
- **Deletes fixed enrollment caps** for grades 9–12 that previously limited enrollment to:
  - **1,300** (SY **2021-2022**)
  - **1,350** (SY **2022-2023**)
  - **1,400** (SY **2023-2024**)
  - **1,450** (SY **2024-2025 and all years thereafter**)  
- **Retains** lottery admissions framework when applications exceed capacity, proportional admissions by Sussex sending district (based on **total county 8th grade enrollment**), limited preferences (including **30.0 hours/week** employee threshold), and protections regarding lottery eligibility and discipline-related removal (with § **4130** expulsion exception).
