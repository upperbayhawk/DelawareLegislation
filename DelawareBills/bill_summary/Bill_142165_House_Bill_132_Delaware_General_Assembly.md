## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Eliminate three court-imposed fees/surcharges in Delaware law (Court Security assessment, Videophone fee, and Victim’s Rights fee), and discharge any outstanding balances on those fees after repeal. The bill also conditions repeal of the Court Security assessment on a specified General Fund appropriation to replace its spending authority.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Repeals the **Court Security assessment** in **10 Del. C. § 8505** (a supplemental assessment **not to exceed $10** on each initial civil filing and on each criminal/traffic/delinquency charge with a conviction/finding/voluntary assessment paid).  
- Repeals the **Videophone fee** in **11 Del. C. § 4101(d)** (an additional **$1.00** penalty imposed on criminal defendants/children adjudicated delinquent).  
- Repeals the **Victims’ Rights Fund** statute in **11 Del. C. § 9418** and removes the related **seatbelt-case Victim’s Rights fee** in **21 Del. C. § 4802(g)(2)a** (previously **40% of the base fine up to $20**).  
- Requires that **30 days after the effective dates**, **any outstanding balances owed** on the repealed fees **are discharged**.  
- Makes Sections **2–7 effective immediately**; makes Section **1** (Court Security fee repeal) effective only after a **notice is published in the Register of Regulations** confirming **$3,180,100** in **General Fund** money was appropriated to the Judicial Branch.  
- Fiscal note estimates recurring General Fund costs in **FY 2026–FY 2028** of **$3,304,057 (minimum) to $3,497,200 (maximum)**, or **$2,541,357 to $2,734,500** if certain FY2026 budget recommendations are adopted.  
- The bill follows recommendations from the **Criminal Legal System Imposed Debt Study Group** (report dated **December 7, 2023**) to repeal these fees and replace revenue with General Funds as needed.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces court-imposed financial obligations** by repealing three specified fees: **up to $10** per qualifying filing/charge (Court Security), **$1** per qualifying criminal/delinquency case (Videophone), and **40% of base fine up to $20** in seatbelt-related contexts (Victim’s Rights). (Bill text; fiscal note descriptions)
- **Clears unpaid debt** tied to these fees by requiring discharge of **outstanding balances** **30 days after** the relevant effective dates. (Section 7)
- **Stabilizes funding source (per bill synopsis rationale)** by shifting away from fee-based revenue that can be unstable during recessions/pandemics/major economic events. (Synopsis)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Loss of dedicated fee revenue** previously supporting:
  - Judicial Branch security personnel/equipment/training (Court Security Fund). (10 Del. C. § 8505 text; fiscal note)
  - Videophone system line charges/maintenance/upgrades for criminal justice agencies (Videophone Fund). (11 Del. C. § 4101(d) text; fiscal note)
  - Victim notification initiatives (Victims’ Rights Fund). (11 Del. C. § 9418 text; fiscal note)
- **Requires replacement funding from the General Fund** to maintain these functions; fiscal note projects recurring costs of **$3.304M–$3.497M** annually (FY26–FY28) absent certain budget changes. (Fiscal note)
- **Court Security repeal is delayed/conditional**, potentially creating an interim period where the fee remains until the specified appropriation and publication notice occur. (Section 8)

## Key Policy Changes
- **Repeals 10 Del. C. § 8505** (Court security assessment). (Section 1)
- **Repeals videophone assessment provisions** in **11 Del. C. § 4101(d)**. (Section 2)
- Conforming change in **11 Del. C. § 4101(j)** removing priority language referencing the **Court Security Fund** created by repealed § 8505. (Section 3, amending priority order text)
- **Repeals 11 Del. C. § 9418** (Victims’ Rights Fund statute). (Section 4)
- Conforming changes in **21 Del. C. § 4170A(g)** removing references so that certain assessments (including those tied to repealed provisions) are treated differently for speed monitoring violations (text removes cross-references to repealed fee authorities). (Section 5)
- **Removes seatbelt-related Victim’s Rights penalty assessment** in **21 Del. C. § 4802(g)(2)a** that previously imposed **40% not to exceed $20** for credit to the Victim’s Rights Fund in specified circumstances. (Section 6)
- **Discharges outstanding balances** on the repealed fees **30 days after the effective dates**. (Section 7)

## Who Benefits
- **Civil litigants and criminal/traffic/delinquency defendants** who would otherwise pay the **up to $10 Court Security assessment** (once Section 1 is effective). (10 Del. C. § 8505; Sections 1 & 8)
- **Criminal defendants and children adjudicated delinquent** who would otherwise pay the **$1 Videophone fee**. (11 Del. C. § 4101(d); Section 2)
- **Individuals cited/penalized in seatbelt-related cases** who would otherwise owe the **Victim’s Rights fee (40% up to $20)** in the specified sentencing context. (21 Del. C. § 4802(g)(2)a; Section 6)
- **People with unpaid balances** of these fees, because balances are **discharged** after the required period. (Section 7)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Judicial Branch security operations** previously supported by Court Security fee revenue (personnel/equipment/training), to the extent replacement funding is delayed or insufficient. (10 Del. C. § 8505 description; fiscal note Assumption 3; conditional effective date in Section 8)
- **Criminal Justice Council and participating agencies** relying on Videophone Fund revenues for videophone systems. (11 Del. C. § 4101(d); fiscal note Assumption 4)
- **Department of Justice victim notification initiatives** formerly supported by Victims’ Rights Fund revenue. (11 Del. C. § 9418; fiscal note Assumption 5)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Sections 2 through 7:** **Effective immediately**. (Section 9)
- **Section 1 (Court Security fee repeal):** Effective on the **date of publication in the Register of Regulations** of a notice by the Controller General that **$3,180,100 in General Fund moneys** were appropriated to the Judicial Branch; Controller General must provide the notice to the Registrar of Regulations. (Section 8)
- **Debt discharge deadline:** **30 days after the effective dates of this Act**, outstanding balances on the repealed fees are discharged. (Section 7)
- Fiscal note assumption: Act becomes effective **upon signature by the Governor**, but bill text specifies immediate effect for Sections 2–7 and a conditional effect for Section 1. (Fiscal note Assumption 1; Sections 8–9)

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Mandatory discharge of outstanding balances** on the repealed fees **30 days after** the applicable effective dates. (Section 7)
- **Controller General notice requirement:** must notify the Registrar of Regulations that **$3,180,100** GF was appropriated to the Judicial Branch; the repeal of Court Security fee hinges on **publication** of that notice. (Section 8)
- No new penalties are created; the bill primarily **repeals** assessments/fees and makes conforming edits. (Bill text)
