## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s Fiscal Year (FY) 2025 Bond and Capital Improvements Act (fiscal year ending **June 30, 2025**) to revise bond authorizations, adjust specific capital allocations and matching requirements, and add/modify spending-transfer authorities and project directives across multiple agencies and school construction programs.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes issuance of **20-year, full faith and credit** State bonds to provide **$317,480,000** in proceeds, and increases the **local share of school bonds** from **$90,357,205** to **$115,354,405** (at district discretion).
- Adjusts school construction financing assumptions, including an updated **Colonial School District (William Penn HS Renovations)** local share entry (table reflects a large local share value of **$27,067,362** for one listed line item).
- Allows **OMB Director**, with concurrence of the **Controller General** and **Co-Chairs of the Joint Committee on Capital Improvement (JCCI)**, to transfer any necessary spending authority to the **Department of State** to disburse **Cultural Access Fund** matching grants (default **2:1 match**, waiver possible with approvals).
- Revises allocations for **City of Dover / Downtown Dover Partnership**: water-sewer line project reduced to **$1,800,000** (from **$2,500,000**); Downtown Dover Partnership increased to **$8,200,000** (from **$7,500,000**); **$420,000** remains for Dover Police training facility capital needs.
- Clarifies **University of Delaware** deferred maintenance appropriation description and earmarks **$4,000,000** for design/upgrade of the softball field.
- Modifies **Enhanced Minor Capital Improvements (Enhanced MCI)** requirements: removes prior-year MCI obligation prerequisite; retains reporting deadline **January 1, 2025**; authorizes optional local match effective **July 1, 2024** up to **60/40 state/local** (local share capped at **40%**).
- Authorizes transfers/reallocations: Legislative Hall remaining funds to **Legislative Hall Garage/Expansion**; remaining **$225,000** feasibility-study funds to **Family Justice Center** startup; National Guard funds to **Bethany Beach Training Site Barracks** (including up to **$500,000** MCI).
- Requires OMB to deliver a **Delaware State Police Troop 7** adaptive reuse study to the Controller General and JCCI Co-Chairs **no later than May 1, 2025**; removes a prior deadline that required Sussex County Council adoption of 9 Del. C. §6961(d) **by December 31, 2024**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Flexibility to align funding with project realities:** Increases authorized **local share of school bonds** to **$115,354,405**, and adjusts specific project allocations and transfer authorities.
- **Streamlined grant disbursement:** Allows OMB (with required concurrence) to transfer spending authority needed to disburse **Cultural Access Fund** grants; includes an explicit **2:1 match** structure with a waiver pathway.
- **Supports school safety tooling:** Directs minimum **$138,000** for school mapping/reporting/threat assessment tools (DEMA) and up to **$160,000** for a Real-Time Crime Center application (State Police / SBI) to enhance safety around schools.
- **Enables timely use of school facility funds:** Removes the requirement that districts/charters must obligate prior-year MCI funds before using **Enhanced MCI** funds.
- **Targeted community and transportation reimbursements:** Authorizes one-time reimbursements for specified local projects (e.g., **$295,000** for Route 8 road/sidewalk improvements in Dover; **$165,000** parking lot project for Smyrna-Clayton Little League).
- **Improves planning/oversight for state facility reuse:** Requires a public-input process and a firm deadline (**May 1, 2025**) for the Troop 7 adaptive reuse study submission.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher potential local borrowing exposure for school projects:** Raising the authorized **local share of school bonds** from **$90,357,205** to **$115,354,405** could increase local debt issuance (district discretion).
- **Reduced earmark for a Dover water-sewer project:** Allocation for the water-sewer line project is lowered from **$2,500,000** to **$1,800,000**.
- **Reprogramming may displace original recipients/projects:** The Community Reinvestment Fund section reallocates funds away from **Downtown Milford, Inc.** to other entities/projects (e.g., Delaware State Fair, Milford library, Kent Sussex Industries, Milford Historical Society).
- **Potentially weaker guardrails on Enhanced MCI sequencing:** Eliminating the prior-year MCI obligation prerequisite reduces a constraint that may have promoted spending down older appropriations first (the bill does not replace it with an alternative prioritization rule).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities

- **School safety allocations:** minimum **$138,000** to DEMA; up to **$160,000** to DSP/SBI for Real-Time Crime Center application.
- **Enhanced MCI:** removes the prior-year MCI obligation requirement; keeps **Jan 1, 2025** reporting; optional local match effective **July 1, 2024** up to **60/40**.
- **Reprogramming/transfer authority:** Legislative Hall funds to garage/expansion; feasibility-study remainder to Family Justice Center startup; National Guard funds to Bethany Beach barracks (incl. up to **$500,000** MCI).
