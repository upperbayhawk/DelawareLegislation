## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To designate **January 2026** as **“National Blood Donor Month”** in Delaware and encourage blood donation and blood drives.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Formally recognizes **January 2026** as **National Blood Donor Month** in the State of Delaware.
- Honors **voluntary blood donors** and encourages more people to donate when supplies are “historically low.”
- Cites that a **blood transfusion occurs in the U.S. every 2 seconds** and that only **3% of the eligible population** donates blood.
- Notes **O-negative (O-)** is the universal blood type and represents **6% of the population**, making it frequently scarce during emergencies.
- Highlights that **low-titer O-positive** blood is carried by the **Delaware State Police Aviation Unit** and **New Castle, Kent, and Sussex County EMS** for pre-hospital transfusions.
- Urges **eligible donors** to donate regularly and urges **businesses and organizations** to sponsor community-based blood drives, including with **Blood Bank of Delmarva**.
- The measure is a **Senate Concurrent Resolution (SCR 128)** (non-codified, ceremonial recognition).
