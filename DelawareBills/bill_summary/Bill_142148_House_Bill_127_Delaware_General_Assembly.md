## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Reduce the size of the Manufactured Home Installation Board and make related conforming/technical drafting corrections in Title 24 of the Delaware Code.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Reduces the Manufactured Home Installation Board from **9 members to 5 members** (24 Del. C. § 4413(a)).
- Changes board composition by reducing **manufactured home installation inspectors from 2 to 1** and **public members from 3 to 2** (24 Del. C. § 4413(a)(2), (5)).
- Keeps **2 licensed manufactured home installers**, **1 retailer representative**, and **1 registered professional engineer (≥ 6 years experience)** on the Board (24 Del. C. § 4413(a)(1), (3), (4)).
- Simplifies required officer positions: the Board will annually elect only a **president and vice-president** (removes secretary, complaint officer, education officer) (24 Del. C. § 4415(b)).
- Adjusts officer term rule to allow service of up to **2 consecutive terms** (24 Del. C. § 4415(b)).
- Updates quorum/disciplinary voting to reflect the smaller board: disciplinary actions require at least **3 affirmative votes** instead of **5** (24 Del. C. § 4415(c)).
- Act is **approved June 24, 2025** (enactment date shown in the law text).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Streamlined governance:** A smaller board (**5 vs. 9**) may be easier to convene and manage (24 Del. C. § 4413(a)).
- **Reduced administrative burden for officer roles:** Eliminating mandated positions (secretary/complaint officer/education officer) narrows required internal roles to **president and vice-president** (24 Del. C. § 4415(b)).
- **Disciplinary decisions remain possible with fewer members:** The threshold is updated to **3 affirmative votes** to match a 5-member board (24 Del. C. § 4415(c)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced stakeholder representation:** Inspector representation decreases from **2 to 1**, and public members from **3 to 2**, potentially reducing viewpoints in deliberations (24 Del. C. § 4413(a)(2), (5)).
- **Fewer required governance functions:** Removing required officer roles for complaints and education could reduce formal emphasis on those functions, as the statute no longer requires dedicated officers (24 Del. C. § 4415(b)).
- **Lower disciplinary vote threshold:** Disciplinary action can be taken with **3 votes** instead of **5**, which is a lower absolute number of votes (24 Del. C. § 4415(c)).
