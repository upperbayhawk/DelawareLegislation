## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14 to remove or relax certain statutory limitations on Sussex County Vocational-Technical High School District, aligning its authorities and practices more closely with other Delaware vo-tech districts (as stated in the synopsis).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Sets Sussex County vo-tech’s maximum **current expense tax rate** at **29.50 cents per $100 of assessed real property value** for **tax year 2024 and all years thereafter** (with prior-year caps listed for 2021–2023).
- Sets the district’s maximum **grades 9–12 enrollment** at **1,450 students** for **school year 2024-2025 and all years thereafter** (with prior-year caps listed for 2021-2022 through 2023-2024).
- Requires a **random lottery** for admission when applications exceed available seats.
- Limits admissions “preferences” to: **(1) siblings of current students** and **(2) children of employees** who work **at least 30.0 hours/week** on a permanent basis during the school year.
- Prohibits excluding applicants from the lottery due to **GPA below the 70th percentile** or **failure of any eighth-grade course**, unless the student is not academically eligible for promotion under **Department of Education** standards.
- Restricts disciplinary removals: except for expulsion under **§ 4130**, the district may not remove a student for disciplinary reasons without consent of **parents/guardians** and the **student’s district of residence**.
- Synopsis states the bill “removes certain limitations” so Sussex County Vo-Tech has the “same rights and privileges” as **Polytech** and **New Castle Vo-Tech**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded access and equity in admissions:** Applicants cannot be excluded from the lottery solely for being below the **70th percentile GPA** or having **failed an eighth-grade course** (subject to DOE promotion eligibility).
- **More predictable, standardized admissions process:** Requires a **random lottery** when oversubscribed and restricts preferences to two specific categories.
- **Increased capacity:** Raises/sets enrollment cap to **1,450 students** for **2024-2025 and thereafter**, potentially allowing more students to attend.
- **Greater student retention protections:** Limits disciplinary removals without consent (except expulsion under **§ 4130**), which may reduce involuntary transfers.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced local discretion in admissions screening:** The district is prohibited from excluding certain applicants from the lottery based on academic indicators (GPA percentile / failed course), limiting the district’s ability to use those factors for selection when oversubscribed.
- **Administrative complexity:** The proportional cap by sending district (based on Sussex County total eighth-grade enrollment percentages) and the lottery process may require additional tracking and verification.
- **Potential impact on discipline management:** The restriction on removing students for disciplinary reasons without multiple consents (parents/guardians and district of residence) could limit the district’s ability to respond quickly to certain disciplinary situations (except expulsions under **§ 4130**).
- **Tax authority confirmation/continuation:** The statute reflects a current-expense tax rate cap of **29.50 cents per $100** for **2024 and thereafter**, which may be perceived as enabling higher taxation compared with earlier years’ listed caps.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Tax rate cap for current expense (Sussex County real property):**
  - **26.50 cents per $100** for **tax year 2021**
  - **27.50 cents per $100** for **tax year 2022**
  - **28.50 cents per $100** for **tax year 2023**
  - **29.50 cents per $100** for **tax year 2024 and all years thereafter**
- **Enrollment cap (grades 9–12):**
  - **1,300** for **SY 2021-2022**
  - **1,350** for **SY 2022-2023**
  - **1,400** for **SY 2023-2024**
  - **1,450** for **SY 2024-2025 and all years thereafter**
- **Admissions when oversubscribed:** Must conduct a **random lottery**; admissions proceed in order until seats are filled.
- **Sending-district proportional cap:** Students admitted from any one Sussex County school district may not exceed that district’s share of the vo-tech’s total enrollment cap, based on the district’s percentage of **Sussex County total eighth-grade enrollment**.
- **Admissions preferences limited to:**
  1) **Siblings** of currently enrolled students  
  2) **Children of employees** working **≥ 30.0 hours/week** on a permanent basis during the school year
- **Academic exclusion limits:** Prohibits denying an applicant entry into the lottery due to **GPA below the 70th percentile** or **failure of any eighth-grade course**, unless not eligible for promotion under **DOE** standards.
- **Disciplinary removal restriction:** Except for expulsion under **14 Del. C. § 4130**, cannot remove a student for disciplinary reasons without consent of **parents/guardians** and the **district of residence**.
