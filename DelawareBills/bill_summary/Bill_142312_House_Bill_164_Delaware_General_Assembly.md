## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Increase and update Delaware DMV-related fees and create new “alternative fuel” vehicle registration fees (electric, plug-in hybrid, non-plug-in hybrid, and other fuel vehicles) to raise revenue for the Transportation Trust Fund (TTF) to support DelDOT responsibilities, including managing Delaware’s transit system and funding road/bridge improvements.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new annual **additional registration fee** for **electric motor vehicles, plug-in electric vehicles, non-plug-in electric vehicles, and “other fuel vehicles”** based on **gross load weight** (thresholds: **≤6,000 lbs; 6,001–10,000 lbs; 10,001–26,000 lbs; >26,000 lbs**).
- Adds a new rule for **alternative-fuel motorcycles**: an **additional $15/year**; **mopeds are exempt** from the additional fee.
- Increases the **driver’s license issuance/renewal fee** from **$40 to $50** (8-year renewal cycle remains).
- Increases **commercial driver license (CDL)** fees from **$48 to $55** for issuance and renewal; increases CDL endorsement fee from **$5 to $10**; sets a **$10** fee for **removal of a CDL restriction**.
- Sets/raises replacement and name-change fees: **duplicate/replacement driver’s license $20**; **driver’s license name change $10**; **ID card duplicate/replacement fee $10**; **ID card name change $10** (as reflected in the bill text).
- Increases **taxicab authorization and renewal** from **$3.45 to $10.00**.
- Establishes/raises dealer licensing fees: **dealer license application fee $100** and **renewal $50**.
- Increases the **motor vehicle document fee** from **$4.25 per $100** of purchase price to **$5.25 per $100** (special transition rules apply through **November 1, 2025**).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Stabilizes transportation funding:** The Act directs added revenues to the **Transportation Trust Fund**, intended to support DelDOT responsibilities and transit system management (source text: “All funds are deposited to the Transportation Trust Fund (TTF)”).
- **Addresses declining motor fuel tax revenues:** The synopsis states the alternative fuel fee is intended to “make up for declining motor fuel tax revenue impacts.”
- **Aligns fees with roadway impact (weight-based tiers):** Alternative fuel fees increase with vehicle weight (tiers up to **>26,000 lbs**, with highest annual fees **$900** for certain categories), consistent with stated rationale about heavier vehicles and roadway impacts.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher costs for alternative-fuel vehicle owners:** New annual add-on fees apply to electric, plug-in, non-plug-in, and other fuel vehicles (e.g., **$110/year** for EVs **≤6,000 lbs**, up to **$900/year** for EVs **>26,000 lbs**).
- **Higher DMV transaction costs for drivers:** Driver’s license fee increases **$40 → $50**; CDL **$48 → $55**; endorsement **$5 → $10**; multiple replacement/name-change fees are increased or newly imposed.
- **Higher cost to purchase/title vehicles:** Document fee increases from **$4.25 per $100** to **$5.25 per $100** of purchase price (rounded to nearest dollar), increasing costs at sale/title.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Definition boundaries for “other fuel vehicle”:** The bill lists examples (propane, natural gas, ethanol, hydrogen, etc.), but does not specify how borderline technologies are treated beyond the statutory definition.  
- **ID card duplicate fee amount inconsistency between sources:** The fee impact sheet lists “Replacement ID Card Fee $5 → $10,” while the synopsis states it “Makes the fee for a duplicate identification card… set at $20,” but the enacted bill text amends §3105 to show **$10.00**. (The bill text controls, but the discrepancy is present in the provided source materials.)
- **Use of increased revenues across programs:** The fee impact sheet says intended use is to support DelDOT responsibilities and transit management via TTF, but does not allocate amounts to specific projects or programs.

## Key Policy Changes
1. **Creates new §2151A “Alternative fuel registration fees” (annual add-on, weight-tiered):**
   - **Electric motor vehicle (EV):**  
     - **≤6,000 lbs:** **$110/year**  
     - **6,001–10,000 lbs:** **$150/year**  
     - **10,001–26,000 lbs:** **$260/year**  
     - **>26,000 lbs:** **$900/year**
   - **Plug-in electric vehicle:**  
     - **≤6,000 lbs:** **$85/year**  
     - **6,001–10,000 lbs:** **$115/year**  
     - **10,001–26,000 lbs:** **$200/year**  
     - **>26,000 lbs:** **$600/year**
   - **Non-plug-in electric vehicle:**  
     - **≤6,000 lbs:** **$60/year**  
     - **6,001–10,000 lbs:** **$80/year**  
     - **10,001–26,000 lbs:** **$140/year**  
     - **>26,000 lbs:** **$480/year**
   - **Other fuel vehicle:**  
     - **≤6,000 lbs:** **$110/year**  
     - **6,001–10,000 lbs:** **$150/year**  
     - **10,001–26,000 lbs:** **$260/year**  
     - **>26,000 lbs:** **$900/year**
   - **Alternative-fuel motorcycle add-on:** **$15/year**; **moped exempt**.
2. **Adds/updates definitions in Title 21 §101:** Defines **electric motor vehicle**, **plug-in electric vehicle**, **non-plug-in electric vehicle**, and **other fuel vehicle**.
3. **Nonprofit special plate setup fee:** Requires a **one-time $200** setup fee for a nonprofit organization to establish a special license plate (added to §2140).
4. **Driver’s license fee:** Increases from **$40 to $50** (Title 21 §2715).
5. **Sex offender coded replacement license (Y restriction):** Fee increases **$5.00 → $10.00** (Title 21 §2718).
6. **Commercial learner’s permit (CLP):** If exams are not passed within the 1-year permit period, the CLP is void and the **$10 fee is forfeited** (Title 21 §2608).
7. **CDL fees and related charges (Title 21 §2611):**
   - CDL issuance **$48 → $55**
   - CDL renewal **$48 → $55**
   - CDL endorsement add-on **$5 → $10**
   - CDL restriction removal fee **$10.00**
   - Non-CDL Class A/B renewal **$40 → $50**
   - CDL late fee remains **$10** (language updates “shall be is assessed”).
8. **Duplicate/replacement driver’s license and name change fees (Title 21 §2720):**
   - Duplicate/replacement (lost/destroyed): **$20**
   - Name change replacement: **$10**
   - Exemptions maintained for holders of a valid Delaware personal credential card and certain eligible applicants (children in DSCYF custody or meeting §9011A(g) Title 29).
9. **Taxicab authorization:** **$3.45 → $10.00** (and renewals to **$10.00**) (Title 21 §2764).
10. **ID card duplicate/replacement fee:** **$5.00 → $10.00** (Title 21 §3105) with credential-card exemption retained.
11. **Dealer licensing fees (Title 21 §6302):**
   - Application fee: **$100**
   - Renewal fee: **$50**
12. **Motor vehicle document fee (Title 30 §3002):**
   - Increases from **$4.25 per $100** to **$5.25 per $100** of purchase price (rounded to nearest dollar).
   - Mobile home increment remains **$3.75 per $100**.

## Who Benefits
- **DelDOT / Transportation Trust Fund (TTF):** Receives increased revenues to support DelDOT responsibilities and transit system management (explicit in fee impact sheet).
- **Users of Delaware transportation infrastructure and transit:** Indirectly supported through increased TTF funding for transit management and road/bridge improvements (as described in synopsis/fee impact sheet).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Owners of electric vehicles, plug-in hybrids, non-plug-in hybrids, and other fuel vehicles:** Subject to new annual add-on registration fees (up to **$900/year** depending on weight and category).
- **Motorists and drivers needing DMV services:** Pay increased fees for licenses, renewals, duplicates, endorsements, name changes, and taxicab authorizations.
- **Vehicle purchasers:** Pay higher document fees at titling due to **$5.25 per $100** increment (up from **$4.25 per $100**).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **General effective date:** **October 1, 2025** (Section 14), except as provided for the document fee transition.
- **Document fee transition rules (Section 15):**
  - **FY 2025 document fee rate** applies to **title work received by DMV prior to October 1, 2025** (including mailed title work **postmarked prior to October 1, 2025** but received after), even if processed after October 1.
  - Vehicles **sold prior to October 1, 2025** (shown by **bill of sale**) must be processed at the **FY 2025 fee rate**.
  - **All titles processed on or after November 1, 2025** must use the **new fee rates effective October 1, 2025**, **regardless of purchase date**.
- **Act approval date:** **June 24, 2025** (enacted Chapter 40, Volume 85).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **CLP forfeiture:** If the applicant does not pass required examinations within the 1-year CLP period, the permit is void and the **$10 fee is forfeited** (Title 21 §2608).
- **Fee payment as condition of issuance:** Alternative fuel add-on fees and other updated fees are required “at the time of application” and “prior to issuance” of registration/credentials (as stated in §§2151/2151A and related sections).
- **Dealer licensing compliance:** Dealerships must keep the required business license(s) at the business location and available for inspection (Title 21 §6302).
