## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Establish the Office of Suicide Prevention within the Division of Substance Abuse and Mental Health (DSAMH) in the Department of Health and Social Services, and assign it statewide coordination, planning, reporting, and grant-related duties for suicide prevention, intervention, and postvention.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a statutory **Office of Suicide Prevention** under **DSAMH** (Title 16, Ch. 62, new § 6203).  
- Directs the Office to serve (with **DSCYF**) as a centralized place to reach **all suicide prevention resources** in Delaware.  
- Requires the Office to **create the State’s Suicide Prevention Plan** (referenced in § 6201) **by January 1, 2026**, with approval of the **suicide prevention coalition**.  
- Requires the Plan be **updated no fewer than every 3 years**.  
- Requires an **annual report** to the General Assembly and Governor; **first report due October 15, 2026**.  
- Provides the Office will be led by a **Director** reporting to the **Director of DSAMH**; additional employees may be hired if recommended and funded.  
- Fiscal note anticipates **1.0 FTE Director**, estimated **$104,909 annually** (includes **OECs at 33.34%**), with first-year costs estimated at **9 months**.  
- Funding assumption: use an **existing vacant FTE** reclassified; cover costs with federal grants (Mental Health Block Grant + **988 Cooperative Agreement Grant**) until the grant **expires September 2026**, then shift to the **988 Surcharge Fund**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Centralized statewide coordination**: The Office is tasked to be a statewide resource hub (in coordination with **DSCYF**) for suicide prevention resources.  
- **Statewide planning requirement with deadlines**: Requires a State Suicide Prevention Plan by **January 1, 2026** and updates at least **every 3 years**, creating an ongoing planning cycle.  
- **Accountability through reporting**: Requires an annual report to the General Assembly and Governor, with the first due **October 15, 2026**.  
- **Grant-seeking and funding oversight role**: Explicitly authorizes overseeing funding application processes and seeking grants to support prevention/intervention/postvention activities.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)

- **Future funding dependency/transition**: Fiscal note assumes reliance on federal grant funding (including the **988 Cooperative Agreement Grant**) that **expires in September 2026**, after which funding is assumed to shift to the **988 Surcharge Fund**.  
- **Administrative workload requirements**: Mandated plan creation by **January 1, 2026**, triennial updates, and annual reporting impose continuing administrative duties.
