## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Extend the payment deadline for certain New Castle County property tax bills for the **2025–2026 tax year** from **November 30, 2025** to **December 31, 2025** when those bills are **supplemented or adjusted** under Chapter 135, Volume 85.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes the deadline for payment of certain **2025–2026** property tax bills in **New Castle County** from **11/30/2025** to **12/31/2025**.
- Applies specifically to property tax bills that are **“supplemented or adjusted”** after New Castle County receives a **new warrant** and revises billing using **new local school tax rates** set by the district.
- Enacted due to delays in timely delivery of property tax invoices caused by **expedited litigation** in the Court of Chancery: **Newark Property Association, et al. v. State of Delaware, et al., C.A. No. 2025-1031-LWW**.
- Intended to give taxpayers a **commercially reasonable period** to review and pay invoices.
- Intended to help prevent **statutorily-mandated penalties and interest** from being imposed inequitably due to late invoice delivery.
- Act was **approved November 14, 2025**.
