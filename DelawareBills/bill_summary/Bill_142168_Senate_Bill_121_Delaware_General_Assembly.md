## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the Charter of the Town of Dewey Beach to (1) align/streamline charter budget language, (2) add “parking administration” to the Town Manager’s enumerated duties, (3) allow the Town Manager (with Audit Committee concurrence) to terminate the independent auditor contract, (4) require an uncertified Chief of Police hire to obtain Delaware police training certification within **1 year**, (5) require the Beach Patrol Captain to serve under a contract with a term of **no more than 3 years**, and (6) clarify/centralize charter provisions on municipal borrowing and tax-exempt debt with an overall debt cap of **$3,000,000** (unless exceeded via **referendum** as stated in Section 26).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Removes duplicative charter language on budget requirements/process in **Section 8(c)** in favor of budget language “currently included in **Section 22 – Town Budget**.”  
- Adds **parking administration** to the Town Manager’s listed responsibilities (Charter **Section 16(f)**).  
- Authorizes the **Town Manager**, **with concurrence of the Audit Committee**, to terminate the independent audit firm’s contract (Charter **Section 17(a)**).  
- Requires any Chief of Police candidate lacking Delaware Council on Police Training certification to obtain certification **within 1 year of employment** as a **condition of employment** (Charter **Section 19(b)**; Regulation 800, Title 1 of the Delaware Administrative Code referenced).  
- Requires the **Beach Patrol Captain** to serve under an employment contract with a **fixed term not exceeding 3 years**; contract **must not automatically renew** (Charter **Section 19(d)(1)**).  
- Strikes redundant borrowing-power language in **Section 23(a)(10)** in favor of the borrowing section (**Section 26**).  
- Clarifies that Town borrowing is capped at **$3,000,000** aggregate unless **approved by referendum**, and that Town debt instruments are **exempt from all State, county, or municipal taxes** (Charter **Section 26(a)**).  
- Act was **approved June 30, 2025**.
