## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Propose the first leg of a Delaware Constitution amendment (Article V) to (1) create an explicit constitutional right for any duly registered, qualified elector to vote by absentee ballot **without an excuse**, (2) establish an **election-cycle** absentee ballot request framework (with limited **permanent absentee status** exceptions), and (3) require an absentee-ballot **oath/affirmation** that the vote is free from improper influence.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a constitutional **right to vote by absentee ballot without an excuse** for any duly registered, qualified Delaware elector.  
- Replaces the current constitutional list of allowable absentee excuses with a broad no-excuse right, addressing **Albence v. Higgins, 2022 Del. LEXIS 377 (Del. 2022)** (per synopsis).  
- Requires an absentee ballot to be **requested once per “election cycle”** to receive absentee ballots for elections in that cycle.  
- Defines **“election cycle”** as **the day after the most recent general election through the date of the next general election**.  
- Allows **permanent absentee status** for specified categories (including UOCAVA and permanent disability), largely mirroring current statutory reasons (synopsis reference to **15 Del. C. § 5503(k)**).  
- Requires an **oath/affirmation on each absentee ballot** that the voter’s vote is **free from improper influence**.  
- For voters on **permanent absentee status**, requires an **oath/affirmation each election** that they remain eligible for permanent status.  
- As a constitutional amendment, it requires **two-thirds vote in each house** and must pass **again after the next general election** by the next General Assembly (synopsis).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expands access to absentee voting** by establishing an “absolute right” to vote absentee **without an excuse** (new Art. V, §4A(a); synopsis).  
- **Reduces repeat paperwork within an election cycle** by allowing one absentee request to cover all elections in the cycle (Art. V, §4A(b)(1)b).  
- **Provides an integrity-related attestation** by requiring an oath/affirmation that the vote is free from improper influence (Art. V, §4A(b)(2)).  
- **Provides continuity for specific voters** (e.g., uniformed/overseas, permanent disability) through **permanent absentee status** (Art. V, §4A(c)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Still requires an affirmative request** at least once per election cycle for most voters (Art. V, §4A(b)(1)a–b), which may be a barrier for voters who expect automatic absentee ballot delivery.  
- **Adds an ongoing eligibility attestation requirement** for permanent absentee voters for **each election** they vote absentee (Art. V, §4A(d)).  
- **Constitutional amendment timing delays implementation certainty**, because the same changes must be passed again **after the next general election** (synopsis).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Article V, §4A rewrite/expansion** to provide:  
  - **No-excuse absentee voting right**: “A qualified elector…has the right to vote by absentee ballot without an excuse.” (Art. V, §4A(a)).  
  - **Election-cycle request model**:  
    - An absentee ballot must be requested for an election occurring in an **election cycle** (Art. V, §4A(b)(1)a).  
    - **One request per election cycle**: if requested once in the cycle, “no further requests are required” for elections in that cycle (Art. V, §4A(b)(1)b).  
    - **Definition** of “election cycle”: day after most recent general election through the next general election (Art. V, §4A(b)(1)c).  
  - **Improper influence oath** on each absentee ballot (Art. V, §4A(b)(2)).  
  - **Permanent absentee status** eligibility categories (Art. V, §4A(c)(1)–(9)), including: public service voters/spouses/dependents accompanying; armed forces/merchant marine; Red Cross/USO attached to armed forces; illness/injury from service; UOCAVA; other federal-law authorization; sick or permanently physically disabled; caregiving occupation for parent/spouse/child at home needing constant care; U.S. citizens temporarily residing outside U.S. territorial limits.  
  - **Per-election eligibility oath** for permanent absentee voters (Art. V, §4A(d)).  
- **Article V, §3(c) change**: clarifies that the absentee-ballot oath/affirmation required under §4A is **in lieu of** the oath/affirmation otherwise required under §3 (Art. V, §3(c) as amended).

## Who Benefits
- Any **duly registered, qualified elector** who prefers or needs to vote absentee **without stating an excuse** (Art. V, §4A(a)).  
- Voters who want to make **one absentee request per election cycle** rather than for each election (Art. V, §4A(b)(1)b).  
- Voters eligible for **permanent absentee status**, including UOCAVA-covered voters, certain service-related categories, permanently disabled voters, certain caregivers, and certain U.S. citizens temporarily living abroad (Art. V, §4A(c)).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Voters who might have difficulty making an absentee request at least once per election cycle (Art. V, §4A(b)(1)a–b).  
- Voters on permanent absentee status who must complete an **oath/affirmation each election** to confirm ongoing eligibility (Art. V, §4A(d)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Compliance requirements**:  
  - Absentee voting requires an absentee ballot request per **election cycle** unless permanent absentee status applies (Art. V, §4A(b)(1); §4A(c)).

  - Permanent absentee voters must make an **oath or affirmation each election** that they remain eligible (Art. V, §4A(d)).
