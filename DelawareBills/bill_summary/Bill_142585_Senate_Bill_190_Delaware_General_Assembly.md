## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Require the State of Delaware and political subdivisions (e.g., counties, municipalities) to notify the Delaware Farm Bureau when seeking bids to lease certain publicly owned agricultural land, to broaden farmer awareness of bidding opportunities.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Applies to “State-owned agricultural land” defined as **land of 10 acres or more** that is **owned by the State or a political subdivision** and **used or intended for agricultural use** (as defined in **Title 3, § 902**).
- When the State or a political subdivision **seeks bids** to lease covered land (short-term or long-term), it must provide **notice of the request for bids** to the **Delaware Farm Bureau (or its successor)** for distribution/making available to farmers.
- This Farm Bureau notice is **in addition to** any other notice already required under **state or local** procurement laws, ordinances, or regulations.
- The Farm Bureau notice requirement **does not apply** to **renewals** of existing leases **when the tenant remains the same**.
- The act clarifies applicability to **political subdivisions**, not only State-owned land (technical/title and drafting clarifications).
- **Approved August 12, 2025** (as enacted in Volume 85, Chapter 127).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Broader dissemination of bid opportunities to farmers:** The bill requires notice be sent to the **Delaware Farm Bureau** for distribution or availability to farmers, potentially increasing awareness of leasing opportunities for qualifying public agricultural land.
- **Adds transparency without replacing existing requirements:** Because the notice is **in addition to** other legally required notices, it increases outreach while keeping existing procurement/public-notice processes intact.
- **Uniform requirement across government levels:** Explicitly covers **both the State and political subdivisions**, creating a consistent outreach step for covered leases.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Adds an administrative step to leasing processes:** Agencies and political subdivisions must provide Farm Bureau notice whenever they seek bids for covered leases (except same-tenant renewals), which may add process time or staff workload.
- **Limited distribution channel specified:** Notice must go to the **Delaware Farm Bureau (or successor)**; the bill does not specify alternative or additional farmer outreach methods beyond existing legal notice requirements.
