## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14, §122 of the Delaware Code to update Delaware Department of Education (DOE) regulation requirements on maintaining personnel records so the requirements explicitly include **charter schools**, and to make technical drafting corrections.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires DOE regulations to cover **personnel record retention for employees of both school districts and charter schools**.  
- Directs DOE regulations to ensure **permanent maintenance** of personnel records for **at least the number of years required by the Delaware Public Archives**.  
- Clarifies that retention requirements apply to **employees whose employment has terminated**.  
- Specifies categories of records that must be retained for each employee, including **annual salaries and “sick salaries”** and **sick leave and vacation information**.  
- Requires DOE regulations to set a retention period that **equals or exceeds** Delaware Public Archives’ required retention period.  
- Makes **technical corrections** to align with the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual.  
- No dollar amounts, appropriations, or explicit compliance deadlines are included in the bill text.

- **Improves record continuity for separated employees**: The amended language explicitly includes records of employees “whose employment has terminated,” supporting consistent documentation retention.  
- **Aligns retention time with Delaware Public Archives requirements**: Tying the retention period to Public Archives standards may reduce ambiguity about minimum retention length.
