## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Update Delaware driver’s license “veteran” designation law to use the broader term “uniformed services of the United States,” explicitly including certain non–Department of Defense uniformed services (U.S. Public Health Service Commissioned Corps and National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), and to recognize additional documents as “satisfactory proof” for those veterans.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Replaces “United States Armed Forces or of the National Guard” with “uniformed services of the United States” in 21 Del. C. § 2718(g).  
- Defines “uniformed services of the United States” to include: Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard, U.S. Public Health Service Commissioned Corps (USPHS), NOAA, reserve components, and the National Guard.  
- Maintains DMV’s duty to add a veteran designation on a driver license upon submission of “satisfactory proof.”  
- Clarifies eligibility: service in the uniformed services and discharge/release under conditions other than dishonorable.  
- Adds specific “satisfactory proof” documents for USPHS and NOAA veterans: **PHS Form 1867** and **NOAA Form 56-16**.  
- Makes technical drafting corrections to conform with the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual.  
- Enacted as **Volume 85, Chapter 36**, approved **June 24, 2025**.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Terminology change:** Replaces “United States Armed Forces or of the National Guard” with **“uniformed services of the United States.”** (21 Del. C. § 2718(g))  
- **New statutory definition:** “Uniformed services of the United States” expressly includes:
  - United States Army  
  - United States Marine Corps  
  - United States Navy  
  - United States Air Force  
  - United States Space Force  
  - United States Coast Guard  
  - **United States Public Health Service Commissioned Corps**  
  - **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA)**  
  - a reserve component of any of the service branches  
  - the National Guard  
- **Eligibility standard maintained/clarified:** Veteran designation available if applicant served in covered services and was discharged/released under conditions **other than dishonorable**.  
- **Adds proof documents:**  
  - **PHS Form 1867** (USPHS Statement of Service – Verification of Status of Commissioned Officers)  
  - **NOAA Form 56-16** (Report of Transfer or Discharge)
