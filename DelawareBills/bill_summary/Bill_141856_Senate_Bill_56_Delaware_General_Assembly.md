## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 25 (manufactured home communities) to clarify and update procedures for transferring title of a manufactured home located in a manufactured home community, including transfers upon a homeowner’s death, and to reduce ambiguities that could be used to block home or lease transfers. (Synopsis; §§ 7003, 7007, 7008, 7013)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Defines “heir” to include people receiving an interest in a manufactured home via will, intestate succession, or transfer-on-death designation. (§ 7003)
- Prohibits lot-rental agreements from terminating solely due to the death of any tenant (including the last remaining tenant). (§ 7008(b)(22); § 7013(b)(2))
- Provides that lot-rental agreements transfer automatically to: (i) a buyer/transferee from a prior homeowner or heir, (ii) heirs, and (iii) the estate of a deceased homeowner. (§ 7013(b)(1))
- Limits what an heir can be required to do: no tenant application unless the heir intends to reside; if intending to reside, only the criminal background portion may be required. (§ 7013(b)(3))
- Creates timing protections after death: community owner may not start a summary possession action for at least **90 days** after the death of the last remaining tenant (locks may be changed for safety). (§ 7013(b)(5))
- Revises right-of-first-refusal mechanics: homeowner must give notice at least **3 weeks** before sale/transfer; community owner has **7 days** to respond; if exercised, owner pays **10% above** contract price and provides a **5% nonrefundable deposit**. (§ 7013(c))
- Requires exterior inspection deadlines and cure timelines: inspection within **10 days** of notice; violation list within **3 days** after inspection; buyer/transferee generally has **3 years** to complete required changes or face summary possession. (§ 7013(d)(2)-(5))
- Requires written reasons for rejecting a prospective transferee and shortens decision deadline from **15 days to 10 days** after receipt of a completed application package. (§ 7013(b)(3)-(4))

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Protects inherited home value and continuity of housing:** lease cannot end just because a tenant dies, and heirs/estates are explicitly covered for transfers and occupancy. (§§ 7008(b)(22), 7013(b)(1)-(3))
- **Adds due process and clarity:** requires written, specific grounds for rejecting a transferee and sets firm deadlines (e.g., **10 days** for application decision; **7 days** for right-of-first-refusal response). (§ 7013(b)(3)-(4); § 7013(c)(1)b.1)
- **Reduces ability to delay transfers through information requests/inspections:** the **7-day** right-of-first-refusal decision period “may not be extended” due to owner requests. (§ 7013(c)(1)b.1)
- **Limits “age-only” exclusion standards:** landlord standards cannot use age as the exclusive or dominant criterion barring sale/retention. (§ 7007(c)(1))
- **Sets structured post-sale compliance timeframe:** buyers/transferees get up to **3 years** to complete non-safety standard-related changes. (§ 7013(d)(4))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increases community owner purchase cost exposure:** if the owner exercises the right of first refusal, purchase price is **10% higher** than the contract price and requires a **5% nonrefundable deposit**. (§ 7013(c)(1)b.2)
- **Creates additional administrative timelines and notice burdens:** mandatory inspection and written list deadlines (inspection within **10 days**; list within **3 days**), plus required written rejection reasons and notice requirements. (§ 7013(d)(2)-(3); § 7013(b)(3)-(4))
- **Limits community owner screening for heirs who will reside:** only criminal background portion of an application may be required, and partial-interest heirs cannot be barred from residing. (§ 7013(b)(3)(a)-(b))
- **Extends time before eviction proceedings after death:** community owner must wait at least **90 days** after death of last tenant before starting summary possession action. (§ 7013(b)(5))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Adds definition of “Heir.”** (§ 7003)
- **Bans lease provisions terminating on death** of any tenant, including last remaining tenant. (§ 7008(b)(22))
- **Automatic transfer of lot-rental agreement** to buyers/transferees, heirs, and estates upon death-related events. (§ 7013(b)(1))
- **Heir protections regarding residency and screening:**
  - no application required unless the heir intends to reside;
  - if intending to reside, only criminal background portion may be required;
  - partial-interest heirs cannot be prohibited from residing;
  - heirs not occupying is not a lease violation (but non-conflicting lease terms still apply). (§ 7013(b)(3)-(4))
- **Estate administration defense to nonpayment summary possession**, though late fees and court costs may still apply. (§ 7013(b)(4))
- **90-day waiting period** after death of last tenant before commencing summary possession; locks may be changed for safety. (§ 7013(b)(5))
- **Notice duties** for personal representatives/heirs “as soon as practicable” to provide ownership/residency contact details. (§ 7013(b)(6))
- **Right of first refusal / community owner purchase option** with specified notice (**≥3 weeks**), response (**7 days**), price premium (**10%**), deposit (**5% nonrefundable**), and same settlement date requirement; with listed exceptions (foreclosure, certain family transfers/trusts, co-owners, gifts/devise/operation of law). (§ 7013(c)(1)-(2))
- **Exterior inspection and compliance timeline framework:** inspection within **10 days** of notice; violations list within **3 days** post-inspection; **3 years** to complete changes; failure can lead to summary possession. (§ 7013(d))
- **Transferee application processing requirements:** written specific rejection reasons; decision deadline reduced to **10 days** after completed application package receipt. (§ 7013(b)(3)-(4))
- **If prospective transferee with a written purchase agreement is rejected,** community owner must purchase the home as if it exercised its purchase right, using the same price/timing requirements. (§ 7013(b)(5))
- **Creates/clarifies tenant/owner remedies for “undue influence/pressure,”** including treble damages and limits on the landlord’s ability to exercise purchase rights for **365 days** upon willful misconduct findings, and treats violations as unlawful practices under Title 6. (Page 6 lines 159-168; Page 7 lines 174-182)

## Who Benefits
- **Heirs, estates, and family members** of deceased manufactured home owners (automatic lease transfer; limits on termination; occupancy protections; 90-day delay before summary possession). (§ 7013(b))
- **Current homeowners/sellers** seeking to sell while keeping the home in the community (clear notice/inspection timelines; limits on age-only standards; written reasons for rejection; constraints on delaying tactics). (§§ 7007(c), 7013(b)-(d))
- **Prospective buyers/transferees** (defined timelines; 3-year period to complete non-safety changes; required written explanation for rejection). (§ 7013(b)(3)-(4); § 7013(d)(4))

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Manufactured home community owners/landlords** (restricted ability to terminate after death; increased procedural requirements and deadlines; potential purchase obligations and higher purchase premium if exercising right of first refusal; potential liability for willful undue influence/pressure). (§ 7013(b)(2), (b)(5), (c), (d), (c)(6) language on damages/penalties)
- **Some buyers/sellers** could be affected by community-owner purchase rights that may alter the planned third-party transaction (right of first refusal and mandatory sale if exercised). (§ 7013(c)(1)b.3)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Not specified** for when the Act takes effect.
- Operational deadlines created/clarified in the bill text include:
  - Tenant request for reevaluation of standards compliance: landlord must reevaluate within **10 days** of written request. (§ 7007(c)(2))
  - Seller notice before sale/transfer: at least **3 weeks** prior. (§ 7013(c)(1))
  - Community owner response to exercise purchase option: within **7 days** of the homeowner’s notice; period cannot be extended. (§ 7013(c)(1)b.1)
  - Deposit if purchase option exercised: **5% nonrefundable** deposit at time of notice of intent to exercise. (§ 7013(c)(1)b.2)
  - Exterior inspection (if no purchase): within **10 days** of notice. (§ 7013(d)(2))
  - Written list of violations: within **3 days** of inspection. (§ 7013(d)(3))
  - Time to complete required changes: **3 years** from resale/transfer date (with “reasonable time” completion once work begins). (§ 7013(d)(4))
  - Application decision: within **10 days** of receipt of a completed application package (changed from 15). (§ 7013(b)(4))
  - After death of last tenant: no summary possession action for at least **90 days**. (§ 7013(b)(5))
  - Landlord disqualification from exercising purchase right after willful undue influence/pressure finding: **365 days** (each offense subject to a 365-day penalty). (Page 7 lines 180-182)
  - Historic standards clause references **January 1, 2003** and **January 1, 2004** (appears to be legacy statutory language retained in text). (§ 7013(e))

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Summary possession exposure**:
  - Buyer/transferee who does not complete required changes under written standards may be subject to summary possession proceedings under Chapter 57. (§ 7013(d)(5))
  - After death, community owner must wait **90 days** before starting summary possession; may change locks for safety. (§ 7013(b)(5))
- **Civil remedies for undue influence/pressure and related misconduct**:
  - Tenant may sue for actual damages where landlord willfully exerted undue influence/pressure, failed fair evaluation, unreasonably denied application, or engaged in actions aimed at preventing transfer. (Page 6 lines 161-170)
  - If willful conduct found: landlord liable for **3 times actual damages** and reasonable court costs **or** **3 times the monthly rent**, whichever is higher. (Page 7 lines 174-176)
  - Additional consequence: landlord may not exercise its right to buy any tenant’s home for **365 days**; “each offense” carries a **365-day** penalty. (Page 7 lines 180-182)
- **Unlawful practice designation**: violations by a community owner of this section are an unlawful practice under **§ 2513 of Title 6** and a violation of **Subchapter II, Chapter 25 of Title 6**. (Page 7 lines 177-178)
- **Compliance requirements**: written standards limited in scope; required written notices; required written rejection reasons; mandatory inspection/list deadlines; required notice to community owner by heirs/personal representative “as soon as practicable.” (§§ 7007(c), 7013(b)(6), 7013(b)(3)-(4), 7013(c)-(d))
