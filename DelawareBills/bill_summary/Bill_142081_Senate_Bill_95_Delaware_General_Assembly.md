## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s General Corporation Law (Title 8) to update rules on (1) fee-shifting and forum-selection provisions affecting stockholder litigation, (2) Secretary of State filing corrections (including nullification), (3) registered office/agent requirements, (4) bearer-form equity instruments, and (5) certain franchise tax administration rules and timing.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Expands the ban on “fee-shifting” provisions** in certificates of incorporation and bylaws to cover **internal corporate claims and other stockholder-brought claims** brought in a stockholder capacity or in the right of the corporation. (§§ 102(f), 109(b))
- **Authorizes forum-selection provisions for certain non-internal claims** related to corporate affairs, but **must allow** stockholders to bring such claims in **at least 1 court in Delaware with jurisdiction**. (§ 115(c))
- **Allows a “certificate of correction” to nullify** a previously filed instrument with the Secretary of State (not just correct it), and sets effectiveness rules for those substantially adversely affected. (§ 103(f))
- **Clarifies “registered office” meaning** as the **address of the registered agent in Delaware**; removes automatic treatment of registered office as “principal office/place of business.” (§ 131)
- **Prohibits registered agents from operating solely via virtual office and/or mail forwarding**; defines “virtual office” as performing duties solely via internet/remote communication. (§ 132(b)(2))
- **Eliminates bearer-form scrip/warrants** for fractional shares; registered form remains permitted. (§ 155)
- **Updates filings and franchise tax administration**, including: added disclosures in revocation/restoration filings; payment/filing obligations when validating acts during void/forfeited periods; limits refunds tied to correction/validation; and **repeals the regulated investment company (RIC) alternative franchise tax rate** effective for tax years beginning **on or after January 1, 2026**. (§§ 311, 312(g), 377(e), 503, 505)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Protects stockholders from fee-shifting exposure** in governing documents by prohibiting provisions imposing liability for attorneys’ fees/expenses on stockholders for covered claims. (§§ 102(f), 109(b))
- **Reduces Delaware-jurisdiction “lockout” risk** for certain non-internal claims by requiring at least **one Delaware court with jurisdiction** remain available under forum-selection provisions. (§ 115(c))
- **Improves clarity/correctability of state filings** by expressly permitting **nullification** of inaccurate/defective filings through a certificate of correction. (§ 103(f))
- **Tightens registered agent operational requirements** (no purely virtual/mail-forwarding-only operations), potentially strengthening service-of-process reliability and identity verification practices referenced in § 132(b). (§ 132(b))
- **Aligns fractional-share instrument rules with federal policy** cited in the synopsis (Corporate Transparency Act context) by removing bearer-form scrip/warrants. (§ 155)
- **Enhances franchise tax compliance** by requiring back annual reports/taxes (with interest) when a validation relates to a period when the corporation was forfeited/void. (§ 312(g))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduces corporations’ flexibility to deter litigation via fee-shifting in charters/bylaws** because such provisions are prohibited for internal corporate claims and other covered stockholder-brought claims. (§§ 102(f), 109(b))
- **Limits registered agent business models** by barring performance of registered-agent functions solely through a virtual office and/or mail forwarding service. (§ 132(b)(2))
- **Removes an alternative franchise tax method for regulated investment companies** by repealing § 503(h), potentially affecting entities that previously qualified for that calculation (repeal effective for tax years beginning on or after **January 1, 2026**). (§ 503(h) repealed; § 16)
- **Restricts use of bearer-form instruments** for fractional equity interests by eliminating bearer-form scrip/warrants. (§ 155)
- **Eliminates refunds tied to certain corrective/validation filings**, stating no refund will be granted in connection with certificates of correction (§ 103(f)) or validation (§ 204) filings. (§ 505(a); also § 503(e))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Scope of “claims that are not internal corporate claims”** covered by § 115(c) depends on whether they “relate to the business of the corporation, the conduct of its affairs, or the rights or powers” of the corporation and specified persons; the statute does not enumerate claim types. (§ 115(c); synopsis notes no specific definition)
- **Application details of the registered-agent prohibition**: the bill defines “virtual office,” but does not detail enforcement mechanisms or how “solely” will be assessed operationally. (§ 132(b)(2))
- **Practical impact of repealing the RIC alternative rate**: the bill repeals § 503(h) but does not state replacement guidance beyond default computations under other subsections. (§ 503(h) repealed)

## Fiscal or Administrative Impact
- **Administrative changes for corporations and registered agents**:
  - Certificates of correction may now be used to **nullify** filings. (§ 103(f))
  - Revocation/restoration filings must include **dates of original incorporation filing and dissolution filing**. (§ 311(a)(4))
  - Foreign corporations seeking reinstatement must file **all annual reports** and pay **all required fees** that would have been due during forfeiture. (§ 377(e))
  - Annual franchise tax report due **on or before March 1** continues; content requirements are revised, including disclosures about principal place of business and directors, and “nature of the business.” (§ 502(a))
- **Franchise tax consequences**:
  - If a § 204 certificate of validation relates to a forfeited/void period, the corporation must file/pay **annual reports and annual franchise taxes that would have been required** during that period, **including interest**, at the time of validation filing. (§ 312(g))
  - Filing a certificate of validation **does not reduce** prior-period franchise tax/interest, **does not entitle to a refund**, and additional amounts for prior periods are calculated at **current rates**. (§ 503(e))
  - **No refund** is granted in connection with a certificate of correction (§ 103(f)) or validation (§ 204). (§ 505(a))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Fee-shifting prohibition expanded**: Certificates and bylaws may not impose liability on a stockholder for attorneys’ fees/expenses of the corporation or any other party in connection with:
  - an **internal corporate claim** (as defined in § 115), **or**
  - **any other claim** a stockholder brings **acting as a stockholder or in the right of the corporation**. (§§ 102(f), 109(b))
- **Forum selection for non-internal claims**: Certificates/bylaws may require specified forums for certain non-internal claims, but the provision must:
  - be consistent with jurisdictional requirements, and
  - allow suit in **at least one Delaware court with jurisdiction** over the claim. (§ 115(c))
- **Secretary of State filings—correction or nullification**: A certificate of correction may now **correct or nullify** an instrument; nullification must specify the defect/inaccuracy and provide for nullification; effectiveness is generally retroactive to original filing date except for substantially adversely affected persons. (§ 103(f))
- **Registered office definition and treatment updated**: “Registered office” means the **registered agent’s Delaware address**; Title 8 no longer automatically treats registered office as principal office/place of business. (§ 131)
- **Registered agent operational rule**: Registered agents cannot perform duties solely through a **virtual office** and/or **mail forwarding service**; “virtual office” is defined. (§ 132(b)(2))
- **Fractional shares**: Removes ability to issue scrip/warrants in **bearer form** (registered form remains). (§ 155)
- **Merger certificate content**: Removes requirement in § 252(c) to list the authorized capital stock of each foreign constituent corporation that ceases to exist. (§ 252(c))
- **Revocation/restoration filing content**: Adds required dates (original certificate filing date; dissolution filing date). (§ 311)
- **Validation during void/forfeited periods—tax/report catch-up**: Requires filing/paying missed annual reports/taxes (with interest) when validation relates to forfeited/void time. (§ 312(g))
- **RIC franchise tax alternative repealed**: Repeals § 503(h) effective for tax years beginning **on or after January 1, 2026**. (§ 16)

## Who Benefits
- **Stockholders**: benefit from expanded statutory protection against charter/bylaw fee-shifting for covered claims. (§§ 102(f), 109(b))
- **Stockholders bringing certain non-internal claims**: benefit from requirement that forum-selection provisions must allow such claims in at least **one Delaware court with jurisdiction**. (§ 115(c))
- **State filing users (corporations and practitioners)**: benefit from explicit ability to **nullify** defective/inaccurate filed instruments through a certificate of correction. (§ 103(f))
- **Delaware courts/Delaware forum availability**: strengthened role for Delaware forums by requiring at least one in-state court with jurisdiction for covered non-internal claims under forum provisions. (§ 115(c))

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Corporations seeking to use fee-shifting in charters/bylaws**: prohibited for internal corporate claims and other covered stockholder-brought claims. (§§ 102(f), 109(b))
- **Registered agent businesses relying on purely virtual/mail-forwarding models**: prohibited from performing duties solely through those means. (§ 132(b)(2))
- **Regulated investment companies (RICs)**: lose the option to use the repealed alternative franchise tax computation under § 503(h) for tax years beginning **on or after January 1, 2026**. (§ 16)
- **Corporations using bearer-form fractional-interest instruments**: bearer-form scrip/warrants are no longer permitted. (§ 155)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Mandatory prohibitions (governing documents):**
  - Certificates/bylaws **may not** include prohibited fee-shifting provisions for covered claims. (§§ 102(f), 109(b))
- **Forum-selection compliance condition:**
  - For covered non-internal claims, any forum-selection requirement must allow suit in **at least 1 Delaware court with jurisdiction**. (§ 115(c))
- **Registered agent compliance:**
  - Registered agents **may not** perform duties solely via a **virtual office** and/or **mail forwarding service**. (§ 132(b)(2))
  - Registered agents must satisfy regulations established by the Secretary regarding **verification of identity** of contacts/individuals for which records are maintained (as referenced in § 132(b)(1)e). (§ 132(b)(1)e)
- **Tax/report compliance triggers:**
  - If a § 204 validation relates to a forfeited/void period, **annual reports and annual franchise taxes that would have been required**, **including interest**, must be filed/paid **at the time of filing** the certificate of validation. (§ 312(g))
  - **No refund** is granted in connection with a certificate of correction (§ 103(f)) or certificate of validation (§ 204). (§ 505(a); also § 503(e))
