## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29 (29 Del. C. § 5532) to provide specified percentage increases to certain retired State employees’ monthly service, disability, and survivor pensions, effective July 1, 2025, subject to annual appropriation.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases certain monthly service, disability, and survivor pensions payable on **July 1, 2025**.  
- Provides a **3%** increase for pensions that were effective **on or before June 30, 2005**.  
- Provides a **2%** increase for pensions that were effective **after June 30, 2005, but on or before June 30, 2020**.  
- Provides **no increase** for pensions that were effective **after June 30, 2020** (per fiscal note assumptions).  
- Increases must be paid through **June 30, 2026**, and each fiscal year thereafter **only if funds are appropriated** by the General Assembly pursuant to **§ 5544 and § 5548**.  
- Applies to pensions in the **State Employees’ Pension Plan**, the **State Judiciary Retirement Fund**, and the **New State Police Retirement Fund** (per fiscal note).  
- Fiscal note estimates an actuarial liability increase of **$127,090,900**, with a first-year funding requirement of **$28,674,500** starting in **FY 2026** (assuming 5-year amortization).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Higher monthly benefits** for eligible retirees and survivors starting **July 1, 2025**: **3%** or **2%** depending on pension effective date (29 Del. C. § 5532(t)).  
- Targets increases to **older pension cohorts** (effective dates up to **June 30, 2020**) while excluding the most recent cohort (after **June 30, 2020**) (fiscal note; bill text includes the date brackets).  
- Establishes that increases are to continue beyond the first year, **through June 30, 2026 and every fiscal year thereafter**, contingent on appropriations (bill text).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Creates significant added pension cost: fiscal note estimates **$127,090,900** increase in actuarial liability across three plans.  
- Requires additional annual contributions: first-year funding requirement **$28,674,500** in **FY 2026**, with subsequent payments assumed to rise **2.5% annually** over the remaining four years of amortization (fiscal note).  
- **No increase** for pensions effective **after June 30, 2020** (fiscal note), creating unequal treatment among retirees based on retirement date.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The bill text conditions ongoing payments on appropriations under **§ 5544** and **§ 5548**, but does not specify what occurs operationally if appropriations are not made in a later fiscal year (bill text).  
- The bill text in § 5532(t) does not explicitly state the “**after June 30, 2020, no increase**” rule; that appears in the **fiscal note assumptions**.  
- The bill text references pensions “**payable on July 1, 2025**” but does not define treatment for pensions commencing shortly after that date (bill text).

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds a new pension increase provision in **29 Del. C. § 5532(t)**:
  - **3%** increase effective **July 1, 2025** for any monthly service, disability, and survivor pension based on a former service/disability pension **effective on or before June 30, 2005**, and **payable on July 1, 2025**.  
  - **2%** increase effective **July 1, 2025** for any monthly service, disability, and survivor pension based on a former service/disability pension **effective after June 30, 2005, but on or before June 30, 2020**, and **payable on July 1, 2025**.  
  - Provides that these increases must continue to be paid through **June 30, 2026** and **every fiscal year thereafter**, **provided that funds are appropriated** in accordance with **§ 5544** and **§ 5548**.

## Who Benefits
- Retired State employees (and eligible beneficiaries) receiving monthly **service, disability, or survivor pensions** that were effective:
  - **on or before June 30, 2005** (eligible for **3%**), or  
  - **after June 30, 2005 but on or before June 30, 2020** (eligible for **2%**),  
  and **payable on July 1, 2025** (bill text).  
- Members/beneficiaries of the **State Employees’ Pension Plan**, **State Judiciary Retirement Fund**, and **New State Police Retirement Fund** (fiscal note).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- State budgets/funding sources supporting pension contributions, including the **General Fund** and **Special Funds**, due to increased required contributions (fiscal note).  
- Retirees whose pensions were effective **after June 30, 2020**, who receive **no increase** (fiscal note).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- Act effective **upon signature by the Governor** (fiscal note assumption).  
- Pension increases effective **July 1, 2025** (bill text and fiscal note).  
- Increases must continue through **June 30, 2026**, and every fiscal year thereafter **if funds are appropriated** (bill text).  
- Fiscal note amortization assumption begins **FY 2026** (fiscal note).
