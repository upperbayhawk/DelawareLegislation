## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To strengthen Delaware’s health care workforce planning by expanding and formalizing the collection and sharing (for limited official purposes) of workforce-related licensing, demographic, and employment data during provider licensure and renewal—while restricting use and excluding certain personal data.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes the Delaware Division of Public Health (DPH) and the Delaware Health Care Commission (DHCC) to request and receive licensing, demographic, and employment-related data that the Division of Professional Regulation (DPR) collects and electronically stores for licensure purposes, to support workforce research and planning.  
- Requires DPR to support health care workforce research and planning by collaborating with relevant boards/agencies to identify workforce-related data and to collect the identified data during licensing or renewal.  
- Limits workforce-planning data collection to exclude “personal data such as home address or personal financial information.”  
- Clarifies that licensing data may be used only for “official state business,” expressly including: tracking supply; identifying HPSAs/MCTAs/MUA/Ps; workforce research and planning; understanding supply/demand/distribution/use; and informing workforce policy.  
- Allows DPH and DHCC to share licensing data with their contractors to carry out these purposes.  
- Expands/clarifies the list of affected licensing boards (including, for example, funeral services, massage/bodywork, and veterinary medicine, in addition to multiple human health professions).  
- Reaffirms confidentiality of individual data and prohibits state employees/commission members/contractors from using data for non-authorized purposes.  
- Enacted as Chapter 204, Volume 85; **approved September 3, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved workforce planning data**: The bill is expressly intended to address inadequate and inconsistent workforce data and to support “workforce research and planning” and policy development using more comprehensive information collected at licensure/renewal.  
- **Better targeting of shortage designations and initiatives**: Explicitly supports identifying and tracking federal shortage-area initiatives (HPSAs, MCTAs, MUA/Ps), which can guide resource allocation and program planning.  
- **Privacy guardrails in statute**: Explicitly bars inclusion of certain personal data (e.g., **home address** and **personal financial information**) for workforce planning purposes and reaffirms confidentiality and restricted use.  
- **Uses an existing administrative touchpoint**: Collecting data during licensing/renewal is described in the bill’s findings as a way to improve data “without overly burdening providers.”

## Key Policy Changes
- **Title 16, §123(c)(1)**: Authorizes DPH and DHCC to request and receive DPR licensing data (including license types and demographic/employment-related data to the extent collected and electronically stored) for licensure and for workforce research and planning under **Title 29, §8735(y)**.  
- **Data limitation**: Workforce research/planning data **may not include** “personal data such as home address or personal financial information.”  
- **Official state business purposes clarified/expanded**: Licensing data may only be used for official state business, including:
  - Measuring/tracking supply of licensed health care professionals  
  - Identifying/tracking HHS initiatives including **HPSAs**, **MCTAs**, **MUA/Ps** (as defined by HRSA)  
  - Workforce research and planning  
  - Understanding supply/demand/distribution/use of health care workers  
  - Informing health care workforce policy  
- **Contractor sharing authorized**: DPH and DHCC may share licensing data with contractors to carry out the subsection’s purposes.  
- **Boards affected list expanded/clarified**: Specifies affected boards, including (among others) medical, dentistry, nursing, mental health/chemical dependency, social work, pharmacy, PT/athletic trainers, speech/language/audiology/hearing aid dispensers, nursing home administrators, dietetics/nutrition, **funeral services**, **massage and bodywork**, and **veterinary medicine**.  
- **Confidentiality and use restrictions reaffirmed**: Individual data is confidential; public employees/commission members/contractors may not use data for purposes other than authorized statistical/forecasting/workforce research and planning/program purposes.  
- **Title 16, §123(h)**: Requires DPH Office of Healthcare Provider Resources to collaborate with DHCC and DPR to identify data DPR must collect during licensing/renewal for the authorized purposes.  
- **Title 29, §8735(y)**: Directs DPR to support workforce research and planning by collaborating to identify pertinent data and collecting it from applicants during licensing or renewal.
