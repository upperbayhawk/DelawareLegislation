## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Rename the Department of Human Resources’ “Division of Diversity, Equity and Inclusion” to the “Division of People and Culture” in Title 29, and update cross-references in Title 4 (marijuana oversight committee membership) and Title 29 (collaboration authority for the Office of Women’s Advancement and Advocacy).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Renames the “Division of Diversity, Equity and Inclusion” within the Department of Human Resources to “Division of People and Culture.”  
- Keeps the division established within the Department of Human Resources and retains its listed powers/duties (e.g., oversight previously assigned to the Governor’s Council on Equal Employment Opportunity).  
- Continues the role of the **Chief Diversity Officer** and the enumerated responsibilities (data analysis, education on nondiscrimination, recruitment/retention collaboration, metrics).  
- Updates the Delaware Marijuana Control Act Oversight Committee statute to reflect the renamed division in naming the Chief Diversity Officer as a committee member (or designee).  
- Updates statute for the Office of Women’s Advancement and Advocacy to reflect the renamed division as a collaboration partner.
