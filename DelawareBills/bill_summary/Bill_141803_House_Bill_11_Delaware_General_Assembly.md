## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s special education records law (14 Del. C. § 3130) to align with the federal Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) and clarify that a parent’s representative may obtain copies of a student’s special education records.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates 14 Del. C. § 3130 to better align with IDEA and its implementing regulations (per Synopsis).
- Clarifies that parents may act “personally or through a representative” to **inspect and review** relevant special education records.
- Clarifies that parents (personally or through a representative) have the right to **obtain copies** of records related to identification, evaluation, program/placement, and provision of FAPE.
- Keeps an explicit exception: copies may be obtained for all records **except “the actual evaluation or examination instrument.”**
- Allows copies to be provided **without charge** or, at the district/state agency’s discretion, for a fee **not to exceed actual cost**.
- Prohibits fees that **effectively prevent** parents from exercising their rights to inspect, review, and copy records.
- Maintains the right for parents (personally or through a representative) to **visit and observe** the child’s current or proposed public educational program.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clearer access for representatives:** Explicitly confirms that a parent’s representative may obtain copies of special education records (Synopsis; § 3130(a) and (b) wording).
- **Improved alignment with federal law:** Aligning state statute with IDEA and its regulations may reduce confusion and compliance conflicts (Synopsis).
- **Cost protections remain:** Caps fees at **actual cost** and forbids fees that block access, supporting parent access to records (§ 3130(b)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential administrative burden:** School districts/state agencies may have increased requests for copies by parent representatives (clarification in § 3130(b)); the bill does not provide additional resources.
- **No new procedural detail:** The bill does not specify timelines or processes for fulfilling requests, which may leave disputes unresolved by statute. (Not specified in source text.)

## Key Policy Changes
- **Records inspection/review:** Parents of a child with a disability may inspect and review relevant records **personally or through a representative** (§ 3130(a)).
- **Copies of records:** Parents (personally or through a representative) have the right to obtain copies of all records described in § 3130(a), **except the actual evaluation or examination instrument** (§ 3130(b)).
- **Fees:** Copies must be provided **without charge** or for a fee **not to exceed actual cost** (district/state agency discretion), and **fees cannot effectively prevent** exercise of rights (§ 3130(b)).
- **Observation rights preserved:** Parents may visit and observe their child’s current or proposed public educational program, personally or through a representative (§ 3130(c)).
- **Conforming/alignment change:** Act is intended to align state law with IDEA and its regulations (Synopsis).
