## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Allow licensed dental hygienists in Delaware to administer **local anesthesia** **under the direct supervision of a licensed dentist**, and require the State Board of Dentistry and Dental Hygiene to adopt implementing regulations.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands the statutory definition of the **“practice of dental hygiene”** to include **administration of local anesthesia** when performed **under direct supervision** and **in accordance with Board regulations**.  
- Defines **“local anesthesia”** to include elimination of sensation via **topical application** or **regional injection**, including **infiltration** and **nerve block** techniques.  
- Adds/clarifies **“direct supervision”**: the dentist must be **physically present for the duration**, **diagnose**, **authorize**, and **examine the patient before dismissal**.  
- Directs the State Board of Dentistry and Dental Hygiene to establish, by regulation, **requirements/standards (including permits)** for dental hygienists to administer local anesthesia under direct supervision.  
- Updates hygienist practice rules to state that while hygienists may practice under **general supervision** in authorized settings, they may **only administer local anesthesia under direct supervision** in **any setting**.  
- Sets a regulatory deadline: Board must adopt regulations **no later than January 1, 2026**.  
- Effective date for Sections 1–3 is the **earlier of**: **January 1, 2026**, or **publication of notice in the Register of Regulations** that final implementing regulations have been promulgated.  
- The enacted act was **approved August 25, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded service capacity within dental offices/facilities:** By allowing hygienists to administer local anesthesia (under direct supervision), dentists may be able to delegate this task consistent with Board-set standards. (Amended §1101; §1106; §1121)  
- **Clear supervision safeguard:** The bill specifies “direct supervision” requires **physical presence** and specific dentist actions (diagnosis, authorization, exam before dismissal). (Definition of “Direct supervision,” §1101)  
- **Regulatory standard-setting:** Requires the Board to adopt regulations and standards (including a permit framework) governing hygienist administration of local anesthesia. (§1106(a)(17); Section 4)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Implementation depends on regulations:** The practical ability for hygienists to administer local anesthesia hinges on Board regulations and standards that must be adopted. (Section 4; Section 5)  
- **Limits flexibility by requiring physical presence:** “Direct supervision” requires the dentist be **physically present for the duration** of the procedure, which may constrain use in settings where dentists are not continuously onsite. (§1101 definition of “Direct supervision”; §1121)

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
