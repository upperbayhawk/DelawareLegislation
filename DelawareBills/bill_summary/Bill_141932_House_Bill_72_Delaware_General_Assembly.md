## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware Code Title 14 to reduce the senior property tax credit residency (legal domicile) requirement from **10 consecutive years** to **3 consecutive years** for applicable seniors.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes eligibility for the **senior property tax credit** by lowering the required Delaware **legal domicile** period to **at least 3 consecutive years** for covered claimants.
- Maintains the basic eligibility framework: a **“qualified person”** must be **age 65 or more as of June 30** immediately prior to the county fiscal year, and the property must be **owned and occupied as the principal residence**.
- Codifies different rules by domicile-establishment date ranges, including a special rule that the **10-year** requirement applies **only for years 2018 through 2025** for certain claimants.
- For **all claimants after December 31, 2025**, eligibility requires **3 consecutive years** of legal domicile (as measured on **June 30** prior to the county fiscal year).
- Fiscal Note: in **FY2025**, **71,537** homeowners received the credit with an **average value of $429**; the State’s reimbursement appropriation was **$29.7 million**.
- Fiscal Note estimates additional State cost of **$10.3 million** in **FY2026**, **FY2027**, and **FY2028**.
- Act is assumed (Fiscal Note) to become effective **upon signature by the Governor**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expands eligibility** to seniors who have been legally domiciled in Delaware for **3 years** rather than **10 years**, increasing access to the credit (bill synopsis and amended §1917(c)(1)a.).
- Provides potential **property tax relief** (credit is **50% of the school tax levied or $500, whichever is less**—described in the Fiscal Note as current law).
- Creates a clear post-2025 statewide rule: **after December 31, 2025**, the domicile requirement is **3 consecutive years** (statutory text).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increases State costs** because the State reimburses school districts for issued credits; Fiscal Note estimates **$10.3 million** per year in **FY2026–FY2028**.
- May require increased annual budget appropriations above the referenced **FY2025 appropriation of $29.7 million** (Fiscal Note).

## Key Policy Changes
- Amends **14 Del. C. § 1917(c)(1)a.** to set domicile-based eligibility rules:
  - For claimants establishing legal domicile **after December 31, 2012 but on/before December 31, 2017**: eligible if legally domiciled **at least 3 consecutive years** (as of **June 30** prior to county fiscal year).
  - For claimants establishing legal domicile **after December 31, 2017**: must be legally domiciled **at least 10 consecutive years**, but **only for determining qualification for years 2018 through 2025**.
  - **For all claimants after December 31, 2025**: must be legally domiciled **at least 3 consecutive years** (as of **June 30** prior to county fiscal year).
- Leaves in place other eligibility elements in the excerpt: **age 65+ as of June 30**, property **owned and occupied** as **principal residence**, and school board authorization framework cross-referencing **29 Del. C. § 6102(q)**.
