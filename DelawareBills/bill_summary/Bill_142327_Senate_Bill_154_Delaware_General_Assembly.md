## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Clarifies how “service credit” is counted for Delaware General Assembly members’ pensions under 29 Del. C. § 5527 by specifying that a member elected on Election Day in November and serving through the next Election Day in November receives **full-service credit for that term**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **Title 29, § 5527(d)(1)** (State Employees’ Pension Plan provisions for elected officials) to add a specific rule about **full-service credit** for certain legislative terms.
- Provides that a General Assembly member **elected on Election Day in November** and who **serves through the next Election Day in November** gets **full-service credit for that term**.
- The change affects how **years of service** are credited for pension calculations for members of the **House** (2-year terms) and **Senate** (4-year terms).
- The act’s synopsis gives examples: **5 House terms** under these conditions = **10 years** of credited service; **5 Senate terms** = **20 years** of credited service.
- Does not change the statute’s stated **age/service eligibility thresholds** for when an elected official can begin receiving a pension (different rules for those elected **before vs. on/after January 1, 2012**).
- Does not address pensions for **non-elected** credited service; those remain handled as a **separate pension** under the remainder of the chapter.
- Enacted as **Chapter 123, Volume 85**, approved **July 30, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Administrative clarity and consistency:** Adds an explicit rule that a November-to-November legislative term counts as **full-service credit**, reducing uncertainty in service-credit calculations (text: “will receive full-service credit for that term”).
- **More predictable pension eligibility/crediting:** Members can more clearly determine credited service totals (examples in synopsis: 5 House terms = 10 years; 5 Senate terms = 20 years).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential increase in pension liabilities/earlier eligibility for some members:** Crediting a full term (as defined) could increase credited years of service used in benefit calculations and eligibility thresholds (the bill changes service credit counting; it does not provide cost estimates).
- **Limited scope may leave other service-credit timing questions unresolved:** The amendment addresses only the November Election Day-to-next-November scenario, potentially leaving other entry/exit timing situations unaddressed.

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds to **29 Del. C. § 5527(d)(1)**:  
  “For the purposes of this section, a member of the General Assembly who is elected on Election Day in November and serves through the next Election Day in November will receive **full-service credit for that term**.”
- Confirms that “Any pension for credited service other than as an elected official shall be determined … as a **separate pension**” (existing framework retained).
- Does **not** change the stated pension commencement eligibility rules already in § 5527(d)(1), including:
  - **Elected prior to January 1, 2012:** pension can begin after **age 60** with **≥ 5 years** of service, or after **age 55** with **≥ 10 years**.
  - **Elected on or after January 1, 2012:** pension can begin after **age 60** with **≥ 20 years**, or after **age 65** with **≥ 10 years**.
