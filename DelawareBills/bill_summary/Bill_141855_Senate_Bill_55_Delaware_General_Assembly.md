## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 16 of the Delaware Code to prohibit “gender transition procedures” for children, restrict public funding and insurance coverage for such procedures for children, and create enforcement mechanisms (professional discipline and civil actions).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits a physician or other healthcare professional from **providing** “gender transition procedures” to a **child**. (§ 3004R(a))
- Prohibits a physician or other healthcare professional from **referring** a child to another provider for “gender transition procedures.” (§ 3004R(b))
- Creates **limited exceptions** allowing certain care for disorders of sex development, emergency/imminent danger situations, and treatment of complications from prior procedures. (§ 3004R(c))
- Bars **public funds** from being used “directly or indirectly” for entities/individuals that provide “gender transition procedures” to a child. (§ 3005R(a))
- Prohibits such procedures for children in **state/county/local government-owned facilities** and by **government-employed** clinicians. (§ 3005R(b))
- Prohibits **Delaware Medicaid** from reimbursing or covering “gender transition procedures” for a child. (§ 3005R(c))
- Makes providing/referring for prohibited procedures **unprofessional conduct** subject to licensing discipline; also authorizes civil suits and Attorney General enforcement. (§ 3006R)
- Prohibits **health benefit plans issued in Delaware** from reimbursing “gender transition procedures” for a child. (§ 3007R)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Restricts publicly funded spending** (including Medicaid) on “gender transition procedures” for children. (§ 3005R(a), (c))
- **Creates multiple enforcement pathways** (licensing discipline, private civil actions, and Attorney General actions). (§ 3006R(a), (b), (f))
- **Defines key terms** (e.g., “puberty-blocking drugs,” “cross-sex hormones,” “gender transition procedure”) which may improve statutory clarity for covered services. (§ 3003R)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Limits access to specified medical services for children** by prohibiting provision and even **referral** for “gender transition procedures,” except for the listed exceptions. (§ 3004R(a)-(c))
- **Insurance and Medicaid coverage limits**: prohibits reimbursement/coverage by Delaware Medicaid and by health benefit plans issued in Delaware for such procedures for children. (§ 3005R(c); § 3007R)
- **Potential liability exposure and litigation costs**: allows compensatory damages, injunctive/declaratory relief, and requires courts to grant reasonable attorneys’ fees to prevailing parties who establish a violation. (§ 3006(b), (e))

## Health Impact
- Prohibits specified medical/surgical services and prescribed drugs for children when undertaken “for the purpose of assisting…with a gender transition,” including **puberty-blocking drugs**, **cross-sex hormones**, and **genital or nongenital gender reassignment surgery**. (§ 3003R(6), (11); § 3004R(a))
- Allows exceptions for:
  - Children with a “medically verifiable disorder of sex development” and certain diagnosed disorders of sexual development confirmed via genetic/biochemical testing. (§ 3004R(c)(1)-(2))
  - Treatment of infection/injury/disease/disorder caused by or exacerbated by prior gender transition procedures. (§ 3004R(c)(3))
  - Procedures certified by a physician as necessary to avoid “imminent danger or death or impairment of a major bodily function.” (§ 3004R(c)(4))
