## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To explicitly authorize **commissioned constables** to **direct, control, or regulate vehicle and pedestrian traffic** while performing the **lawful duties of employment**, and to require drivers and pedestrians to obey constables’ lawful traffic directions under Delaware traffic law.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds **“a commissioned constable”** to the list of persons whose lawful traffic directions must be obeyed under **21 Del. C. § 4103**.
- Amends **24 Del. C. § 5609** to state that a commissioned constable may exercise peace officer/law-enforcement powers **including traffic direction**, **while in performance of lawful duties of employment**.
- Clarifies that constables’ traffic-direction authority is tied to protecting **life and property** and is limited to their **lawful employment duties**.
- Keeps the existing rule that drivers must still operate with **due regard for safety**, even when obeying traffic directions.
- Intended to enable additional public safety support for employers **such as schools**, including during **pickup and drop-off** (per Synopsis).
- Includes **technical corrections** to align with the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual (per Synopsis).
- Enacted as **Volume 85, Chapter 9** and **approved April 14, 2025**.

- **Codified scope during employment duties:** **§ 5609** specifies the authority applies **while in the performance of lawful duties of employment**, which clarifies when constables may direct traffic.

## Key Policy Changes
- **21 Del. C. § 4103 (Obedience to authorized persons directing traffic):**
  - Adds **“a commissioned constable”** to the enumerated list of persons invested by law with authority to direct, control, or regulate vehicle and pedestrian traffic, whose **lawful orders/directions must be obeyed**.
  - Retains the rule that compliance does not relieve a driver’s duty to operate with **due regard to safety**.
- **24 Del. C. § 5609 (Powers and duties of constables):**
  - Specifies that a commissioned constable shall exercise peace officer/law-enforcement powers, **including the power to direct, control, or regulate vehicle and pedestrian traffic**, **to protect life and property**, **while performing lawful duties of employment**.
