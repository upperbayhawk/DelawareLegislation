## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend 29 Del. C. § 7915 governing the Council for Services for Aging and Adults with Physical Disabilities to align Council composition and add a conflict-of-interest policy to comply with federal Older Americans Act (OAA) final rule requirements.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates statutory provisions for the Council’s **role** to focus on the **coordination and effectiveness of programs and services** for older Delawareans and adults with physical disabilities.  
- Revises **Council composition** to require **at least 15 members** appointed by the Governor, with **terms up to 3 years** and **staggering** so **no more than 7 members’ terms expire in one year**.  
- Requires that **more than 50%** of Council members be **older persons (age 60+)**, including minority individuals.  
- Specifies additional membership categories (e.g., **health care provider organizations**, **supportive service providers**, **private/voluntary sector leaders**, **local elected officials**, **general public**, **family caregivers**).  
- Retains/ensures **two members** from agencies serving **alleged victims** as defined in **31 Del. C. § 3902**.  
- Adds a **Conflict of Interest Policy** tied to **45 CFR § 1321.47 and § 1321.63**: service-provider representatives may serve but **must not have personal or financial interests** and **may not receive DSAAPD funding**.  
- Directs DSAAAPD to **adopt/amend/repeal regulations** to mitigate conflicts of interest and meet federal requirements.  
- Bill synopsis states it **must be enacted by October 1, 2025**; the enrolled act reflects **approval on August 25, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Federal compliance support:** Adds explicit conflict-of-interest requirements referencing **45 CFR § 1321.47 and § 1321.63**, intended to ensure compliance with the **Older Americans Act final rule**.  
- **Stronger consumer representation:** Requires **>50%** of members be **older persons (60+)**, increasing representation by the population served.  
- **Broader stakeholder input:** Adds specified representation categories (health care, supportive services, elected officials, general public, family caregivers), potentially improving advisory breadth for statewide aging/disability services coordination.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Membership eligibility limits for providers:** Service-provider representatives **may not receive funding from DSAAAPD** and must lack personal/financial interests, which may **exclude** otherwise knowledgeable provider stakeholders (as stated in the conflict-of-interest section).  
- **Regulatory workload:** Requires DSAAAPD to **adopt/amend/repeal regulations** to mitigate conflicts of interest, creating added administrative steps (no staffing level specified).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Council role language updated** to advisory work focused on **coordination and effectiveness** of programs/services for older Delawareans and adults with physical disabilities.  
- **Council size/composition revised:**  
  - **At least 15 members** appointed by the Governor.  
  - **Terms up to 3 years**; **staggered** so **no more than 7** expire in a year.  
  - **More than 50%** must be **older persons (60+)**, including minority individuals.  
  - Required representation categories include: caregivers; health care provider organizations; supportive service providers; private/voluntary sector leadership; local elected officials; general public; **two** members from agencies serving alleged victims under **31 Del. C. § 3902**; an adult with a physical disability (or caregiver); family caregivers; and, **as available**, Tribal representatives and older relative caregivers (including kin/grandparent caregivers of individuals **age 18–59 with a disability**).  
- **Conflict of Interest Policy added:** Provider-representative members must **not** have **personal or financial interest** and **may not receive funding from DSAAAPD**; DSAAAPD must promulgate/adjust regulations to mitigate conflicts.  
- **Chair rules:** Chair elected for a **2-year term**, alternating each term between a representative of the aging community and a representative of adults with physical disabilities; chair may serve **2 nonconsecutive terms**.  
- **Attendance rule retained:** Chair must discharge a member absent from **more than 3 consecutive meetings** unless excused by the Council.  
- **Decisionmaking:** Quorum is a **majority** of members; policy/budget decisions require a **majority of members present** at a meeting with a quorum.

## Who Benefits
- **Older Delawareans (age 60+)**, who must constitute **more than 50%** of Council membership.  
- **Adults with physical disabilities** and **caregivers**, through specified representation categories and advisory focus on service coordination/effectiveness.  
- **Minority older individuals**, explicitly included in the older-persons membership requirement.  
- **Family caregivers** (including older relative caregivers) through explicit inclusion as a membership category.  
- The State/DSAAAPD, by having statutory structure aimed at **federal compliance** with OAA final rule conflict-of-interest requirements.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Service providers receiving DSAAAPD funding** who might otherwise serve on the Council: the conflict-of-interest section states provider-representative members **may not receive funding from DSAAAPD**.  
- Potential Council applicants with **personal or financial interests** (as defined by reference to **29 Del. C. § 5805** and **§ 5804(5)** and applicable federal law) that disqualify them under the new policy.
