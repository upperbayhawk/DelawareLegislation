## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14, § 2905 of the Delaware Code to clarify that the Department of Education (DOE) has authority to adopt rules and regulations regarding the transportation of **public school students**, in addition to rules governing transportation for **nonpublic, nonprofit** elementary and secondary students.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands/clarifies DOE’s rulemaking authority in § 2905 to cover **transportation of public elementary and secondary students**, not just nonpublic, nonprofit students.
- Retains the requirement that rules for transporting students to **nonpublic, nonprofit schools** be the **same as those applicable to public schools**.
- Continues limiting DOE transportation rules for nonpublic, nonprofit students to **elementary and secondary** schools (with exceptions where Title 14 assigns DOE responsibility).
- Maintains the rule that nonpublic, nonprofit students are entitled to transportation **only within the boundaries of a public school district**, not beyond.
- Makes technical/clarifying edits to terminology (e.g., “pupils” to “students”) and restructuring within § 2905.
- No explicit appropriations, fees, penalties, or effective dates are stated in the bill text provided.
