## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Create a statutory right to free legal counsel for certain relative guardians pursuing termination of parental rights (TPR) for the purpose of adoption, and require the Department of Services for Children, Youth and Their Families (DSCYF) to prepare and file required social reports at no cost to those relatives.  

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates new **13 Del. C. § 1104A** giving **free, court-appointed legal counsel** to certain **relatives** seeking **TPR for the purpose of adoption**.  
- Eligibility requires: (1) petitioner is a **relative**; (2) petitioner has **guardianship** of the child; (3) guardianship order was entered **when the child was in Department custody** and remains **in effect throughout the TPR proceedings**.  
- Counsel must be appointed **upon request**, **regardless of petitioner indigency**, and continues through **pre-filing preparation**, **simultaneously filed adoption proceedings**, and **appeals to the Delaware Supreme Court**.  
- If a petitioner is represented by counsel appointed under § 1104A, **DSCYF must prepare and file the social report at no cost to the petitioner** (changes in **§ 1107(b)** and **§ 912(e)**).  
- Clarifies that if an appeal is filed and the appellant has counsel appointed under § 1104A, that counsel’s appointment **continues through the appeal** (change in **§ 917(b)**).  
- Fiscal note assumes the Act is effective **upon the Governor’s signature**.  
- Fiscal note estimates substantial costs: **FY 2026 total $2,374,940** with **$924,789 one-time**, and **FY 2027 $976,093**, **FY 2028 $979,969**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces legal barriers for relative guardians** by guaranteeing **free counsel** even when the relative is not indigent (new **§ 1104A(b)**).  
- **Ensures continuity of representation** from **pre-filing through Supreme Court appeal**, supporting more consistent case handling (new **§ 1104A(b)**; amended **§ 917(b)**).  
- **Eliminates petitioner-paid social report costs** when represented under § 1104A, shifting costs to DSCYF (amended **§ 1107(b)**; **§ 912(e)**).  
- May support timely progression of TPR/adoption filings by enabling counsel appointment **before** a petition is filed (“pre-filing preparation” in **§ 1104A(b)**; fiscal note describes need for a pre-filing request process).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increases State costs** for court-appointed counsel, DSCYF social reports, and potentially other representation (fiscal note estimates significant one-time and recurring expenditures, including **FY 2026 $2,374,940**).  
- Creates **new administrative workload** for the Family Court to process counsel requests **before petitions are filed**, requiring additional staffing (fiscal note: **2.0 FTE** and startup costs).  
- Could increase demand on related legal services, including **parental representation** and **child representation** costs (fiscal note projects added expenditures for parent counsel and Office of the Child Advocate services).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates 13 Del. C. § 1104A** establishing a right to **free legal counsel** for certain relative guardians seeking **TPR for adoption**, with counsel provided:
  - **Upon request**, **regardless of indigency**;  
  - Through **pre-filing**, **simultaneously filed adoption proceedings**, and **appeals** (including to the **Delaware Supreme Court**).
- Amends **13 Del. C. § 1107(b)** to require that **if petitioner is represented by § 1104A counsel**, **DSCYF must prepare and file the social report at no cost** to the petitioner.
- Amends **13 Del. C. § 912(e)** to require **the Department** to prepare and file the **social report at no cost** to a petitioner represented by § 1104A counsel.
- Amends **13 Del. C. § 917(b)** to ensure that if an appellant is represented by § 1104A appointed counsel, that appointment **continues through the appeal**.
- Makes “technical corrections” to conform with the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual (synopsis; no additional substantive details specified).

## Who Benefits
- **Relative guardians** of children who were in **DSCYF custody** at the time guardianship was granted, who seek **TPR and adoption**—receive **free appointed counsel** and **no-cost social reports**.  
- **Children** in these proceedings, to the extent increased representation occurs across parties (fiscal note indicates OCA expects to represent additional children when all other parties are provided counsel).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **State agencies and courts** (Family Court, DSCYF, ODS/OCA) due to increased **caseload and costs** identified in the fiscal note.  
- **Taxpayers/State budget** due to the increased appropriations implied by the fiscal note amounts.

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date:** Assumed **upon signature by the Governor** (fiscal note assumption).  
- **Social report deadline:** When a TPR petition is filed and DSCYF or a licensed agency is not a party, the Court orders DSCYF or a licensed agency to file a social report **within 6 months** (existing framework referenced in amended **§ 1107(b)**).  
- **Hearing timing (as stated in amended § 1107(b)):** hearing to take place after the report is filed, but **not earlier than 12 months after filing of the petition** for proceedings under **§ 1103(a)(6)**.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Court obligation:** If eligibility criteria are met and the petitioner requests appointment, the Court **shall appoint** counsel (mandatory duty in **§ 1104A(b)**).  
- **DSCYF obligation:** If petitioner has § 1104A-appointed counsel, DSCYF **shall prepare and file** required social report(s) **at no cost to the petitioner** (amended **§ 1107(b)** and **§ 912(e)**).  
- **Continuation of counsel on appeal:** If appellant has § 1104A-appointed counsel, the appointment **must continue** through the appeal (amended **§ 917(b)**).
