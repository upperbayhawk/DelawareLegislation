## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the statutory definition of “community-owned energy generating facility” in Title 26, Chapter 10, § 1001(5) to add a requirement that the facility’s **point of interconnection** be located in the **service area of a utility regulated by the Delaware Public Service Commission (PSC)**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes only a **definition** in **26 Del. C. § 1001(5)** (Chapter 10).
- Adds a new condition: the facility’s **point of interconnection must be located in the service area of a utility under PSC regulation**.
- Applies to “community-owned energy generating facility” projects that are **renewable energy generating facilities** with **multiple owners/customers sharing output**.
- Retains existing features: facility may be **stand-alone** or **behind the meter**, must be **interconnected to the distribution system**, and **operated in parallel** with an electric distribution company’s transmission and distribution facilities.
- Narrows/clarifies which projects qualify as “community-owned” for purposes of Chapter 10 by tying eligibility to **PSC-regulated utility service areas**.
- No dollar amounts, percentages, or numeric thresholds are included in the bill text.
- Effective date is **not stated** in the provided text.
