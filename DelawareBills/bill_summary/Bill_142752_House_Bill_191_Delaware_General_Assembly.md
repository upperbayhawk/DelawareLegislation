## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 24 of the Delaware Code to expressly prohibit “nonhuman entities,” including artificial intelligence (AI) agents, from being licensed/certified in certain medical professions and from using specified protected medical titles and abbreviations.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits a **nonhuman entity (including an AI agent)** from being **licensed** to practice **professional nursing**, **advanced practice registered nursing (APRN)**, or **practical nursing** (Title 24, § 1920(h)).  
- Prohibits a nonhuman entity (including an AI agent) from using specified **nursing-related titles/abbreviations**, including **“Nurse,” “RN,” “LPN,” “APRN,” “CRNA,” “CNS,” “CNP,” “CNM,”** and also **“Doctor/Dr.”** (Title 24, § 1920(i)).  
- Prohibits a nonhuman entity (including an AI agent) from being **certified to practice medicine** in Delaware (Title 24, § 1720(k)).  
- Prohibits a nonhuman entity (including an AI agent) from using specified **physician titles**, including **Doctor/Dr., Physician or surgeon, MD, DO** (Title 24, § 1720(l)).  
- Prohibits a nonhuman entity (including an AI agent) from being **licensed as a physician assistant** and from using **“physician assistant”** or **“PA”** (Title 24, § 1773(d)).  
- The bill is framed as a **clarification** that these licenses/certifications and titles are limited to humans (SYNOPSIS).  
- **No dollar amounts, fee changes, or funding appropriations** are stated in the text provided.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clarifies licensure/certification eligibility** by explicitly stating that **AI agents/nonhuman entities cannot hold** nursing, physician, or physician assistant credentials (Title 24, §§ 1920(h), 1720(k), 1773(d)).  
- **Reduces risk of public confusion** by prohibiting nonhuman entities from using **human professional titles/abbreviations** (e.g., RN, MD, PA) (Title 24, §§ 1920(i), 1720(l), 1773(d)).  
- **Supports title integrity** across multiple professions by listing specific restricted titles and abbreviations (Title 24, §§ 1920(i), 1720(l)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
