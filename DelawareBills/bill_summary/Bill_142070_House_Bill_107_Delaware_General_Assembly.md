## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 18, § 6601(2) of the Delaware Code to expand eligibility for line-of-duty death benefits to include **previously covered persons** (e.g., retirees) whose death **proximately results from a retaliatory action** tied to the performance of duties in their former office or position.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands the definition of “covered person” to include a **person who previously qualified** as a covered person and later dies as the **proximate result** of **retaliation** for prior official duties.  
- Applies to deaths caused by an action of a person **seeking retaliation** related to the previously covered person’s **former office or position**.  
- Under current benefit structure described in the fiscal note, qualifying deaths trigger a **$375,000** benefit payable in annual installments, with a **maximum of $50,000 per calendar year**.  
- Additional benefits described in the fiscal note include **tuition up to 4 years per dependent child** (if enrolled in a degree/certificate program) and **health insurance for dependent children** (unless covered by federal government/Tricare/similar).  
- The act is assumed (per fiscal note) to be **effective upon signature by the Governor**.  
- Fiscal impact is **indeterminable** for FY2026, FY2027, and FY2028 due to unknown eligible population and unknown incidence of retaliatory actions.  
- The fiscal note states the act provides **no minimum length of service** as a covered person to qualify (as analyzed), implying potentially broad eligibility among former covered persons.
