## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 4 of the Delaware Code to create a licensing framework that allows certain wine producers (in Delaware and out-of-state) to ship wine directly to Delaware households **through permitted common carriers**, with volume limits, tax payment, reporting, age-verification, and enforcement provisions.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a **“wine direct shipper license”** allowing licensed wine producers to ship wine to Delaware households, **only via a carrier holding a Delaware “carrier permit.”**
- Sets household limit: a licensee may ship **up to 3 “9-liter cases” annually per household** (must be for personal use; no resale).
- Sets statewide shipper cap: a wine direct shipper may not ship **more than 1,800 9-liter cases per year** into Delaware; reaching 1,800 cases triggers a requirement to use Delaware’s **three-tier system via a licensed wholesaler**.
- Prohibits shipping by producers **currently represented in Delaware by a licensed Delaware wholesaler** (including certain affiliates/subsidiaries described in the bill).
- Establishes fees: **$100 application fee** for wine direct shipper license; **$400 biennial fee** (up to **200 cases annually**) or **$3,600 biennial fee** (more than **200 cases annually**). Carrier permit: **$500 application** and **$500 biennial renewal**.
- Requires quarterly reporting (shipper and carrier) including **names/addresses of recipients, volumes, and tracking numbers**; reported data is **exempt from Title 29, Chapter 100** public records requirements.
- Requires delivery safeguards: packages labeled “**CONTAINS ALCOHOL: SIGNATURE OF INDIVIDUAL AGE 21 OR OLDER REQUIRED FOR DELIVERY**”; delivery requires **signature by a person 21+** and **ID scan/retention as proof of age verification**; carrier delivery personnel must have **server training**.
- Directs the Commissioner to **study impacts on Delaware wine retailers** and report to the Governor and General Assembly **by June 1, 2026**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Consumer access:** Delaware households can receive shipments directly from eligible wine producers, within the cap of **3 9-liter cases per household per year**.
- **Tax compliance structure:** Requires payment of **excise taxes due** on sales to Delaware residents, calculated as if the sale occurred in Delaware at the delivery location.
- **Age-verification requirements:** Requires **21+ signature** at delivery plus **ID scanning and retention** as proof of age verification.
- **Traceability & oversight:** Quarterly reporting by both shippers and carriers requires **tracking numbers**, recipient information, and shipment totals, supporting enforcement.
- **Revenue generation:** Establishes application and biennial fees with estimated revenue of **$3,290,000 to $4,110,000** (per Fee Impact document).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Market restrictions:** A wine direct shipper licensee may not ship if the producer is **currently represented in Delaware by a licensed Delaware wholesaler**, which limits participation by some producers.
- **Administrative and compliance burden:** Requires licensing, biennial renewal, quarterly reporting, audits upon request, labeling, and delivery verification procedures.
- **Operational costs to the State:** Fiscal note anticipates significant additional staffing and operating costs (see Budget/Fiscal sections).
- **Shipment caps:** Limits shipments to **3 9-liter cases per household annually** and **1,800 9-liter cases per shipper per year**, which may constrain higher-volume direct-to-consumer programs.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates/authorizes wine direct-to-consumer shipping** for wine producers licensed in Delaware or another state, conditioned on obtaining a **wine direct shipper license**.
- **Household shipment limit:** Up to **3 9-liter cases annually per household**.
- **Per-shipper annual cap:** Up to **1,800 9-liter cases per year** into Delaware via this section; at **1,800 cases** in one year, shipper must use the **three-tier system** via a **licensed Delaware wholesaler**.
- **Eligibility restriction:** Prohibits shipping if the producer is **currently represented in Delaware by a licensed Delaware wholesaler**, including certain subsidiaries/affiliates described in the bill text.
- **Carrier permitting system (§701A):** Requires carriers transporting wine from a wine direct shipper to a Delaware household to obtain a **carrier permit**.
- **Delivery requirements:**  
  - Conspicuous labeling requirement with specific warning text.  
  - Delivery only with **signature by a person age 21+**.  
  - Delivery person must **scan and retain** proof of age verification (state-issued driver’s license or ID).
  - Carrier delivery personnel must complete **approved server training tailored to direct-to-consumer shipments**.
- **Reporting & audits:**  
  - Quarterly reporting by out-of-state and in-state shippers (including totals shipped, purchaser names/addresses, shipment tracking numbers).  
  - Quarterly reporting by carriers (shipper names/addresses, recipient names/addresses, tracking numbers).  
  - Shippers must permit **DATE or Division of Revenue audits** upon request.  
  - Certain reported information is **exempt** from Title 29, Chapter 100.
- **Fees established:** $100 shipper application; $400/$3,600 biennial shipper fee tiers; $500 carrier permit application and $500 biennial renewal.
- **Study requirement:** Commissioner must study impacts on Delaware wine retailers and report **by June 1, 2026**.

## Who Benefits
- **Delaware residents (age 21+)** seeking direct shipment access to wine from eligible producers, within the **3 9-liter cases/household/year** limit.
- **Wine producers** (Delaware and out-of-state) who are not disqualified by Delaware wholesaler representation and who want a licensed path to ship into Delaware.
- **Permitted common carriers** that obtain a **carrier permit** to deliver wine shipments in Delaware.
- **State revenue recipients (General Fund)** due to application and biennial licensing fees, and collection of applicable excise taxes.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Delaware wine retailers** potentially affected by shifts to direct-to-consumer shipping (the bill itself requires a study of retailer impacts).
- **Wine producers already represented by a licensed Delaware wholesaler**, who are barred from shipping under this direct shipper license framework.
- **Agencies administering/enforcing the program** (OABCC/DATE/Division of Revenue) due to additional workload and staffing requirements (with associated costs).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Licensing required:** Only licensed wine direct shippers may ship under this framework; carriers must have a **carrier permit** to transport to Delaware households.
- **Compliance requirements (shippers):** labeling; shipment caps (**3 9-liter cases/household/year**, **1,800 9-liter cases/year** total); no shipment to under-21; no resale; required reports; tax payments; audit access; jurisdiction consent.
- **Compliance requirements (carriers):** permit; biennial renewal; quarterly reporting; 21+ signature; ID scan/retention; server training for delivery personnel; must notify entities (after notice from Commissioner/DATE) that they must obtain a wine direct shipper license before tendering packages for Delaware delivery.
- **Enforcement authority:** DATE may enforce via administrative proceedings before the Commissioner; Commissioner may **approve, deny, fine, suspend, and revoke** a wine direct shipper license.
- **Carrier permit penalties:** After a hearing, violations can result in **a fine** and **suspension**; repeated/continuous violations can lead to **revocation**.
  - **Fine cap:** may not exceed **10% of the average gross monthly fees** earned by the carrier permit holder from utilizing its carrier permit in Delaware **or $1,000, whichever is greater**.
  - Commissioner may **suspend all or part of the fine** for good cause shown.
