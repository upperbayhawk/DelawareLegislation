## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To support eligible family members and legal dependents of individuals who died by suicide in Delaware by providing financial assistance and services through the Department of Justice’s Victims’ Compensation Assistance Program (VCAP), funded by a dedicated telephone service surcharge (the “Elizabeth Barnes Act”). (§ 9001A; Synopsis)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new Chapter 90A in Title 11 establishing assistance for “families of suicide victims,” administered by VCAP/DOJ. (§ 9001A; § 9003A(a))
- Establishes the “Suicide Assistance Fund” to pay eligible claims and certain agency administrative expenses. (§ 9003A(b)(1), (4))
- Imposes a **$0.12 per month** surcharge on **residential, business, and wireless** telephone subscribers (with specified exceptions) and a **$0.12 per prepaid wireless retail transaction** surcharge. (§ 9003A(c), (d))
- Sets specific benefit categories and several **not-to-exceed** amounts, including funeral (**≤ $5,000**), burial (**≤ $2,500**), temporary housing (**≤ $1,500**), moving (**≤ $1,000**), and suicide scene cleanup (**≤ $1,000**). (§ 9004A(a))
- Requires claims to be paid **after other sources are exhausted**, including **life insurance, health insurance, and homeowner’s insurance**. (§ 9004A(d))
- Provides a **30-day** window to request reconsideration after an agency decision is mailed. (§ 9004A(c))
- Fiscal documents project surcharge revenue of **$1,575,000 (FY2026)**, **$2,121,000 (FY2027)**, **$2,142,210 (FY2028)**, and claims costs of **$427,000–$644,000 (FY2026)** and **$854,000–$1,288,000 (FY2027/FY2028)**. (Fee Impact; Fiscal Note)
- Effective date structure: most provisions effective upon enactment, but **benefit distributions under § 9004A begin January 1 following enactment**. (Section 3; Fiscal Note assumption)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Direct support to families after suicide:** Explicitly authorizes assistance/services for eligible family members and dependents, including counseling/psychiatric care and other supports. (§ 9004A(a)(1), (13))
- **Dedicated funding source:** Creates an ongoing funding stream via a **$0.12** surcharge on telecom services and prepaid transactions, deposited into a special fund. (§ 9003A(c), (d))
- **Transparency and oversight:** Requires a written report on fund expenditures **by the second Tuesday of January each year** to specified officials and agencies, and requires annual reporting on services and families assisted. (§ 9003A(b)(4)c; § 9005A)
- **Defined benefit caps for several categories:** Provides clarity on maximum amounts for several supports (e.g., funeral/burial/housing/moving/cleanup). (§ 9004A(a)(2)-(6), (8))
- **Appeal pathway:** Provides a reconsideration process with a **30-day** request window. (§ 9004A(c))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **New surcharge on telecom services:** Imposes additional monthly charges on most residential, business, and wireless subscribers and per-transaction charges on prepaid wireless consumers (**$0.12**), except as excluded (e.g., Lifeline, wholesale, state government). (§ 9003A(c), (d))
- **Administrative costs increase:** DOJ/VCAP anticipates staffing and system costs (e.g., **$200,378 recurring FY2026**, **$120,010 one-time** for DOJ; **$50,000 one-time** for Division of Revenue). (Fiscal Note)
- **Benefit availability delayed relative to enactment:** Assistance section takes effect **January 1 following enactment**, delaying distributions even if surcharge collection starts earlier. (Section 3; Fiscal Note assumption 2a)
- **“Payor of last resort” requirement:** Claimants must exhaust other compensation sources first (life/health/homeowner’s insurance), which may delay or reduce assistance. (§ 9004A(d))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Fund balance treatment conflict:** Bill text states fund balances on **June 30 carry forward and do not revert** to the General Fund (§ 9003A(f)), while the Synopsis states **“Any funds in excess of $5,000,000 at the end of the fiscal year will be returned to the general fund.”** (Synopsis). The operative text provided does not include the $5,000,000 reversion mechanism.
- **Retroactivity/eligibility for prior suicides:** Fiscal note assumes projections **do not account** for claims for deaths **before the effective date**, but the bill text excerpt provided does not clearly state whether pre-enactment deaths are eligible. (Fiscal Note assumption 6e)
- **Undefined caps for several assistance categories:** Some eligible services have no specified maximum in the text provided (e.g., mental health counseling/psychiatric care, medical/hospital expenses, lost wages, “other support services”). (§ 9004A(a)(1), (7), (10)-(13))
- **Provider “credit” amount not quantified:** Providers may recover “actual incremental costs” through a credit, but the bill text does not specify a percentage cap (distinct from the 3% seller retention for prepaid). (§ 9003A(c)(6); § 9003A(d)(9))

## Health Impact
- Authorizes assistance/services including **mental health counseling and psychiatric care**, and also **medical** and **hospital** expenses. (§ 9004A(a)(1), (10), (12))

## Key Policy Changes
- Creates **Title 11, Chapter 90A (Families of Suicide Victims)**, declaring a state purpose to support families/dependents of individuals who died by suicide with assistance similar to crime-victim compensation. (§ 9001A)
- Defines eligibility and key terms, including “eligible family member” (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or other legal dependent) and “suicide” as intentional taking of one’s own life **as listed on the death certificate**. (§ 9002A(5), (13))
- Establishes the **Suicide Assistance Fund** administered by VCAP, with investment/interest crediting, separate administration, and permissible uses including administrative expenses. (§ 9003A(a)-(b))
- Imposes and structures a **$0.12 surcharge** on telecom subscription services and prepaid wireless retail transactions, with invoice line-item requirements and exemptions (e.g., Lifeline, wholesale services, state government). (§ 9003A(c)-(d))
- Creates an assistance framework listing covered services and several caps (e.g., funeral/burial/housing/moving/cleanup; loss of support capped at **$3,000** with conditions). (§ 9004A(a)(2)-(6), (8))
- Establishes reconsideration rights: request within **30 days** after the agency decision is mailed. (§ 9004A(c))
- Makes claims under this chapter subject to existing VCAP protections and penalties under **§§ 9013, 9015, and 9020**. (§ 9006A(b))

## Who Benefits
- **Eligible family members** (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or other legal dependent) of individuals who died by suicide within Delaware, who may receive covered assistance/services. (§ 9002A(5); § 9004A(a))
- Claimants acting on behalf of eligible family members. (§ 9002A(3))

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Telecommunications subscribers/consumers** paying the surcharge:
  - Residential/business/wireless subscribers: **$0.12 per month** (subject to exceptions). (§ 9003A(c))
  - Prepaid wireless consumers: **$0.12 per retail transaction** (subject to exceptions). (§ 9003A(d))
- **Telecom providers and prepaid wireless sellers** required to bill, collect, separately state, and remit surcharges and comply with reporting/audit processes. (§ 9003A(c)(3), (5); § 9003A(d)(1)-(2), (7); § 9003A(e))

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective upon enactment:** All provisions except § 9004A. (Section 3)
- **January 1 following enactment:** § 9004A (Assistance to eligible family members) takes effect; Fiscal Note also assumes distributions do not begin until this date. (Section 3; Fiscal Note assumption 2a)
- **Provider remittance deadline (monthly):** Providers must pay collected surcharges to Department of Finance **no later than the 15th day of the month following collection**. (§ 9003A(c)(5))
- **Prepaid seller remittance deadline (quarterly):** Sellers remit surcharges **within 30 days after the end of the prior quarter**. (§ 9003A(d)(7))
- **Reconsideration deadline:** Claimants may request reconsideration **within 30 days** after the agency decision is mailed. (§ 9004A(c))
- **Annual written expenditure report deadline:** **No later than the second Tuesday of January each year** (covering expenditures in the previous fiscal year). (§ 9003A(b)(4)c)
- **Annual report content requirement:** Agency annual report must include a section on fund activities/expenditures, number of families assisted, and funding sources. (§ 9005A)

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Surcharge billing and collection compliance:**
  - Providers must itemize surcharge as a separate line on subscriber invoices. (§ 9003A(c)(3))
  - Providers must remit monthly and deposit into the Fund monthly. (§ 9003A(c)(5))
  - Prepaid wireless sellers must separately state surcharge on receipt/invoice and remit quarterly; may retain **3%**. (§ 9003A(d)(1), (7), (9))
  - Department of Finance may adopt regulations/policies; Title 30 audit/appeal provisions apply “to the extent practicable.” (§ 9003A(e)(1), (3))
- **Provider liability protections/limits:**
  - Providers are not responsible for uncollectable surcharges; State/Agency may still pursue collection actions. (§ 9003A(c)(7))
  - Providers collecting surcharge perform a governmental function and are generally immune from suit for damages; limited refund liability tied to failure to collect/remit as required. (§ 9003A(c)(8))
- **Claims administration and penalties:**
  - Agency must promulgate rules/regulations to investigate/administer claims. (§ 9004A(b))
  - Claims under Chapter 90A receive the same protections and are subject to the same penalties as provided under **§§ 9013, 9015, and 9020**. (§ 9006A(b))
