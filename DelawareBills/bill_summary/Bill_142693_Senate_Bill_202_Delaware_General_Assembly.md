## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 9 to require each county’s tax collecting authority to prepare and submit standardized **quarterly reports** on specified **county property tax data**, with an optional section on **tax liens**, and to set an **expiration (sunset) date** for the requirement.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires each county tax collecting authority to produce a **quarterly property tax report** beginning **September 30, 2025**, using data collected starting with the quarter beginning **July 1, 2025**.  
- Sets a deadline: the report must be prepared **no later than 30 days after** each quarter-end date (**March 31, June 30, September 30, December 31**).  
- Mandates reporting on **tax payment plans** by parcel (parcel ID, classification, total taxes owed under plan, and whether current or delinquent).  
- Mandates reporting on **property assessment appeals** (number filed by zip code; number successful; number dismissed).  
- Mandates reporting on **parcel sales** (parcels sold plus purchased value and assessed value, itemized by zip code).  
- Mandates reporting on **property tax revenue collected and distributed to each school district** within the county, itemized by school district.  
- Permits (but does not require) counties to include **tax lien** data (parcel ID, classification, lien amount, and date filed).  
- The Act **expires January 1, 2028**, unless terminated sooner or extended by the General Assembly.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved legislative and executive visibility into county property tax administration** by requiring routine reporting to the General Assembly, Governor, OMB, and Finance officials (specified recipients in § 8619(c)).  
- **Greater transparency on tax payment plans and delinquencies** by requiring parcel-level reporting of whether owners are “current or delinquent” on payment plans (§ 8619(a)(1)(d)).  
- **Better tracking of assessment appeal activity** through required counts (filed by zip code; successful; dismissed) (§ 8619(a)(2)).  
- **Enhanced insight into sale price vs. assessed value patterns** via required reporting of purchased and assessed values by zip code (§ 8619(a)(3)).  
- **Clearer reporting on school-district-directed property tax distributions** by requiring itemized totals by school district (§ 8619(a)(4)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative workload increase for counties** due to mandatory quarterly reporting with a **30-day post-quarter deadline** (§ 8619(a)).  
- **Inconsistent statewide tax lien data** because lien reporting is optional (“may include”) rather than required (§ 8619(b)).  
- **Short operational runway for initial reporting**: data collection begins with the quarter starting **July 1, 2025**, and reporting begins **September 30, 2025** (§ 8619(a)).

## Key Policy Changes
- Creates/updates § 8619 to require **quarterly property tax reporting** by each county tax collecting authority.  
- Establishes mandatory report content for each reporting quarter:
  1. **Tax payment plan parcels**: parcel ID, classification, total taxes owed under the plan, and whether current or delinquent (§ 8619(a)(1)).  
  2. **Assessment appeals data**: number filed (by zip code), number successful, number dismissed (§ 8619(a)(2)).  
  3. **Parcels sold**: parcel sold plus purchased value and assessed value, itemized by zip code (§ 8619(a)(3)).  
  4. **School district property tax distributions**: totals collected and distributed to each school district, itemized by district (§ 8619(a)(4)).  
- Allows optional inclusion of **tax lien** details (parcel ID, classification, lien amount, date filed) (§ 8619(b)).  
- Requires submission of the report to specified **legislative and executive branch offices** (§ 8619(c)).  
- Adds a sunset: **expires January 1, 2028** unless terminated sooner or extended (Section 2).

## Who Benefits
- **General Assembly members** (reports distributed to all Senators and Representatives via specified offices) (§ 8619(c)(1)-(2)).  
- **Governor, Office of Management and Budget, and Department of Finance leadership** (required recipients) (§ 8619(c)(3)-(5)).  
- **Division of Legislative Services** (Director and Librarian receive reports) (§ 8619(c)(6)).  
- Potentially, stakeholders interested in **school district funding flows**, since itemized distribution data must be compiled (§ 8619(a)(4)).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Data collection start**: quarter beginning **July 1, 2025** (§ 8619(a), (b)).  
- **Reporting begins**: **September 30, 2025** (§ 8619(a), (b)).  
- **Quarterly report preparation deadline**: **no later than 30 days after** each of:

  - **June 30**
  - **September 30**

- **Sunset/expiration**: **January 1, 2028** (unless terminated sooner or extended) (Section 2).
