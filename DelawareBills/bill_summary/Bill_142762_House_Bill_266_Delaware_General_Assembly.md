## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To amend Title 21, § 4198Q of the Delaware Code to allow certain municipalities to permit low-speed motorized scooter rental services by local ordinance or regulation.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes the default rule for scooter rentals: rentals remain prohibited unless specifically allowed locally.
- Allows low-speed motorized scooters to be operated as part of a **rental service** if **explicitly permitted** by **municipal ordinance or other regulation**.
- Limits that local authority to municipalities with a **population of 30,000 or more**.
- Does not itself create a statewide scooter rental program; it only enables qualifying municipalities to authorize rentals.
- Does not specify statewide operating rules for rentals beyond requiring local permission in qualifying municipalities.
- Bill text shown is a single amendment to **21 Del. C. § 4198Q(h)**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Local control for larger municipalities:** Municipalities with **30,000+** population may choose to authorize scooter rentals via ordinance/regulation (explicitly stated).
- **Enables rental services where desired:** Removes the absolute statutory ban on rental services in qualifying municipalities, if they take affirmative action to permit them (explicitly stated).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)

- **No statewide standards included:** The amendment does not set statewide requirements for rental operations (e.g., parking, fleet caps, safety requirements) and instead relies on local ordinances/regulations (explicitly stated mechanism; absence of statewide detail in text).
