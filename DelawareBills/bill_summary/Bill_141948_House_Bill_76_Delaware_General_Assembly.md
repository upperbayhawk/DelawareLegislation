## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware Code Title 11, § 2514 to remove authority for a **private person** to make a **warrantless arrest** based on information that someone is **charged in another state** with a **felony-level offense** (crime punishable by death or imprisonment exceeding 1 year).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **11 Del. C. § 2514 (Arrest without warrant)**.
- Removes the phrase allowing arrest “by any peace officer **or a private person**” in the covered circumstance.
- After the change, **peace officers** retain authority to make the specified warrantless arrest; **private persons do not** under this section.
- The covered circumstance is when there is “**reasonable information**” that the person “stands charged in the courts of a state” with a crime punishable by **death** or **imprisonment exceeding 1 year**.
- The existing requirement remains that, when such an arrest occurs, the accused must be taken before a judge/justice of the peace “**with all practicable speed**,” and a **sworn complaint** must be made stating grounds for arrest (as in **§ 2513**).
- Bill synopsis states the intent: eliminate authorization for a **private person’s warrantless arrest** of an individual accused in another state of a **felony**.
- **No funding, program, or agency implementation details** are specified in the bill text.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Delaware Code section amended:** **11 Del. C. § 2514**.
- **Change made:** Removes authorization for a **private person** to make a **warrantless arrest** under § 2514’s specified condition.
- **Scope/threshold retained:** Arrest without warrant may be made upon “**reasonable information**” that the accused is charged in another state with a crime punishable by:
  - **death**, or
  - **imprisonment for a term exceeding 1 year**.
- **Procedural requirements retained:** The person arrested must be taken before a judge/justice of the peace “**with all practicable speed**,” and a **complaint under oath** must be made stating the grounds for arrest (as in **§ 2513**).

## Who Benefits
- **Individuals accused in another state of a felony-level offense** (punishable by death or imprisonment exceeding 1 year), to the extent they are no longer subject to a **private person’s** warrantless arrest authority under § 2514.
- **Peace officers**, as the statute’s specified warrantless arrest authority in this scenario is retained for them (and removed for private persons).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Private persons/citizens**, who lose the statutory authority under § 2514 to make a warrantless arrest in the specified out-of-state felony-charge circumstance.
