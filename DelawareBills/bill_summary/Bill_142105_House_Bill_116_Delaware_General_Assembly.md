## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 26 to allow the Delaware Public Service Commission (PSC) to authorize a discounted residential gas or electric utility distribution rate for qualified low-income customers, with costs recovered through a surcharge on all customer classes.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes the PSC to approve an **individual or joint** discounted rate for **electric or gas public utilities** for low-income residential customers.  
- Eligibility is tied to participation in the **Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)** or an **equivalent program**.  
- Any approved low-income rate must provide a **20% discount from standard residential distribution costs**.  
- Before authorizing a rate, the PSC must find the rate is **in the public interest**.  
- Program **credits** and **incremental administrative costs** must be recorded as a **regulatory asset** and recovered through a **surcharge applicable to all customer classes**.  
- **Utilities** that participate must determine customer **eligibility annually**.  
- The PSC must **review the discount rate every 5 years** and decide whether to **re-authorize** it.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Direct bill reduction for eligible households:** Requires a **20% discount** off standard residential **distribution** charges for qualified low-income customers.  
- **PSC oversight and public-interest finding:** The PSC must determine the rate is **in the public interest** before approval.  
- **Periodic review requirement:** Mandatory **5-year PSC review** may ensure the discount is revisited and formally re-authorized as appropriate.  
- **Defined cost recovery mechanism:** Allows utilities to defer program credits and incremental administrative costs as a **regulatory asset** and recover them via a broad **surcharge**.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Cost shift to other customers:** The bill explicitly requires recovery via a **surcharge applicable to all customer classes**, which may increase charges for non-participating customers.  
- **Administrative burden on utilities:** Participating utilities must **determine eligibility annually** and manage associated incremental administrative costs (which are then recovered).  
- **Discount limited to distribution costs only:** The required **20% discount** applies to **standard residential distribution costs**, not explicitly to supply/generation charges.
