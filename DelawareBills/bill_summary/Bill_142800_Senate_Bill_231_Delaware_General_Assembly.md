## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14 to add school social workers (meeting specified licensure/exam criteria) to the list of school employees eligible for a **6% base-salary supplement** for certain national certifications.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds **school social workers** to 14 Del. C. § 1305(l)’s list of employees eligible for a **6%** supplement to their base salary.
- Eligibility requires **both**: (1) passing the **Association of Social Work Boards (ASWB) National Clinical Examination** and (2) holding a **Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW)** license.
- The supplement is calculated as **6% of the base salary** derived from § 1305(a) and (b).
- The bill leaves in place the existing **12%** supplement for National Board for Professional Teaching Standards (or equivalent program approved by the State Board).
- Makes “technical corrections” to conform existing law to the **Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual** (no further detail provided in the text).
- No appropriation, funding source, or implementation timeline is stated in the provided bill text.
