## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29, § 8711A of the Delaware Code to allow certain affordable housing projects to be eligible for Site Readiness Fund funding if the applicant demonstrates the housing will increase economic development in Delaware.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands eligibility for the **Site Readiness Fund** to include applicants seeking to create **affordable housing**.  
- Creates a specific exception: **“Notwithstanding subsections (a) and (b)”** an affordable housing applicant may be approved if certain criteria are met.  
- Conditions approval on the applicant demonstrating the affordable housing **“will increase economic development in the State.”**  
- Defines “affordable housing” as housing affordable to households with income **at or below the median State income**.  
- Adds a cost-burden standard: housing must cost **no more than 30% of a household’s gross annual income**.  
- Clarifies that the 30% cap applies to either **(rental + utilities)** or **(mortgage + property taxes + required insurance)**.  
- Does not specify an appropriation amount, program cap changes, or a dedicated funding set-aside for housing.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Eligibility expansion:** Allows an applicant seeking to create **affordable housing** to be approved for Site Readiness Fund funding **notwithstanding subsections (a) and (b)**, if the applicant demonstrates the project will increase economic development in Delaware.  
- **New definition of “affordable housing” for this section:** Must meet both:
  - Household income eligibility: **at or below the median State income**; and  
  - Affordability/cost burden: **≤ 30% of household gross annual income**, measured as:
    - **total rental and utility costs**, or  
    - **combined mortgage, property taxes, and required insurance**.

## Who Benefits

- Households with income **at or below median State income** who obtain housing meeting the **30%** cost standard (by definition of eligible “affordable housing” in the bill).
