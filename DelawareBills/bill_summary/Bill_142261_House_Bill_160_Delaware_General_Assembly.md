## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14 (Delaware Code) to change Delaware National Guard education benefits from a member reimbursement model (member pays upfront, then gets reimbursed) to a direct-payment model where the Delaware National Guard pays certain tuition and fees directly to an eligible Delaware postsecondary institution, and to make related technical/clarifying changes.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Shifts education benefits from reimbursing service members after they pay tuition/fees to **direct disbursement to the postsecondary institution** on the member’s behalf.  
- Requires members to **apply in advance** for education benefits **before starting** a class/course, per a schedule set by the Adjutant General.  
- Maintains the requirement that members must first pursue **all other reasonably available tuition assistance** (e.g., **scholarships, grants, civilian employer programs**).  
- For **state-supported Delaware institutions**, requires the institution to charge the member the **in-state resident rate** and treat the member as **in-state regardless of residence**; benefits pay tuition/fees **up to the in-state resident rate**.  
- For **Delaware private institutions**, benefits pay **tuition and fees equal to the average** tuition/fees of Delaware **state-supported** institutions offering the class/course, **based on the in-state resident rate**.  
- Sets/retains eligibility conditions including a **6-year satisfactory service commitment** and **minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0 on a 4.0 scale** (or equivalent), with restrictions if GPA falls below 2.0.  
- Establishes a **60-day payment timeline**: within **60 days after receiving a member’s bill**, the Guard must disburse payment directly to the school.  
- Effective immediately, with program changes **implemented on July 1, 2026**; Act approved **July 23, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces upfront cost barrier for members**: Direct payment to institutions addresses the bill’s stated concern that “young service members often cannot take advantage” because they “cannot afford to pay upfront.” (Synopsis; §3480(d)(2))  
- **Supports recruiting/retention per stated legislative intent**: Synopsis states the change “will improve…recruiting and retention” and maintain staffing needed for state emergencies.  
- **Standardizes in-state pricing at state-supported institutions**: Schools must charge Guard members the **in-state rate** and treat them as in-state, regardless of residence (§3480(a)(3)a).  
- **Provides clearer prioritization when funding is insufficient**: If applications exceed appropriations, **master’s degree benefits must be reduced before** certificate/associate/bachelor benefits (§3480(d)(1)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Program is capped by appropriations**: Benefits remain “subject to the limits” of funding appropriated by the General Assembly and approved by the Governor (§3480(a)(8)); if demand exceeds funds, benefits are reduced (§3480(d)(1)).  
- **Master’s-level participants are explicitly deprioritized**: When funding is insufficient, reductions occur first to **master’s degree** benefits (§3480(d)(1)).  
- **Repayment obligation with interest for noncompliance/termination for cause**: Members who fail to meet requirements (including service commitment) must repay **all previously received benefits, including interest, pro rata** (§3480(e)).  
- **GPA condition can halt future benefits**: If cumulative GPA falls below **2.0**, the member **may not receive further benefits** until the GPA is raised to the minimum (§3480(b)(2)).

## Educational Impact
- Expands practical access to postsecondary education benefits by eliminating the need for members to pay tuition/fees upfront (Synopsis; §3480(d)(2)).  
- Limits covered coursework to classes required for an identified **certificate, associate’s, bachelor’s, or master’s degree** (§3480(a)(4)).  
- Limits benefits to **one program per level** (certificate; associate; bachelor’s; master’s) per member (§3480(a)(5)).  
- Requires maintenance of **2.0 cumulative GPA (4.0 scale or equivalent)**; members below 2.0 cannot receive further benefits until restored (§3480(b)(2)).  
- Allows both **full-time or part-time** enrollment (§3480(a)(6)).  
- Enforces a **15-year usage window** from the first funded course, extendable by time on active duty/activation that interrupts benefits (§3480(a)(7)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Direct payment**: Delaware National Guard must disburse education benefit payments **directly to a postsecondary institution** on the member’s behalf, within **60 days** after receiving the member’s bill (§3480(d)(2)).  
- **Advance application requirement clarified**: Member must apply for benefits **before starting** a class/course and per Adjutant General schedule (§3480(a)(2)).  
- **Clarifies private school benefit calculation**: For Delaware private institutions, tuition/fees paid must equal the **average** tuition/fees at Delaware state-supported institutions offering the class/course, based on in-state rates (§3480(a)(3)b).  
- **In-state treatment mandated at state-supported schools**: Must charge **in-state rates** and treat the member as an in-state student/applicant regardless of residence (§3480(a)(3)a).  
- **Prioritization under insufficient funds**: If applied-for benefits exceed appropriations, reductions must occur first to **master’s degree** benefits (§3480(d)(1)).  
- **GPA enforcement adjusted**: Instead of forfeiting semester funding while below 2.0, the text provides that the member **may not receive further benefits** until the cumulative GPA is raised to at least **2.0** (§3480(b)(2)).  
- **Repayment rules**: Noncompliance/termination for cause triggers repayment of benefits **including interest**, **pro rata** (§3480(e)); termination not for cause does **not** require repayment (§3480(f)).

## Who Benefits
- **Active members of federally recognized units of the Delaware National Guard** who meet “satisfactory membership” requirements and otherwise qualify (§3480(a)).  
- Members who lack the ability to pay tuition/fees upfront, because benefits are paid directly to schools (Synopsis; §3480(d)(2)).  
- Delaware postsecondary institutions (state-supported and private located in Delaware) that receive direct payments on members’ behalf (§3480(a)(3); §3480(d)(2)).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Members pursuing **master’s degrees**, because their benefits must be reduced first if appropriated funds are insufficient (§3480(d)(1)).  
- Members who fail to meet requirements (e.g., service commitment, compliance requirements) or are terminated for cause, due to repayment obligations **including interest** (§3480(e)).  
- Members unable to maintain the required **2.0 cumulative GPA**, who become ineligible for further benefits until the GPA is raised (§3480(b)(2)).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective immediately** (Section 2).  
- **Implemented on July 1, 2026** (Section 2).  
- **Approved July 23, 2025** (chapter law).  
- Member must apply **before starting** a class/course, per a schedule announced by the Adjutant General (§3480(a)(2)).  
- Payment timing: Guard must pay the institution **within 60 days** after receiving a member’s bill (§3480(d)(2)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Service commitment**: Must agree to serve satisfactorily for **at least 6 years** (can include time before/during/after receiving benefits) (§3480(b)(1)).  
- **Academic requirement**: Must maintain **cumulative GPA ≥ 2.0 (4.0 scale or equivalent)**; if below, member may not receive further benefits until restored (§3480(b)(2)).  
- **Advance submission/application**: Before class start, member must provide the Adjutant General (or designee) a **completed application** and, if applicable, a **most recent transcript** (§3480(b)(3)).  
- **Repayment/penalty**: If member does not fulfill service commitment, is terminated for cause, or otherwise fails to comply, member becomes immediately ineligible and must **repay all previously received benefits, including interest, pro rata**; amount determined by the Adjutant General (§3480(e)).  
- **No repayment when termination is not for cause** (§3480(f)).  
- **No payment after termination**: Member may not receive benefits not disbursed before termination for any reason (§3480(g)).  
- **Rulemaking/administration**: Adjutant General appoints a board to adopt rules/regulations and forms (§3480(c)).  
- **Annual report**: Adjutant General must report specified participation and financial metrics to the General Assembly by fiscal year (§3480(h)).
