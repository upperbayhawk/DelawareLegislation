## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend **11 Del. C. § 9906** to **expand and re-balance the membership** of the **Family Justice Center Council** by increasing the number of at-large seats and specifying additional represented constituencies.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases the Council’s **at-large members from 10 to 12** (11 Del. C. § 9906(c)(10)).
- Replaces one combined at-large seat representing the **health-care community including mental health providers and sexual assault nurse examiners** with more specific representation.
- Specifies an at-large seat for the **mental health care provider community**.
- Adds an at-large seat for the **sexual assault nurse examiner (SANE) or forensic nurse examiner** community.
- Adds an at-large seat for **system-based police or prosecution victim service specialists**.
- Keeps the existing requirement that selection of at-large members must consider **geographic diversity** and **ensure representation of each county**.
- The amended roles apply to **full members** of the Council (at-large members are full members).
- The act was **approved August 12, 2025** (Chapter 130, Volume 85).
