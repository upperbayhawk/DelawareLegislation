## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To prohibit Delaware law-enforcement agencies and related state/local entities from entering into or continuing certain agreements with federal immigration authorities—specifically agreements to exercise federal civil immigration authority (including under INA § 287(g)) or to detain/house individuals in federal immigration custody—and to require termination of existing immigration enforcement agreements within a set timeframe.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits a “law-enforcement agency” from entering into, **modifying, renewing, or extending** agreements **to exercise federal civil immigration authority under INA § 287(g)**.  
- Prohibits agreements **to detain or house individuals in the custody of a federal immigration authority** for violations of federal immigration law.  
- Requires **each unit of local government, law-enforcement agency, and State agency** with an existing immigration enforcement agreement to **exercise the termination provision** in that agreement **within 30 days after the Act’s effective date**.  
- Defines “law-enforcement agency” broadly to include entities such as **Delaware State Police**, **municipal police**, **DNREC**, **Delaware Capitol Police**, **Probation and Parole** (DOC and DSCYF), **State Fire Marshal**, **Department of Justice**, and certain specialized enforcement units.  
- Clarifies the Act **does not limit enforcement of valid court-issued warrants or orders**.  
- Clarifies the Act **does not prohibit compliance with federal disclosure requirements** for criminal history record information under **28 C.F.R. § 20 et seq.**  
- Law is enacted as **Volume 85, Chapter 75**, and was **approved July 14, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces state/local involvement in federal civil immigration enforcement** by barring § 287(g) arrangements and custodial housing/detention agreements (text: § 8402B(a)(1)-(2)).  
- **Creates a clear, uniform statewide rule** covering a wide range of agencies and units listed in the definition of “law-enforcement agency” (§ 8401B).  
- **Preserves core public-safety functions** by expressly allowing enforcement of **valid court-issued warrants or orders** (§ 8402B(c)).  
- **Preserves compliance with certain federal information-sharing rules** by stating the Act does not prohibit compliance with **28 C.F.R. § 20 et seq.** (§ 8402B(c)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Limits formal cooperation mechanisms** with federal immigration authorities by prohibiting agreements to exercise federal civil immigration authority under **INA § 287(g)** (§ 8402B(a)(1)).  
- **Eliminates the option** for covered entities to contract or arrange to **detain/house individuals** in federal immigration custody for immigration-law violations (§ 8402B(a)(2)).  
- **Imposes a rapid implementation deadline**: existing agreements must be terminated **within 30 days after the effective date** (§ 8402B(b)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The Act requires terminating an “existing immigration enforcement agreement” **within 30 days after the effective date**, but **does not define** “immigration enforcement agreement” beyond the categories addressed in § 8402B(a)-(b).  
- The statute prohibits entering into/altering certain agreements, but **does not describe** any reporting, certification, or documentation requirements to demonstrate termination or compliance.  
- The bill text provides **no explicit enforcement mechanism or penalty** for noncompliance (see “Enforcement” section below).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Expands prohibited actions** to include entering into, **modifying, renewing, or extending** covered agreements (final enacted text, § 8402B(a)).  
- **Bans agreements**:
  - **To exercise federal civil immigration authority under INA § 287(g)** (§ 8402B(a)(1)); and
  - **To detain or house individuals** in federal immigration authority custody for violations of federal immigration law (§ 8402B(a)(2)).  
- **Mandates termination** of existing immigration enforcement agreements by covered entities **within 30 days after the effective date** (§ 8402B(b)).  
- **Savings clauses / limits**:
  - Does not limit enforcing **valid court-issued warrants or orders** (§ 8402B(c)).  
  - Does not prohibit compliance with federal criminal history record information disclosure requirements under **28 C.F.R. § 20 et seq.** (§ 8402B(c)).

## Who Benefits
- Individuals in Delaware who might otherwise be subject to **state/local enforcement of federal civil immigration authority** via § 287(g) agreements or **detention/housing arrangements** tied to federal immigration custody (as restricted by § 8402B(a)).  
- Covered law-enforcement agencies and units that receive clearer statutory direction on prohibited agreement types (definition in § 8401B and prohibitions in § 8402B).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Federal immigration authorities and related operations that would rely on **Delaware agencies** to exercise federal civil immigration authority via **INA § 287(g)** agreements or to **detain/house** individuals in federal immigration custody (prohibited by § 8402B(a)).  
- Local/state entities that currently have such agreements and must terminate them on the statutory timeline (§ 8402B(b)).
