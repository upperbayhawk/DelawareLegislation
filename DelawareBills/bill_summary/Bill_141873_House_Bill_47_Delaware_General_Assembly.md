## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 31 to require fingerprint-based Delaware and national criminal background checks plus Child Protection Registry checks for private schools and youth camps (including those operated by private schools), remove prior opt-outs/name-based options, and make related administrative and cost-allocation changes. Establish a separate statutory section (§ 309A) specifically for private-school background checks.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **all employees, contractors, and volunteers of private schools** to undergo a **fingerprint-based Delaware (SBI) + national (FBI) background check** and a **DSCYF Child Protection Registry check**.  
- Eliminates prior provisions allowing private schools/youth camps to **use name-based checks** (DELJIS) and third-party out-of-state checks, or to **choose not to perform checks** with parental acknowledgement (**repealed**).  
- Expands/clarifies “child-serving entity” to include **public and private schools (including DOE employees)**, **licensed child-care providers**, and **youth camps/summer schools exempt from child-care licensing**.  
- Sets cost rules: generally **applicant pays**, but **State must bear costs for applicants to child-care facilities** licensed under **Title 14, Chapter 30A**; DOE and DSCYF **may pay** for background checks for prospective or current employees.  
- Authorizes the **Superintendent of State Police** to issue **regulations** governing **re-use** of a background check (by written authorization and limited scope).  
- Requires **SBI and DELJIS** to provide **subsequent Delaware criminal history** to the entity that received the original background check information, and if available, provide “Rap Back System” updates.  
- Creates new **31 Del. C. § 309A** to separately codify private-school background-check requirements (noted as requested by the federal government).  
- Effective **July 1, 2025**, with compliance required by **September 1, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Removes opt-out and name-based alternatives** for private schools and youth camps, establishing a uniform requirement for **fingerprint-based SBI + FBI checks** plus **Child Protection Registry checks** for staff/volunteers/contractors.  
- Requires provision of **subsequent criminal history** (and Rap Back updates if available) to the entity that received the original record, potentially improving ongoing awareness of disqualifying criminal history.  
- Allows **State coverage of background-check costs** for **child-care facility applicants** (Title 14, Ch. 30A), reducing applicant financial burden for that group.  
- Creates a dedicated private-school section (**§ 309A**), which the bill synopsis states responds to a **federal request** to separate private-school authorization from state/local government authorities.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Imposes additional compliance obligations on private schools and youth camps by requiring **fingerprint-based SBI + FBI** checks and **DSCYF registry checks** for **all employees, contractors, and volunteers**, and removing prior flexibility to use name-based checks or opt out.  
- Fiscal Note identifies **indeterminable State costs** for paying background checks for child-care applicants because the **number of applicants is unknown**; costs could increase with any expansion of **State-funded Pre-K services** (Fiscal Note assumption).  
- Potential administrative/technology impacts: **SBI** may need additional staffing/resources in future years due to increased volume; **DELJIS** anticipates **CJIS programming changes** with **unknown** future-year cost estimates (Fiscal Note).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Repeals**:  
  - The option for private schools and youth camps to perform **name-based Delaware checks** through **DELJIS** plus third-party out-of-state checks (formerly valid for **5 years**).  
  - The option for private schools (including youth camps operated by the private school) to **choose not to perform checks** if parents/guardians sign an acknowledgement retained for **at least 1 year**.  
- **Adds/clarifies**:  
  - “Child-serving entity” includes **public and private schools (including DOE employees)**, **licensed child-care providers**, and **youth camps/summer schools exempt from licensing**.  
  - Cost allocation: applicant generally pays, but **State bears costs for applicants to licensed child-care facilities**; DOE and DSCYF **may pay** for their employees’ checks.  
  - **Regulatory authority** for Superintendent of State Police to govern **re-use** of background checks (written authorization; limited scope tied to purpose/relationship/type of license/position/benefit).  
  - Requires **SBI and DELJIS** to provide **subsequent Delaware criminal history** to the original recipient entity; includes Rap Back updates if available.  
  - Creates **31 Del. C. § 309A** requiring background checks for all private-school employees/contractors/volunteers and setting procedures for SBI and DSCYF reporting and confidentiality.

## Who Benefits
- **Students/children** in private schools and youth camps, to the extent mandatory fingerprint-based and registry checks increase screening rigor.  
- **Parents/guardians**, by removal of the prior structure that allowed private schools/youth camps to operate without minimum checks (with parental acknowledgement).  
- **Applicants to licensed child-care facilities (Title 14, Ch. 30A)**, because the **State must bear the costs** of their background checks.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Private schools and youth camps** (including those operated by private schools) facing increased administrative requirements and loss of prior lower-burden/name-based or opt-out pathways.  
- **Applicants/employees/volunteers/contractors** subject to fingerprint-based checks and associated processes (e.g., release statement for Child Protection Registry check), and potentially costs where the applicant remains responsible.  
- **State agencies/systems (SBI, DELJIS, DOE)** if increased volume requires added resources or programming changes (Fiscal Note).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date:** **July 1, 2025**.  
- **Compliance deadline:** child-serving entities and private schools **must comply by September 1, 2025**.  
- Fiscal Note assumption: effective **July 1, 2025 following signature by the Governor**.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Private schools** must conduct a “background check” defined as **fingerprint-based SBI (Delaware) + FBI (national) checks** plus a **DSCYF Child Protection Registry check** for **all employees, contractors, and volunteers** (§ 309A).  
- Prospective employees/contractors/volunteers must submit to fingerprinting and identification procedures established by the **Superintendent of State Police** (§ 309A(c)(1)).  
- Private schools must obtain a **signed statement** authorizing release of Child Protection Registry check results and attach it to the request submitted for the registry check (§ 309A(c)(2)).  
- Private school must determine whether an individual is prohibited based on **§ 309(d)** prohibitions (§ 309A(d)(1)).  
- Criminal history and Child Protection Registry information is **confidential** and may only be disclosed to the **head of school or designee** (§ 309A(d)(2)).
