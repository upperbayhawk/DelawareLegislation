## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To supplement federal protections for servicemembers, their families, and veterans by adding **“military status”** as a protected class across multiple Delaware anti-discrimination statutes (public accommodations, housing, insurance, education/charter admissions, employment, and state classified service). (Synopsis; multiple code amendments)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds **“military status”** as an explicitly protected category in **public accommodations** law (Title 6, Ch. 45).  
- Adds **“military status”** as a protected basis in **housing discrimination** law (Title 6, Ch. 46) and related provisions in **Title 25** fair housing sections.  
- Adds **“military status”** to **charter school admissions non-discrimination** restrictions (Title 14 § 506).  
- Adds **“military status”** to the definition of **unfair discrimination in insurance policies/premiums** (Title 18 § 2304(22)) and related complaint handling language.  
- Adds **“military status”** to **employment discrimination** definitions and unlawful practices (Title 19, Ch. 7).  
- Adds **“military status”** to **State classified service** employment non-discrimination protections (Title 29 § 5953).  
- Defines “military status” consistently across sections as: **(1)** member of uniformed forces (10 U.S.C. § 101) or reserve component (10 U.S.C. § 10101), **(2)** veteran (38 U.S.C. § 101), or **(3)** dependent of a servicemember (50 U.S.C. § 3911).  
- Makes “technical corrections” to conform with the Legislative Drafting Manual. (Synopsis)

- **Public accommodations:** Prohibits denial/withholding of accommodations and prohibits discriminatory notices/advertising based on military status. (Section 1; § 4504)
