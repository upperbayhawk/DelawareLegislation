## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Prohibit Delaware school districts and charter schools from selling or serving any breakfast, lunch, or “competitive food” on campus during the school day if it contains Red dye 40 (CAS no. 25956-17-6), and authorize the Delaware Department of Education (DOE) to implement the prohibition through rules or regulations.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new prohibition in Title 14 (§ 4137B) on school foods and beverages containing **Red dye 40 (CAS no. 25956-17-6)**.
- Applies to **breakfast, lunch, and competitive foods** sold/served **on campus** during the **school day**.
- “School day” is defined as **midnight before** through **30 minutes after** the end of the official school day.
- “Competitive food” includes foods/beverages other than federally reimbursed meals, including items **sold a la carte** and **vending machine items** sold through school nutrition programs.
- DOE **may adopt rules or promulgate regulations** to implement the section.
- The Act is **effective immediately** (approved **May 22, 2025**) but must be **implemented beginning July 1, 2026**.
- The bill text provides a **planning and preparation year** leading up to the implementation date (per synopsis).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces student exposure** to Red dye 40 in school-provided/sold foods by prohibiting its presence in breakfast, lunch, and competitive foods on campus during the school day. (Text: § 4137B(b))
- Creates a **clear, uniform statewide standard** for school districts and charter schools regarding Red dye 40 in foods/beverages sold or served at school. (Text: statewide prohibition in Title 14)
- Allows DOE to create **implementation rules/regulations**, which can support consistent compliance across districts/charters. (Text: § 4137B(c))
- Provides lead time: implementation starts **July 1, 2026**, allowing procurement/menu planning adjustments. (Text: Section 2; synopsis)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- May require school nutrition programs to **change product sourcing, recipes, or vendors** for items that contain Red dye 40, including **a la carte and vending** offerings. (Text: applies to competitive foods; synopsis references vending/a la carte)
- Could **limit the range of products** available for sale/serve on campus during the school day if Red dye 40 is common in certain items. (Text: categorical prohibition in § 4137B(b))
- Implementation may impose **administrative compliance work** (ingredient verification, vendor specifications) because the bill establishes a prohibition but does not specify a streamlined compliance method. (Text: prohibition + DOE rulemaking authority, no compliance process described)
