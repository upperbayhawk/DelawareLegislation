## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 18 of the Delaware Code to give the Division of Public Health (DPH) authority to approve supervised clinical training rotations for mental health providers at school-based health centers (SBHCs).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes DPH to approve **supervised clinical training rotations** for **mental health providers** at SBHCs.  
- Makes this change in **two places** in Title 18: **§ 3365** and **§ 3571G**.  
- Adds a new subsection **(h)** in both sections stating DPH “shall have the authority” to approve these rotations.  
- Clarifies SBHC service language by adding: **“Except as provided by subsection (h)”** before the SBHC “provides through licensed professionals…” clause in both sections.  
- Does **not** create a requirement that SBHCs host trainees; it only grants DPH approval authority.  
- Does **not** specify funding, fees, reporting, or implementation timelines in the bill text.  
- Signed into law: **Approved September 2, 2025** (Chapter 196, Volume 85).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Increases training placement options:** Explicitly allows DPH to approve supervised clinical training rotations **at SBHCs**, which could expand approved clinical sites for mental health provider training (new § 3365(h) and § 3571G(h)).  
- **Supports mental health service capacity in schools:** SBHCs include mental health and substance use disorder services in their defined scope, and the Act authorizes training rotations specifically for mental health providers within that setting (SBHC definition in both sections, plus new subsection (h)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative workload for DPH:** DPH must exercise an approval function for supervised clinical training rotations; the bill does not specify resources or processes to support this new authority.  
- **Lack of guardrails in statute:** The bill grants approval authority but does not specify statutory standards, oversight requirements, or minimum supervision parameters for these rotations (beyond “supervised clinical training rotations”).
