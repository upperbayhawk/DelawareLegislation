## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 14 to require the Delaware Department of Education (through the Office of Child Care Licensing (OCCL)) to establish a dedicated **Child Care Complaint Investigative Unit** to intake, investigate, resolve, document, and publicly report on complaints involving **licensed child care facilities and providers**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires the Department to **establish a Child Care Complaint Investigative Unit within OCCL** to handle child care complaints.  
- Mandates the Unit to **receive and document complaints** about alleged violations, including **health and safety, supervision, licensing requirements, and staff conduct**.  
- Requires **clear investigation policies/procedures**, including **processes for substantiating complaints and administering consequences**.  
- Requires coordination with **Division of Family Services and law enforcement** when complaints involve suspected **child abuse, neglect, or criminal conduct**.  
- Requires a **centralized, confidential record-keeping system** for complaints, findings, and resolutions.  
- Requires an **annual public report** summarizing complaint volume/types, investigation outcomes, and **any enforcement actions**.  
- Requires **substantiated complaint findings** to be publicly accessible through a **searchable online database**, consistent with privacy laws.  
- Requires implementation using **existing OCCL staff and operational resources** (no new funding) and implementation **no later than 90 days after enactment**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Greater transparency**: Requires a **public annual report** and a **searchable online database** of **substantiated** complaint findings (with privacy protections).  
- **More consistent complaint handling**: Requires **clear policies, procedures, and consequences** and processes for **substantiating** complaints.  
- **Improved cross-agency response for serious allegations**: Explicitly requires coordination with **Division of Family Services and law enforcement** for suspected abuse/neglect/criminal conduct.  
- **Centralized documentation**: Requires a centralized system for complaints, findings, and resolutions, which may support consistency and oversight.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Resource strain risk within existing staffing**: The bill requires the Department to assign personnel/resources **from within existing OCCL staff and operational resources** (Section 2), while adding multiple ongoing obligations (database, annual report, centralized system).  
- **Potential confidentiality sensitivities**: While personally identifiable information must remain confidential, the bill states **“Complaint reporting may not be confidential”** (Section 1(d)), which could raise practical concerns about how complaint narratives are handled (implementation-dependent).
