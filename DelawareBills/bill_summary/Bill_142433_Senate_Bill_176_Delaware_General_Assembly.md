## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Authorize Sussex County to add a building permit surcharge (impact-fee-like) to help fund the **local share** of **school capital construction programs** that have received a **Certification of Necessity** from the Delaware Department of Education.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Allows **Sussex County** to add a **surcharge** to **building permit applications** to fund the **local share** of certain school construction projects.  
- The surcharge may be **no greater than 1.25%** of the **construction value** applicable to the building permit application.  
- The surcharge funding is limited to school capital construction programs that have received a **Certification of Necessity** under **29 Del. C. § 7510**.  
- An exemption applies: **no surcharge** may be added to a building permit application with a **construction value of $30,000 or less** **if** the application is submitted **after issuance of the original certificate of occupancy**.  
- Existing law remains: permit fees are determined by the **County Council of Sussex County**.  
- The Act is enacted as **Chapter 210, Volume 85, Laws of Delaware** (formerly Senate Bill 176).  
- The bill was **approved September 3, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Creates a dedicated local funding mechanism**: Explicitly allows Sussex County to raise funds for the **local share** of eligible school capital construction programs (those with a **Certification of Necessity**).
- **Caps the surcharge**: Limits the surcharge to **≤ 1.25%** of construction value, providing a defined ceiling.
- **Provides a small-project/after-occupancy exemption**: Exempts certain smaller post-occupancy projects (**≤ $30,000 construction value**, submitted **after** original certificate of occupancy), reducing added costs for those permits.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increases permit-related costs** for many building permit applicants in Sussex County by up to **1.25%** of construction value (where applied).
- **Narrow exemption scope**: The **$30,000 or less** exemption applies only when the permit is submitted **after** issuance of the **original certificate of occupancy**, so small projects before occupancy (or without that condition) may still be subject to the surcharge.
- **Potential uneven impacts by location/school district**: The synopsis references applying the fee “within the respective school district,” which may result in different effective burdens depending on where construction occurs.

## Key Policy Changes
- Amends **9 Del. C. Ch. 63, § 6305 (Fees)** to add subsection **(b)** authorizing Sussex County to:
  - Add a building permit application surcharge **≤ 1.25%** of the **construction value** to fund the local share of qualifying school capital construction programs (those with a **Certification of Necessity** under **29 Del. C. § 7510**).
