## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To expedite Court of Chancery proceedings when a parent or legal guardian seeks a court order compelling a custodian to provide access to a deceased minor’s digital assets or digital accounts, and to make technical drafting corrections in Title 12.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **12 Del. C. § 5005(d)** regarding recovery of digital assets/digital accounts from a custodian.
- Keeps the rule that a **custodian must comply with a valid written request within 60 days** after receiving it.
- If a custodian does not comply, the fiduciary may apply to the **Court of Chancery** for an order directing compliance.
- Creates an expedited court process when the fiduciary is a **parent or legal guardian** and the account holder is a **deceased minor**.
- In that deceased-minor scenario, the Court **must treat the application as expedited** and **aim to resolve it within 30 days** after the application is filed.
- Includes **technical corrections** to align with the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual (e.g., wording/capitalization such as “Court”).
- **Approved September 19, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Faster court resolution for grieving families:** Requires the Court to treat certain applications as expedited and aim for a decision **within 30 days** when a parent/guardian seeks access to a deceased minor’s digital assets/accounts.
