## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware Code Title 29, Chapter 60A to (1) increase the authorized employer match for state employee deferred compensation, (2) expand/adjust eligibility rules for receiving the match, and (3) allow employer matching contributions based on qualified student loan payments (with the match deposited into a retirement account).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases the “maximum match” from **$10 to $20 per pay period** for employer matching contributions to deferred compensation plans.  
- Applies to employer contributions credited to participant **§457(b), §403(b), or §401(a)** accounts.  
- Redefines “qualified participant” eligibility by requiring either **(a) 6 consecutive months enrollment** before receiving the match, or **(b) deferral of the IRS maximum** within the prior 6 months.  
- Adds “qualified student loan payment” definition by reference to **26 U.S.C. § 401(m)**.  
- Requires the Plans Management Board, **by not later than January 1 following the Act’s effective date**, to approve plan amendments allowing match eligibility based on **qualified student loan payments** (as permitted under federal law).  
- Caps the **aggregate annual** employer contributions per participant to the **maximum annual match** (maximum match × number of pay periods in the plan year).  
- Gives the Plans Management Board discretion to select which plan(s) receive employer contributions, considering **administrative cost reduction** and minimizing burdens of **multiple accounts**.  
- Fiscal note assumes the match suspension would be lifted and match would be payable for contributions made after **January 1, 2026**, with projected total costs of **$4,157,140 (FY2026)**, **$8,563,708 (FY2027)**, and **$8,820,619 (FY2028)**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Larger retirement savings incentive:** Doubling the authorized match from **$10 to $20 per paycheck** increases potential employer contributions for participants.  
- **Student-loan-based matching option:** Allows employees with **qualified student loan payments** to receive employer contributions into a retirement account even if they are prioritizing loan repayment (plan amendments required by **January 1 following the effective date**).  
- **Potential administrative streamlining:** Board must consider reducing administrative costs and avoiding participant burdens from maintaining **multiple plan accounts** when selecting the deposit plan(s).  
- **Clarified annual cap:** Explicitly limits annual employer contributions to the **maximum annual match**, providing a defined ceiling tied to pay periods.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Significant ongoing cost if funded/implemented:** Fiscal note projects total costs of **$4,157,140 (FY2026)** rising to **$8,820,619 (FY2028)** if the match is reinstated and funded.  
- **Benefit is contingent on appropriations:** The statute states program fiscal/operational aspects are **contingent upon funding by the General Assembly**, meaning the enhanced match may not occur without annual funding.  
- **Administrative compliance requirements:** Requires creation of a participant **certification process** for student loan payments and related procedures, which may add administrative workload.  

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Whether/when the match is actually reinstated:** Bill updates authorization, but the prior match has been suspended; reinstatement depends on **funding/appropriations** (the bill text does not itself appropriate funds).  
- **Which specific plan(s) will receive employer contributions:** The Board is granted discretion to select the plan(s), but no selection is specified in the bill text.  
- **Details of student loan certification process:** The Board must establish a compliant process, but timing/manner details are left to Board rules and procedures.  
- **Number of pay periods used to calculate “maximum annual match”:** Defined as “maximum match × number of pay periods,” but the bill text does not state the pay-period count.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Increase match authorization:** “Maximum match” set at **$20** per pay period (from $10).  
- **Revise qualified participant definition:** Employee must satisfy either:  
  - **Enrolled** in deferred compensation program for **≥ 6 consecutive months** immediately preceding receipt of match; **or**  
  - Deferred the **IRS maximum allowable** within the **6 months** preceding receipt of match.  
- **Add student loan matching feature:** By **January 1 following the effective date**, Board must approve amendments allowing match eligibility based on **qualified student loan payments** (as permitted under **26 U.S.C. § 401(m)**), with participant certification required.  
- **Annual cap:** Aggregate annual employer contributions may not exceed the **maximum annual match** within a plan year.  
- **Board discretion on deposit plan:** Board selects which plan(s) receive employer contributions to reduce administrative costs and participant burdens.  
- **Appropriation contingency:** Program parameters (match amount modifications, percentage matched, number of pay periods matched, and other aspects) are contingent on **General Assembly funding** and may be administered via Board rules consistent with applicable federal law.

## Who Benefits
- **State employees (including school district employees)** who participate in deferred compensation plans covered by Chapter 60A.  
- Employees with **qualified student loan payments** who may receive employer contributions into a retirement account even if not making equivalent retirement deferrals (subject to plan amendments and certification process).  
- Potentially, administrative stakeholders/participants if the Board’s plan-selection reduces the need to maintain **multiple plan accounts**.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **State budget / contributing funds** (General Fund and Special Funds) if the match is reinstated and funded, due to projected multi-million-dollar annual costs (see Budget Impact).  
- Potentially, participants facing additional administrative steps to document/certify **student loan payments** (process details not specified in statute; to be set by the Board).
