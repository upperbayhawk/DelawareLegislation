## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Create a new Chapter 25C in Title 16 (“End of Life Options”) establishing a regulated process for a terminally ill **adult Delaware resident** with **decision-making capacity** to request and **self-administer** prescribed medication to end life “in a humane and dignified manner,” with specified safeguards, provider duties, reporting/oversight, confidentiality, and insurance/contract protections.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes a “qualified patient” (adult Delaware resident; terminal illness; decision-making capacity; voluntary, informed request) to obtain medication they may **self-administer** to end life. (Title 16, Ch. 25C; §§ 2501C, 2504C, 2502C(17))
- Defines “terminal illness” as incurable/irreversible and expected to result in death within **6 months** (as a medical probability). (§ 2502C(17))
- Requires a multi-step request: **2 oral requests** and **1 written request**; the second oral request must be **no fewer than 15 days** after the first. (§ 2505C(b))
- Prohibits prescribing before required waiting periods: **≥ 15 days** after the first oral request and **≥ 48 hours** after the written request. (§ 2507C(a)-(b))
- Allows the individual to **rescind at any time** (including “without regard to the individual’s mental state”); prescribing is barred unless an **opportunity to rescind** is offered. (§ 2506C)
- Requires attending and consulting physician/APRN evaluations; mandates psychiatric/psychological referral if capacity is questioned; **no prescription** if the evaluator finds lack of capacity. (§§ 2508C-2510C)
- Directs DHSS to issue regulations; requires provider reporting; DHSS publishes an **annual statistical report**; collected information is generally **not a public record**. (§ 2511C)
- Effective immediately, with **implementation** on the earlier of DHSS notice that final regulations are promulgated or **January 1, 2026**. (Implementation clause)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Patient autonomy with safeguards:** permits self-administered end-of-life medication for eligible terminally ill adults with a structured request process and rescission rights. (§§ 2501C, 2505C-2507C)
- **Informed decision requirements:** providers must disclose diagnosis/prognosis, risks, expected result, and alternatives (comfort care, hospice, pain control), and the chapter does not reduce other informed-consent duties. (§ 2503C; § 2502C(10))
- **Coercion and capacity protections:** required private coercion screening; required mental-health referral when capacity is in doubt; disqualification if capacity is lacking. (§§ 2508C(5), 2510C)
- **Insurance/contract and benefit protections:** self-administration does not invalidate life/health/accident insurance or annuities; prohibits benefit denial/alteration or coercion based on availability/request; bars conditioning issuance/rates on request/rescission; invalidates contract/will provisions restricting the ability to request/rescind. (§ 2501C(d); § 2512C)
- **Legal clarity and protections for participants:** good-faith compliance immunity from civil/criminal liability and professional discipline for specified acts (including prescribing/dispensing or refusing, and being present at self-administration), while preserving liability for negligence/recklessness/intentional misconduct. (§ 2512C; § 2501C(c))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Access delays due to mandatory waiting periods:** at least **15 days** after the first oral request and **48 hours** after the written request before prescribing. (§ 2507C(a)-(b))
- **Administrative and documentation burden:** attending/consulting clinician duties include residency verification request, extensive counseling, referrals, detailed medical-record documentation, and reporting to DHSS. (§§ 2508C-2509C; § 2511C)
- **Institutional restrictions may limit locations of access:** a health-care institution may prohibit prescribing on the institution’s premises if it provides notice to providers and the public. (§ 2512C)
- **Witness eligibility limits may be difficult for some patients:** written request requires 2 adult witnesses with conflict-of-interest restrictions; attending clinician cannot be a witness. (§ 2505C(c)-(e))

## Health Impact
- Establishes a regulated end-of-life option allowing qualified terminally ill adults (prognosis **≤ 6 months**) to obtain medication for **self-administration** to end life, with mandatory informed-consent counseling (including hospice/comfort care/pain control alternatives), coercion screening, capacity safeguards, and clinical documentation. (§§ 2501C, 2502C(17), 2503C, 2508C-2510C)
- Prohibits denial/alteration of otherwise available health-care benefits and coercion tied to end-of-life medication requests/availability. (§ 2512C)

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates new Title 16, Chapter 25C (“End of Life Options”).**
- **Eligibility:** “qualified patient” must be an **adult Delaware resident**; have terminal illness (death expected within **6 months**); have decision-making capacity; make an informed, voluntary request; and complete attending/consulting clinician processes. (§§ 2502C(17), 2504C, 2508C-2509C)
- **No surrogate/advance-directive requests:** the request must be made personally; not by guardian/surrogate and not via advance health-care directive. (§ 2504C(c); § 2501C(b))
- **Request steps & witnesses:** **2 oral** + **1 written** request; written request witnessed by **2 adults** with conflict limits; attending clinician cannot witness. (§ 2505C)
- **Waiting periods & expiration:** second oral request ≥ **15 days** after first; prescribing barred until ≥ **15 days** after first oral and ≥ **48 hours** after written request; first oral request expires after **1 year** if no prescription is written. (§ 2505C(b); § 2507C(a)-(c))
- **Rescission:** may rescind at any time; prescribing barred unless opportunity to rescind is offered. (§ 2506C)
- **Provider duties:** attending and consulting clinician evaluations/confirmations; coercion screening privately; required counseling (including notification/presence, safekeeping/disposal, not ingesting in public); detailed medical-record documentation; dispensing/prescription routing rules (including signature-required delivery if mailed/messenger). (§§ 2508C-2509C)
- **Capacity evaluation:** mandatory psychiatric/psychological referral when capacity is questioned; no prescription if lack of capacity is found. (§ 2510C)
- **Oversight & confidentiality:** DHSS rulemaking; mandatory provider reporting; annual statistical report; collected information generally not a public record; potential sharing with Division of Professional Regulation upon suspected noncompliance. (§ 2511C)
- **Insurance/contract protections & legal characterizations:** protections for insurance/annuities and against benefit changes/coercion; contract/will provisions restricting request/rescission are invalid; compliant actions are not characterized as suicide/assisted-suicide/homicide/elder abuse/euthanasia per the chapter’s construction language; immunity for good-faith compliance, with negligence/recklessness/intentional misconduct carved out. (§§ 2501C(d), 2512C)

## Who Benefits
- Terminally ill **adult Delaware residents** with decision-making capacity who want the option to self-administer life-ending medication under defined safeguards. (§§ 2501C, 2504C; § 2502C(17))
- Health-care providers acting in good faith compliance (immunity/professional-discipline protections) and those who decline participation (express ability to refuse). (§ 2512C; § 2501C(c))
- Insured individuals and patients generally, via protections against insurance/benefit impacts and coercion related to end-of-life medication requests/availability. (§ 2512C)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Patients seeking faster access, due to required waiting periods (**15 days**, **48 hours**) and possible restart if the process lapses (**1 year** expiration of first oral request). (§ 2507C)
- Patients in facilities where institutions prohibit on-premises prescribing (if the institution adopts and notices such a policy). (§ 2512C)
- Participating clinicians/pharmacies subject to additional counseling, documentation, and reporting requirements. (§§ 2508C-2509C; § 2511C)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Approved:** **May 20, 2025**. (Notes)
- **Effective:** **immediately**. (Implementation clause)
- **Implementation trigger:** earlier of (1) DHSS Secretary notice in the Register of Regulations that final regulations under **§ 2511C** are promulgated, or (2) **January 1, 2026**.
- **Patient-process timing:**
  - Second oral request: **no fewer than 15 days** after first oral request. (§ 2505C(b))
  - Prescription may not be written **< 15 days** after first oral request. (§ 2507C(a))
  - Prescription may not be written **< 48 hours** after written request. (§ 2507C(b))
  - First oral request expires after **1 year** if no prescription is written. (§ 2507C(c))
- **Reporting:** DHSS must issue an **annual statistical report** (no specific publication date stated in notes). (§ 2511C(e))

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements

- **Oversight:** DHSS rulemaking and data collection; DHSS may review a sample of records annually; DHSS may share information with the Division of Professional Regulation when noncompliance is suspected; DHSS may access Prescription Monitoring Program reports for compliance assessment.
