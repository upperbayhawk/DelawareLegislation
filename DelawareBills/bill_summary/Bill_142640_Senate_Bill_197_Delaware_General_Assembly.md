## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware law to require most **school district and charter school public works contracts** executed after **December 31, 2026** to include a **project labor agreement (PLA)** with the **Delaware Building and Construction Trades Council**, with a limited exception when there was **only 1 bid for the craft** under the contract.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires a **PLA** for a **public works project** contract for a **school district or charter school**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(1))
- Applies only to **contracts executed after December 31, 2026**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(2)a)
- **Exception:** PLA requirement does **not** apply if there was **only 1 bid for the craft** under the contract. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(2)b)
- Defines “project labor agreement” as a **pre-hire collective bargaining agreement** for a specific project and clarifies it **includes community workforce agreements**. (29 Del. C. § 6902(24))
- Makes PLAs **binding on all contractors and subcontractors** via bid specifications and bid documents. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(3))
- Requires PLAs to include **no-strike/no-lockout guarantees** and **dispute resolution procedures** through completion. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(c)(1)-(2))
- Limits PLA coverage to **work at the construction sites**, not off-site work. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(1)a)
- Adds a condition related to certain **union trust funds** and the **ERISA building and construction industry exemption (29 U.S.C. § 1383(b))**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(1)b)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces work stoppage risk** by requiring PLAs to include **guarantees against strikes, lock-outs, or similar actions**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(c)(1))
- Promotes **timely dispute handling** by requiring **effective, immediate, and mutually binding procedures** for labor/jurisdictional disputes before completion. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(c)(2))
- Supports **standardized labor conditions** across a school project because the PLA is **binding on all contractors and subcontractors**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b), (b)(3))
- Limits labor-agreement reach to the jobsite by stating PLA provisions apply **only to work at construction sites**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(1)a)
- Allows optional PLA terms to **retain a percentage of a contractor’s current workforce**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(2)a)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- May **restrict procurement flexibility** by mandating a PLA with a specific entity (the **Delaware Building and Construction Trades Council**) for covered school public works contracts after **12/31/2026**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(1)-(2))
- Creates an added **contracting requirement** that could increase administrative/legal work to ensure PLA terms are included and incorporated into **bid specifications** and documents. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(3))
- Exception depends on a potentially unclear trigger—**“only 1 bid for the craft”**—which could complicate procurement determinations. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(2)b)

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- What exactly constitutes **“only 1 bid for the craft under the contract”** (e.g., per trade package, per subcontract, per prime bid, or per classification)? The bill does not define “craft” or bid-count methodology. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(2)b)
- The bill requires a PLA **“with the Delaware Building and Construction Trades Council”** but does not describe the negotiation process, timing, or what happens if agreement cannot be reached. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(1))
- The bill states a PLA must “advance the interests of the school” regarding **cost, efficiency, quality, timeliness, skilled labor force, and safety**, but provides no metric or decision standard for evaluating those interests. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(c))
- The term **“public works project”** is not newly defined in the provided text; applicability may depend on existing Title 29 definitions not included in the excerpt. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(1))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Title 14 change:** Requires school construction and renovation projects under Title 14, Ch. 23 to comply with **PLA requirements in 29 Del. C. § 6960B**. (14 Del. C. § 2304(a))
- **Definition update (Title 29):** “Project labor agreement” includes **community workforce agreements**. (29 Del. C. § 6902(24))
- **New/updated PLA mandate (Title 29):**
  - Covered contracts must include a **PLA with the Delaware Building and Construction Trades Council**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(1))
  - Applies to **contracts executed after December 31, 2026**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(2)a)
  - **Does not apply** if there was **only 1 bid for the craft** under the contract. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(2)b)
  - PLA is **binding on all contractors/subcontractors**, implemented through **bid specifications** and **bid documents**. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(3))
  - Required PLA terms include jobsite-only application, certain **ERISA-related trust fund** provision, no-strike/no-lockout, and dispute resolution procedures. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(1), (c)(1)-(2))
  - Permitted PLA terms may include allowing contractors to **retain a percentage of their current workforce** and limiting labor-agreement coverage to the project only. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(2))

## Who Benefits
- **School districts and charter schools** (defined as “school” for this section) that seek contractual assurances like **no-strike/no-lockout** clauses and structured dispute resolution for public works projects. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(3), (c)(1)-(2))
- **Delaware Building and Construction Trades Council**, as the required PLA counterparty for covered contracts. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(1))
- Contractors/subcontractors on covered projects may benefit from uniform labor rules and from provisions that may allow retention of a **percentage of current workforce** (if included). (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(2)a)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Contractors/subcontractors** that prefer not to operate under a PLA may face reduced flexibility because a PLA is required for covered contracts after **12/31/2026** (unless the single-bid-for-craft exception applies). (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(1)-(2))
- Procurement officials may face added compliance steps to ensure PLAs are included and made binding through **bid specifications** and documents. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(b)(3))

## Effective Dates and Deadlines

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Compliance requirement:** Covered school public works contracts must include a **PLA** meeting statutory requirements. (29 Del. C. § 6960B(a)(1), (b), (c))
