## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29 to require Delaware institutions of higher education that receive State funds to **keep, retain, and provide specified compensation-related records** (as required under **29 Del. C. § 5822**) and to authorize the **State Auditor** to request those records and impose **administrative penalties** for knowing violations.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Applies to **any public or private institution of higher education located in Delaware** that awards degrees (associate’s, bachelor’s, or advanced) and **receives moneys from the State**, including via the **annual appropriations act**.  
- Requires covered institutions to **keep records required under 29 Del. C. § 5822**.  
- Clarifies the obligation applies **for employees paid in whole or in part with State funds**.  
- Requires institutions to **provide those records to the State Auditor upon request**.  
- Creates **administrative penalties** if the State Auditor finds (by case decision) an institution is **knowingly** violating recordkeeping/provision requirements.  
- Penalties: **$10,000** for a **first violation**; **$50,000** for a **second or subsequent violation within 5 years** of the first.  
- If a penalty is imposed, the State Auditor must report the violation and penalty amount to the **Public Integrity Commission**, **Department of Justice**, and **Office of the Controller General**.  
- Requires records to be **retained for 5 years** and takes effect **1 year after enactment** (enacted/approved **May 22, 2025**).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves accountability and auditability** for compensation practices tied to **State-funded pay**, by requiring recordkeeping and production to the State Auditor upon request.  
- **Deters noncompliance** through defined penalties (**$10,000 / $50,000**) for knowing violations.  
- **Creates cross-agency visibility** when violations occur via mandatory reporting to the Public Integrity Commission, DOJ, and Controller General.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative burden** on institutions to keep and retain § 5822 records and respond to State Auditor requests, including a **5-year retention** requirement.  
- **Financial exposure** for institutions found knowingly noncompliant: **$10,000** for first violation and **$50,000** for subsequent violations within **5 years**.
