## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 9, § 8603 of the Delaware Code to allow **all three counties** to accept payments of **real estate taxes** on a **quarterly or monthly basis**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **9 Del. C. § 8603** (section heading updated to “Quarterly payments. Tax payments.”).
- Authorizes a county’s **receiver of taxes and county treasurer or director of finance** to accept **real estate tax payments** on a **quarterly or monthly** basis.
- Removes the existing exception stating the section **does not apply to New Castle County**.
- Result: **New Castle County may accept** quarterly or monthly payments, consistent with the other counties.
- The bill is permissive (“**may accept**”), not a mandate.
- Applies to taxes “**due and payable on real estate**.”
- No specific implementation date, deadlines, or penalties are stated in the bill text.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded payment flexibility statewide:** By removing the New Castle County exclusion, all three counties are allowed to accept **quarterly or monthly** real estate tax payments (Synopsis and § 8603 as amended).
- **Potential for improved taxpayer cash flow management:** The text expressly allows **more frequent payment intervals** (quarterly/monthly), which can reduce need for lump-sum payments (as described in § 8603).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential administrative changes for New Castle County:** Allowing quarterly/monthly payments may require **billing, payment processing, or accounting adjustments** for counties choosing to implement it (implied by the authorization to accept payments on different schedules; no operational details provided).
- **Potential complexity from optional adoption:** Because counties “may accept” (not “shall”), taxpayers may face **different options by county** depending on local implementation choices (based on the permissive language).
