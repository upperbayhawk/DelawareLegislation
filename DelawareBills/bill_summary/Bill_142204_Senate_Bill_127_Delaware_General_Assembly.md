## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the Charter of the City of New Castle to authorize the City to impose and collect a rental tax, with an exemption for certain directly subsidized rental properties.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes New Castle City Council to “impose and collect a tax of no more than five percent (5%) on the gross rents” within City boundaries.  
- Sets the maximum tax rate at **5%** of **gross rents**.  
- Specifies the tax **may not apply** to rental properties **directly subsidized** via **Federal, State, or City of New Castle rental assistance funding**.  
- Places this authority in **Section 11 (Municipal Power)** of the City Charter.  
- Does not specify the actual tax rate to be imposed (only the cap), implementation rules, or administrative process in the bill text.  
- Does not specify an effective date or compliance/enforcement mechanism in the bill text.  

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **New local revenue option:** Allows the City to raise revenue through a rental tax up to **5% of gross rents** (authorization is explicit in amended charter language).  
- **Protection for subsidized rentals:** The tax **may not apply** to rental properties **directly subsidized** by federal, state, or City rental assistance funding (explicit exemption).  

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential cost pass-through:** A tax on **gross rents** could increase costs for rental housing operations (the bill authorizes the tax; it does not restrict how costs are absorbed or passed on).  
- **Administrative complexity:** Excluding “directly subsidized” rental properties may require verification of subsidy status (the exemption exists, but procedures are not specified).
