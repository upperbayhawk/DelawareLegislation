## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Create a new six-member Joint Grants-in-Aid Committee to review Grants-in-Aid requests made to the Joint Finance Committee (JFC) and to draft a recommended Grants-in-Aid bill for the JFC, with required procedures and added legislator compensation.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a **Joint Grants-in-Aid Committee** in **Title 29, Chapter 64B** to **review Grants-in-Aid requests** submitted to the **Joint Finance Committee** and **draft a recommended Grants-in-Aid bill** for the JFC.  
- Sets committee membership at **6 legislators**: **3 Senators** (appointed by Senate President Pro Tempore) and **3 Representatives** (appointed by House Speaker), with **no more than 2 from the same political party** in each chamber’s appointees.  
- Establishes alternating leadership by year: in **odd-numbered years**, Senate appoints **Chair** and House appoints **Vice Chair**; in **even-numbered years**, House appoints **Chair** and Senate appoints **Vice Chair**.  
- Requires the Committee to review applicant **performance**, **financial stability**, **financial controls**, **audits**, and **efficiency**, and to require specific financial disclosures (state appropriations, other revenues, grants, and donations).  
- Authorizes the Committee to require **other financial documents** it deems relevant.  
- Requires the **Office of the Controller General** to provide staffing for the Committee.  
- Adds annual legislator compensation for this committee: **$4,670** for **Chair and Vice Chair**; **$3,929** for **members**.  
- Directs the Chair/Vice Chair (with the Controller General) to develop review and recommendation procedures and submit them to JFC leadership **by February 1, 2026** for approval.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Increased financial transparency and diligence**: The Committee must require **itemized disclosure** of (1) all **State appropriations** and other government revenues, (2) **grants received**, and (3) **summary of donations** (Section 6403B(a)(2)-(4)).  
- **More structured oversight of applicants**: Mandated review of **financial stability, financial controls, audits, efficiency, and performance** (Section 6403B(a)(1)).  
- **Formalized process for Grants-in-Aid recommendations**: The Committee must **draft a recommended grants-in-aid bill** for the JFC (Sections 6401B(b)(2) and 6403B(a)(6)).  
- **Bipartisan composition constraints**: Not more than **2 appointees from the same political party** per chamber’s three appointments (Section 6402B(a)(1)-(2)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **New ongoing costs to the State**: Fiscal note estimates ongoing costs including committee compensation and operating expenses plus Controller General staffing and operations (see Budget Impact).  
- **Added administrative burden for grant applicants**: Applicants may be required to produce detailed, itemized disclosures and potentially **additional financial documents** the Committee deems relevant (Section 6403B(a)-(b)).  
- **Potential duplication of review functions**: The Committee reviews requests made to the JFC and drafts a recommended bill for the JFC, which may add an additional step in the Grants-in-Aid process (Sections 6401B(b), 6403B(a)(5)-(6)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates new Chapter 64B in Title 29** establishing the **Joint Grants-in-Aid Committee** and its purpose (Section 1; §6401B).  
- **Sets membership and political party limits**: 3 Senators + 3 Representatives; no more than 2 from same party in each chamber’s appointees (§6402B(a)).  
- **Establishes chair/vice-chair rotation by odd/even years** (§6402B(b)).  
- **Defines required duties** including review of applicant performance/financial condition and required disclosures; authorizes requests for additional financial documents (§6403B).  
- **Requires Controller General’s Office staffing support** (§6404B).  
- **Adds annual compensation amounts** for Chair/Vice Chair (**$4,670**) and members (**$3,929**) of the new committee by amending **29 Del. C. § 710(c)** (Section 2).  
- **Procedure deadline**: Chair and Vice Chair (with Controller General) must develop procedures and submit them for approval to JFC leadership **by February 1, 2026** (Section 3).

## Who Benefits
- **Joint Finance Committee**, which receives a **recommended grants-in-aid bill** developed by the new Committee (Sections 6401B(b)(2), 6403B(a)(6)).  
- **General Assembly leadership and members**, through a more formal review structure and through **additional compensation** for service on the Committee (29 Del. C. §710(c)(13)-(14)).  
- **Public/State oversight interests**, through required review of audits/financial controls and mandated disclosures for grant applicants (§6403B).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Grant-in-Aid applicants**, due to added documentation and disclosure requirements and potential requests for other financial documents (§6403B).  
- **State budget**, due to new ongoing administrative and compensation costs (fiscal note).

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
