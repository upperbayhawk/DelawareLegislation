## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the Charter of the City of Newark to authorize Newark to levy and collect a per-student, per-semester tax on colleges and universities that host **in-person classes within Newark city limits**, including otherwise tax-exempt or state-affiliated entities (expressly including the University of Delaware), and to restrict payment methods for that tax.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Authorizes Newark City Council to levy a **tax of up to $50 per student per semester** (fall and spring) on any **college or university hosting in-person classes** within Newark.  
- Applies **per full-time or part-time** **undergraduate or postgraduate** student, but **only** to students **enrolled in classes taking place within the City of Newark**.  
- Applies to **all colleges and universities**, including entities that are **subdivisions/agencies of the State of Delaware** or are otherwise **tax-exempt**, including **but not limited to the University of Delaware**.  
- Requires that tax payments **cannot be made via credit card**; payments must be processed via **ACH** or another **approved payment method** directed by the **City Manager**.  
- The enacted (approved) version **does not include** the introduced-version provision that would have adjusted the tax annually by CPI-U starting **January 1, 2026**.  
- The Act is a municipal charter amendment and therefore required a **two-thirds vote** of the members elected to each house (noted in the bill synopsis).  
- **Approved June 24, 2025**.
