## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14 of the Delaware Code to restrict **school resource officers (SROs)** and **school constables** from cooperating with **federal agencies on immigration enforcement activities** unless they first receive **authorization from the Delaware Attorney General**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits **SROs and school constables** from cooperating with a **federal agency** in **immigration enforcement-related activity** unless the cooperation is **first authorized by the Delaware Attorney General**.  
- Requires that authorization be **requested by the supervising authority** of the SRO or school constable (not by the officer/constable directly).  
- Requires the authorization request to be **in writing**.  
- Requires the request be submitted **at least 72 hours prior** to the date of the activity.  
- Requires the request to include: the **federal enforcement agency name**, **reason** for the request, **dates and duration** of the activity, and a **detailed description of scope**.  
- The scope description must include: **purpose**, **list of records sought**, **names of individuals who may be impacted**, and **how the activity will be carried out**.  
- Creates a new/updated code section: **14 Del. C. § 4112G (Federal law enforcement cooperation; restrictions).**

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- Establishes a clear **state-level approval checkpoint** (Attorney General authorization) before SROs/constables participate in **immigration enforcement-related activities**. (14 Del. C. § 4112G(a))  
- Imposes **minimum notice and documentation** requirements (written request, **72-hour** advance submission, specific required details), which may improve **process consistency and accountability**. (14 Del. C. § 4112G(b))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Adds procedural requirements (written request with specified elements and **72-hour** advance submission) that may **delay or prevent cooperation** with federal immigration enforcement activities in school contexts. (14 Del. C. § 4112G(b)(2))  
- Requires inclusion of **names of individuals who may be impacted** in the request, which could increase **administrative burden** and may raise handling/privacy considerations (though the bill does not specify safeguards). (14 Del. C. § 4112G(b)(6)c)

## Key Policy Changes
- **New restriction:** SROs and school constables **may not cooperate** with a federal agency in immigration enforcement-related activity **unless** the Delaware Attorney General **first authorizes** the specific cooperation. (§ 4112G(a))

- **Request requirements:** must be **in writing** and submitted **≥ 72 hours** before the activity. (§ 4112G(b)(1)-(2))
