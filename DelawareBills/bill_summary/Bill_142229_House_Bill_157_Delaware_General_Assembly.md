## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Repeal Chapter 26A of Title 16 of the Delaware Code, thereby eliminating the Hearing Aid Loan Bank Program, because the program is no longer operational and is described as obsolete.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Repeals **Chapter 26A, Title 16** of the Delaware Code **in its entirety**, removing the statutory basis for the Hearing Aid Loan Bank Program.
- The program was created in **2003** (Volume **74**, Chapter **109** of the Laws of Delaware) to lend hearing aids **temporarily** to **children under 3 years old**.
- Initial loans were **6 months**, with possible **3-month extensions** granted by the Program Manager (as described in the synopsis).
- The bill states program need has **steadily decreased** and the program is now **obsolete**.
- The **Division of Public Health no longer stocks** the Hearing Aid Loan Bank (per synopsis).
- The enacted law was **approved August 25, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Removes obsolete law from the Code:** The bill explicitly repeals a program described as **“no longer operational”** and **“obsolete.”**
- **Aligns statute with current practice:** The synopsis states the Division of Public Health **no longer stocks** the loan bank, and repeal aligns legal requirements with actual operations.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Eliminates a statutory program for children under 3:** The program’s stated purpose was lending hearing aids temporarily to **children under age 3**; repeal removes this Code-based option regardless of whether any limited need remains.
