## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 18, § 321 of the Delaware Code to clarify that providing documents, materials, or information to the Delaware Insurance Commissioner during an insurance examination does **not** waive any applicable privilege or confidentiality claim.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **18 Del. C. § 321 (Examination report)** by adding/clarifying a **no-waiver** rule in subsection **(h)**.
- Provides that **no waiver** of **any applicable privilege** or **claim of confidentiality** occurs solely because information is disclosed to the **Insurance Commissioner** under § 321.
- The synopsis states this protection applies to **privilege between a policyholder or claimant and an insurance company**.
- The synopsis further states the rule applies **whether or not the information is redacted** when submitted.
- The enacted law appears in **Laws of Delaware, Volume 85, Chapter 180** (formerly **House Bill 74**).
- The Act was **approved August 25, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Protects privileges/confidentiality during regulatory examinations:** The text states that disclosure to the Commissioner under § 321 does **not** waive privilege or confidentiality, which may reduce reluctance to share sensitive materials in examinations.
