## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Make a technical correction to Title 4, § 1347 (background checks) under the Delaware Marijuana Control Act to align the categories of people subject to fingerprint-based background checks with federal requirements under Public Law 92-544 for access to FBI criminal history record information.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **4 Del. C. § 1347(a)(3)** (background checks) for marijuana establishment licensing.
- Clarifies the set of persons required to complete a background check when they are, or seek to become, **a director, officer, board member, or agent** associated with a **licensed marijuana establishment** or an **applicant business entity**.
- The bill is characterized in the synopsis as a **technical correction** intended to comply with **Pub. L. 92-544** requirements related to access to **FBI criminal history record information**.
- Does not create a new background-check program; it modifies statutory wording describing covered categories.
- Keeps in place that background checks apply to persons with an **ownership interest of 10% or more** (referenced in § 1347(f) text shown).
- Maintains the requirement that the **State Bureau of Identification** provides background-check information to the **Division** (as described in § 1347(f)).
- **Approved July 30, 2025** (as enacted in Chapter 114, Volume 85).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)

## Key Policy Changes
- **Amends 4 Del. C. § 1347(a)(3)** to define/clarify persons required to complete a background check under the chapter, specifically those who are or seek to become:
  - a **director**,
  - **officer**,
  - **board member**, or
  - **agent**
  of a **licensed marijuana establishment** or a **business entity applying** for a marijuana establishment license.
- Leaves intact (in the excerpt shown) the provision in **§ 1347(f)** referencing background checks for:
  - a **current applicant**,
  - a **person performing work at or for a marijuana establishment**,
  - a **director, officer, board member, agent**, or
  - a person holding **10% or more ownership interest**
  and requiring the **State Bureau of Identification** to provide specified information to the **Division**.
