## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware election law to require voters to present specific forms of **photographic identification** before voting, and amend DMV law to make the **first nondriver identification card free** (and lower the renewal fee) to facilitate access to qualifying ID.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires a voter, before being permitted to vote, to present **photographic identification**: **Delaware driver’s license**, **DMV-issued nondriver ID**, **U.S. passport**, or **official military ID**.  
- If the voter does not have ID with them, the voter must **sign an affidavit of affirmation** that they are the person listed on the election district record.  
- Makes the **first DMV nondriver identification card free of charge** (currently **$40**).  
- Sets the fee for **subsequent nondriver identification cards issued after expiration** at **$20** (currently **$5** for subsequent cards, per fiscal note).  
- Fiscal note projects **Transportation Trust Fund (TTF) revenue losses** and **TTF administrative costs** due to the fee change.  
- Fiscal note notes nondriver ID fee revenue is **pledged to Delaware Transportation Authority (DTA) Revenue Bonds**, and suspension of fees is restricted until bonds are defeased; outstanding amount cited: **$975,830,000**.  
- Bill synopsis states each county **Department of Elections** will be required to conduct **at least 2 countywide programs** at accessible locations (details not codified in provided bill text sections).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Standardizes photo ID presentation for in-person voting** by specifying acceptable IDs (driver’s license, DMV nondriver ID, passport, military ID). (15 Del. C. § 4937 amendment)
- **Reduces upfront cost barrier to obtaining an ID** by making the **first nondriver ID card free** (from **$40**). (21 Del. C. § 3103 amendment)
- Maintains a **fallback mechanism** for voters without ID at the polling place through an **affidavit of affirmation**. (15 Del. C. § 4937 amendment)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential increased administrative burden at polling places** to verify photo IDs and manage affidavit process when ID is not present. (15 Del. C. § 4937 amendment)
- **TTF revenue reductions** from eliminating the $40 initial fee, with projected losses of **$1,096,220 (FY2026)**, **$1,129,106 (FY2027)**, **$1,162,979 (FY2028)**. (Fiscal note)
- Raises a **bond-pledge conflict**: fiscal note states nondriver ID revenues are pledged to DTA bonds and the State/DTA are prohibited from suspending fees until bonds are defeased; bill nevertheless sets first card fee to $0. (Fiscal note; 21 Del. C. § 3103 amendment)

## Key Policy Changes
- **Voting ID requirement (Title 15):** Voter must provide **photographic identification**, specifically:  
  - **Driver’s license**, or  
  - **DMV-issued Nondriver Identification Card**, or  
  - **United States Passport**, or  
  - **Official military identification card**.  
  If voter does not have ID with them, voter must **sign an affidavit of affirmation**. (Amends 15 Del. C. § 4937)
- **DMV nondriver ID fees (Title 21):**
  - **First identification card:** **free of charge** (replacing **$40** fee).  
  - **Subsequent identification cards after expiration:** **$20.00** fee (replacing prior fee structure described in fiscal note).  
  - Card expiration remains the **eighth anniversary** of birth date following issuance (with Feb. 29 exception), with limits for temporary foreign nationals. (Amends 21 Del. C. § 3103)

## Who Benefits
- Eligible voters who possess one of the specified photo IDs and prefer a standardized ID check process.
- Delaware residents who need a nondriver ID and will now receive the **first card at $0** instead of **$40**. (21 Del. C. § 3103 amendment)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected

- Voters who arrive without any of the specified photo IDs may face an added step of completing an **affidavit of affirmation** before voting. (15 Del. C. § 4937 amendment)
