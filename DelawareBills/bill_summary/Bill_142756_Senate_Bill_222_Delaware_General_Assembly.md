## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Propose a constitutional amendment to impose term limits on members of the Delaware General Assembly and on certain statewide elected officers (Attorney General, Insurance Commissioner, Auditor of Accounts, and State Treasurer).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **Article II, § 3** to add legislative term limits: a person may not be elected a **6th** time as **Senator** or a **9th** time as **Representative** (i.e., capped at **5** Senate elections and **8** House elections).  
- Amends **Article III, § 21** to limit the Attorney General, Insurance Commissioner, Auditor of Accounts, and State Treasurer to **no more than 2 terms** in the same office (whether appointed or elected).  
- Provides that the Act is **effective immediately on enactment**, but term-limit rules are **implemented for each office at the next election held for that office**.  
- Provides that any term completed **before the implementation date for that office does not count** toward the new term limits.  
- Clarifies this bill is the **first leg** of a constitutional amendment; the same changes must pass again **after the next general election** by the **next General Assembly**.  
- Requires a **two-thirds vote** of all members elected to each chamber to pass (per **Delaware Constitution, Article XVI, § 1**).  
- The synopsis indicates the amendment would take effect upon passage of the **second leg in the 154th General Assembly**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Creates explicit term limits** for legislators and specified statewide officers (Senators capped at **5 elections**, Representatives at **8 elections**, and certain statewide officers at **2 terms**) (Article II §3(f); Article III §21).  
- **Avoids retroactive counting** of prior service by excluding terms completed before the implementation date for each office from eligibility calculations (Section 4).  
- **Phased-in implementation tied to the next election for each office** may provide administrative clarity about when limits start applying (Section 3).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Restricts voter choice** by prohibiting election beyond specified limits (Senate beyond a **5th** election; House beyond an **8th** election) and limiting statewide officers to **2 terms** (Article II §3(f); Article III §21).  
- **Does not count prior completed terms** toward limits, which may delay when limits materially constrain incumbents (Section 4).  
- Constitutional amendment process requires a **second passage after the next general election**, which can delay realization of the policy change (Synopsis; Article XVI, § 1 reference).
