## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Propose the first passage of a Delaware constitutional amendment (Article V, § 4) to (1) remove constitutional timing restrictions that require voter registration to close a set number of days before an election, and (2) require the General Assembly to provide by law means to ensure the accuracy and integrity of voter registration.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **Article V, § 4** of the Delaware Constitution to allow the General Assembly to enact voter registration laws **without constitutional restrictions on how long before an election registration must close**.  
- Adds a constitutional directive that the General Assembly **“shall provide by law means to ensure the integrity and accuracy of voter registration.”**  
- The bill is described as a response to **Albence v. Higgin, 2022 Del. LEXIS 377 (Del. 2022)**, which found same-day registration incompatible with the existing constitutional registration deadline structure.  
- Leaves in place (in the text provided) provisions about registration days and an appeal/correction process, while changing the constitutional framing to reduce deadline restrictions.  
- This is the **“first leg”** of a constitutional amendment; the same amendment must pass again **after the next general election** by the next General Assembly.  
- Requires a **two-thirds vote** in each chamber to pass, per **Article XVI, § 1** (explicitly stated in the synopsis).  
- Does not itself enact same-day registration; it changes constitutional authority so the General Assembly may legislate registration timing with fewer constitutional constraints.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Enables policy flexibility on registration timing:** Removes constitutional “restrictions on the time period in which registration must be closed prior to an election,” allowing future statutes (e.g., same-day registration) to be enacted without being blocked by the current constitutional deadline structure (Synopsis; Albence v. Higgin reference).  
- **Constitutional mandate for integrity measures:** Requires the General Assembly to provide by law “means to ensure the integrity and accuracy of voter registration,” potentially strengthening statewide list maintenance requirements (new constitutional language).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced constitutional specificity on deadlines:** By removing constitutional restrictions on when registration must close, the timing becomes primarily statutory and could change through ordinary legislation (Synopsis).  
- **Implementation details deferred:** The amendment requires integrity/accuracy measures to be provided “by law,” but does not specify what those measures are, leaving key operational choices to later legislation.
