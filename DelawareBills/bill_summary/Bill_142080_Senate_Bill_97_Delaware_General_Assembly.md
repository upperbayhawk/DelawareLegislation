## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Update Delaware’s Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act (Title 6, Chapter 17) to clarify registered-agent requirements, ratification/waiver rules for void or voidable acts, service of process consent, filing/execution rules for dissolved partnerships/series, correction/nullification of filed certificates, and certain merger/consolidation and foreign registration provisions. (Synopsis; Sections 1–13)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits a registered agent from performing its duties **solely** via a **virtual office** and/or **mail forwarding service**; defines “virtual office” as duties performed solely via the internet or other remote communications. (Section 1; §17-104(e)(2))
- Expands and clarifies the statute governing **ratification/waiver of void or voidable acts** to cover acts “**by or in respect of**” a limited partnership, including acts by **any partner or other person**, and permits ratification/waiver to be **express or implied** (including by action/inaction/acquiescence). (Section 2; §17-106(e))
- Clarifies that when §17-106(e) requires notice after a ratification/waiver, **giving notice is not a condition** to the ratification/waiver’s effectiveness. (Section 2; §17-106(e))
- Provides that **serving as** a general partner or liquidating trustee itself constitutes **consent** to Delaware service of process via the partnership’s registered agent (or Secretary of State if none). (Section 3; §17-109(a))
- Requires amendments to certificates for **dissolved** limited partnerships and **dissolved registered series** to identify **liquidating trustees** (or state limited partners are winding up), and protects liquidating trustees from general partner liability by reason of such amendment. (Section 4; §17-202(f), new (g))
- Confirms certificates of correction may **nullify** (not just correct) previously filed certificates, including certificates of cancellation. (Sections 5 and 8; §§17-203(b), 17-213(a))
- Clarifies execution/signature authority for certain filings, including allowing foreign limited partnership registration-related filings to be executed by **any authorized person** (not necessarily a general partner). (Sections 10–12; §§17-902, 17-905, 17-906)
- Confirms that if an entity’s existence/registration will cease by filing a certificate, the **full annual tax for that calendar year** must be paid **before** filing; annual tax due date remains **June 1** and unpaid tax accrues **1.5% interest per month**. (Section 13; §17-1109(b))

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Registered-agent integrity / anti-misuse measure:** Requiring more than a purely virtual/mail-forwarding setup may improve reliability for service of process and communications. (Section 1; §17-104(e)(2))
- **Greater clarity and flexibility for curing defective actions:** Explicitly permits ratification/waiver by partners or other persons, including by conduct (action/inaction/acquiescence), and clarifies timing/effect. (Section 2; §17-106(e))
- **Clearer service-of-process consent rule:** Makes consent hinge on “serving as” a general partner/liquidating trustee, not on execution of the certificate, which may reduce disputes about jurisdiction/service. (Section 3; §17-109(a))
- **Clearer winding-up documentation:** Requires certificates to identify who is winding up dissolved partnerships/registered series (liquidating trustees or limited partners), improving public record clarity. (Section 4; §17-202(f), (g))
- **Administrative correction tools expanded:** Allowing certificate nullification via a certificate of correction provides a defined mechanism to unwind erroneous filings. (Sections 5 & 8; §§17-203(b), 17-213(a))
- **Streamlined foreign filing authority:** Permitting “any person authorized” to execute foreign LP registration-related documents may reduce transactional friction. (Sections 10–12; §§17-902, 17-905, 17-906)
- **Tax timing certainty for dissolution/termination filings:** Confirms tax must be paid before a cessation filing becomes effective, potentially reducing unpaid balances at termination. (Section 13; §17-1109(b))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Higher compliance burden on registered agents:** Registered agents cannot provide services solely through virtual offices/mail forwarding, potentially requiring in-state staffing/presence beyond remote-only models. (Section 1; §17-104(e)(2))
- **Broader ratification by “implied” conduct may reduce certainty for some stakeholders:** Allowing implied ratification/waiver by action/inaction/acquiescence may increase disputes about whether conduct constituted approval. (Section 2; §17-106(e))
- **Notice not required for effectiveness:** Where notice is required after ratification/waiver, the ratification/waiver remains effective even if notice is not given, potentially disadvantaging parties who would otherwise expect notice before changes take effect. (Section 2; §17-106(e))
- **Expanded service-of-process consent trigger:** Individuals serving as general partners/liquidating trustees are deemed to consent to Delaware service via registered agent/Secretary of State based on service alone. (Section 3; §17-109(a))
- **Additional filing steps during winding up:** Dissolved partnerships/series may need certificate amendments to add liquidating trustees (or state limited partners are winding up). (Section 4; §17-202(f), (g))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Scope of “solely” virtual/mail-forwarding restriction:** The bill prohibits performing duties “solely” via virtual office/mail forwarding, but does not specify what minimum physical presence/resources are sufficient beyond the existing “generally open”/“generally present” language. (Section 1; §17-104(e)(1)–(2))
- **Standards for Secretary of State verification regulations:** Registered agents must “satisfy and adhere to regulations” on identity verification for entity contacts/individuals, but the bill text does not specify the content, timing, or enforcement approach for those regulations. (Section 1; §17-104(e)(1)e.)
- **“Promptly” timing:** Foreign LPs must “promptly” file a corrective certificate when an application becomes false, but no deadline (e.g., number of days) is specified. (Section 11; §17-905)

## Key Policy Changes
- **Registered agent limitation:** Registered agents may not perform duties solely through a **virtual office** and/or **mail forwarding service**; “virtual office” defined as duties performed solely via internet or other remote communication. (Section 1; §17-104(e)(2))
- **Registered agent verification compliance:** Registered agents must comply with Secretary of State regulations verifying identity of entity contacts/individuals to reduce risk of unlawful business purposes. (Section 1; §17-104(e)(1)e.)
- **Ratification/waiver expanded and clarified:** Applies to acts “by or in respect of” the LP, including acts by any partner or other person; ratification/waiver may be express or implied (including action/inaction/acquiescence); ratified/waived act deemed valid at time taken; notice (when required) is not a condition to effectiveness; Chancery can review on application. (Section 2; §17-106(e))
- **Service of process consent:** Serving as a general partner or liquidating trustee constitutes consent to service via registered agent/Secretary of State and makes appointment irrevocable. (Section 3; §17-109(a))
- **Dissolution/winding-up certificate updates:** Requires adding liquidating trustees (or stating limited partners are winding up) in certificates for dissolved LPs and dissolved registered series; liquidating trustees not subject to general partner liability by reason of such amendment. (Section 4; §17-202(f), (g))
- **Certificate of cancellation can be nullified:** Erroneously executed cancellation filed before dissolution/completion of winding up may be corrected **or nullified** via certificate of correction. (Section 5; §17-203(b))
- **Execution/signature rules in dissolution context:** Adds rules for who must sign required certificates when a dissolved LP/registered series is being wound up by persons not shown as general partners—generally **all liquidating trustees**, or limited partners owning **more than 50%** of profits interests if limited partners are liquidating trustees. (Section 6; §17-204(e); also conforms §17-204(a)(3), (12))
- **Consolidation filing attachment:** Certificate of limited partnership must be attached to a certificate of consolidation when the resulting entity is a domestic limited partnership. (Section 7; §17-211(c)(5))
- **Certificate of correction may nullify any filed certificate:** Provides explicit framework for correction vs. nullification; effectiveness relates back to original filing date except for persons “substantially and adversely affected,” for whom effective date is the correction filing date. (Section 8; §17-213(a))
- **Partnership agreement amendments cross-references expanded:** Confirms amendment mechanisms may include those permitted by §§ **17-220(f)** and **17-224(e)** (division and merger of registered series contexts). (Section 9; §17-302(f))
- **Foreign LP filings can be executed by any authorized person:** Applies to application for registration, amendments/corrections to application, and cancellation of registration. (Sections 10–12; §§17-902(1), 17-905, 17-906)
- **Annual tax prepayment for cessation filings:** If existence/series/registration will cease by filing a certificate, full annual tax for that calendar year is due **before** filing; due date remains **June 1**; interest remains **1.5% per month** if unpaid after due date. (Section 13; §17-1109(b))

## Who Benefits
- **Limited partnerships and their partners** needing statutory clarity on ratifying/waiving void or voidable acts and related notice/effect rules. (Section 2; §17-106(e))
- **Parties seeking to serve process** on general partners/liquidating trustees in Delaware actions involving or relating to LP business or duty violations. (Section 3; §17-109(a))
- **Dissolved LPs/registered series and stakeholders** needing clear rules on certificate amendments and execution during winding up. (Sections 4 & 6; §§17-202, 17-204)
- **Foreign limited partnerships** by allowing registration-related filings to be executed by any authorized person. (Sections 10–12; §§17-902, 17-905, 17-906)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Registered agent businesses** that operate using only remote/virtual office and/or mail-forwarding models, due to the “not solely” restriction and verification-regulation compliance requirement. (Section 1; §17-104(e))
- **General partners and liquidating trustees** who may be subject to Delaware service of process based on “serving as” in that role. (Section 3; §17-109(a))
- **LPs/series in dissolution** that must complete additional certificate amendments and signature steps to identify liquidating trustees/limited partners winding up. (Sections 4 & 6; §§17-202, 17-204)

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Effective date of Act:** **August 1, 2025.** (Section 14)
- **Act approval date (as enacted chapter):** **June 30, 2025.** (Chapter text)
- **Annual tax due date:** **June 1** following the close of the calendar year (domestic LPs, registered series, foreign LPs), or upon cancellation (as applicable). (Section 13; §17-1109(b))
- **Annual tax prepayment requirement:** Full annual tax for the calendar year is due **before filing** a certificate that will cause existence/registration to cease. (Section 13; §17-1109(b))
- **Foreign LP application corrections:** Must be filed **promptly** when the application becomes false. (Section 11; §17-905)

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Registered agent compliance requirement:** Must not perform duties solely through virtual office/mail forwarding; must adhere to Secretary of State identity-verification regulations for certain records. (Section 1; §17-104(e))
- **Tax interest penalty:** If annual tax remains unpaid after the due date, it accrues interest at **1 1/2% per month (or portion thereof)** until fully paid. (Section 13; §17-1109(b))
- **Process/service mechanism:** Service on the registered agent is deemed service on the limited partnership for certain Chancery applications under §17-106(e). (Section 2; §17-106(e))
- **Execution/signature thresholds:** Certain certificates (including cancellations in specified winding-up circumstances) may require signatures of limited partners owning **more than 50%** of the profits interests of all limited partners when limited partners are winding up. (Section 6; §17-204(a)(3), (a)(12), and (e))
