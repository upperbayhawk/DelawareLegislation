## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Delaware’s criminal code definitions and switchblade-related offense provisions to remove certain prohibitions affecting knives that can be opened with one hand, while retaining the ability to prosecute knife use in assaults or attacks under other deadly-weapon-related laws (as described in the bill synopsis).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates the definition of “ordinary pocketknife” in **11 Del. C. § 222** by increasing the maximum blade length from **3 inches to 3.75 inches**.
- The bill’s synopsis states it **removes prohibitions** in Title 11 related to owning/possessing certain knives that may be opened by one hand.
- Leaves in statute a definition section listing “switchblade knife” as a “deadly weapon” category in **11 Del. C. § 222(6)** (as shown in the bill text), while separately amending the switchblade dealing statute in **11 Del. C. § 1446**.
- Amends **11 Del. C. § 1446** (unlawfully dealing with a switchblade knife), which addresses selling, offering for sale, or possessing a knife whose blade is released by **spring mechanism or gravity**.
- Maintains § 1446 offense classification as an **unclassified misdemeanor**.
- Enacted as **Chapter 119, Volume 85, Laws of Delaware**; **Approved July 30, 2025**.
- Practical effect indicated by synopsis: reduces exposure of “otherwise law-abiding Delawareans” to criminal prosecution for lawful tool/outdoor use, while preserving prosecution when used to cause **death or serious physical injury**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded lawful carry/use of common tools:** Raising the “ordinary pocketknife” blade limit to **3.75 inches** may reduce criminal exposure for carrying common folding knives used for work or outdoor activities (supported by synopsis discussion of trades/outdoor use).
- **Reduced criminalization of one-hand-opening knives (per synopsis):** The synopsis states the Act “removes prohibitions…related to owning or possessing certain types of knives that may be opened by one hand,” potentially decreasing arrests/charges for mere possession.
- **Preserves prosecution for violent misuse (per synopsis):** The synopsis states individuals would still be subject to deadly-weapon-related prosecution when a knife is used in an attack or assault aimed at causing death or serious injury.

- **Switchblade dealing statute retained and amended within Subchapter VII:** Maintains **11 Del. C. § 1446** language describing a knife whose blade is released by **spring mechanism or gravity**, and keeps the offense as an **unclassified misdemeanor**.
- **Stated policy intent (synopsis):** Remove certain prohibitions related to owning/possessing one-hand-opening knives used as tools, while keeping liability for use in attacks/assaults causing death or serious injury.
