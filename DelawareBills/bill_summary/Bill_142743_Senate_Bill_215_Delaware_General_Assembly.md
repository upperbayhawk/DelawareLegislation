## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 14, Chapter 84 (“Fairness in Girls’ Sports Act”) to require Delaware public school athletic teams/sports to be designated by sex and to generally require student athletes to compete on teams consistent with their “biological sex” as recorded on a birth certificate (or other government record if a birth certificate is unobtainable), with a limited exception allowing girls to play on boys’ teams when no corresponding girls’ team/sport is offered.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires each school district, charter school, and Delaware Interscholastic Athletic Association (DIAA) “member school” to designate each school-sponsored athletic team or sport as **(1) males/men/boys, (2) females/women/girls, or (3) coed/mixed**. (§ 8404(a))
- Prohibits schools from allowing a student to compete on a team/sport designated for the opposite “biological sex,” **unless an exception applies**. (§ 8404(b))
- Defines “biological sex” documentation as the student’s **official birth certificate**, or **another government record** if a birth certificate is unobtainable. (§ 8404(b)(1))
- Limits when a record can be treated as “correctly stated”: it must be entered **at or near birth**, or modified **only to correct a scrivener/clerical error**. (§ 8404(b)(2))
- Creates an exception permitting a **female student** to compete on a **male-designated** team/sport if a corresponding **female** team/sport is **not offered or available**. (§ 8404(c))
- Bars governmental entities, licensing/accrediting organizations, and athletic associations/organizations from accepting complaints, investigating, or taking adverse action against a school for **complying** with the chapter. (§ 8405)
- Creates civil causes of action for certain students, schools, and others; sets a **2-year statute of limitations** from when the alleged harm occurred; and allows recovery of **damages (including psychological/emotional/physical harm), attorney fees/costs, and other relief**. (§ 8406(d)-(e))
- Makes the Act **effective immediately** but requires implementation **beginning with the 2026–2027 school year**. (Section 2)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clarifies team designation categories** (boys, girls, coed/mixed), which may provide uniform labeling across school-sponsored athletics. (§ 8404(a))
- **Explicit exception for girls** to play on boys’ teams when no girls’ team/sport exists, which may preserve access to athletic participation where girls’ offerings are unavailable. (§ 8404(c))
- **Creates enforceable legal remedies** (injunctions, damages, attorney fees) for students who are deprived of opportunities or harmed by violations, potentially strengthening compliance incentives. (§ 8406(a), (e))
- **Protects schools from certain external enforcement actions** when they are complying (complaints/investigations/adverse action by specified entities). (§ 8405)
- Requires DIAA to adopt implementing/enforcement regulations, which may standardize operational rules statewide. (§ 8407)

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Restricts participation based on “biological sex” documentation** (birth certificate or other government record) and limits recognition of changes except clerical/scrivener corrections, which may exclude students whose records differ from these criteria. (§ 8404(b)(1)-(2))
- **Potential increase in litigation exposure** for schools, charter schools, member schools, DIAA, and athletic associations/organizations due to multiple causes of action and availability of damages and attorney fees. (§ 8406(a)-(c), (e))
- **Administrative burden** to confirm eligibility using specified records and to manage disputes and compliance documentation. (§ 8404(b))
- **Asymmetrical exception**: the explicit exception is only for **female students** to play on male teams when no corresponding female option exists; no parallel exception is stated for male students. (§ 8404(c))

## Key Policy Changes
- Mandates sex-based (and coed/mixed) **designation of athletic teams/sports** for school-sponsored athletics. (§ 8404(a))
- Establishes an eligibility rule requiring students to compete according to “**biological sex**” as “correctly stated” on specified records. (§ 8404(b)(1)-(2))
- Creates a specific **exception** allowing girls to participate on boys’ teams when no corresponding girls’ option is offered/available. (§ 8404(c))
- Prohibits specified external entities from pursuing complaints/investigations/adverse action against schools for **complying** with the chapter. (§ 8405)

- **Compliance requirements:**
