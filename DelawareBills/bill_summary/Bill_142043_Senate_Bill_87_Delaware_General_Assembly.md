## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Require Delaware local governments to allow accessory dwelling units (ADUs) to expand housing supply—especially affordable and diverse housing options—by reducing local zoning and permitting barriers. (25 Del. C., new Ch. 17, §1701)

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new **Title 25, Chapter 17 (Accessory Dwelling Units)** establishing statewide ADU requirements. (§1701–§1703)
- Requires **each local government** (municipality, county, or other political subdivision) to adopt ADU ordinances **by 1 year after the Act’s effective date**. (§1703(a))
- Local ordinances must allow **at least 1 ADU “by right”** on any lot/parceI with a **single-family dwelling**. (§1703(a)(1))
- “By right” approval cannot require **public hearing, variance, conditional/special permit/exemption, or other discretionary zoning action** (beyond confirming site plan conformance). (§1702(2))
- ADUs must be allowed in multiple configurations: **attached**, **detached**, or **within** the primary dwelling. (§1703(a)(2))
- ADUs must be allowed to be used as **rental housing**. (§1703(a)(3))
- Certain private land-use restrictions that **effectively prohibit or unreasonably restrict** ADUs become **void and unenforceable** if not in effect on **June 30, 2027**, created after **June 30, 2027**, or used as the basis for rules created/effective after that date. (§1703(c))
- The bill states it carries a **two-thirds (2/3) vote requirement** because it may indirectly affect municipal charters. (Synopsis)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Increases permitted housing options statewide:** Requires every local government to allow at least **one ADU by right** on single-family lots. (§1703(a)(1))
- **Reduces approval barriers/time:** Prohibits requiring hearings, variances, and discretionary special/conditional permits for ADUs approved “by right.” (§1702(2))
- **Supports rental supply:** Requires that ADUs be allowed as **rental housing**. (§1703(a)(3))
- **Standardizes allowable ADU types:** Ensures attached, detached, and interior ADUs are permitted. (§1703(a)(2))
- **Limits private restrictions after a date certain:** Makes certain post-**June 30, 2027** restrictions/rules that block or unreasonably restrict ADUs **void and unenforceable**. (§1703(c))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduces local zoning discretion:** Local governments must adopt ordinances that allow ADUs “by right” and cannot impose “prohibitive barriers or onerous application or zoning requirements.” (§1701; §1703(a); §1703(d)(2))
- **Constrains certain private covenants/rules:** Post-**June 30, 2027** instruments or rules that effectively prohibit or unreasonably restrict ADUs are unenforceable, limiting use of some HOA/condo or deed-based controls. (§1703(c))
- **Administrative workload for local governments:** Each local government must draft and adopt conforming ordinances within **1 year after the Act’s effective date**. (§1703(a))

## Key Policy Changes
- **Creates new ADU chapter in Title 25** with definitions and statewide requirements. (§1701–§1703)
- **Mandates local ADU ordinances** by **1 year after the Act’s effective date**. (§1703(a))
- Requires local ordinances to:
  - Allow **minimum 1 ADU by right** on lots with a **single-family dwelling**. (§1703(a)(1))
  - Allow ADUs that are **attached**, **detached**, or **within** the single-family dwelling. (§1703(a)(2))
  - Allow ADUs to be used as **rental housing**. (§1703(a)(3))
- Defines “**by right**” to exclude hearings, variances, conditional/special permits/exemptions, and other discretionary zoning actions (aside from site plan conformance). (§1702(2))
- Voids/enjoins enforcement of certain restrictive covenants/rules affecting ADUs if not in effect on **June 30, 2027**, created after **June 30, 2027**, or used as the basis for rules created/effective after that date. (§1703(c))
- Limits local permitting processes by prohibiting **planning board approval** and additional restrictions contrary to the chapter’s purpose/intent. (§1703(d))

## Who Benefits
- **Homeowners** on lots with a legally established **single-family dwelling** who want to add an ADU. (§1702(1); §1703(a)(1))
- **Renters** seeking additional rental options, because ADUs must be allowed as **rental housing**. (§1703(a)(3))
- **Family members/seniors/students/other residents** seeking semi-independent living arrangements (as stated in legislative findings/purpose). (Preamble; §1701)

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Local governments** that must update zoning ordinances and align processes with the “by right” and planning-board limits. (§1703(a); §1703(d))
- **Entities relying on new or future private restrictions** (e.g., deed restrictions, Unit Property Act declarations/rules) to limit ADUs after **June 30, 2027**, to the extent those restrictions would effectively prohibit or unreasonably restrict ADUs. (§1703(c))
