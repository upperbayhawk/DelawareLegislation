## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 10 of the Delaware Code to allow a child who pleads guilty or nolo contendere in Superior Court to **directly appeal** certain **Family Court non-amenability/transfer decisions** and **Superior Court denials of transfer** to the Delaware Supreme Court, and to bar plea conditions that waive that appeal right.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Allows a child to **appeal to the Delaware Supreme Court within 30 days of sentencing** after pleading **guilty or nolo contendere** in Superior Court when the **Family Court found the child not amenable and transferred the case**. (10 Del. C. § 1010(e))
- Allows a child to **appeal within 30 days of sentencing** after pleading **guilty or nolo contendere** when the **Superior Court denied an application to transfer the case to Family Court**. (10 Del. C. § 1011(e))
- Prohibits any plea from being **conditioned on waiving** the child’s new appeal rights under these provisions. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e))
- Clarifies Superior Court **retains jurisdiction** for sentencing/postconviction when it has jurisdiction over the child, **unless the prior Family Court order is reversed by the Supreme Court** under the new appeal subsection. (10 Del. C. § 1010(d))
- Clarifies Superior Court retention of jurisdiction over certain cases **does not apply if the prior Superior Court order is reversed by the Supreme Court**. (10 Del. C. § 1011(f))
- Makes technical conforming edits reflecting 2021 law (H.B. 115, enacted **November 8, 2021**) that limited transfers to Superior Court for children under 16 except for specified serious offenses (e.g., changes from **14th** to **16th** birthday in certain triggers). (Synopsis; 10 Del. C. § 1010(c)(2)b; § 1010(f))

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Creates an earlier appellate pathway**: children can obtain Delaware Supreme Court review of a transfer/non-amenability decision **without waiting for full final adjudication after trial**, by pleading and then appealing **within 30 days of sentencing**. (Synopsis; 10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e))
- **Protects appeal rights in plea negotiations** by barring pleas conditioned on waiving the right to appeal these transfer rulings. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e))
- **Clarifies jurisdictional consequences** if the Supreme Court reverses the underlying transfer-related order, reducing uncertainty about which court retains the case. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(d), 1011(f))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **May increase appellate workload** by creating an express right to direct appeal in these scenarios within a defined time window. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e))
- **Could lengthen case timelines post-sentencing** if appeals are filed within the **30-day** window, potentially delaying final resolution. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The bill provides a **30-day deadline “of their/the child’s sentence”** but does not specify whether “sentence” means the **oral pronouncement** or the **written sentencing order entry date** for purposes of calculating the appeal deadline. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e))
- The bill states “directly appeal … to the Supreme Court” but does not specify **procedural mechanics** (e.g., record preparation, stays, expedited treatment), leaving that to existing appellate rules not included in the text. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e))

## Key Policy Changes
- **New appeal right after plea (Family Court non-amenability/transfer):** If Family Court finds a child **not amenable** and the child later pleads **guilty or nolo contendere** in Superior Court, the child may **appeal the non-amenability finding and transfer** to the Delaware Supreme Court **within 30 days of sentencing**. (10 Del. C. § 1010(e))
- **Ban on waiver as a plea condition (Family Court transfer appeals):** “No plea may be conditioned on a child waiving” the § 1010(e) appeal right. (10 Del. C. § 1010(e))
- **New appeal right after plea (Superior Court denial of transfer to Family Court):** If Superior Court denies transfer to Family Court and the child later pleads **guilty or nolo contendere**, the child may **appeal the denial** to the Delaware Supreme Court **within 30 days of sentencing**. (10 Del. C. § 1011(e))
- **Ban on waiver as a plea condition (Superior Court transfer-denial appeals):** No plea may be conditioned on waiving the § 1011(e) appeal right. (10 Del. C. § 1011(e))
- **Jurisdiction clarification (Family Court order reversal):** Superior Court retains jurisdiction for sentencing/postconviction even if convicted of a lesser included crime after trial or plea, **unless the prior Family Court order has been reversed** by the Supreme Court under § 1010(e). (10 Del. C. § 1010(d))
- **Jurisdiction clarification (Superior Court order reversal):** Superior Court retains jurisdiction over specified categories of cases involving a child **unless the prior Superior Court order has been reversed by the Supreme Court** under § 1011. (10 Del. C. § 1011(f))
- **Technical corrections conforming to 2021 changes:** Edits change certain triggers from **age 14 to age 16** for Attorney General motion practice and related hearing triggers, aligning with post–**November 8, 2021** law referenced in the Synopsis. (10 Del. C. § 1010(c)(2)b; § 1010(f); Synopsis)

## Who Benefits
- **Children charged in Superior Court** whose cases were transferred from Family Court based on a **non-amenability finding**, by gaining a defined **30-day post-sentencing** direct appeal right after a guilty/nolo plea. (10 Del. C. § 1010(e))
- **Children whose transfer-to-Family-Court request was denied in Superior Court**, by gaining a **30-day post-sentencing** direct appeal right after a guilty/nolo plea. (10 Del. C. § 1011(e))
- **Courts and parties** who benefit from clarified jurisdictional rules when a transfer-related order is reversed on appeal. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(d), 1011(f))

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Delaware Supreme Court and court administration**, to the extent the new direct appeal rights increase the number of appeals filed within the **30-day** window. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e))
- **Prosecutors and victims**, to the extent appeals could extend the post-sentencing process in cases where an appeal is filed. (10 Del. C. §§ 1010(e), 1011(e)) *(Impact described only as a process effect tied to the new appeal right; no victim-specific provisions are in the text.)*

## Effective Dates and Deadlines
- **Approved August 20, 2025.** (Chapter law)
- **Deadline:** A child must file the authorized direct appeal **within 30 days of sentencing**:
  - for appeal of Family Court non-amenability finding/transfer after a guilty or nolo contendere plea in Superior Court (10 Del. C. § 1010(e)); and
  - for appeal of Superior Court denial of transfer to Family Court after a guilty or nolo contendere plea (10 Del. C. § 1011(e)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Plea agreement restriction (compliance requirement):** Pleas **may not be conditioned** on a child waiving the right to appeal under:
  - 10 Del. C. § 1010(e) (appeal of non-amenability/transfer), and
  - 10 Del. C. § 1011(e) (appeal of denial of transfer to Family Court).
