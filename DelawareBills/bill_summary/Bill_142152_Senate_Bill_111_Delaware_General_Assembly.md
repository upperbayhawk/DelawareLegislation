## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the Town of Odessa charter to expressly authorize the Town Treasurer to collect taxes and other charges using the same powers and procedures available to the New Castle County Department of Finance under **Title 9, Chapter 87**, including the **monition method of sale** (particularly **Subchapter II**).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates **Section 12.4** of the Town of Odessa charter (Section 12, Chapter 141, Volume 68, Laws of Delaware, as amended).
- Authorizes the **Odessa Treasurer** to use **all powers** conferred on the **New Castle County Department of Finance** for collection of debts owed to the Town.
- Applies to collecting **taxes and other charges owed to the Town of Odessa**.
- Specifically references **Title 9 of the Delaware Code, Chapter 87**, and highlights **Subchapter II**.
- Expressly permits use of the **monition method of sale** as a collection mechanism.
- The Act became law upon approval on **June 24, 2025**.
- Enacted with the constitutional requirement of **two-thirds of all members elected to each house** concurring (stated in the bill).
