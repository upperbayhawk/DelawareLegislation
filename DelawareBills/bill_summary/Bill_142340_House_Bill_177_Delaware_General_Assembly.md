## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Delay the effective date of previously enacted changes to Delaware’s trade name registration process and require the Division of Revenue to conduct outreach (including an FAQ) before the new effective date.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Delays the effective date of trade name registration process changes enacted by **Senate Bill 291 (152nd GA)** and **House Bill 40 (153rd GA)**.  
- Moves an effective date from **June 2, 2025** to **February 2, 2026** (as reflected in amendments to prior session laws).  
- Provides that **Section 1** of the prior act remains **effective upon enactment**, but **Sections 2 through 4** are delayed to **February 2, 2026**.  
- Directs the **Division of Revenue** to conduct outreach to **agencies and customers** about the pending trade name registration changes.  
- Requires the outreach to occur **before February 2, 2026**.  
- Requires that outreach include an **FAQ guide** posted on the **Division of Revenue website**.  
- The act was **approved May 27, 2025**.

- **More preparation time:** The delayed effective date (to **February 2, 2026**) provides additional time before process changes take effect.  
- **Improved communication:** Mandated outreach and a required **FAQ** may help agencies and customers understand the upcoming changes before implementation.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)

- **Administrative workload:** The Division of Revenue must conduct outreach and produce/post an FAQ before **February 2, 2026** (additional tasks required by the act).
