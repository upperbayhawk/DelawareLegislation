## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 16, Chapter 69 of the Delaware Code to regulate the sale and advertising of consumer fireworks through permits/licensing, require specific consumer warning notices, increase penalties for violations, and dedicate resulting revenues to enforcement and public education by the Office of the State Fire Marshal.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a **retail permit requirement** to sell fireworks/related products allowed under **16 Del. C. § 6901**, with **a $100 permit fee per retail location per 15-day sales period**.  
- Requires sellers to apply for a retail sales permit **90 days prior** to each applicable **15-day sales period** tied to **July 4, the third day of Diwali, December 31, and January 1**.  
- Imposes **inspection fees for temporary retail structures** (e.g., tents/stands): **$25** to inspect the structure **plus** **$25** to inspect fireworks inventory (separate fees).  
- Creates a **wholesale license** for anyone shipping/advertising/offering/supplying fireworks to a Delaware retailer: **$100 application fee**, **valid for 1 year**, **renewed annually with fee**.  
- Adds a new penalty for selling without the required retail permit: **$1,000 (first violation), $5,000 (second), $10,000 (third and subsequent)**.  
- Increases general chapter penalties (where not otherwise provided) and adds an escalating consequence: after **3 or more violations**, a person is **prohibited for 1 year** from activities otherwise authorized under the chapter; permits/licenses become **immediately void and revoked** upon **third or subsequent conviction**, with a **1-year bar on reapplying**.  
- Regulates fireworks advertising in Delaware by requiring specific warnings; violations carry **$1,000 civil fine per violation**, with **each printed copy** and **each exposure/view/hearing** in Delaware treated as a separate violation.  
- Directs **all permit fees, license fees, and civil penalties** into a **special fund** for the State Fire Marshal to use for **enforcement and public education**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved oversight of fireworks sales**: retail permits (per location and per sales period) and wholesale licensing create a registration/approval mechanism for the Office of the State Fire Marshal.  
- **Stronger deterrence for unlawful sales**: specific penalties for failing to obtain a permit are set at **$1,000 / $5,000 / $10,000** depending on repeat violations.  
- **Enhanced consumer awareness and safety messaging**: requires (1) advertising warnings that fireworks use is generally illegal without a permit and products may be illegal, and (2) a **written notice at point of sale** warning of illegality and risk of death/serious injury.  
- **Dedicated enforcement/public education funding**: revenues are placed in a **special fund** for State Fire Marshal enforcement and public education, rather than the general fund.  
- **Clearer standards reference**: updates sales/use/storage regulation language to require compliance with **rules and regulations promulgated by the State Fire Prevention Commission** (replacing prior NFPA1124 reference in § 6901(d)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **New/added compliance costs for businesses**: retail sellers must pay **$100 per permit application per location per 15-day sales period**, plus (for temporary structures) **$25 + $25 inspection fees**. Wholesale suppliers must pay **$100 annually**.  
- **Administrative burden and timing constraints**: sellers must apply **90 days prior** to each **15-day sales period**, and must apply separately for each location and each sales period.  
- **High exposure to advertising penalties**: advertising violations are **$1,000 per violation**, and the bill treats **each printed copy** and **each instance of exposure** within Delaware as a separate violation, potentially multiplying liability.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The bill references “**15-day sales period prior to and including July 4, the third day of Diwali, December 31, and January 1**,” but does not specify the exact calendar start/end dates for each 15-day period (e.g., how the 15 days are counted for each holiday).  
- The required point-of-sale written notice may include “**such other information as the State Fire Prevention Commission may require by regulation**,” but the bill text does not specify what additional information might be required or when regulations would be issued.  
- The advertising warning states “**use of fireworks is generally illegal without a permit in Delaware**,” but the bill text does not define the phrase “generally illegal” or how it should be phrased beyond the listed warning elements.  
- The bill creates fees and a special fund, but does not specify **appropriation mechanics** or whether additional staffing/resources will be added for inspections.

## Key Policy Changes
- **§ 6901(d)**: Changes compliance language for listed consumer fireworks items—sale/use/storage/possession must comply with **State Fire Prevention Commission rules/regulations** (and retains the age limit **18+** and use limited to **July 4, third day of Diwali, December 31, January 1**; sale limited to a **15-day period** prior to and including those days).  
- **§ 6903**:  
  - Keeps public display permit framework (including **30-day prior application** and **$1,000,000 minimum liability insurance per event**).  
  - Adds **retail permit for sale** requirements: apply **90 days prior**, **separate application per retail location**, **separate application per 15-day sales period**, **$100 permit fee**, and (temporary structures) **$25 structure inspection + $25 inventory inspection**.  
  - Adds escalating penalties for selling without a required permit: **$1,000 / $5,000 / $10,000**.  
- **§ 6905**:  
  - Revises general penalties (where not otherwise provided): **$25–$250 (first)**, **$250–$500 (second)**, **up to $1,000 (subsequent)**.  
  - Adds: after **3+ violations**, a **1-year prohibition** from otherwise-authorized activities; **revocation** of any permit/license upon **third/subsequent conviction**; **1-year reapplication bar**.  
- **§ 6907** (new/updated):  
  - Requires specific **warnings in advertising**; **$1,000 civil fine per violation**, with each printed copy and each exposure treated separately.  
  - Requires **point-of-sale written notice** on **letter-sized paper in large, bold font** with specified warnings.  
- **§ 6908** (new): Establishes **wholesale license** requirement to supply Delaware retailers; **$100 fee**; license valid **1 year**; **annual renewal**.  
- **§ 6909** (new): Creates **special fund** for State Fire Marshal from **permit fees, license fees, and civil penalties** for **enforcement and public education**.

## Who Benefits

- **Office of the State Fire Marshal**: gains dedicated funding and a permit/license system to support enforcement and education.  
- **Local fire companies** (listed as affected entities in fiscal note): may benefit from improved compliance/education and reduced fireworks-related incidents.

- **Wholesale fireworks suppliers**: must obtain and renew an annual wholesale license (**$100 per year**).  
- **Advertisers/retailers/manufacturers** marketing fireworks in Delaware: subject to required warnings and potentially significant cumulative civil fines (**$1,000 per violation**, with each copy/exposure counted separately).  
- **Repeat violators**: subject to activity prohibition and permit/license revocation after **3+ convictions**, with **1-year** ineligibility to reapply.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Permit required to sell** fireworks/related products under § 6901; separate applications required **per retail location** and **per 15-day sales period**; **$100 fee** per application (§ 6903(b)(2)-(4)).  
- **Inspections for temporary structures**: **$25** for structure inspection **plus** **$25** for inventory inspection (§ 6903(b)(5)).  
- **Penalty for selling without permit**: **$1,000 first**, **$5,000 second**, **$10,000 third and subsequent** (§ 6903(c)).  
- **Advertising compliance**: ads must include required warnings; noncompliance is a **$1,000 civil fine per violation**; **each printed copy** and **each exposure** within Delaware counts as a separate violation (§ 6907(a)-(b)).  
- **Point-of-sale notice required**: purchaser must be given a written notice on **letter-sized paper in large, bold font** with specified warnings (§ 6907(c)).  
- **Wholesale license required** to ship/advertise/offer/supply fireworks to Delaware retailers; **$100 application fee**; **annual renewal** (§ 6908).  
- **General penalties (if not otherwise provided)**: **$25–$250 first**, **$250–$500 second**, **up to $1,000 subsequent** (§ 6905(a)).  
- **Repeat-violation consequences**: after **3+ violations**, **1-year prohibition**; permit/license **immediately void and revoked** upon **third/subsequent conviction**; **1-year** waiting period to reapply (§ 6905(b)).  
- **Jurisdiction**: **Justices of the peace** have jurisdiction over violations of the chapter (§ 6905(c)).  
- **Revenue handling**: all permit/license/civil penalty money placed into a special fund for State Fire Marshal **enforcement and public education** (§ 6909).
