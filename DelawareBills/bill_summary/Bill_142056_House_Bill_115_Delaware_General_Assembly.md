## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Delaware law to prohibit discrimination based on **weight, height, or body size** across multiple contexts (including transportation network companies, public accommodations, housing, employment, jury service, education-related bullying policies, public administration, and public works contracting) and adds/updates related statutory definitions.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds **weight, height, and body size** as protected characteristics in multiple Delaware nondiscrimination laws, including **public accommodations**, **housing**, and **employment**.
- Requires **Transportation Network Companies (TNCs)** to adopt and communicate a nondiscrimination policy that includes **weight, height, and body size**, and requires TNC drivers to comply with applicable nondiscrimination laws including these traits.
- Expands **housing** protections to cover discrimination based on weight/height/body size in: sales/rentals (**Title 6 § 4603**), real-estate related transactions (**§ 4604**), and access to brokerage/MLS services (**§ 4605**); also addresses discriminatory housing advertising and misrepresentation of availability.
- Extends **criminal housing-related intimidation/interference** provisions to acts motivated by weight/height/body size, with specified penalty tiers up to **life imprisonment** if death results (**Title 6 § 4619**).
- Adds weight/height/body size protections in additional areas including **jury service** (no exclusion “on account of” these traits), **school bullying policy investigations** (must consider whether targeting was “wholly or in part” due to these traits), **state classified service**, and **public works contract EEO clauses**.
- Adds or updates broad definitions of **“body size,” “height,” and “weight”** across amended chapters, including **measurements, ratios, component measurements, and “impressions”** (perceived tall/short or fat/thin) even without numerical measures.
- Also expands **race** to include traits historically associated with race, including **hair texture and a protective hairstyle**, and defines **protective hairstyle** as including **braids, locks, and twists**.
- Fiscal note anticipates increased enforcement workload requiring **2.0 total new FTE** (Department of State and Department of Justice) and provides multi-year cost estimates (see Budget Impact).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- Creates explicit statutory protections against discrimination based on **weight, height, or body size** in covered areas (e.g., housing, employment, public accommodations, TNC services, jury service, public works contracting).
- Requires **TNC nondiscrimination policy** adoption/driver notice, supporting clearer compliance expectations in that sector.
- Broad definitions (including “impressions” and component measurements) may improve coverage and consistency in complaint handling across agencies and settings.
- Adds protections against **housing-related intimidation/interference** motivated by weight/height/body size, backed by explicit criminal penalties.
- Clarifies **protective hairstyle** and expands **race** definition to include hair texture/protective hairstyle, strengthening coverage for those traits.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Transportation Network Companies (Title 2, Ch. 19):** TNCs must adopt/communicate a nondiscrimination policy including **weight, height, body size**; drivers must comply with applicable nondiscrimination laws including these traits.
- **Public accommodations (Title 6, Ch. 45):** Adds weight/height/body size to protected coverage; prohibits denial of services and discriminatory communications/advertising based on these traits.
- **Housing—sales/rentals and related markets (Title 6, Ch. 46):**
  - Adds weight/height/body size to prohibited bases for refusing to sell/rent/negotiate; discrimination in terms/conditions/services; discriminatory notices/ads; misrepresentation of availability; and inducements (“blockbusting”-type) (Title 6 **§ 4603(b)(1)-(5)**).
  - Adds weight/height/body size protections in **residential real estate-related transactions** (Title 6 **§ 4604(a)**) and **brokerage/MLS access** (Title 6 **§ 4605**).
  - Clarifies **occupancy limits** remain permissible if applied to all occupants and do not discriminate or have discriminatory effect, including on weight/height/body size (Title 6 **§ 4607(c)**), with an unclear **Dec. 31, 2028** transition note.
  - Extends **intimidation/interference** protections to acts motivated by weight/height/body size (Title 6 **§ 4619**).
- **Jury service (Title 10, § 4502):** Adds weight/height/body size as prohibited bases for exclusion from jury service.
- **Employment discrimination (Title 19, Ch. 7, Subch. II):** Adds weight/height/body size to unlawful employment practices for employers, employment agencies, and labor organizations; extends reasonable-accommodation provisions to known limitations related to **pregnancy, weight, height, or body size** subject to existing “undue hardship” framework; includes apprenticeship/training and anti-retaliation provisions.
- **Fair housing rentals (Title 25, Ch. 51):** Prohibits refusing to rent/cancelling rental agreements or charging higher rent because of weight/height/body size; retains senior-only rental unit allowance; includes unclear **Dec. 31, 2028** transition note for § 5116.
- **State classified service (Title 29 § 5953):** Adds weight/height/body size to prohibited bases for being favored/discriminated against in classified service employment (also references housing status).
- **Public works contracts (Title 29 § 6962):** Expands required equal employment opportunity and posting/advertising clauses to include weight/height/body size; requires contractors to take specified nondiscrimination “positive steps.”
- **County employment (Title 9 § 1183):** Adds weight/height/body size to prohibited bases for favor/discrimination in county positions.
- **Recordation of discriminatory restrictions (Title 9 § 9605):** Bars recorders from knowingly recording/filing real property instruments containing discriminatory restrictions including weight/height/body size.
- **Race/protective hairstyle:** Defines **protective hairstyle** (includes **braids, locks, twists**) and expands **race** to include traits historically associated with race, including **hair texture and a protective hairstyle**.

## Who Benefits

- Individuals protected from **housing-related intimidation/interference** motivated by weight/height/body size.
- Individuals whose race-related discrimination involves **hair texture** or **protective hairstyles** (braids, locks, twists).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **TNCs:** Must adopt and notify drivers of a nondiscrimination policy including **weight, height, body size**; drivers must comply with applicable nondiscrimination laws including these traits.
- **Schools (bullying policies):** Must include procedures to investigate and determine whether a target was selected “wholly or in part” due to **weight, height, or body size** (as part of required policy elements).
- **Public works contractors (Title 29 § 6962):** Must not discriminate on listed bases including weight/height/body size; must take specified “positive steps” across employment actions; must **post** nondiscrimination notices; and must include nondiscrimination language in solicitations/advertising.
- **Recorders (Title 9 § 9605):** May not **knowingly** record/receive for filing instruments containing discriminatory restrictions including weight/height/body size (no penalty specified in the notes).
- **Criminal penalties—housing intimidation/interference (Title 6 § 4619):**
  - Fine **≤ $2,500** or imprisonment **≤ 1 year**, or both.
  - If **bodily injury results:** fine **≤ $10,000** or imprisonment **≤ 10 years**, or both.
  - If **death results:** imprisonment for **any term of years or for life**.
- Enforcement staffing reflected in fiscal note: investigations by Department of State (Division of Human and Civil Rights) and prosecution by Department of Justice; other remedies/procedures are **not specified in the provided notes**.
