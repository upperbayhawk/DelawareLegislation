## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 21, Chapter 21, § 2148 of the Delaware Code to prevent the Department of Transportation from refusing vehicle registration or renewal based only on on-board diagnostics trouble codes (DTCs) that indicate a **non-emission-related** issue.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits the Department from refusing to **register or renew registration** if inspection shows **DTC codes indicating a non-emission-related problem**.  
- Targets **on-board diagnostics trouble codes (DTC)** as a disallowed sole basis for denial when the issue is **not emissions-related**.  
- Applies at the point of **vehicle inspection** connected to registration/renewal decisions.  
- Does **not** change how emissions-related DTCs are handled (no change stated).  
- Creates a clear limitation on Department discretion: denial cannot be **solely** because of non-emission DTCs.  
- No funding, fees, or penalty changes are stated in the bill text.  

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Prevents registration/renewal denials** based solely on **non-emission** diagnostic codes, reducing barriers for vehicle owners when the issue is unrelated to emissions (Section 1; § 2148).  
- Provides clearer, more consistent standards for registration decisions by specifying what **cannot** be used as the only reason for refusal (Section 1; § 2148).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Limits the Department’s ability to use **non-emission** diagnostic information as a basis to refuse registration/renewal, even if those codes might reflect other vehicle issues (Section 1; § 2148).  
- The bill does not specify alternative safety-related criteria or how other non-emission problems should be addressed within the inspection/registration process (no additional text beyond § 2148 change).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Amends 21 Del. C. § 2148** to state: the Department **may not refuse** to register or renew registration of a vehicle **if**, upon inspection, the vehicle’s **DTC codes indicate** the vehicle may have a **non-emission-related** problem.  
- Establishes that **non-emission-related DTCs alone** cannot be the reason for a registration or renewal refusal.

## Who Benefits
- Vehicle owners whose vehicles display **non-emission-related** on-board diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs) during inspection but who seek **registration or renewal**.  
- Individuals needing continued legal operation/registration where the only flagged issue is a **non-emission** DTC code.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- The Department of Transportation, to the extent it previously used **non-emission** DTCs as a basis to refuse registration/renewal (Section 1; § 2148).  
- Parties who prefer broader Department discretion to deny registration based on diagnostic indicators beyond emissions (implied by the limitation in § 2148; no further detail provided).
