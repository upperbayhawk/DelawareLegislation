## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Redirect and expand certain energy-related funding streams to (1) supplement Delaware’s existing Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP) and Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP), and (2) create a new Delaware Energy Fund to provide energy-cost assistance to households with income **below 350% of the federal poverty level**, while requiring participating households to engage in energy efficiency programs.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Reallocates **CO₂ allowance (RGGI) auction proceeds** so that **65%** goes to the **Sustainable Energy Utility (SEU)** for specified energy purposes, including potential **allocations to the Delaware Energy Fund**.  
- Directs **15%** of CO₂ allowance proceeds to low-income consumers: **10% to WAP** and **up to 5% to LIHEAP**; proceeds **may be used for financial assistance for energy costs for qualifying residents**.  
- Adds a requirement: LIHEAP participants funded under this section **must also participate in WAP within 2 years** of receiving LIHEAP assistance, **subject to funding availability**.  
- Amends the **Green Energy Fund** provisions to require **DNREC** to work with **DHSS** to coordinate a transfer of certain compliance payments to address LIHEAP deficits for the **sole benefit of Delmarva Power electric customers** (when funding is deficit).  
- Establishes the **Delaware Energy Fund**, administered by the **SEU**, to provide energy-cost assistance to households with income **below 350% of the federal poverty level**.  
- Requires Delaware Energy Fund recipients to participate in **energy savings and efficiency programs offered by the SEU**.  
- Requires the SEU to submit an **annual report** on the Delaware Energy Fund’s receipt and distribution of moneys to the General Assembly and specified offices.  
- The Act takes effect **upon enactment**, the SEU must begin operation of the Delaware Energy Fund **within 30 days of enactment**, and the Act **expires 3 years after enactment** unless extended.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Directs substantial funding to energy efficiency and assistance:** Explicitly allocates **65%** of CO₂ allowance proceeds to the SEU for conservation/efficiency/renewables/financing and permits **allocations to the Delaware Energy Fund**.  
- **Expands financial assistance eligibility beyond LIHEAP:** Creates the Delaware Energy Fund for households under **350% of the federal poverty level**, which is broader than LIHEAP eligibility (bill does not state LIHEAP threshold, but does establish the Delaware Energy Fund threshold).  
- **Links bill assistance to energy-saving measures:** Requires Delaware Energy Fund recipients to participate in SEU energy savings/efficiency programs, and requires certain LIHEAP recipients to participate in WAP within **2 years** (subject to funding).  
- **Improves accountability:** Requires **annual reporting** by DHSS on Green Energy Fund use for Delmarva Power customers and annual SEU reporting on Delaware Energy Fund receipts/distributions.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **New participation mandates may create barriers to access:** LIHEAP recipients funded under this section must participate in WAP within **2 years** (subject to funding availability), and Delaware Energy Fund recipients **must** participate in SEU efficiency programs.  
- **Program is temporary unless extended:** The entire Act **expires 3 years after enactment**, potentially limiting long-term planning for recipients and administrators.  
- **Delmarva Power-only limitation for certain transferred funds:** Green Energy Fund transfers described in §8057(a)(1) are for the **sole benefit of Delmarva Power electric customers** when LIHEAP funding is deficit, which may exclude similarly situated customers of other utilities.

## Key Policy Changes
- **CO₂ allowance proceeds allocation (Title 7, §6046(c)):**
  - **65%** directed to the **SEU** for conservation, efficiency, renewables, energy financing, and **allocations to the Delaware Energy Fund**.
  - **15%** directed to low-income consumers: **10%** to **WAP** and **up to 5%** to **LIHEAP**; funds may be used for **financial assistance for energy costs** for qualifying residents.
  - Adds requirement: LIHEAP participants funded here must also participate in **WAP within 2 years**, **subject to funding availability**.
- **Green Energy Fund (Title 29, §8057):**
  - **DNREC + DHSS** must coordinate transfer of alternative compliance payments/solar alternative compliance payments to supplement LIHEAP deficits for the **sole benefit of Delmarva Power electric customers**.
  - **DHSS annual report** to DNREC, Division of the Public Advocate, and Public Service Commission on use for Delmarva customers.
- **Creates Delaware Energy Fund (Title 29, §8059A):**
  - Administered by **SEU**.
  - Provides financial assistance for energy costs to households **below 350% FPL**.
  - Requires redirecting applicants who qualify for **LIHEAP** to **DHSS Division of State Service Centers**.
  - Requires recipients to participate in SEU energy savings/efficiency programs.
  - Requires **annual SEU reporting** to specified legislative and executive recipients.
  - Authorizes SEU to promulgate **rules and regulations**.
