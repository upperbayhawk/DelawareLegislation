## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To strengthen and clarify enforcement tools and penalties in Delaware’s massage and bodywork laws (Title 24, Chapter 53) by (1) requiring an official placard be posted at **any entrance** of an unlicensed or noncompliant massage establishment, (2) prohibiting placard removal without approval from the **Division of Professional Regulation**, and (3) making **unlawful placard removal** a **Class A misdemeanor** with specified maximum jail and fine penalties.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires an Attorney General-provided placard to be prominently displayed at **any entrance** of a massage/bodywork establishment that **has not obtained a valid license** or whose license is **suspended, revoked, or expired**.  
- Specifies that the placard **may not be removed** unless removal is **approved by the Division of Professional Regulation**.  
- Creates/clarifies a criminal penalty: **unlawful removal of the placard** is a **Class A misdemeanor**, punishable by **up to 1 year in jail** and **up to a $2,300 fine**, or both.  
- Clarifies fines for unlicensed practice/false representation as a practitioner: **$100–$500** for a **first offense**; **$500–$1,000** for a **second or subsequent conviction** (per offense).  
- Treats refusal to **immediately** open a locked door during an inspection as “unlawfully operating or managing” a massage establishment under **§ 5317(c)** (Class A misdemeanor framework).  
- Confirms **Superior Court jurisdiction** over violations under the penalties section.  
- Includes technical drafting corrections to conform with the Legislative Drafting Manual.  
- Law was **approved September 26, 2025**.

- **Placard removal restriction**: Placard **may not be removed** unless removal is **approved by the Division of Professional Regulation** (§ 5320(a)).  
- **New/clarified criminal penalty for placard removal**: Unlawful placard removal is a **Class A misdemeanor** punishable by **up to 1 year** imprisonment and **up to $2,300** fine, or both (§ 5317(d)).  
- **Inspection access enforcement linkage**: Refusal to **immediately** open locked doors upon an inspector’s request is treated as **unlawfully operating or managing** under **§ 5317(c)** (Class A misdemeanor framework) (§ 5319(f)(2)).  
- **Clarified fine schedule** for unlicensed practice/false representation: **$100–$500** first offense; **$500–$1,000** second or subsequent conviction (per offense) (§ 5317(b)).
