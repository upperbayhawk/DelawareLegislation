## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 15 of the Delaware Code to allow voters in **primary and general elections** to vote at **any polling place within their county of registration**, and to authorize the State Election Commissioner to **consolidate polling places into centrally located voting centers** subject to specified limits.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Allows a voter in a **primary election** to vote at **any polling place for any election district in the county** where the voter is registered. (15 Del. C. § 3128, as amended)
- Allows a voter in an **election (general election)** to vote at **any polling place for any election district in the county** where the voter is registered. (15 Del. C. § 4517, as amended)
- Expands the Commissioner’s authority to **consolidate polling places** into centrally located voting centers, **as long as**:
  - **each election district retains at least one polling place**, and
  - consolidation **does not result in an undue burden** on voters’ access. (15 Del. C. § 302(18), as amended)
- The bill’s stated goal is to **increase access to voting** by allowing voters to choose the most convenient polling place in their county. (Synopsis)
- Fiscal note anticipates **increased post-election reconciliation/audit workload** for the Board of Canvass due to cross-referencing voters who may vote outside their assigned election district.
- Estimated one-time cost: **$160,000 in Fiscal Year 2027** to audit/certify the **November 2026 general election** results; **no costs projected in FY 2026 or FY 2028**. (Fiscal note)
- Assumes the Act is **effective upon signature by the Governor**. (Fiscal note assumption)

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved voter convenience and access:** Voters may use the polling location in their county that is “most convenient to them,” rather than being limited to a designated election-district site. (Synopsis; §§ 3128, 4517)
- **Administrative flexibility to create voting centers:** Explicit authority for the Commissioner to consolidate into centrally located centers, potentially improving service delivery while maintaining guardrails (at least one polling place per district; no undue burden). (§ 302(18))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increased election audit/reconciliation burden and cost:** The fiscal note states the Superior Court expects “significantly increase[d]” reconciliation efforts to review/audit machine tapes and cross-reference registration data across districts when voters can vote outside their designated election district. (Fiscal note assumptions 3–4)
- **One-time added expenditure projected:** Approximately **$160,000** in **FY 2027** for Board of Canvass duties related to auditing/certifying general election results. (Fiscal note)
- **Potential access concerns tied to consolidation standard:** Consolidation is allowed provided it does not create an “undue burden,” but the bill text does not define that term (could affect implementation and disputes). (§ 302(18))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Definition/standard for “undue burden”:** The bill prohibits consolidation that results in an “undue burden on voters’ access,” but does not define how that is measured or enforced. (§ 302(18))
- **Scope of “In an election” in § 4517:** The section heading is “Voting location” and the synopsis refers to “general elections,” but the amended text says “In an election,” without further limitation in the excerpt provided. (§ 4517)

## Key Policy Changes
- **Primary elections:** Amend **15 Del. C. § 3128** to allow a voter to vote at **any polling place for any election district in the county** in which the voter is registered.
- **General elections:** Amend **15 Del. C. § 4517** to allow a voter to vote at **any polling place for any election district in the county** in which the voter is registered.
