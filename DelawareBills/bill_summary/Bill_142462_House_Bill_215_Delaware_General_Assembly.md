## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 30 of the Delaware Code to (1) expand/clarify what counts as “tobacco products” for tax purposes, (2) increase excise tax rates on cigarettes and other tobacco products (including vapor products), and (3) increase annual tobacco-related licensing fees.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Redefines “tobacco products”** to include products “containing, made of, or derived from tobacco or nicotine,” specifically listing **nicotine pouches**; excludes **marijuana-containing products** and **FDA-regulated drugs/therapeutic devices**.  
- **Raises cigarette tax** from **105 mills to 180 mills per cigarette** (synopsis: from **$2.10 to $3.60 per 20-pack**).  
- **Raises “other tobacco products” tax** (excluding cigarettes, moist snuff, and vapor products) from **30% to 45% of wholesale price**.  
- **Raises moist snuff tax** from **$0.92/oz to $1.23/oz**, computed on manufacturer-listed net weight.  
- **Raises vapor products tax** from **$0.05 to $0.25 per fluid milliliter**, computed on manufacturer-listed milliliters; invoices must state milliliters.  
- Imposes a **floor/stock tax** on certain stamped inventory and unused stamps held as of **midnight Aug. 31, 2025**; **payment due Dec. 31, 2025**.  
- **Increases license fees effective Jan. 1, 2026**: wholesale **$200→$400**, retail **$50→$100**, vending machine **$15→$30**, replacement license **$10→$20**, affixing agent **$200→$400** (with single fee if both wholesale dealer and affixing agent).  
- Fiscal note: estimated **additional General Fund revenue** of **$80,115 (FY2027)**, **$44,365 (FY2028)**, **$40,365 (FY2029)**.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Definition change (30 Del. C. §5301(15))**:  
  - “Tobacco products” includes products primarily containing/made of/derived from **tobacco or nicotine** for individual consumption, including **cigarettes, cigars, pipe tobacco, vapor products, and nicotine pouches**.  
  - Excludes: (1) **marijuana-containing** products/substances (even if also containing nicotine); (2) products regulated by the **U.S. FDA** as a **drug or therapeutic device** under Chapter V of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.
- **Tax rate increases (30 Del. C. §5305)**:  
  - Cigarettes: **105 mills → 180 mills per cigarette** (synopsis: **$2.10 → $3.60 per 20-pack**).  
  - Other tobacco products (excluding those taxed under §5305(a) or (c)): **30% → 45% of wholesale price**.  
  - Moist snuff: **$0.92/oz → $1.23/oz**.  
  - Vapor products: **$0.05/ml → $0.25/ml**; invoices must state milliliters.
- **License fee increases (30 Del. C. §5308; §5312)**:  
  - Wholesale license: **$200 → $400** per place of business.  
  - Retail license: **$50 → $100** per place of business.  
  - Vending machine identification stamp/license: **$15 → $30** per machine mechanism (including sets fastened together).  
  - Affixing agent license: **$200 → $400**; only one fee if both wholesale dealer and affixing agent.  
  - Replacement license: **$10 → $20**.

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Floor tax compliance**: tax must be imposed on (1) **stocks/inventories** in possession of any person liable for the tax as of **midnight Aug. 31, 2025** that have been affixed with a stamp/indicia of payment, and (2) **stamps purchased but not yet affixed** before that time; the amount due equals **new-rate tax minus previously paid tax** and must be paid by **Dec. 31, 2025**.  
- **Vapor product invoicing requirement**: invoices issued by a manufacturer **must state milliliters** of vapor product for purposes of computing the **per-milliliter** tax.  
- **Licensing compliance**: separate licenses required **per place of business** for wholesale/retail; vending machines must have the required identification stamp/license.
