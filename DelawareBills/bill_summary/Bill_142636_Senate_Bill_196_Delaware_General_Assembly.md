## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 16 (Chapter 11) to require fuller disclosure of long-term care facility ownership (including controlling persons and certain non-publicly traded ownership interests), require advance notice and disclosures to residents before a transfer of ownership, and authorize the Department to delay or deny licensure for new owners with a pattern of serious violations/enforcement actions.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Defines “Affiliate” and “Facility Owner,” and requires reporting of owners and **percentage ownership** for each person.  
- Requires facilities to disclose to the Department the **current Facility Owner**, including **controlling persons** of an entity or **affiliate** that is **not a publicly traded corporation**.  
- Requires the Department to publish current Facility Owner information (including controlling persons) for each long-term care facility on the Department’s website.  
- Requires facilities to **prominently and conspicuously** post Facility Owner information on the facility’s website and on **advertising, marketing, or promotional materials**.  
- Creates transfer-of-ownership disclosures: a person assuming ownership must disclose to the Department other facilities they (or affiliates) owned/operated/managed in the **preceding year**, and any **enforcement actions** in that period.  
- Requires at least **60 days before** a transfer of ownership that the new owner disclose specified information to **each current resident** and, if applicable, each resident’s **authorized representative**.  
- Authorizes the Department to **delay or deny licensure** for an incoming owner if there is a **pattern of serious violations or enforcement actions** raising compliance concerns.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved transparency for consumers/residents:** Residents and the public can identify the **current Facility Owner** and controlling persons, including certain non-publicly traded ownership interests (Sections amending §1108(e)–(g)).  
- **More informed resident decision-making before ownership changes:** Residents (and authorized representatives) must receive key ownership and enforcement-history information **at least 60 days** before transfer (new §1108A(b)).  
- **Stronger regulatory screening of ownership transfers:** The Department may **delay or deny licensure** when the incoming owner/affiliates show a **pattern of serious violations or enforcement actions** (new §1108A(c)).  
- **Public access via state website:** The Department must publish the disclosed current ownership information for each facility on its website (amended §1108(f)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Added compliance and administrative workload for facilities/new owners:** Required disclosures to the Department and required postings on websites and **all advertising/marketing/promotional materials** (amended §1108(e)–(g); new §1108A(a)–(b)).  
- **Potential delays in ownership transfers:** The Department is explicitly authorized to **delay** licensure based on the disclosed/verified history (new §1108A(c)), and residents must receive notice **60 days** before a transfer (new §1108A(b)).  
- **Disclosure obligations may be complex for multi-entity ownership structures:** The bill requires disclosure of **all controlling persons** of certain entities/affiliates and details about **other facilities** and **enforcement actions** in the preceding year (amended §1108(e); new §1108A(a)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Meaning of “controlling persons”:** The bill requires disclosure of “controlling persons” but does not define the term in the provided text.  
- **Scope of “pattern of serious violations or enforcement actions”:** The standard for what constitutes a “pattern” or what qualifies as “serious” is not further defined (new §1108A(c)).  
- **Department-required “manner and form”:** Multiple obligations depend on the Department’s determined “manner and form,” but the bill text does not specify what forms, formats, or verification processes will be required (amended §1108(e); new §1108A(a)).  
- **What qualifies as “advertising, marketing, or promotional materials”:** The bill requires ownership posting on such materials but does not define the universe of materials covered (amended §1108(g)).  
- **Timing beyond the 60-day resident notice:** The bill specifies resident disclosure at least **60 days before transfer**, but does not specify when the Department disclosure must be made relative to the transfer beyond what the Department may require (new §1108A(a)–(b)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **New/expanded definitions (§1102):**
  - Adds **“Affiliate”** (cross-referencing the definition of “person” in **§ 302 of Title 1**) and defining control relationships.
  - Adds **“Facility Owner”** and requires inclusion of:
    - **All owners** and the **percentage ownership** of each person.
    - **All owners and controlling persons** of an entity or non-publicly traded corporation that owns the facility.
- **Ownership disclosure and publication (§1108):**
  - Facility must disclose current Facility Owner to the Department, including **controlling persons** of an entity or affiliate that is **not a publicly traded corporation** (§1108(e)).
  - Department must publish disclosed current Facility Owner information (including controlling persons) for each facility on its website (§1108(f)).
  - Facility must post current Facility Owner information prominently on:
    - the facility’s **website**, and
    - **advertising, marketing, or promotional materials**, including controlling persons of any **non-publicly traded entity** with an ownership stake (§1108(g)).
- **Transfer-of-ownership disclosures (§1108A):**
  - Incoming owner must disclose to the Department (in the Department’s required manner/form) information about:
    - other licensed long-term care facilities owned/operated/managed by the person or affiliates in the **preceding year** in any U.S. state/territory or D.C. (§1108A(a)(1));
    - **enforcement actions** imposed in the **preceding year** against any facility owned/operated/managed by the person or affiliates (§1108A(a)(2)).
  - At least **60 days before** transfer, incoming owner must disclose to **each current resident** and, if applicable, each resident’s **authorized representative**:
    - name of the new facility owner (§1108A(b)(1));
    - other facilities owned/operated/managed by the person or affiliates in the **preceding year** (§1108A(b)(2));
    - **enforcement actions** in the **preceding year** (§1108A(b)(3)).
  - Department may **delay or deny licensure** based on disclosed/verified information showing a **pattern of serious violations or enforcement actions** raising compliance concerns (§1108A(c)).

## Who Benefits
- **Current residents and their authorized representatives:** Receive mandatory pre-transfer disclosures at least **60 days** before ownership changes (new §1108A(b)).  
- **Prospective residents and the public:** Access ownership and controlling-person information via facility websites/materials and the Department’s website (amended §1108(f)–(g)).  
- **Regulators (the Department):** Gains explicit authority to consider disclosed/verified enforcement history and delay/deny licensure for concerning ownership patterns (new §1108A(c)).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- **Facility owners and incoming purchasers/operators (and affiliates):** Must compile and disclose detailed ownership and enforcement-history information and ensure postings across websites and marketing materials (amended §1108(e)–(g); new §1108A(a)–(b)).  
- **Entities with complex ownership structures (especially non-publicly traded):** Increased exposure of controlling persons and ownership details (amended §1108(e), §1108(g); definition of “Facility Owner” in §1102).
