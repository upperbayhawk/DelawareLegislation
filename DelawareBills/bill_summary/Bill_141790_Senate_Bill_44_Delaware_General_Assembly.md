## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29, § 8781 of the Delaware Code to revise governance of the Diamond State Port Corporation (DSPC), primarily by (1) clarifying board size, and (2) changing how the Board Chair is selected and confirmed, with a short transition rule for the current Chair.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- **Board size is clarified as 14 members (not 15)** to reflect the prior removal of the Director of the Division of Small Business from the board.  
- **Cabinet/ex officio directors are reduced from 8 to 7** consistent with that prior removal.  
- **Chair selection shifts from Governor appointment to Board election**: the **Board elects the Chair by majority vote** from among the **cabinet board directors**.  
- **Chair serves at the pleasure of the Board** (replacing “pleasure of the Governor”).  
- **Senate confirmation is narrowed**: the Chair is **subject to Senate advice and consent only for the additional Chair duties**, and it **does not affect prior confirmation** of the Cabinet Secretary.  
- **Private/public/organized labor directors remain 7**, appointed by the Governor with Senate advice and consent, with existing geographic and political-party balance requirements unchanged.  
- **Transition rule**: the person serving as Chair on the Act’s enactment date remains Chair **until the conclusion of the second Board meeting after enactment**.  
- Bill notes it **requires a two-thirds vote** in each chamber because it amends a special act of incorporation (Delaware Constitution, Article IX, § 1).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clearer statutory board composition**: explicitly sets total membership at **14**, correcting a mismatch created when a position was previously removed.  
- **More board-driven leadership selection**: Chair is elected by the **Board (majority vote)** rather than appointed by the Governor, which may align leadership choice more directly with board governance.  
- **Continuity of leadership**: transition language keeps the existing Chair in place **through the second meeting after enactment**, reducing risk of an immediate vacancy.  
- **Reduced confirmation scope**: Senate confirmation is expressly limited to the **additional Chair duties**, potentially simplifying confirmation issues for already-confirmed Cabinet officers.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced gubernatorial control over Chair selection**: the Governor no longer appoints the Chair; the **Board elects** the Chair instead.  
- **Potential governance uncertainty during transition**: Chair remains in place only until **the conclusion of the second meeting** after enactment; the bill does not state what happens if the Board does not elect a Chair by then (see ambiguities).  
- **Confirmation process still applies (in some form)**: Chair remains “subject to the advice and consent of the Senate” for Chair duties, which may still require administrative coordination.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Effective date is not stated**: the bill references “the enactment date,” but provides no explicit calendar date.  
- **What if the Board does not elect a Chair by the end of the second meeting after enactment?** The bill sets the transition endpoint but does not specify a contingency for failure to elect.  
- **Meaning of “cabinet board directors” count**: the text references election “from among the **5 cabinet board directors**,” while also listing **7** ex officio directors; the relationship between “cabinet” directors and the full set of ex officio directors is not fully clarified in the provided text.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Amends 29 Del. C. § 8781(b)(1)** to confirm DSPC board consists of **14 members** (all Delaware residents).  
- **Updates ex officio membership count**: “Eight” becomes **“Seven”** and retains listed officials (Secretary of State; Secretary of Transportation; Secretary of Finance; Controller General; Co-Chairs of the Joint Legislative Committee on the Capital Improvement Program or designee(s); Secretary of the Department of Safety and Homeland Security).  
- **Changes Chair selection method (29 Del. C. § 8781(b)(2))**:  
  - From: Governor appoints Chair, with Senate advice and consent.  
  - To: **Board elects Chair by majority vote**, from among the **cabinet board directors**; **Chair serves at the pleasure of the Board**.  
  - **Senate advice and consent applies only to the additional Chair duties**, not to the person’s prior Cabinet confirmation.  
- **Retains structure for remaining 7 directors** (29 Del. C. § 8781(b)(3)): appointed by Governor with Senate advice/consent; must include representation across **all 3 counties**, **City of Wilmington**, **3 at-large**; **no more than 4** from the same major political party; **3-year terms** for noncabinet directors.  
- **Transition provision (Section 2)**: current Chair on enactment remains Chair **until the conclusion of the second Board meeting following enactment**.

## Who Benefits
