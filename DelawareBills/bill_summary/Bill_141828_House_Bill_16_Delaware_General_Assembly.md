## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 29 of the Delaware Code to add an annually appointed **high school student** as a **nonvoting** member of the Delaware Artificial Intelligence (AI) Commission.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires the AI Commission Chair to **appoint annually 1 nonvoting student member**.  
- The student must be (at appointment) **in 10th, 11th, or 12th grade**, or **will be in the next school year**, in a **Delaware public or private school**.  
- The Chair must give **priority** to a student who has demonstrated an **interest in and commitment to the study of AI**.  
- Leaves the Commission’s **23 voting members** structure in place; the student is **additional and nonvoting**.  
- Retains existing provisions that appointed voting members serve **up to 3-year terms** and may be **reappointed**.  
- Retains existing attendance rule: missing **3 successive meetings without just cause** may result in the appointed voting member’s seat being deemed vacant.  
- The AI Commission provisions referenced continue to have an **expiration date of July 17, 2034** (per existing law referenced in the bill text).  
- The Act was **approved May 13, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Adds a youth perspective** to the AI Commission through a required **annual** student appointment (new § 9043C(e)).  
- **Encourages student engagement in AI** by prioritizing students with demonstrated **interest and commitment** to AI study (new § 9043C(e)).  
- Does not alter existing voting membership or agency representation; the student role is **nonvoting**, reducing governance disruption (new § 9043C(e)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- The student member is **nonvoting**, so the role may have **limited formal influence** on Commission decisions (new § 9043C(e)).  
- Selection standard is **priority** (not a requirement) for AI interest/commitment, which may lead to **inconsistent selection criteria** (new § 9043C(e)).  
- No term length, duties, or attendance expectations are specified for the student, potentially creating **unclear participation expectations** (new § 9043C(e)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities

- **Attendance/removal**: the “3 successive meetings” vacancy provision applies to “appointed voting member,” not the student; no parallel rule is provided (existing § 9043C(c); new § 9043C(e)).
