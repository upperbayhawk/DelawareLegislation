## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To terminate Delaware’s participation in the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative (RGGI) by repealing the state law provisions that authorize the CO₂ emission trading program.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Repeals **Subchapter II-A of Chapter 60, Title 7** of the Delaware Code **in its entirety**, removing Delaware from **RGGI**.
- Bill asserts RGGI’s original goal was a **10% CO₂ reduction by 2019** from **Delaware electrical power plants over 25 megawatts**.
- Bill states Delaware has already exceeded that goal with a **45% reduction** in CO₂ emissions.
- Legislative findings cite a **Forbes study** comparing GDP growth and electricity prices: fastest-growing states averaged **18.6%** real GDP growth over **5 years** with **10.7 cents/kWh in 2024**.
- Findings state Delaware ranked **46th** in GDP growth, with **3%** real GDP growth over the same period and an average electricity price of **13.4 cents/kWh**.
- Findings state RGGI compliance costs are passed through to consumers, increasing electricity costs for households and businesses.
- The bill’s stated intent is to improve **energy affordability** and support **economic growth** by exiting RGGI.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **May reduce electricity cost pressure**: The bill states RGGI compliance costs are “ultimately passed down to consumers,” implying repealing the program could reduce that cost component.
- **Regulatory simplification**: Repealing Subchapter II-A removes the statutory basis for a CO₂ trading program, potentially reducing compliance and administrative burdens tied to participation.
- **Policy flexibility**: The synopsis states exiting RGGI will allow Delaware to pursue other policies supporting “economic growth and energy affordability.”

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Eliminates a cap-and-trade framework**: Repealing Subchapter II-A ends Delaware’s statutory participation in RGGI, removing a structured mechanism intended to reduce CO₂ emissions from covered power plants.
- **No replacement policy included**: The bill repeals the program but does not specify an alternative emissions-reduction or energy-cost strategy in the statutory text provided.
