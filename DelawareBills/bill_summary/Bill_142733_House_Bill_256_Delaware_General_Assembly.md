## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware law governing required safety hotline information printed on pupil (grades 7–12) and public higher-education student identification cards by (1) making certain hotline numbers mandatory for grades 7–12 ID cards, and (2) removing prior “application” school-year dates to avoid implying the requirements were temporary, setting a single effective date.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires public schools serving **grades 7–12** that issue pupil ID cards to print additional hotline information that was previously optional: **Teen Dating Violence Hotline** and **Stop Bullying Now Hotline**.  
- Keeps existing required printing for grades 7–12 pupil ID cards: **988**, **National Domestic Violence Hotline**, and **Crisis Text Line**.  
- For **public institutions of higher learning** that issue student ID cards, retains required printing: **988**, **National Domestic Violence Hotline**, **Crisis Text Line**, and **local campus police/security phone number**.  
- Allows (but does not require) printing the **National Sexual Assault Hotline (1-800-656-4673)** on both pupil and student ID cards.  
- Requires that if any listed numbers are updated, the **most updated numbers must be printed when the card is reissued or reprinted**.  
- Allows schools and public higher-ed institutions to **use existing noncompliant unissued card stock until supplies are depleted**.  
- Removes prior effective “application” language tied to the **2022–2023** and **2023–2024** school years and sets a new effective date of **July 1, 2026**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expanded access to safety resources for grades 7–12 students** by making Teen Dating Violence and Stop Bullying hotline information mandatory on ID cards (Synopsis; amended 14 Del. C. § 4144(a)).  
- **Clearer statutory permanence** by removing prior school-year “application” dates that could be interpreted as temporary (Synopsis; Sections 1–2).  
- **Improved accuracy over time** because updated hotline numbers must be used when cards are reissued or reprinted (14 Del. C. § 4144(c); § 9001B(c)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential administrative/design burden** on schools (grades 7–12) to modify ID card layouts to include additional required hotline numbers (14 Del. C. § 4144(a)).  
- **Possible inconsistency in near-term compliance visibility** because noncompliant unissued cards may still be issued until supplies are depleted (14 Del. C. § 4144(d); § 9001B(d)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- Whether “Stop Bullying Now Hotline: **1-800-273-8255 (TALK)**” is intended to remain as written, given **988** is also listed as the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline number in the same section (14 Del. C. § 4144(a)(1) and (a)(5)); the bill does not address any overlap or potential number changes beyond the general “updated numbers” clause.  
- The bill requires “most updated numbers” but does not specify **who determines** that a number has been updated or **how** schools/institutions must verify updates (14 Del. C. § 4144(c); § 9001B(c)).  
- “Until supply is depleted” is not defined (e.g., no time cap or documentation requirement) (14 Del. C. § 4144(d); § 9001B(d)).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Grades 7–12 pupil ID cards (14 Del. C. § 4144):**
  - Makes **Teen Dating Violence Hotline** and **Stop Bullying Now Hotline** mandatory to print on pupil ID cards (moved into the required list in subsection (a); Synopsis).  
  - Continues required printing of **988**, **National Domestic Violence Hotline**, and **Crisis Text Line**.  
  - Allows optional printing of **National Sexual Assault Hotline (1-800-656-4673)**.  
  - Requires updated numbers to be used **when the card is reissued or reprinted**.  
  - Permits issuance of existing unissued noncompliant cards until **supplies are depleted**.
- **Public higher-ed student ID cards (14 Del. C. § 9001B):**
  - Maintains required printing of **988**, **National Domestic Violence Hotline**, **Crisis Text Line**, and **local campus police/security phone number**.  
  - Allows optional printing of **National Sexual Assault Hotline (1-800-656-4673)**.  
  - Requires updated numbers to be used **when reissued or reprinted**.  
  - Permits issuance of existing unissued noncompliant cards until **supplies are depleted**.
- **Removes prior application dates** stating effectiveness “for the **2022–2023 school year**” and “for the **2023–2024 school year**,” and instead sets a single effective date (Sections 1–2; Synopsis).

## Who Benefits
