## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends the Public School Employment Relations Act (Title 14) and the Public Employment Relations Act (Title 19) to allow an employee organization to be certified as the exclusive bargaining representative through a “majority verification” (card-check style) process when **over 50%** of employees in an appropriate bargaining unit provide dated authorization signatures, in which case the Board **must certify** the organization and **may not order an election**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new **“majority verification procedure”** for public school employees (new **14 Del. C. § 4011A**) and other public employees (new **19 Del. C. § 1311B**).
- Allows certification without an election if the Board verifies **over 50%** of employees in an appropriate bargaining unit signed dated authorization/representation cards.
- Requires the Board to **investigate** the petition and authorization signatures submitted under the majority verification procedure.
- If the Board finds the **over-50% threshold** is met, it **may not order an election** and **shall certify** the employee organization.
- Retains existing petition/election framework requiring **30%** uncoerced signatures to initiate certification/decertification petitions (and **10%** showing to get another organization on the ballot).
- Maintains existing timing windows tied to contract expiration for unit challenges and decertification petitions: **180–120 days** before expiration of a qualifying agreement, plus other existing timing rules.
- Includes technical drafting corrections (e.g., conforming formatting such as “30%,” “10%,” and minor wording fixes).
- Bill was enacted (Chapter 193, Volume 85) and **approved September 2, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Faster certification where majority support is clear:** If **over 50%** sign, the Board must certify without running an election (new **§ 4011A(b)** and **§ 1311B(b)**).
- **Reduced administrative burden of elections in majority-support cases:** The text prohibits ordering an election when the **over-50%** verification is met, potentially avoiding election procedures in those cases.
- **Parallel process across school and non-school public employment:** Adds substantially identical procedures to both Title 14 and Title 19, creating consistent options for certification.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced use of secret-ballot elections in some cases:** The statute requires certification without an election when the Board verifies **over 50%** authorization signatures (new **§ 4011A(b)** / **§ 1311B(b)**), eliminating an election option in those circumstances.
- **Potential disputes over signature validity could become more consequential:** Certification hinges on verifying “dated representation authorization signatures” and the “over 50%” threshold, which may increase the stakes of signature challenges (the bill requires investigation but does not detail methods).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The bill requires the Board to “investigate” majority-verification petitions, but **does not specify** in statute the investigation standards, timelines, or procedures for validating/contesting signatures.  
- The bill does not define what form “dated representation authorization signatures” must take beyond being “dated,” nor how revocations (if any) are handled.  
- “Over 50%” is required, but the bill does not specify how the denominator is calculated if there are disputes about unit composition during unit-determination proceedings.

## Key Policy Changes
- **New majority-verification certification path (public school):**  
  - Employee organization may petition for certification based on verification that **over 50%** of employees in the proposed appropriate bargaining unit want representation (**14 Del. C. § 4011A(a)**).  
  - Board must require **dated** authorization signatures as verification (**§ 4011A(a)**).  
  - If Board determines **over 50%** signed, Board **may not order an election** and **shall certify** (**§ 4011A(b)**).
- **New majority-verification certification path (other public employees):**  
  - Same structure and thresholds for public employees under Title 19 (**19 Del. C. § 1311B(a)–(b)**).
- **Retains existing thresholds and timing rules in current processes (not newly created but restated/technically corrected):**
  - **30%** uncoerced signatures to file certain representation-related petitions (e.g., **§ 4010(a), § 4011(a), § 4011(b); § 1310(a), § 1311(a), § 1311(b)**).  
  - Signatures for certain petitions: **not valid if signed more than 12 months** before petition filing (**§ 4011(a); § 1311(a)**).  
  - Ballot access for other organizations: written proof of at least **10%** representation (**§ 4011(c); § 1311(c)**).  
  - Contract-window limitations for certain challenges/decertifications: **not more than 180 days nor less than 120 days** before expiration of a qualifying agreement (**§ 4010(f); § 4011(b); § 1310(f); § 1311(b)**).  
  - No election within **12 months** of a preceding valid election (**§ 4011(e); § 1311(e)**).

## Who Benefits
- Employee organizations seeking to become the exclusive representative that can demonstrate **over 50%** employee authorization signatures in an appropriate bargaining unit (Titles 14 and 19).
- Employees who prefer certification via authorization signatures rather than an election, where **over 50%** support can be verified.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- Employees who would prefer a secret-ballot election in cases where **over 50%** authorization signatures are verified, because the Board **may not order an election** in that circumstance.
- Competing employee organizations that might otherwise seek to contest representation via election when majority verification results in immediate certification.
