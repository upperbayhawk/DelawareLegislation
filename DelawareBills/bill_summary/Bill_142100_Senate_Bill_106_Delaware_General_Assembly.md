## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14 of the Delaware Code to require every school district and charter school to adopt, publish, and enforce a student cell phone use policy during school hours, with specified minimum policy elements and educator input.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **each school district and charter school** to adopt a **student cell phone use policy during school hours** with **educator input**.  
- Sets **minimum required policy elements**, including acceptable use rules, limits during **instructional time**, and designated times/places for use.  
- Requires policies to include **communication guidelines** for schools, parents/guardians, and students, plus **consequences** for violations.  
- Requires **exceptions** for **emergencies** and **medical or educational accommodations**.  
- Requires each district/charter to **provide its adopted policy to the Delaware Department of Education**.  
- Requires posting the policy on the district/charter website by **August 1, 2025**.  
- Requires enforcement to be **fair and consistent** and **not solely the responsibility of classroom educators**.  
- Allows policy amendments; amended policies must be sent to the Department **within 90 days** and posted on the website.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Minimum statewide baseline** for cell phone policies (acceptable use, instructional-time limits, consequences, exceptions), which may increase consistency across districts/charters.  
- **Explicit instructional-time limitation requirement** may reduce in-class distraction because each policy must “limit cell phone use during instructional time.”  
- **Emergency and accommodation exceptions** are required, supporting continuity for students with **medical or educational accommodations**.  
- **Shared enforcement responsibility**: districts/charters must ensure implementation/enforcement is **not solely on classroom educators**, which may reduce teacher burden.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative workload**: each district/charter must draft/adopt a policy, coordinate educator input, submit it to the Department, and post it online by a fixed deadline (**August 1, 2025**).  
- **Ongoing compliance burden** when amending policies: amended policy must be provided to the Department **within 90 days** and posted online.  
- **Local variation** remains: while minimum elements are required, the bill does not set uniform statewide rules (e.g., exact restrictions or consequence types), which may result in inconsistent student experiences across schools.
