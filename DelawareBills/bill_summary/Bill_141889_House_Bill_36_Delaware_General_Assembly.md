## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Align Delaware’s narrower, entity-specific nondiscrimination provisions across multiple Titles with the broader statewide nondiscrimination frameworks by adding cross-references that prohibit discrimination on **“any other basis protected”** under the applicable broad law (Equal Accommodations Law, Fair Housing Act, and unlawful employment practices). The Act also clarifies that the **Title 6 definition of “place of public accommodation” includes entities and services licensed or regulated under Title 5 (Banking).**

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands multiple narrow nondiscrimination clauses to also cover **“any other basis protected”** under the applicable broad chapter: **Chapter 45, Title 6 (public accommodations); Chapter 46, Title 6 (fair housing); Chapter 7, Title 19 (employment).**
- Updates nondiscrimination requirements for **transportation network companies (TNCs)** and their drivers by tying protected bases to **Chapter 45 of Title 6** (public accommodations).
- Clarifies that **“place of public accommodation”** (Title 6, Ch. 45) includes **entities and services licensed or regulated under Title 5 (Banking).**
- Updates fair housing “purpose” language (Title 6, § 4601) to expressly include **gender identity** among protected bases.
- Aligns nondiscrimination provisions for certain **professional licensing boards** (podiatry, chiropractic, medical licensure & discipline, funeral services) to incorporate **all Chapter 45 protected bases**.
- Aligns employment-related nondiscrimination provisions for **New Castle County positions, state classified service, apprenticeship programs, and large public works contracts** to incorporate **all Chapter 7, Title 19 protected bases**.
- Aligns property/housing-related provisions (e.g., **recording discriminatory covenants; landlord-tenant; manufactured home community “comparable housing”**) to incorporate **all Chapter 46 protected bases**.
- **Approved August 20, 2025**. (The bill text provided does not state a separate effective date section.)

## Key Policy Changes
- **Title 2 (TNCs):** TNC nondiscrimination policy and driver compliance expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 6, Chapter 45** (Title 2, § 1917).
- **Title 6 (Public accommodations definition):** “Place of public accommodation” revised/clarified to include:
  - establishments offering goods/services to the public including government agencies and state-funded agencies performing public functions;  
  - **hotels and motels**; and  
  - **entities and services licensed or regulated under Title 5** (Title 6, § 4502(19)).
- **Title 6 (Fair Housing Act purpose):** purpose statement updated to include **gender identity** (Title 6, § 4601).
- **Title 9 (County employment):** New Castle County employment nondiscrimination expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 19, Chapter 7** (Title 9, § 1183).
- **Title 9 (Civic parkland):** parkland access requirement expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 6, Chapter 45** (Title 9, § 8110).
- **Title 9 (Recording discriminatory covenants):** recorder prohibited from knowingly recording instruments with discriminatory restrictions on **any basis protected under Title 6, Chapter 46** (Title 9, § 9605).
- **Title 10 (Jury service):** jury service nondiscrimination expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 6, Chapter 45** (Title 10, § 4502).
- **Title 14 (Trade schools):** permitting/approval consequences expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 6, Chapter 45** (Title 14, § 8516).
- **Title 19 (Apprenticeships):** nondiscrimination expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 19, Chapter 7** (Title 19, § 204(g)).
- **Title 20 (Veterans cemetery):** burial eligibility nondiscrimination expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 6, Chapter 45** (Title 20, § 1204(c)).
- **Title 24 (Licensing boards):** nondiscrimination provisions updated for Boards of Podiatry, Chiropractic, Medical Licensure & Discipline, and Funeral Services to include **any basis protected under Title 6, Chapter 45** (Title 24, §§ 506, 706, 1713, 3102).
- **Title 25 (Landlord-tenant fair housing provisions):**
  - aligns § 5116(a), (b), and (d) with **Title 6, Chapter 46**; and
  - replaces “has a child or children in the family” concept with **“familial status”** and adds “any other basis protected…” (Title 25, § 5116).
- **Title 25 (Manufactured home community conversions):** “comparable housing” must be open to all persons regardless of **any basis protected under Title 6, Chapter 46** (Title 25, § 7102).
- **Title 29 (State classified service):** nondiscrimination expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 19, Chapter 7** (Title 29, § 5953).
- **Title 29 (Large public works contracts):** required contract clauses expanded to include **any basis protected under Title 19, Chapter 7**, including in postings and solicitations/ads (Title 29, § 6962(d)(7)).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Compliance requirements expanded by cross-reference** to the applicable broad chapters:
  - **TNCs** must adopt and notify drivers of a nondiscrimination policy covering any basis protected under **Title 6, Chapter 45**; **TNC drivers** must comply accordingly (Title 2, § 1917).
  - **Recorders** may not knowingly record certain real property instruments containing discriminatory restrictions on any basis protected under **Title 6, Chapter 46** (Title 9, § 9605(b)(1)).
  - **Public works contractors** on projects financed in whole or part by state appropriation must include nondiscrimination clauses, take “positive steps,” post notices, and include nondiscrimination language in solicitations/ads—covering any basis protected under **Title 19, Chapter 7** (Title 29, § 6962(d)(7)).
  - **Trade school approvals/permits:** the Department may refuse/renew/revoke based on refusal to admit applicants on any basis protected under **Title 6, Chapter 45** (Title 14, § 8516(11)).
  - **Landlord-tenant damages:** tenant may recover damages for discrimination under Title 25, § 5116 (including “reasonable expenditures necessary to obtain adequate substitute housing”) (Title 25, § 5116(c)).
