## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware criminal law and school child-safety education law to (1) clarify and update the sexual extortion statute in Title 11 and (2) require that Erin’s Law-related school programming explicitly include information about sexual extortion.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates **11 Del. C. § 774 (Sexual extortion)** to clarify conduct covered, including compelling someone to engage in a sexual act or **produce a visual depiction** of nudity/sexual conduct, using specified threats.  
- Keeps sexual extortion as a **Class E felony** in general, but makes it a **Class B felony** if aggravating circumstances apply.  
- **Class B felony triggers** include: (1) **adult defendant** with a **child** victim (per **§ 1100**) or **vulnerable adult** victim (per **§ 1105**), or (2) the offense **causes serious physical injury or death**.  
- Bill synopsis states penalties: **Class E felony** is punishable by **up to 5 years** imprisonment; **Class B felony** is punishable by **minimum 2 years** up to **maximum 25 years** imprisonment (as described in the synopsis).  
- Amends **14 Del. C. § 4163 (Erin’s Law programming)** to require employee training to include **laws and penalties**, specifically naming the **crime of sexual extortion under § 774**.  
- Requires student instruction to include information on **sexual exploitation as defined in § 774 of Title 11** (as written in the amendment text).  
- Requires parent information to include **methods to discuss sexual extortion** and **warning signs** a child may be a victim of sexual extortion.  
- Requires certain school staff “additional training” to include **coordination with law enforcement and Title IX coordinators** for suspected violations, including sexual extortion.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Stronger penalties for adult-on-child/vulnerable adult cases and severe outcomes:** Elevates sexual extortion to a **Class B felony** when the defendant is an **adult** and the victim is a **child or vulnerable adult**, or when the victim suffers **serious physical injury or death**.  
- **Explicit integration into school safety education:** Requires Erin’s Law programming to include sexual extortion information for **employees, students, and parents**, including **warning signs** and how to discuss/report concerns.  
- **Improved clarity/technical corrections:** The Act states it makes **technical corrections** and clarifies the meaning of **§ 774**, potentially improving comprehension and application.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)

- **Additional training/content requirements for schools:** School districts and charter schools must ensure programming includes newly specified sexual extortion content, potentially adding compliance and implementation workload. (No funding mechanism is specified in the text.)

## Key Policy Changes
- **11 Del. C. § 774 changes (sexual extortion):**
  - Clarifies the prohibited conduct (compelling/inducing another to engage in sexual acts or produce visual depictions of nudity/sexual conduct) and the threatening means used to compel the conduct (including threats to physically harm, damage property, commit crimes, accuse someone of a crime, expose secrets/claims, interfere with legal claims, disseminate nude/sexual depictions, or materially harm health/safety/business/career/finances/reputation/relationships).
  - Keeps baseline grading as **Class E felony**.
  - Creates/clarifies **Class B felony** grading when:
    1) **Adult defendant** + victim is a **child (§ 1100)** or **vulnerable adult (§ 1105)**, or  
    2) the violation **causes serious physical injury or death**.
- **14 Del. C. § 4163 changes (Erin’s Law programming):**
  - Requires training/instruction/parent materials to include sexual extortion specifically (laws/penalties; discussion methods; warning signs; coordination with law enforcement/Title IX).
