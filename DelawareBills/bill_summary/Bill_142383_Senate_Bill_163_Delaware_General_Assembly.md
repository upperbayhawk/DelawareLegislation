## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend **29 Del. C. § 5813(c)** to change the annual deadline for **public officers’ electronic financial disclosure reports** from **March 15** to **“on or before the third Wednesday of March”** so the deadline falls on a weekday and aligns with the Public Integrity Commission’s practice.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Changes the annual financial disclosure filing deadline from **March 15** to **“on or before the third Wednesday of March”** each year.  
- Keeps the initial filing requirement: a new public officer must file **within 14 days** after becoming a public officer (per **§ 5812** definition).  
- Codifies that filings may be submitted **“on or before”** the annual deadline (not only on the deadline date).  
- The bill is aimed at avoiding deadlines that fall on weekends (example cited: **March 15, 2025 was a Saturday**).  
- Filing remains **electronic**, submitted and **electronically verified** through the Commission’s database.  
- No changes are made to what must be disclosed (e.g., **$5,000** ownership/income thresholds; **$1,000** income/capital gain/reimbursement thresholds; **$250** gift threshold; **90 days** and **$1,000** creditor threshold).  
- Approved date in the enacted text: **July 30, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves practical access to assistance:** The synopsis states that when a deadline falls on a weekend, **Public Integrity Commission assistance may not be available**; moving to a weekday deadline addresses that issue.  
- **Clarifies timing flexibility:** Expressly allows filing **“on or before”** the deadline, consistent with Commission practice (per synopsis), which may reduce confusion and late filings caused by misunderstanding.  
- **More predictable administrative workflow:** A consistent weekday deadline may help the Commission manage intake and support.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Deadline change (annual filing):** From **March 15** to **“on or before the third Wednesday of March”** (as amended in **29 Del. C. § 5813(c)**).  
- **Clarification of early filing:** Adds/clarifies “**on or before**” language for the annual filing deadline.  
- **No change** to: electronic filing requirement; disclosure categories; or reporting thresholds including:
  - Business ownership/income disclosure threshold: **> $5,000** fair market value or **> $5,000** income.
  - Professional organization income threshold: **> $5,000**.
  - Reportable income/services from a single source: **> $1,000**.
  - Reportable capital gain from a single source: **> $1,000** (excluding sale of an occupied residence).
  - Reportable reimbursement from a single source: **> $1,000**.
  - Reportable gifts: **> $250** (must identify amount; may rely in good faith on source’s value representation).
  - Reportable creditors: debt **> $1,000** for **90 consecutive days or more**.
