## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 30 (Realty Transfer Tax) definitions/exceptions to (1) clarify that the existing exemption for transfers between “husband and wife” applies to spouses generally, and (2) add an exemption for transfers between grandparents and grandchildren (and the spouse of such a grandchild).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Updates the realty transfer tax exemption language from “husband and wife” to “spouses,” clarifying the exemption applies to spouses generally.  
- Makes a parallel update to the exemption for transfers between formerly married persons who are divorced, replacing “husband and wife” phrasing with “spouses.”  
- Adds a new exemption for conveyances between a **grandparent and grandchild** or the **spouse of such a grandchild**.  
- Changes are made within **30 Del. C. § 5401 (Definitions)**, in the list of what is **not** a “Document” for realty transfer tax purposes (i.e., excluded conveyances).  
- The bill text does **not** state any change to tax rates, percentages, or dollar thresholds.  
- The bill synopsis describes these as “updates” and “clarifies” treatment of spouses, and expands exemptions to include grandparent-to-grandchild transfers.  
- No effective date is included in the provided bill text.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Equal application of an existing exemption:** By replacing “husband and wife” with “spouses,” the exemption explicitly applies to spouses generally (per synopsis: “not solely limited between a husband and wife”).  
- **Reduced tax liability for certain family transfers:** Adding an exemption for **grandparent ↔ grandchild** conveyances could eliminate realty transfer tax for those transfers (as they are excluded from “Document”).
