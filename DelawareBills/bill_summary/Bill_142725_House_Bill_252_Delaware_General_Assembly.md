## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 16, § 4764 of the Delaware Code to change the legal classification and penalties for using or consuming a personal use quantity of certain marijuana/THC-related substances in public areas or in a moving vehicle.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Reclassifies certain conduct from an **unclassified misdemeanor** to a **civil violation**.  
- Applies to **knowing or intentional use/consumption** of **up to a personal use quantity** of a controlled substance or counterfeit controlled substance listed in **§ 4714(d)(19)** (i.e., products containing marijuana or tetrahydrocannabinols (THC)).  
- Covers use/consumption in an **area accessible to the public** or **in a moving vehicle**, unless otherwise authorized by the chapter.  
- Changes penalties to **fines only**: **$50 for a first violation** and **$100 for subsequent violations**.  
- Removes prior criminal penalties of up to **5 days imprisonment** and/or a fine up to **$200** for this conduct.  
- States it **does not affect** law enforcement’s ability to **arrest and charge DUI/drug-impaired driving** under **Title 21**.  
- Makes a **technical correction** to remove a section-title reference to a previously repealed subsection.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces criminalization**: Conduct previously treated as an unclassified misdemeanor becomes a **civil violation** (explicit change in § 4764(d)).  
- **Eliminates jail exposure**: Removes potential penalty of up to **5 days imprisonment** for covered conduct (text deletes “imprisoned not more than 5 days”).  
- **Lower, predictable penalties**: Sets specific fine amounts (**$50 first; $100 subsequent**) rather than a higher cap (previously “not more than **$200**”).  
- **Clarifies DUI enforcement remains**: Explicitly preserves ability to enforce **drug-impaired driving** under **Title 21** (stated in Synopsis).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Continues enforcement and fines for public use**: The conduct remains unlawful and subject to monetary penalties (**$50 / $100**), including for use in an “area accessible to the public” or “in a moving vehicle.”  
- **Escalating penalty for repeat violations**: Subsequent violations increase from **$50** to **$100** (explicit fine structure).

- **Scope**: Applies to controlled substances or counterfeit controlled substances classified in **§ 4714(d)(19)** (products containing marijuana or THC), when used/consumed:
  - In an **area accessible to the public**, or
