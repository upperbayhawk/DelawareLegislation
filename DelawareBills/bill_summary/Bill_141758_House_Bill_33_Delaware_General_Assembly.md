## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 16, § 4795 (Jurisdiction) to allow certain marijuana/THC-related offenses occurring **within the City of Newark** and committed by persons **18 years old or older** to be brought **initially** in the **Alderman’s Court of the City of Newark**, alongside existing state court jurisdiction, subject to Newark adopting an ordinance covering the conduct.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands venue options so specified § 4764 violations occurring **in Newark** by individuals **18+** may be initiated in **Newark Alderman’s Court**.  
- Creates **concurrent jurisdiction** between Newark Alderman’s Court and **Court of Common Pleas** for Newark ordinance violations that criminalize conduct corresponding to **§ 4764(b) and (d)**.  
- Creates **concurrent jurisdiction** between Newark Alderman’s Court and **Justice of the Peace Court** for Newark ordinance violations imposing a **civil penalty** for conduct corresponding to **§ 4764(c)**.  
- Ties Alderman’s Court authority to a **City of Newark ordinance**: the case may be brought initially in Alderman’s Court only “pursuant to” an ordinance that criminalizes or imposes civil penalties for the covered conduct.  
- Leaves in place the general jurisdiction framework: **Superior Court** retains original and exclusive jurisdiction over Chapter violations by persons **18+**, except where carved out.  
- Leaves **Family Court** jurisdiction unchanged for persons **under 18**.  
- Enacted as **Chapter 10, Volume 85**; **Approved April 14, 2025**.

- **More flexible forum options:** Establishes **concurrent jurisdiction** with state courts (Court of Common Pleas or Justice of the Peace Court), giving prosecutors/city flexibility on where cases begin for the specified conduct.  
- **Clarifies Newark’s pathway to enforce local ordinances aligned with § 4764:** The amended § 4795(e) explicitly recognizes bringing matters initially in Alderman’s Court when Newark has an ordinance that criminalizes § 4764(b)/(d)-type conduct or civilly penalizes § 4764(c)-type conduct.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Adds § 4795(e)** (as amended) providing that conduct constituting a violation of **§ 4764** alleged to have occurred **within the City of Newark** by persons **18+** **may be brought initially** before Newark Alderman’s Court **pursuant to a Newark ordinance** that:
  - **criminalizes** conduct corresponding to **§ 4764(b) or § 4764(d)**; or
  - **imposes a civil penalty** for conduct corresponding to **§ 4764(c)**.
