## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To condemn deaths involving agents of the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (including ICE and the U.S. Border Patrol), call for transparency and accountability (including independent, state-level investigations) when federal agents use deadly force, and affirm First Amendment rights to peaceful protest.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expresses the Delaware Senate’s condemnation and “outrage over tragic killings at the hands of federal agents.”
- States that peaceful protest is a fundamental First Amendment right and should be respected by federal officials.
- Cites “recent weeks and months” in which multiple deaths occurred from actions of ICE and Border Patrol agents.
- Notes state-level officials have faced “federal barriers” to investigating conduct and deaths occurring in their jurisdictions.
- Calls for the “immediate cessation of excessive conduct” by federal law enforcement, including deadly force in response to “non-violent conduct.”
- Calls for accountability when federal agents use deadly force, including “independent, state-level investigations free from automatic federal immunity.”
- Urges DHS agents and federal officials to view peaceful protests as a “defining strength of American democracy,” not a threat.
