## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Clarify Delaware’s Lethal Violence Protective Order (LVPO) law to specify that **only law enforcement officers may request emergency LVPO relief under 10 Del. C. § 7703**, while **non-emergency LVPO petitions under § 7704 may be filed by any “petitioner”** allowed under existing law.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Amends **10 Del. C. § 7702** (commencement/procedure) to clarify who may seek relief under LVPO statutes.
- Specifies that **a “petitioner law enforcement officer”** may request relief under **§ 7703 (Emergency hearings)** or **§ 7704 (Nonemergency hearings)**.
- Specifies that **any petitioner** may request relief under **§ 7704** (non-emergency) only.
- Addresses statutory ambiguity where § 7702 previously suggested “a petitioner” could seek emergency relief, while **§ 7703 describes only a law-enforcement process**.
- Maintains existing venue rule: LVPO petitions must be filed **in the county where the respondent resides**.
- Enacted as **Chapter 147, Volume 85**, and **approved August 20, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clarifies eligibility for emergency LVPO filings**: emergency requests in Justice of the Peace Court must come through **law enforcement**, reducing confusion created by prior wording in § 7702.
- **Aligns emergency LVPO practice with described § 7703 procedures** (which the synopsis indicates only cover law enforcement steps and do not set procedures/burden for individual emergency petitioners).
- **Retains public access to non-emergency LVPO process** by expressly allowing **any petitioner** to file under **§ 7704**.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Limits direct public access to emergency relief**: individuals who qualify as “petitioners” under the broader LVPO definitions may no longer attempt to seek **emergency** LVPO relief directly under **§ 7703** (must route through law enforcement).
- **May increase reliance on law enforcement availability** for emergency petitions, because emergency filing authority is limited to law enforcement officers.

## Key Policy Changes
- **10 Del. C. § 7702(a) revised** to read (in substance): a **petitioner law enforcement officer** may request LVPO relief under **§ 7703 (emergency)** or **§ 7704 (non-emergency)** by filing an affidavit or verified petition.
- **10 Del. C. § 7702(b) revised** to read (in substance): **any petitioner** may request LVPO relief under **§ 7704 (non-emergency)** by filing an affidavit or verified petition.
- **Venue rule retained**: LVPO petition must be filed in the **county where the respondent resides**.
