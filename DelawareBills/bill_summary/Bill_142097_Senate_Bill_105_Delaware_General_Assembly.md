## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware Code Title 16 § 10005 to change the composition and leadership structure of the Enhanced 911 Emergency Reporting System Service Board (“Board”), including making the Secretary of the Department of Safety and Homeland Security (“Secretary”) (or designee) an ex officio member and the Chair of the Board.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Keeps the Board at **7 members**, but changes who fills the seats and how leadership is assigned.  
- Reduces Governor-appointed seats from **6 to 5**; appointments still require **Senate confirmation** (“consent of a majority of all the members elected to the Senate”).  
- Makes the **Secretary (or designee)** a Board member **by virtue of office** (ex officio).  
- Makes the **Secretary (or designee) the Board Chair**, replacing the prior structure where the **Governor designated the chairperson**.  
- Sets the **Secretary/designee term at 3 years** (as stated in amended § 10005(c)(4)).  
- Retains requirements that, among the Governor-appointed members, at least **3** have **telecommunications technical/financial expertise** and at least **1** represents the **Delaware Association of County Governments**.  
- Keeps proxy attendance/voting for Governor-appointed members: allowed **no more than 3 times per calendar year**, with proxy votes having the **same force and effect**.  
- On the **enactment date**, terminates the term of the existing Governor-appointed Board Chair and applies the new chair structure immediately.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Codifies existing practice**: The synopsis states the Secretary “is currently, and historically, an appointed member” and the Act codifies that practice.  
- **Clearer accountability/leadership**: The statute directly assigns Board chair duties to the **Secretary (or designee)** rather than leaving chair selection to the Governor.  
- **Maintains expertise and local government representation requirements**: Continues minimum thresholds for **telecom expertise (≥3 members)** and **county government representation (≥1 member)** among Governor appointees.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Reduced gubernatorial appointment influence**: Governor-appointed seats drop from **6 to 5**, shifting one seat to an ex officio executive-branch official (the Secretary or designee).  
- **Immediate disruption to current chair**: The Act terminates the sitting Governor-appointed Chair’s term **on the enactment date** (explicitly ending that role immediately).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Secretary term vs. “by virtue of position”**: The synopsis describes the Secretary as a member “by virtue of the Secretary’s position,” but amended § 10005(c)(4) also states the Secretary/designee term “is **3 years**,” which may create ambiguity about whether Board membership/chairmanship is tied to holding office or to a fixed Board term.  
- **Chair term/tenure mechanics**: Prior law referenced a chair serving “at the pleasure of the Governor.” The amendment makes the Secretary Chair but does not clearly state whether the chair role changes automatically when a new Secretary takes office (beyond the “by virtue of position” concept in the synopsis).
