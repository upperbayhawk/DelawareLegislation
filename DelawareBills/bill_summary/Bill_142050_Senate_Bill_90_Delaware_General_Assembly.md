## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Allow eligible Delaware State University (DSU) Inspire Scholarship recipients who finish a bachelor’s degree before using all available Inspire-eligible semesters to apply the remaining scholarship “grant balance” to graduate tuition at DSU, with modified eligibility rules for graduate use.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Extends the Delaware State Inspire Scholarship Program so that unused eligible semesters after completing a bachelor’s degree may be applied to **graduate degree program tuition at DSU**.  
- Creates a defined concept of a **“remaining Inspire grant balance”** for students who graduate before the end of their eligibility period.  
- Sets the remaining balance as the Inspire grant amount for **each semester remaining** in the student’s eligibility period after earning the bachelor’s degree.  
- Allows use of remaining balance for graduate study for up to **10 years after completing** the bachelor’s degree.  
- For graduate use, removes two requirements: students **do not need to be full-time** and **do not need continuous enrollment**.  
- Makes “corresponding changes” so individuals who had **additional time under the program due to foster care status** can also use remaining funds for graduate study if they finish undergraduate early (described in synopsis).  
- Fiscal note estimates increased program costs: **FY2026: $57,330–$99,000; FY2027: $81,900–$148,500; FY2028: $81,900–$148,500**.  
- Effective upon Governor’s signature; enacted law shows **Approved September 19, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Increases educational opportunity at DSU:** students may fund **graduate tuition** using unused scholarship eligibility (new § 3417A(b)).  
- **Rewards early completion:** students who complete the bachelor’s degree in fewer semesters can apply remaining eligibility rather than losing it (§ 3417A(a)).  
- **Flexibility for graduate students:** part-time enrollment and non-continuous enrollment are allowed for graduate use (§ 3417A(c)(1)-(2)).  
- **Extended window to use benefits:** graduate-use eligibility lasts **10 years** after completing the bachelor’s degree (§ 3417A(c)).  

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increases state scholarship expenditures:** fiscal note projects additional annual costs (FY2026–FY2028 ranges listed above).  
- **Graduate tuition variability may complicate cost control:** DSU reports graduate tuition is **per credit hour** and varies by program (fiscal note).  
- **Administrative tracking complexity:** program must track each student’s remaining eligible semesters and remaining “grant balance” across undergraduate-to-graduate transition (§ 3417A(a)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- How the “remaining Inspire grant balance” converts to graduate tuition when graduate tuition is charged **per credit hour** and varies by program (fiscal note notes variability; statute defines balance “per semester”).  
- Whether “graduate degree program tuition” includes all DSU graduate programs and fees, or tuition only (text states **tuition**; fees not addressed).

- Interaction with existing eligibility requirements in **§ 3412A and § 3414A** (bill requires meeting them “except as follows,” but does not restate all underlying requirements).

## Key Policy Changes
- **New § 3417A “Grant balance” added/created:**
  - Defines when a student has a remaining Inspire grant balance: completing a bachelor’s degree **before the end of the applicable eligibility period** (§ 3417A(a)(1)).  
  - Defines the remaining balance: Inspire grant amount for **each semester** remaining in the eligibility period **after** bachelor’s completion (§ 3417A(a)(2)).  
  - Authorizes using remaining balance for **graduate degree program tuition at DSU** (§ 3417A(b)).  
  - Sets graduate-use eligibility for **10 years** after bachelor’s completion (§ 3417A(c)).  
  - Removes two requirements for graduate-use eligibility: **no full-time requirement** and **no continuous enrollment requirement** (§ 3417A(c)(1)-(2)).  
- **Technical drafting corrections** to definitions section (§ 3410A) to conform to drafting manual (e.g., “shall mean means”).

## Who Benefits
- DSU students who:
  - Earn a bachelor’s degree in **fewer than the maximum eligible semesters** and therefore have remaining Inspire-eligible semesters (§ 3417A(a)).  
  - Want to pursue a **graduate degree at DSU** and apply unused scholarship value to tuition (§ 3417A(b)).  
  - Need flexibility to attend graduate school **part-time** and/or with **breaks in enrollment** (§ 3417A(c)(1)-(2)).  
- Individuals eligible for additional undergraduate completion time due to **foster care-related provisions** who still finish early enough to have remaining balance (synopsis).
