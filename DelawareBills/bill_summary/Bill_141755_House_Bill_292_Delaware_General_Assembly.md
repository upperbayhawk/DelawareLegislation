## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 15 (elections law) to require the Delaware Department of Elections to review campaign contribution and expenditure reports within a set timeframe and to publicly label each report’s review status on the Department’s website.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires the Department of Elections to **review all contribution and expenditure reports** listed in **15 Del. C. § 8030** **within 60 days of filing** to confirm there are **no violations of Title 15**.  
- Mandates that once filed, each report must be published online and clearly marked **“Submitted but Not Reviewed.”**
- If no violations are found, the Department must mark the report **“Reviewed and Final.”**
- If violations are found, the Department must **work with the candidate** to correct them under the Department’s rules and regulations.
- After a corrected **amended report** is submitted with no violations, the Department must update the online status to **“Reviewed and Final.”**
- **Applies to reports submitted after March 1, 2026.**
- Fiscal note anticipates **2.0 FTE Administrative Specialist II** positions and additional operating costs.
- Estimated costs include **one-time $14,000 (FY 2026)** and recurring General Fund costs that grow annually (with **2% inflation assumption**).

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More timely compliance review:** Requires review **within 60 days** of filing, creating a defined timeline for oversight.
- **Greater public transparency:** Requires clear online labels—**“Submitted but Not Reviewed”** versus **“Reviewed and Final”**—so the public can distinguish between unreviewed and reviewed filings.
- **Structured correction process:** Directs the Department to **work with the candidate to rectify violations** and update status after a compliant amended report is filed.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increased state staffing and costs:** The Department anticipates needing **2.0 FTE** plus supplies/software; recurring costs estimated at **$137,670** and one-time costs **$14,000** (fiscal note).
- **Administrative workload expansion:** Requires review of **all** covered reports within **60 days**, potentially increasing processing demands for the Department.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities

- **Consequences if 60-day deadline is missed:** The bill requires review within **60 days** but does not specify remedies or consequences if the Department does not meet that timeframe.
