## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 14, § 1703 to update the membership, leadership, functions, staffing, and certain administrative processes of the Interagency Resource Management Committee (IRMC) responsible for establishing a comprehensive and coordinated early childhood system in Delaware.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Makes the **Lieutenant Governor the IRMC chairperson** (replacing the Secretary of Education as chair).  
- Adds legislative representation by including **1 House member (Speaker appointment) and 1 Senate member (President Pro Tempore appointment)** as **nonvoting** IRMC members.  
- Updates IRMC’s relationship to statewide planning by referencing a **state early childhood plan that aligns strategy and resources across state agencies** and directing IRMC to **support and coordinate the functions and responsibilities of the Early Childhood Council (ECC)**.  
- Changes IRMC’s funds role from **“allocate”** to **“advise and make recommendations on the allocation”** of covered early-childhood funds (with an exception for certain disability unit funding).  
- Clarifies staffing: IRMC is staffed by the **Early Childhood Support team** in the Department of Education, assisted by the **Office of the Lieutenant Governor**, with **at least 2 Education Specialists and a clerical support position**.  
- Requires funding support from **DHSS** and **DSCYF** **by no later than July of each fiscal year** for operational costs associated with **1 Educational Specialist and clerical support positions**.  
- Keeps existing administrative mechanisms, including annual reporting **by April 15** each fiscal year and an option for programs to receive **20% of the prior year’s allocation** at the start of each fiscal year.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clearer leadership and accountability:** Designating the **Lieutenant Governor as chairperson** centralizes leadership of IRMC in an elected statewide office (membership change in §1703(n)(1)a.).  
- **Improved coordination with ECC:** Explicitly directing IRMC to **support and coordinate ECC functions and responsibilities** may strengthen coordination between interagency work and the ECC (§1703(n)(3)).  
- **More structured staffing support:** Specifies staffing location and composition (DOE Early Childhood Support team; **at least 2 Education Specialists + clerical support**) and identifies assistance by the **Office of the Lieutenant Governor** (§1703(n)(9)).  
- **Earlier fiscal-year continuity for programs:** Continues the ability for funded programs to receive **20% of the prior year’s allocation at the outset of each fiscal year**, supporting continuity of services (§1703(n)(8)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential reduction in direct fiscal authority:** Changing IRMC’s role from **“allocate”** to **“advise and make recommendations on the allocation”** could reduce IRMC’s direct decision-making authority over covered funds (§1703(n)(3)a.).  
- **Additional administrative coordination needs:** Adding **nonvoting** legislative members and additional specified roles may increase coordination and meeting-management needs without changing voting capacity (§1703(n)(1)h.–i.).  
- **Ongoing staffing and interagency funding obligation:** Requires DHSS and DSCYF funding **by no later than July each fiscal year** for specified operational costs, creating a recurring administrative requirement (§1703(n)(9)).

## Open Questions / Ambiguities

- The bill states IRMC will support and coordinate ECC’s functions and responsibilities, but **does not define which specific ECC functions** are to be supported/coordinated (§1703(n)(3)).  
- Funding language specifies DHSS and DSCYF support for costs associated with **“1 Educational Specialist and clerical support positions”** while staffing language requires **at least 2 Education Specialists**; **the source of funding for the second Education Specialist is not specified** (§1703(n)(9)).  
- The bill maintains authority for OMB Director and Controller General to transfer funds with prior agreement of affected secretaries, but **does not specify any additional criteria or limits** beyond prior agreement (§1703(n)(7)).
