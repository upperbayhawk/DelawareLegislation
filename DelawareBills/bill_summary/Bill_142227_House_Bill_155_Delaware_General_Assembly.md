## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29, § 5809 of the Delaware Code to require that **any report prepared by the State Public Integrity Commission** be **made publicly available on the Commission’s website**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **public posting on the Commission’s website** of **any report prepared by the Commission**.  
- Applies to the Commission’s **annual report** and also **“such other reports and studies”** the Commission prepares.  
- Does **not change** the existing requirement to prepare an **annual report by March 1 of each year** covering the prior year’s activities.  
- Changes are made by amending **29 Del. C. § 5809(11)** (powers and duties).  
- The bill text does not specify any exceptions, redactions, or confidentiality carve-outs.  
- No appropriations, fees, or specific fiscal figures are included in the bill text.  

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
