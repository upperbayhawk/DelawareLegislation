## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Expand eligibility for **free school breakfasts and lunches** in certain Delaware public schools to students whose household incomes are **greater than 185% and at or below 225% of the federal poverty level (FPL)**, and require the **Delaware Department of Education (DOE)** to reimburse schools for the added cost (the difference between federal “paid” and “free” meal reimbursement rates).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands free meal eligibility to students with household income **>185% to ≤225% of FPL**.  
- Applies to **public schools participating** in the **School Breakfast Program** and/or **National School Lunch Program**.  
- **Does not apply** to schools using federal alternative reimbursement models: **Community Eligibility Provision (CEP)** or **Provision 1, 2, or 3**.  
- Requires schools to **carry over eligibility determinations** similarly to federal carryover rules under **7 C.F.R. § 245.6(c)**; schools **do not incur liability** for accuracy of carried-over determinations.  
- Requires DOE to **reimburse schools each budget year** for the **difference** between federal **paid** vs. **free** reimbursement rates for breakfasts and lunches.  
- Fiscal note estimates State costs of **$10,205,467 (FY2026)**, **$10,602,508 (FY2027)**, **$11,015,286 (FY2028)**.  
- The Act is **effective immediately upon signature** and must be **implemented beginning July 1, 2026**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More students receive free meals**: newly covers households between **185% and 225% FPL**, who are otherwise ineligible for free/reduced meals under federal thresholds (as described in the Synopsis).  
- **Reduces financial burden on families** in the newly covered income band by converting previously “paid” meals to free meals (bill text requires free breakfast/lunch availability for qualifying students).  
- **Provides a state reimbursement mechanism**: DOE must reimburse schools for the **paid-to-free reimbursement gap**, supporting school food program finances (14 Del. C. § 4137C(e)).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **State cost increase**: fiscal note projects total costs of **$10.205M (FY2026)** rising to **$11.015M (FY2028)**.  
- **Uneven applicability across schools**: the expansion **excludes CEP and Provision 1/2/3 schools**, meaning the policy change is limited to non-CEP/non-Provision schools (14 Del. C. § 4137C(f)).  
- **Administrative workload**: schools must determine eligibility for the **185%–225% FPL** group and manage carryover determinations consistent with federal processes (14 Del. C. § 4137C(d)).

- DOE must **reimburse** public schools for each budget year for the **difference** between federal paid and free reimbursement rates for breakfasts and lunches (14 Del. C. § 4137C(e)).  
- Public schools must:  
  - Provide **free breakfasts** to eligible students if they participate in the **School Breakfast Program** (14 Del. C. § 4137C(b)).  
  - Provide **free lunches** to eligible students if they participate in the **National School Lunch Program** (14 Del. C. § 4137C(c)).  
  - Conduct **carryover determinations** consistent with **7 C.F.R. § 245.6(c)** and are protected from liability for carried-over determination accuracy (14 Del. C. § 4137C(d)).

## Key Policy Changes
- Creates/sets **expanded income eligibility** for free school meals for students with household income **greater than 185% but at or below 225% FPL** (14 Del. C. § 4137C(b)-(c)).  
- Requires **DOE reimbursement** to schools for the **difference** between federal paid vs. free meal reimbursement rates (14 Del. C. § 4137C(e)(2)).  
- Requires **carryover** of eligibility determinations consistent with federal rules and provides **no-liability** protection for the accuracy of carried over determinations (14 Del. C. § 4137C(d)).  
- Excludes schools participating in **CEP** and **Provision 1/2/3** from the section’s requirements (14 Del. C. § 4137C(f)).

## Who Benefits
- Students in eligible Delaware **public schools** whose household incomes are **>185% and ≤225% FPL**, who will receive **free breakfast and/or lunch**.  
- Public schools (non-CEP/non-Provision) that will receive **state reimbursement** for the added cost of providing free meals to this group.

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
- State budget (General Fund or applicable appropriations): increased expenditures of approximately **$10.2M in FY2026**, rising in later years (fiscal note).  
- Schools/School Food Authorities in covered schools may face added **administrative processing** tied to eligibility determinations and carryover handling (requirements in § 4137C(d)).
