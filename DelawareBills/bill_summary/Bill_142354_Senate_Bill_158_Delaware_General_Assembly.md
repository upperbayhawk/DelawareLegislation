## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend **10 Del. C. § 4319** to expand the Delaware State Fire Commission’s role in recognizing/approving certain wellness-support personnel so that **communications with first responders may qualify as confidential** under the statute.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds the **Commissioners of the Delaware State Fire Commission** as an entity that can recognize a **Critical Incident Stress Management (CISM) team member** for purposes of confidentiality under **10 Del. C. § 4319**.
- Updates the definition of “CISM team member” so the qualifying national organization/network may be recognized by the **Police Officer Standards and Training Commission**, the **Delaware State Fire Commission**, **or the Commissioners of the Delaware State Fire Commission**.
- Confirms/expands who may approve a person to function as a CISM team member: the **Delaware State Fire Commission** or the **colonel/chief** of the relevant police/fire/EMS agency.
- Updates the definition of “Trained peer support member” to allow approval by the agency’s **colonel/chief**, **Board of Commissioners**, **or Executive Director of the Delaware State Fire Commission**.
- The practical effect is to broaden pathways for certain peer support/CISM personnel to be formally recognized/approved—supporting application of the **confidential communications** protections in § 4319.
- No dollar amounts, appropriations, or explicit implementation deadlines are included in the bill text.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More avenues for confidentiality protections to apply:** By adding Fire Commission leadership entities as recognized approvers/recognizers, the bill may make it easier for first responders to receive covered CISM/peer support services where confidentiality under **§ 4319** depends on status/approval of the support member (as reflected in the amended definitions).
- **Clarifies Fire Commission authority in this context:** The text explicitly names the **Commissioners**, **Board of Commissioners**, and **Executive Director** roles for recognizing/approving covered support members.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
