## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14 to require Delaware Department of Education (DOE) employees to undergo criminal background checks and be subject to ongoing (“continuous”) criminal history monitoring, and to authorize similar checks/monitoring for certain contractors or third parties who may access student records.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **current DOE employees** and **individuals conditionally offered DOE employment** to submit to **criminal background checks**.  
- Authorizes the **Secretary** to require **current or prospective contractors/third parties** who **may require access to student records** to submit to criminal background checks.  
- Background checks require **fingerprints** and other information submitted to the **State Bureau of Identification (SBI)**.  
- Checks must include both **Delaware state criminal history** (SBI Central Repository) and **federal criminal history** (FBI) reports or “no record” statements.  
- SBI must forward obtained state/federal criminal history information to DOE.  
- Establishes **continuous criminal history monitoring** by requiring SBI to provide **subsequent state criminal history** and, if available, **Rap Back System** information (as defined in **11 Del. C. § 8502**).  
- DOE may review and use the information, as permitted by law, to determine **qualification for employment** or **continued access** to DOE **systems, facilities, or data**.  
- Sets a compliance deadline: **current DOE employees and contractors** must submit required information to SBI **within 30 days** after receiving DOE’s **written request**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)

## Key Policy Changes
- **New/affirmed covered individuals (mandatory):**
  - **Current DOE employees** must submit to a criminal background check.  
  - **Individuals conditionally offered employment** with DOE must submit to a criminal background check.
- **Discretionary expansion (Secretary authority):**
  - The Secretary **may require** a background check for any **current or prospective contractor/third party** that **may require access to student records**.
- **What must be submitted / what records are checked:**
  - Individuals must submit **fingerprints** and other necessary information to **SBI** to obtain:
    - **Entire state criminal history record** (or “no record” statement), and
    - **Entire federal criminal history record** from the **FBI** (or “no record” statement).
- **Information flow and ongoing monitoring:**
  - SBI must forward state and federal results to DOE.
  - For a current employee/contractor/third party who obtains a background check under this section, SBI must provide DOE:
    - **Any subsequent state criminal history record information**, and
    - **Rap Back** information (as defined in **11 Del. C. § 8502**) **if available**.
- **DOE usage authority:**
  - DOE may review and use the information (as permitted by applicable law) to determine **employment qualification** and **continued access** to DOE **systems, facilities, or data**.
- **Compliance deadline:**
  - **Current DOE employees and contractors** must submit required information to SBI **no later than 30 days** after receiving DOE’s **written request**.
