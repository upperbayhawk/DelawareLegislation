## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To create a more uniform, clearer recount threshold and process across Delaware elections (including statewide, legislative, county, Wilmington, and school district elections) by setting a recount trigger at a **vote margin of 1% or less**, and by shifting certain recounts to occur automatically at **state expense**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Sets a **uniform recount threshold of 1% or less** of votes cast (as described in the Synopsis) for covered elections, replacing or modifying prior thresholds such as **½ of 1%** and/or **1,000 votes** in key sections of Title 15.  
- For **primary elections** (Title 15 §3172(e)), requires the Department of Elections to **recount at state expense as part of the canvass** when the margin meets existing numeric/percentage triggers (text retains **<1,000 votes statewide** or **½ of 1%** for other contests).  
- For **general elections for statewide office** (Title 15 §5702(c)), allows a recount when the margin is **<1,000 votes or 1% or less**, whichever is less; recount is conducted **at state expense**.  
- For **State Senator, State Representative, or county office** (Title 15 §5702(e)), recount occurs when the margin is **<1,000 votes or ½ of 1% or less**, whichever is less; recount is conducted **at state expense**.  
- For **public school elections** (Title 14 §1083(e)), removes the requirement that **25 or more voters** petition; provides that the Department of Elections **shall recount all votes cast at state expense** when the margin is **1% or less** (and removes the prior “whichever is larger” structure tied to **10 votes or ½ of 1%**).  
- Keeps an explicit **deadline** in school elections: previously required petition **within 96 hours** after certification; recount to be conducted **no later than 5 business days after verification** of a petition—however, the petition requirement is described in the Synopsis as eliminated, creating a potential internal timing ambiguity.  
- Requires a statewide-office recount request to be presented **before the adjournment of the board of canvass** for that election; recount may not extend beyond the petitioner’s contest.  

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **More recounts in high-turnout contests**: The Synopsis states that switching to a **1%** trigger will “allow for recounts after more elections” for **statewide offices** and **county-wide offices in New Castle County** (where **½ of 1%** often exceeds **1,000 votes**).  
- **Uniformity and clarity (as stated)**: The Act is intended to establish a “**uniform threshold and procedure**” and “**a clear threshold** … **1% or less**” (Synopsis).  
- **Reduced barriers in school district elections**: Eliminates the need for **25 voters** to petition (Synopsis) and provides recount at **state expense** in the statute text for §1083(e).  
- **Administrative alignment**: Makes “technical corrections” to align Title 14 §1083 with current Department of Elections responsibilities and the Delaware Legislative Drafting Manual (Synopsis).

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential increase in recount frequency and state costs**: The bill repeatedly requires recounts “**at state expense**” (Title 14 §1083(e); Title 15 §3172(e); Title 15 §5702(c), (e)), and the Synopsis indicates more recounts may occur for certain offices under the **1%** threshold.  
- **Timing/process ambiguity in school elections**: The statute text for Title 14 §1083(e) retains steps tied to a **petition** (including “verification of the petition” and a “within **96 hours**” petition window), while the Synopsis says the petition requirement is eliminated—this could create implementation confusion until conformed.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Title 14 §1083(e) (public school elections):**
  - Changes recount eligibility threshold to **1% or less** of the total vote (replacing prior “less than 10 votes or **½ of 1%**, whichever is larger”).  
  - Provides that the Department of Elections “**shall recount the ballots cast in that election at state expense**.”  
  - Retains (in the text shown) a petition framework including: **within 96 hours** following certification; recount **no later than 5 business days** after petition verification; and detailed recompilation/recount steps for machine tapes and absentee votes.
- **Title 15 §3172(e) (primary elections):**
  - Requires Department recount “**at state expense as part of the canvass**” when the margin is **<1,000 votes** (statewide) or **½ of 1% or less** (other contests), “whichever is less.”
- **Title 15 §5702(c) (general election, statewide office):**
  - Recount available if margin is **<1,000 votes or 1% or less**, whichever is less; request must be presented **before adjournment of the board of canvass**; recount at **state expense**; recount limited to petitioner’s contest.
- **Title 15 §5702(e) (general election, State Senator/Representative/county office):**
  - Court shall recount at state expense if margin is **<1,000 votes or ½ of 1% or less**, whichever is less.
- **Wilmington applicability (per Synopsis):**
  - Synopsis states the Act covers “**office in the City of Wilmington**” and notes Title 15 Chapters 31 and 57 apply to Wilmington elections per **50 Del. Laws c. 390, § 9 (1955)**.
