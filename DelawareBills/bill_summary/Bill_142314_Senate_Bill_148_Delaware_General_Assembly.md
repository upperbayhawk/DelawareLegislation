## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Designate the Blue Dasher (*Pachydiplax longipennis*) as the official State dragonfly of Delaware by amending Chapter 33, Title 29 of the Delaware Code.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new official State symbol: the Blue Dasher is designated as Delaware’s official State dragonfly (29 Del. C. § 334).
- Enacts the designation via a statutory amendment to Title 29, Chapter 33 (State symbols).
- Bill findings cite dragonflies’ ecosystem role as top insect predators and their benefit in balancing populations such as mosquitoes and agricultural pests.
- Notes Delaware’s biodiversity context: approximately **136** Odonate species can be found in Delaware.
- References DNREC’s ongoing work: DNREC is “finalizing the **2025** Delaware Wildlife Action Plan (DEWAP)” and **76** dragonfly/damselfly species are candidates for the DEWAP “Species of Greatest Conservation Need” list.
- Cites student engagement: nearly **900** Delaware students participated in the Delaware Dragonfly Project and voted to designate the Blue Dasher.
- The enacted law was **approved July 16, 2025**.
