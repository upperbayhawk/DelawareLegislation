## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware Code Title 16 § 9903 to add **optometrists** to the list of providers eligible for the Delaware Health Care Commission’s **Health Care Provider Loan Repayment Program (HCPLRP)**, and to **cap** optometrist awards at **2 grants per year**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Adds **optometrists** to the definition/list of eligible “new primary care or dental provider” categories for HCPLRP awards.  
- Retains the program’s focus: loan repayment grants are limited to **recruitment and retention** of **new** providers in **ambulatory and outpatient settings**.  
- Keeps the “new provider” eligibility condition: must have completed graduate education **within 2 years of the application** being submitted.  
- Creates an explicit annual cap: the Commission may award loan repayment grants to **no more than 2 optometrists per year**.  
- Does not change the listed eligibility of other provider types (e.g., physicians, nurse practitioners, mental health professionals, dentists), except by adding optometrists into the relevant list.  
- Does not specify any changes to grant amounts, service obligations, or funding levels in the provided text.  
- Implementation is assigned to the Delaware Health Care Commission as part of its existing duty to administer HCPLRP.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expands eligible workforce category**: optometrists become eligible for HCPLRP, potentially supporting recruitment/retention of optometrists in **ambulatory and outpatient settings** (per § 9903(j)(4)).  
- **Controls program exposure**: the bill limits optometrist participation to **2 grants per year** (§ 9903(j)(6)), potentially preventing uncapped growth in awards to this newly eligible group.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
