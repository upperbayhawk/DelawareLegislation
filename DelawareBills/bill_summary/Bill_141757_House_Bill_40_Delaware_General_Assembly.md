## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
To (1) delay the effective date of the State’s online “One-Stop” trade name registration changes from **February 1, 2025** to **June 2, 2025**, and (2) create a **special trade name license** with fees and terms for certain **Delaware corporations and Delaware LLCs that do not transact business in Delaware** but want to register a Delaware trade name.

---

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Moves the effective date of Chapter 459, Volume 84 (online trade name registration/One-Stop effort) from **February 1, 2025** to **June 2, 2025**.
- Requires a **special trade name license** before certain entities can register a trade name: **Delaware corporations/Delaware LLCs not engaging in, prosecuting, or transacting business in Delaware** (Title 6 §3108(b); Title 30 §2306(a)).
- Sets the special trade name license fee at **$25** for a **1-year term**, **expiring December 31** of the year issued.  
- Allows optional renewal of the special trade name license for **3 years** for a fee equal to **3 times** the then-current annual fee (i.e., **3 × $25 = $75** if the fee remains $25 at renewal time).
- Sets a **$15** fee to replace a **lost or stolen** license certificate for the unexpired term.
- Requires the Division of Revenue to maintain electronic entries in the **Delaware Trade Name Registry** and collect a **$25 filing fee** for certificates/entries under specified sections (including Title 6 §3103 and §3108(c)).
- Specifies that the **additional license fee** under **30 Del. C. §2301(d)** does **not** apply solely due to trade name registration filings/fees under §3108 and §2306.
- Fiscal estimate in the fee impact document: up to **2,500** licenses annually, generating up to **$62,500/year**, deposited to the **General Fund**.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Effective date change**: Chapter 459, Volume 84 effective date changed from **February 1, 2025** to **June 2, 2025**.
- **Creates/implements special trade name license requirement** (30 Del. C. §2306) for persons/entities registering a trade name under **6 Del. C. §3108(b)** (Delaware corporations/LLCs not doing business in Delaware).
- **License fee and duration**:
  - **$25** for **1 year**, expiring **December 31** of the year issued (30 Del. C. §2306(a)).
  - Optional **3-year renewal** for **3×** the fee in effect at renewal (30 Del. C. §2306(a)).
  - **$15** replacement fee for lost/stolen license certificate (30 Del. C. §2306(a)).
- **Good standing requirement**: Trade name registration under §3108(b) must be accompanied by a **certificate of good standing from the Secretary of State** (6 Del. C. §3108(b)).
- **Registry and fees**:
  - Division must maintain electronic registry entries and collect **$25** for filings/entries (6 Del. C. §3103; §3108(c)).
- **Additional fee limitation**: The additional license fee under **30 Del. C. §2301(d)** does **not** apply solely because of these trade name filings/fees (30 Del. C. §2306(b)).
- **Penalty cross-reference update**: False affidavit provision now includes filings under **§3108** as triggering “false swearing” (6 Del. C. §3105).

## Enforcement, Penalties, or Compliance Requirements
- **Compliance requirement**: To register a trade name under **6 Del. C. §3108(b)**, the registrant must **first acquire** a **special trade name license** (30 Del. C. §2306(a)) and submit a **certificate of good standing** (6 Del. C. §3108(b)).
- **Penalty**: Willfully making or filing a false affidavit under specified sections, now including **§3108**, constitutes **false swearing** and is punishable accordingly (6 Del. C. §3105).
- **Regulatory authority**: Division of Revenue may adopt regulations to implement/administer/enforce §3108 and §2306 (6 Del. C. §3108(d); 30 Del. C. §2306(c)).
