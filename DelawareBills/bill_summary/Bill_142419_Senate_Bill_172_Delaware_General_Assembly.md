## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend **29 Del. C. § 4603** to require that compensation for **assistant attorneys appointed by the Chief Defender** in the **Office of Defense Services (ODS)** be **at least in parity** with the compensation of **Deputy Attorneys General (DAGs)**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires that compensation for **assistant attorneys** appointed by the **Chief Defender** “**must be at least in parity** with” **DAG** compensation.  
- Keeps existing language that the **Chief Defender fixes compensation** for staff appointed under § 4603(b), but adds a **minimum parity floor** for assistant attorneys.  
- Fiscal note states the Act becomes effective **upon signature by the Governor**.  
- Fiscal note: **cost is unknown and indeterminable** for **FY2026, FY2027, FY2028** because DAG compensation may change through collective bargaining.  
- Fiscal note notes the **Delaware Association of Deputy Attorneys General (ADAG)** became the exclusive bargaining representative on **February 14, 2025**, and the outcome of a collective bargaining agreement is **unknown**.  
- Existing statute language in § 4603(e) continues a prohibition on **outside law practice** for salaried ODS attorneys, with an exemption for those with private practices established as of **January 1, 2015** lasting until **May 28, 2025**.  
- Synopsis states the bill codifies a “longstanding custom and practice” of comparable compensation between DAGs and Assistant Public Defenders, and aligns with ABA guidance.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Compensation parity requirement** may support recruitment/retention of ODS assistant attorneys by ensuring pay is **not below** DAG pay (explicitly required by § 4603(d)).  
- Codifies existing practice described in the **Synopsis**, creating clearer statutory direction for compensation decisions.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Uncertain fiscal exposure**: if DAG compensation increases (including through collective bargaining), ODS assistant attorney compensation may need to rise to maintain “**at least in parity**,” with **unknown and indeterminable** costs (fiscal note).  
- Potential for conflict with annual budget controls: fiscal note emphasizes the **Annual Appropriations Act** limitations and override provisions (fiscal note Assumption #3), which could complicate implementation if appropriations do not align with parity.

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds new subsection **29 Del. C. § 4603(d)**:  
  - “**The compensation of assistant attorneys appointed by the Chief Defender… must be at least in parity with the compensation of Deputy Attorneys General.**”
- Retains existing provisions including:  
  - Chief Defender term for appointments made after **July 1, 2015** is **8 years** (§ 4603(a)).  
  - Assistant Public Defenders and conflicts counsel contractors must be **licensed in Delaware** (§ 4603(b)).  
  - Salaried ODS attorneys are **prohibited from outside private practice**, with a legacy exemption for those established as of **January 1, 2015** that expired **May 28, 2025** (§ 4603(e)).
