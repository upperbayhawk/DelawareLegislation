## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 29, Chapter 81 of the Delaware Code to change the composition and required representation on the Governor’s Council on Agriculture.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases the Governor’s Council on Agriculture from **7 members to 9 members**.  
- Council members continue to be **appointed by the Governor** for **3-year terms**.  
- The Governor continues to appoint the **chair**, who serves **at the pleasure of the Governor**, from among Council members.  
- Requires the Council to include **1 member who is a livestock producer**.  
- Requires the Council to include **1 member who is a farmer aged 40 years or younger**.  
- Bill amends **§ 8108(b)** in **Title 29** (Chapter 81).  

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- Adds **two additional seats** (from **7 to 9**) which may broaden participation and input into agricultural policy (based on the explicit expansion in § 8108(b)).  
- Guarantees representation for a **livestock producer**, ensuring livestock-related perspectives are included (explicit requirement in the bill text).  
- Guarantees representation for a **farmer aged 40 years or younger**, ensuring younger farmer perspectives are included (explicit requirement in the bill text).  

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- Reduces appointing flexibility by imposing **two mandatory categories** of membership (livestock producer; farmer **≤ 40**) that must be filled (explicit requirement in bill text).  
- Enlarging the Council from **7 to 9** could increase coordination/administrative burden associated with meetings and governance (change is explicit; operational effects are not quantified in the text).
