## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amends Title 14 of the Delaware Code to require federally funded schools subject to Title IX to (1) post specific Title IX information prominently on the school webpage and (2) provide annual written reminders to staff and faculty about reporting sex-based discrimination and harassment.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Applies to **any school that receives federal funding** and is **subject to Title IX** requirements.  
- Requires covered schools to **ensure** students, staff, and faculty are protected from **sex-based discrimination**, including **sex-based harassment**.  
- Requires a **prominent** Title IX section on the **school webpage** listing Title IX roles (coordinator, investigator, decision-maker).  
- Webpage must include **Title IX coordinator contact information** (name, email, office address, phone).  
- Webpage must link to the school’s **Title IX policy manual**, the **complaint form**, and the **most recent OCR Title IX FAQ webpage**.  
- Webpage must state the right to file a complaint to start an investigation and the right to **supportive measures** **regardless of whether there is an investigation**.  
- Requires an **annual written reminder** from each Title IX coordinator to **all school staff and faculty** **at the beginning of the academic school year** on reporting policies and how to report.  
- Clarifies schools are **not required to create a website** if they do not already maintain one.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved transparency and access to information:** Required webpage elements (contacts, complaint form link, policy manual link) make it easier for students/staff/faculty to find how to report and who to contact.  
- **Standardized communication:** Mandated annual written reminder at the beginning of each academic year may improve staff/faculty awareness of reporting processes.  
- **Emphasizes supportive measures:** Explicitly states the right to supportive measures **even if no investigation occurs**, which may increase access to assistance.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Administrative burden for covered schools with websites:** Requires creation/maintenance of a prominent Title IX webpage section with multiple specified elements (roles, contacts, links, supportive measures explanation/examples).  
- **Ongoing maintenance requirement:** The required link to the **most recent** U.S. Department of Education OCR Title IX FAQ webpage implies schools must keep that link current.

## Key Policy Changes
- Creates/updates **14 Del. C. § 4150 (Title IX requirements)** to require that covered schools:
  1. **Ensure protection** of students, staff, and faculty from sex-based discrimination, including harassment.  
  2. **Prominently post** on the school webpage a Title IX responsibilities section that includes:
     - Roles/duties of Title IX **coordinator**, **investigator**, and **decision-maker**.  
     - Title IX coordinator **name, email, office address, phone number**.  
     - Link to the school’s **Title IX policy manual**.  
     - Statement of the right to **file a complaint** to start an investigation.  
     - Link to the **complaint form**.  
     - Link to the **most recent OCR Title IX FAQ webpage**.  
     - Statement of the right to **supportive measures** even without an investigation.  
     - Explanation and examples of possible **supportive measures**.  
  3. Require the Title IX coordinator to provide a **written reminder** to all staff and faculty **at the beginning of the academic school year** about reporting policy and how to make a report.
