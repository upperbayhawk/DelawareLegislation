## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the Delaware Child Care Act (Title 14, Chapter 30A) to clarify that **public early education programs run by state or local education agencies** are **not subject to “child care” licensing**, but instead are **monitored under a parallel oversight structure administered by the Office of Child Care Licensing (OCCL)**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Revises definitions to expressly include **early education programs for children below kindergarten operated by public or private schools** within the broader term “child care” (definition section).
- Defines **“Early education program”** as a **public program** operated by an entity under **state ownership and control**, including **local education agencies**, serving children **below kindergarten**.
- Changes §3004A to state that **child care licensing requirements do not apply to an early education program**.
- Establishes that an **early education program must be monitored by OCCL and meet OCCL regulatory requirements** (even though it is not “licensed” under §3004A).
- Keeps the existing exemption that §3004A **does not apply to a maternity ward of a general hospital**.
- The bill’s stated intent is to ensure **health, safety, and child development standards** are met through **OCCL monitoring** rather than licensing for these public early education programs.
- No specific **implementation dates, appropriations, or penalties** are stated in the provided bill text.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Clarifies regulatory treatment**: Explicitly states that public early education programs (below kindergarten) run by state/local education agencies are **not subject to child care licensing** under §3004A. (Synopsis; §3004A(d)(1))
- **Maintains oversight**: Requires these early education programs to be **monitored by OCCL** and to **meet OCCL regulatory requirements**, supporting **health, safety, and child development standards**. (Synopsis; §3004A(d)(1))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Potential compliance/transition burden**: Programs previously treated as licensed under §3004A may need to shift to the bill’s **monitoring-based framework** and confirm which OCCL requirements apply in that parallel structure. (Synopsis; §3004A(d)(1))
- **Reduced clarity on “parallel structure” details**: The bill references a “parallel regulatory and monitoring structure” but does not spell out its mechanics in the text provided. (Synopsis)
