## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 9 to clarify and expand county finance/assessment subpoena authority in real property assessment disputes when income or cost comparison valuation approaches are used, and to specify enforcement mechanisms (including Superior Court contempt and potential corporate charter consequences for Delaware entities).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Defines “county authority” to mean: (1) **Chief Financial Officer of New Castle County Office of Finance**, (2) **Director of the Department of Finance of Kent County**, or (3) **Finance Director of Sussex County**.  
- Authorizes each county board of assessment or county finance office/department to **subpoena witnesses and documents** related to the correctness of a **real property assessed value** when an **income approach** or **cost comparison approach** is used by the county or an appealing property owner.  
- Provides that a subpoena is **effective throughout Delaware** and may be enforced **in other states or countries** “through available legal means.”  
- Specifies service methods for subpoenas on **individuals** (personal service, abode service, or **certified mail**) and on **entities** (service on registered agent or authorized person, including **certified mail**).  
- Allows the **Superior Court**, upon application by the county authority, to issue an **order compelling compliance** with a properly served subpoena.  
- Failure to obey a Superior Court enforcement order may be punished as **contempt of court**.  
- For a **Delaware corporation or other Delaware business entity**, refusing to obey such an order is deemed an **abuse/misuse of corporate powers** under **8 Del. C. § 284**, supporting potential **Attorney General** proceedings to revoke a charter.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improves evidence access in valuation disputes:** Explicit subpoena power for testimony and records when **income** or **cost comparison** approaches are used could help counties and appellants obtain documents relevant to assessed value accuracy (e.g., income/expense or cost data).  
- **Clarifies who can act for counties:** The bill precisely defines “county authority” for all three counties, reducing uncertainty about who may issue/serve subpoenas and seek enforcement.  
- **Creates a clear enforcement pathway:** Superior Court orders and contempt authority provide a concrete mechanism to compel compliance when recipients do not respond.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Greater compliance burden on recipients:** Individuals and entities may face additional legal and administrative burden responding to subpoenas for “books, papers, records, memoranda, or other evidence” in assessment matters.  
- **Escalated consequences for Delaware entities:** Noncompliance by a Delaware corporation or other Delaware business entity can be treated as **abuse/misuse of corporate powers** under **8 Del. C. § 284**, potentially exposing entities to severe remedies (up to charter revocation proceedings) tied to subpoena enforcement orders.

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- The text does not specify **procedural standards** for when a subpoena is appropriate beyond the condition that income/cost approaches are relied upon (e.g., limits on scope, relevance disputes, confidentiality protections).  
- The bill states subpoenas may be enforced “in other states or countries through available legal means,” but does not specify **which mechanisms** or how counties will pursue such enforcement.  
- The bill does not detail how often counties expect to use this authority or what categories of records are most frequently sought.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Definition added/clarified:** “County authority” expressly defined as the senior finance official for **New Castle, Kent, and Sussex** counties.  
- **Subpoena authority expanded/clarified:** Boards of assessment and county finance offices/departments may:
  - **Subpoena witnesses**, compel attendance/testimony, administer oaths/affirmations, take evidence, and require production of records **bearing upon the correctness** of a real property assessed value, **whenever** the county or appealing property owner relies on **income** or **cost comparison** valuation approaches.  
- **Statewide and extraterritorial effect:** Subpoenas are effective throughout Delaware and may be enforced outside Delaware through available legal means.  
- **Service rules specified:** Allows personal service, abode service, and **certified mail** for individuals; service on registered agents or authorized individuals (including **certified mail**) for entities.  
- **Court enforcement and contempt:** Superior Court may order compliance; disobedience may be punished as **contempt of court**.  
- **Corporate consequence linkage:** For a Delaware corporation or other Delaware business entity, refusal to obey a court order enforcing the subpoena is deemed abuse/misuse of corporate powers under **8 Del. C. § 284** (relevant to potential charter revocation proceedings by the Attorney General).

## Who Benefits

- **Property owners appealing assessments** when they rely on income or cost comparison approaches and need compelled testimony/documents to support their position.  
- **Superior Court process** (more explicit statutory basis for issuing enforcement orders and using contempt powers in this context).

## Who May Be Negatively Affected
