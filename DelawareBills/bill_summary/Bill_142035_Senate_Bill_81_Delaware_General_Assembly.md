## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware Code Title 14, § 1312(g) to adjust how eligible teachers’ and specialists’ prior paraprofessional experience is credited on the state salary schedule, and to set when that credit begins.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Provides salary-schedule experience credit to a **teacher or specialist** who previously worked as a **Title I or instructional paraprofessional** in a public school for **at least 4 years full-time**.  
- The credit equals **1 year of salary-schedule experience for every 2 years** of full-time paraprofessional experience.  
- Eligibility requires the individual to have **held a valid paraprofessional permit** issued by the Department of Education (or an equivalent agency in another state) **before** becoming a teacher/specialist.  
- The law text retains the clause: **“This subsection only applies to teachers hired after August 9, 2023.”**  
- Adds a procedural rule: experience credit **must start upon the date of completion and approval of an application to the employer**.  
- Fiscal note: potential **state and local salary costs** from moving eligible employees to higher salary steps; total cost is **indeterminable** for FY2026–FY2028.  
- The Act was **approved September 2, 2025**.

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Higher pay for eligible staff:** Moving an eligible teacher/specialist to a higher step on the salary schedule increases compensation (bill ties credit directly to the **salary schedule under § 1305**).  
- **Clear start date for credit:** Establishes that credit begins when the **application is completed and approved** by the employer, which may improve administrative clarity.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Uncertain fiscal exposure:** Fiscal note states the fiscal impact is **indeterminable** because current placements and adjusted steps are unknown; step-to-step cost differences range from **$423.01 to $4,275.65 (state)** and **$181.29 to $1,832.42 (local)**, including **OECs at 33.34%**.  
- **Potential administrative workload:** Requires an **application** process and employer approval to trigger credit (additional HR/DOE processing implied by the new requirement).

## Key Policy Changes
- **Permit language clarified:** Changes eligibility condition to require the person **“held a valid paraprofessional permit”** (rather than “has been issued a permit”) by DOE or an equivalent out-of-state agency prior to becoming a teacher/specialist.  
- **Adds an application-based start date:** Experience credit for paraprofessional years **must start upon the date of completion and approval of an application to the employer**.  
- **Maintains credit formula and minimum experience threshold:** Requires **at least 4 years** full-time paraprofessional experience; grants **1 year credit per 2 years** worked.  
- **Hire-date applicability (as enacted text reads):** States it **only applies to teachers hired after August 9, 2023**.

## Who Benefits
