## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 10, § 906 of the Delaware Code to add **one additional Family Court judge** and allocate that position to **Sussex County**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Increases the total number of Delaware **Family Court judges from 17 to 18**.  
- Changes the post-appointment county residency allocation to require **4 judges in Sussex County** (up from 3).  
- Keeps the allocation at **10 judges in New Castle County** and **3 judges in Kent County**.  
- Leaves in place the requirement that judges be **Delaware residents for 5 years** immediately prior to appointment.  
- Leaves in place that the **Chief Judge may reside in any county**.  
- Does not change the existing political party limitation language in § 906(b) (as printed in the bill).  
- The synopsis states the added Sussex judge position **was funded in the FY 2025 budget** signed **June 30, 2024**.  
- The enacted law was **approved May 27, 2025**.
