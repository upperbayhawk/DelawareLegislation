## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend the City of Harrington’s charter to expressly authorize the City Council to impose and collect a **municipal lodging tax up to 3% of rent**, on top of the existing **state lodging tax under Chapter 61 of Title 30**, and make this clarification **retroactive to March 4, 2024**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Grants Harrington City Council explicit charter authority to **impose and collect a lodging tax**.
- Sets a cap: the municipal lodging tax may be **no more than 3% of the rent**.
- Applies to occupancy of rooms in **hotels, motels, or tourist homes** as defined in **30 Del. C. § 6101**.
- Tax applies only to establishments **located within Harrington city boundaries**.
- Clarifies the municipal tax is **in addition to** the tax imposed by the State under **Title 30, Chapter 61**.
- The Act takes effect **retroactively beginning March 4, 2024**.
- Bill synopsis notes Harrington adopted a lodging tax ordinance on **March 4, 2024**, and that ordinance **does not apply** to lodging **reservations or contracts executed or completed before** the ordinance’s effective date.
- Requires a **two-thirds vote** in each chamber to pass because it amends a municipal charter (Delaware Constitution, **Article IX, § 1**).
