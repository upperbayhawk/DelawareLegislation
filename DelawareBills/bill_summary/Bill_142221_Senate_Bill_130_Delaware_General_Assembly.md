## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 7 of the Delaware Code to prohibit retail stores and wholesalers from selling, offering for sale, or distributing most expanded polystyrene foam products in Delaware, with limited exemptions and a waiver process administered by the Department of Natural Resources and Environmental Control (DNREC).

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Prohibits **retail stores and wholesalers** from selling, offering for sale, or distributing **expanded polystyrene foam (EPS) products**, including **EPS food service packaging**, **EPS loose fill packaging (packing peanuts)**, and **EPS coolers**.  
- Creates specific **exemptions**, including **portion cups ≤ 2 ounces** (if used for hot foods or foods requiring lids) and **manufacturer pre-packaged food** using EPS.  
- Allows **meat and fish trays** for raw/butchered items sold from refrigeration, but **black EPS packaging is prohibited under any circumstance**.  
- Allows EPS coolers **only when used to ship food that requires cold storage**.  
- Authorizes DNREC to grant **waivers up to 1 year** when alternatives are not available, including a specific pathway for small businesses with **< $500,000 gross annual income**.  
- DNREC must prescribe the **form and manner** of waiver applications and may adopt **rules, regulations, and procedures** to implement the law.  
- **Effective date: January 1, 2027.**

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Reduces litter and landfill waste** from EPS products by prohibiting retail/wholesale distribution of common EPS items (food service packaging, packing peanuts, coolers), consistent with findings that EPS constitutes a “significant and growing portion of litter” and persists in landfills for very long periods.
- **Addresses recycling limitations** noted in the bill findings: EPS is **not accepted in Delaware’s curbside recycling program** and cannot be recycled once used for food or liquids (per legislative findings).
- **Creates targeted flexibility** via DNREC **waivers (up to 1 year)** when alternatives are not feasible/available, including for smaller businesses under a **< $500,000 gross annual income** threshold.

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Compliance and supply-chain changes** for retail stores and wholesalers that currently sell or distribute EPS food service packaging, loose fill packaging, and coolers, due to the prohibition beginning **January 1, 2027**.
- **Potential added administrative burden** on businesses seeking exemptions/waivers, because waivers require a **written application** and DNREC sets the required form and manner.
- **Limited allowance for certain uses** could constrain some packaging options (e.g., prohibition applies broadly, with only enumerated exemptions; black EPS meat/fish trays are barred “under any circumstance”).

## Health Impact
- Legislative findings state EPS contains **styrene and benzene**, described in the bill as **suspected carcinogens and neurotoxins hazardous to humans**, and that hot foods/liquids may break down EPS leading to toxin absorption.  
- The operative provisions regulate **sale/distribution** of EPS products (not direct health standards), but the bill’s stated rationale includes reducing exposure concerns tied to EPS food and beverage containers.

## Key Policy Changes
- Adds/creates **§ 6099B** in Title 7 establishing restrictions on EPS products and key definitions, including:
  - “Expanded polystyrene foam food service packaging” (cups, plates, bowls, trays, hinged/lidded containers), with exclusions for certain raw foods packaging.
  - “Expanded polystyrene foam loose fill packaging” (packing peanuts).
  - “Expanded polystyrene foam cooler” (portable cold storage container), excluding containers for **drugs, medical devices, or biological materials**.
- Prohibits retail stores and wholesalers from selling/offering/distributing EPS products, with exemptions:
  1. **Portion cups of 2 ounces or less**, if used for **hot foods** or foods **requiring lids**.
  2. **Meat and fish trays** for raw/butchered meat/poultry/fish sold from refrigeration, **except black EPS packaging is prohibited under any circumstance**.
  3. EPS coolers **only if used to ship food that requires cold storage**.
  4. Food **pre-packaged by the manufacturer** with EPS packaging.
  5. Any other EPS product **as determined by DNREC**.
- Establishes **DNREC waiver authority** (written application; waiver up to **1 year**) when:
  - no feasible/commercially available alternative exists; or
  - the retail store/wholesaler has **< $500,000 gross annual income** and no reasonably affordable, commercially available alternative exists.
- Conforming amendment reference: **§ 6099A** is amended to adjust wording (“For purposes of this subchapter, section...”), but the provided text does not show further substantive change beyond that line.
