## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 14 to expand the Delaware Educator Apprenticeship Program beyond teacher apprenticeships to also include (1) a paraprofessional registered occupation pathway and (2) registered youth apprenticeship programs, with a required pilot starting in the **2025-2026 school year**.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Expands the Delaware Educator Apprenticeship Program to include **teacher apprenticeships**, **paraprofessional registered occupation**, and **registered youth apprenticeship** programs.  
- Defines **“paraprofessional”** as a public school employee covered by **14 Del. C. § 1324**, including **paraeducator/teacher aide**.  
- Requires a **pilot paraprofessional registered occupation program** between **at least 1 institute of higher education** and **at least 1 district or charter school** for **2025-2026**.  
- Directs that the paraprofessional registered occupation be registered under the K-12 teacher apprenticeship program and designed for aspiring paraprofessionals to obtain an **associate’s degree**, with a “smooth transition” into teacher residency/apprenticeship programs.  
- Requires registered youth apprenticeship to align with **state and federal pre-apprenticeship standards**, offer high school students chances to **accumulate hours toward educator certification**, and earn a **minimum number of higher education credits** (minimum number not specified).  
- Requires the Department of Education (DOE), with the Department of Labor (DOL), to **apply for State Apprenticeship Expansion Formula (SAEF) grants** (formula and competitive) for **2025-2026** (if eligible) and pursue other available state/federal funding.  
- Fiscal note estimates costs of **$240,615 (FY2026)**, **$376,811 (FY2027)**, and **$994,517 (FY2028)**, tied to projected apprenticeship participation and tuition/fee costs.  

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Expands educator pipeline**: Adds pathways (paraprofessional and youth apprenticeship) intended to “support educator recruitment and increase the pipeline of qualified educators” and address an “educator shortage.”  
- **Creates earlier entry points**: Registered youth apprenticeship begins in high school and allows accumulation of certification hours plus higher-education credits (minimum credit requirement referenced but not quantified).  
- **Structured progression**: Paraprofessional pathway is explicitly designed to lead to an **associate’s degree** and provide a “smooth transition” to teacher residency/apprenticeship programs.  
- **Potential federal funding offset**: DOE/DOL must apply for **SAEF grants** for the **2025-2026** pilot year (if eligible), and the fiscal note indicates pilot-year funding could be covered by existing state appropriation if grants are unavailable/insufficient.  

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Significant projected costs**: Fiscal note projects state costs rising to **$994,517 in FY2028**, with per-student annual cost estimates as high as **$23,347–$24,246** at Wilmington University (tuition/fees/books/materials/licensure exams).  
- **Expansion contingent on “interest and funding”**: Growth beyond the pilot is explicitly dependent on interest and funding, which may limit scalability.  
- **Uncertain net cost after aid**: Fiscal note states student aid could reduce costs, but the “exact reduction…is unknown” until candidates are identified and aid eligibility determined.  
- **Minimum higher-ed credit requirement unspecified**: Youth apprenticeship must require a “minimum number” of credits, but the bill text does not set the threshold.

## Key Policy Changes
- **Adds definition**: “Paraprofessional” (public school employee under **14 Del. C. § 1324**, including paraeducator/teacher aide).  
- **Expands program scope**: Delaware Educator Apprenticeship Program now includes:  
  1) Teacher apprenticeships  
  2) **Paraprofessional registered occupation**  
  3) **Registered youth apprenticeship**  
- **Creates pilot requirement**: At least one paraprofessional registered occupation pilot program for **2025-2026**, with expansion based on **interest and funding** in **2026-2027** and beyond (as funding is available).  
- **Mandates grant applications**: DOE/DOL **must apply** for **SAEF grants** (formula and competitive) for **2025-2026** (if eligible) and for other available funding.  
- **Updates applicant selection considerations** to include commitments to youth apprenticeship and paraprofessional pathways and transitions into teacher certification programs.
