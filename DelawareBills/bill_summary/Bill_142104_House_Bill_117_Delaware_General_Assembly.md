## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Title 3 of the Delaware Code to require additional notice—specifically to the Delaware Farm Bureau—when the State or a political subdivision seeks bids to lease certain State-owned agricultural land.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Creates a new chapter in Title 3, Part II: **Chapter 20, “State-Owned Agricultural Land Leases.”**
- Defines **“State-owned agricultural land”** as land **10 acres or more** that is owned by the State or a political subdivision and **used or intended for agricultural use** (as defined in **Title 3, § 902**).
- Requires that when the State or a political subdivision **seeks bids to lease** qualifying land, **notice of the request for bids must be provided to the Delaware Farm Bureau** (or its successor).
- The Farm Bureau notice is intended **to be distributed or made available to farmers**.
- The Farm Bureau notice requirement is **in addition to** any other legally required procurement/notice process for leasing State-owned property.
- Does not specify a required format, timing, or method for providing notice to the Farm Bureau.
- Bill release date shown on the text: **04/10/2025 (11:59 AM)**.
