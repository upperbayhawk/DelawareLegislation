## Distilled Summary

## Purpose
Amend Delaware’s Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) and related education statutes to require public bodies to digitally record meetings, post recordings online within a set timeframe, retain them online for a minimum period, and align/clarify how FOIA meeting rules interact with Title 14 (education) meeting requirements.

## Executive Summary (5-8 bullets)
- Requires **public bodies** to **digitally record all meetings** (audio-only allowed) and **post recordings within 7 business days** after the meeting concludes; recordings must remain posted **at least 1 year**. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5))
- Allows **executive session portions** of recordings to be **withheld** from the posted recording, consistent with existing executive session confidentiality rules. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(2) & (f)(5)b.)
- Specifies that **technology failures** preventing/limiting recording **do not invalidate** the meeting or actions taken. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5)c.)
- For executive-branch public bodies that **meet 4 or fewer times per year**, requires posting **draft minutes within 20 business days** after the meeting (on a State website approved by the Secretary of State). (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(3))
- Requires executive-branch public bodies to post **final approved minutes within 5 business days** of approval (on the State-approved website). (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(4))
- Changes higher-ed Board of Trustees livestream rule: meeting recordings must be posted **within 7 business days** (replacing “within a reasonable time”) and retained **at least 1 year**. (29 Del. C. § 10008(c))
- Updates Title 14 requirements for the **State Board of Education** and **school district/voc-tech/charter boards** to match FOIA’s recording/posting/retention requirements and related exceptions. (14 Del. C. §§ 104(b)(13), 208)
- Clarifies for certain education boards that **when Title 14 conflicts with FOIA, Title 14 controls** (for charter school boards and school district boards). (14 Del. C. §§ 503(c)(1)b., 1048(f)(2))

## Pros (Potential Benefits)
- **Improved public access/transparency**: requires posting meeting recordings **within 7 business days** and keeping them available **for at least 1 year**. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5)b.)
- **Timelier availability of minutes** for some state executive-branch bodies: **draft minutes within 20 business days** (for bodies meeting **4 or fewer times/year**) and **final minutes within 5 business days** of approval. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(3)–(4))
- **Uniform baseline statewide**: FOIA explicitly adds a recording requirement for public bodies, with a standard posting and retention rule. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5))
- **Accessibility accommodation**: Department of Education must provide a **written transcript** or other reasonable accommodation **within 7 business days upon request** for a hearing-impaired individual for a recorded regular monthly State Board meeting. (14 Del. C. § 104(b)(13)d.)
- **Greater clarity for higher education trustees**: replaces “reasonable time” with a defined **7 business day** posting timeline and **1-year** retention. (29 Del. C. § 10008(c))

## Cons (Potential Drawbacks)
- **Increased compliance workload**: requires **digital recording of all meetings** and posting within **7 business days** plus **1-year** retention. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5))
- **Website administration and storage demands**: recordings must remain posted **at least 1 year**, which may require ongoing hosting/records management. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5)b.)
- **Potential inconsistency between FOIA and Title 14** is preserved/heightened: bill expressly states that **Title 14 controls if it conflicts with FOIA** for certain education boards. (14 Del. C. §§ 503(c)(1)b., 1048(f)(2))

## Open Questions / Ambiguities
- **Which specific “website” must host recordings** for non-executive-branch public bodies: FOIA requires posting on “the website where meeting notices and minutes are posted,” but does not specify a uniform platform for all bodies. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5)b.)
- **Scope of “public body” coverage** for the recording requirement: the bill adds a general FOIA requirement, but the text provided does not enumerate which bodies are exempt (if any) beyond executive-session and certain Title 14 carveouts. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f))
- **Meaning of “record all meetings” vs. meeting types**: Title 14 has exclusions (e.g., **workshops/retreats where no voting will take place**), but FOIA recording requirement is framed broadly; interaction may depend on whether those gatherings are “meetings” under FOIA. (14 Del. C. § 208(e); 29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5))

## Key Policy Changes
- **New FOIA requirement**: public bodies must **digitally record all meetings** (audio-only allowed). (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5)a.)
- **Posting/retention requirement**: recordings must be posted within **7 business days** and retained online **for at least 1 year**. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5)b.)
- **Executive session carveout for recordings**: executive session portions may be withheld from the posted recording. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(2) & (f)(5)b.)
- **Technology failure safe harbor**: recording failures do not invalidate meetings/actions. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5)c.)
- **Minutes timing rules for certain executive-branch bodies**:
  - If subject to FOIA and meets **4 or fewer times/year**: post **draft minutes within 20 business days**. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(3))
  - Post **final approved minutes within 5 business days** of approval. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(4))
- **Higher education trustees**: recording of livestreamed meeting must be posted within **7 business days** and remain **≥1 year**. (29 Del. C. § 10008(c))
- **Title 14 conforming and conflict rules**:
  - For charter school boards: if FOIA conflicts with Title 14, **Title 14 applies**. (14 Del. C. § 503(c)(1)b.)
  - For school district boards: if FOIA conflicts with Title 14, **Title 14 applies**. (14 Del. C. § 1048(f)(2))
- **Technical drafting changes**: consolidates/transfers certain definitions in Title 14 and removes repetitive language. (Synopsis; 14 Del. C. §§ 503, 504B, 1041)

## Who Benefits
- Members of the public and media seeking **timely access** to meeting content via recordings available **within 7 business days** and retained **≥1 year**. (29 Del. C. § 10004(f)(5)b.)
- Individuals unable to attend meetings live, including stakeholders of **school boards** and the **State Board of Education**.
- Hearing-impaired individuals requesting **transcripts/accommodations** for recorded State Board regular monthly meetings (**within 7 business days upon request**). (14 Del. C. § 104(b)(13)d.)
